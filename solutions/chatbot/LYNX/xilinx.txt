LYNX & XILINX

WHAT WE'RE DOING TOGETHER

Lynx has partnered closely with Xilinx (now a wholly-owned part of AMD) for several years. Customers in the aerospace and defense fields like the combination of powerful computing with the flexibility to add custom interfaces and hardware accelerators. Lynx committed to supporting Xilinx’s Arm’s UltraScale+ and MPSoC platforms as one of the first hardware targets for Lynx’s LynxSecure separation kernel hypervisor, as shown 
here
 in late 2018.
With customers such as General Atomics harnessing the LYNX MOSA.ic software platform for its Gray Eagle drones and others writing secure, efficient bare metal code for safety-critical applications, the Xilinx family is one of the most popular System on Chips (SoCs) that we support.
