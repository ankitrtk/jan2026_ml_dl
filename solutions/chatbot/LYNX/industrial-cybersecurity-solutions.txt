The Challenges Facing Modern Industrial Systems

The industrial sector is undergoing a rapid transformation driven by digitalization and automation. But with growth comes challenges, and today’s industrial leaders face complex issues such as:

Cybersecurity Threats
: With IoT adoption increasing, vulnerabilities in industrial control systems (ICS) expose critical operations to potential attacks.

Regulatory Complexity
: Meeting standards like 
IEC 62443
 or 
NIST SP 800-82
 can be daunting and resource-intensive.

System Downtime
: Downtime caused by maintenance issues or cyberattacks can result in millions of dollars in lost productivity.

Lifecycle Management
: Industrial systems often outlive their initial software and hardware, creating gaps in security and functionality over time.
