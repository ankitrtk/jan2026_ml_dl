 Vigiles Enterprise: Transforming SBOM Management for Global Teams 

Discover how a multinational medical device company streamlined security and compliance across 30+ global divisions. By integrating Vigiles Enterprise into their processes, they achieved scalable SBOM management, reduced false positives by 95%, and empowered teams with tools designed for collaboration and efficiency.

Redefining SBOM Management for Global Enterprises

A leading multinational medical device manufacturer faced challenges managing software bills of materials (SBOMs) across multiple divisions and geographies. They needed a scalable, intuitive solution to streamline compliance, eliminate noise from false positives, and enable seamless collaboration. With Vigiles Enterprise, they transformed their vulnerability management process, improved product security, and optimized operations at scale.

This blog
 explains best practices for build-time SBOM generation and how they integrate seamlessly into embedded CI/CD pipelines for stronger, data-driven security.

About the Client: A Leader in Medical Innovation

Industry:
 Medical Devices

Global Presence:
 30+ Divisions Worldwide

Primary Focus:
 Secure, compliant software for medical innovations.

Business Drivers:
 Security, regulatory compliance, operational efficiency

Challenge

Navigating Complexity: The Challenges of Scalable SBOM Management

The company needed a solution to address pressing issues in SBOM management:

Decentralized Operations:
 Independent projects managed across divisions created inconsistencies in SBOM workflows.

Vulnerability Noise:
 Existing tools generated numerous false positives, leading to wasted time and resources.

Collaboration Gaps:
 A lack of unified tools hindered efficient oversight by the central cybersecurity team.

Compliance Risks:
 Regulatory demands required precise SBOM documentation in SPDX and CycloneDX formats.

Without a scalable solution, the company risked delays, compliance failures, and resource inefficiencies.

Solution

Vigiles Enterprise: Empowering Teams with Scalability and Precision

Vigiles Enterprise addressed these challenges through a tailored, scalable approach:

Automated SBOM Generation

Generated SBOMs in SPDX and CycloneDX formats to meet industry standards.

Integrated SBOM workflows into CI/CD pipelines across product lines.

Benefit:
 Simplified compliance documentation and reduced manual work.

Intelligent Vulnerability Triaging

Leveraged curated vulnerability data to eliminate irrelevant CVEs.

Configured intelligent filters to prioritize critical vulnerabilities.

Benefit:
 Reduced noise, saving time and improving product security.

Scalable Enterprise Integration

Deployed single sign-on (SSO) for seamless collaboration among global teams.

Hosted a dedicated on-prem Vigiles Enterprise server to meet data sovereignty needs.

Benefit:
 Enabled secure, efficient global operations with centralized oversight.

Results

Results That Matter: Security, Efficiency, and Collaboration

With Vigiles Enterprise, the company achieved transformative results:

95% Fewer False Positives:
 Curated vulnerability data drastically reduced triaging time.

Enhanced Collaboration:
 Single sign-on simplified teamwork across 30+ global divisions.

Regulatory Confidence:
 Industry-standard SBOMs ensured compliance with medical device regulations.

Future-Ready Operations:
 Scalable solutions positioned the company for evolving security challenges.
