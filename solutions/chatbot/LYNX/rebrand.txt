Why This Rebrand Matters

A Message from Our CEO

Dear Valued Customers and Partners,

As we enter an exciting new chapter in the evolution of Lynx, I am delighted to share some transformative news with you. At the start of this year, Lynx Software Technologies, along with our trusted subsidiaries—Timesys, Thompson Software Solutions, and CoreAVI—united under a single, bold identity: 
Lynx
. This rebranding symbolizes our collective vision, strength, and commitment to driving innovation at the forefront of technology.

Seize the Edge
 is not just our new tagline; it is a call to action. It reflects our unwavering focus on enabling our customers and partners to lead with confidence in an increasingly connected, intelligent, and secure world. By harmonizing our expertise across safety-critical software, open-source solutions, embedded virtualization, and AI-GPU enablement, we are uniquely positioned to help you achieve unparalleled success at the edge.

Your partnership has been instrumental in shaping our journey, and as we embrace this new identity, our commitment to your success remains steadfast. Together, we will navigate the complexities of modern systems and seize the immense opportunities that lie at the edge.

Thank you for your continued trust and collaboration. We look forward to embarking on this transformative journey with you.

Tim Reed, 
CEO 
of Lynx

Who We Are: 
 The Foundations of Lynx’s Strength

Our new identity builds upon decades of leadership, expertise, and innovation from three industry pioneers:

Timesys:
Embedded Linux & Cybersecurity

Timesys brought embedded Linux leadership, offering real-time operating systems (RTOS), security solutions, and custom Linux development services for mission-critical systems. Now part of Lynx, Timesys’ expertise in embedded Linux, SBOM management, cybersecurity, and open-source solutions continues to thrive, supported by Lynx’s expanded resources and innovative solutions.

CoreAVI:
Safety-Critical GPU & Compute

CoreAVI has been instrumental in advancing high-performance GPU technologies for safety-critical compute applications in aviation, automotive, and industrial applications. Their expertise in certified GPU solutions and safety-critical graphics now enhances Lynx’s ability to push the boundaries of mission-critical edge systems, ensuring reliability, security, and performance. 

 Learn More About the CoreAVI Rebrand  

Thompson Software Solutions:
Aerospace & Defense

Thompson Software Solutions was a leader in aerospace and defense systems, providing modular software and certification solutions that met the most stringent regulatory requirements. Now part of Lynx, Thompson’s commitment to safety, security, and modular systems enhances Lynx’s ability to deliver best-in-class solutions to global industries.
