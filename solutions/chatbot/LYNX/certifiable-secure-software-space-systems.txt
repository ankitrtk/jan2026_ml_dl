The Challenges of Space Systems Development

The space industry faces relentless pressures to innovate while ensuring reliability and compliance. At Lynx, we understand the complexities involved in developing space systems that must endure harsh environments and meet strict regulatory requirements.

Unforgiving Environments
: Space systems must endure extreme temperatures, radiation, and the vacuum of space while performing flawlessly.

Complex Certification Processes
: Compliance with standards like 
DO-178C
 and 
ISO 26262
 adds significant time and complexity to development.

Long Lifecycle Maintenance
: Systems must remain secure and operational for decades with limited opportunities for physical updates.

Evolving Cybersecurity Threats
: Supply chains and software vulnerabilities are growing targets for malicious actors.
