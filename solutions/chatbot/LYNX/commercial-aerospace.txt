Navigating the Complexities of Modern Aerospace

The commercial aerospace industry faces unprecedented challenges as programs grow more complex and interconnected. Legacy systems struggle to keep pace, while meeting rigorous safety and compliance standards demands more resources than ever.

Lengthy Certification Timelines
: Delays in achieving compliance with 
DO-178C
 and FAA standards put programs at risk of missed deadlines.

Managing Mixed-Criticality Systems
: Combining safety-critical and non-critical workloads on multicore systems without compromising performance or safety.

Cybersecurity and Lifecycle Risks
: Keeping embedded systems secure for decades in the face of evolving threats and hardware obsolescence.

Vendor Lock-In
: Relying on closed, proprietary systems can lead to long-term inflexibility and higher costs.
