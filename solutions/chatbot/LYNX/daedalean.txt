LYNX & Daedalean

WHAT WE'RE DOING TOGETHER

Founded in 2016, Daedalean is building autonomous piloting software systems for civil aircraft of today and advanced aerial mobility of tomorrow. The company has 100+ people, including 11 PhDs and 9 pilots, with expertise in machine learning, robotics, computer vision, path planning, as well as aviation-grade software engineering, certification, safety assessment, and flight testing.
Lynx is particularly excited with how Daedalean is working with the aviation authorities in 
Europe
 and 
USA
 to find ways to harness artificial intelligence (AI) in safety certifiable systems.
