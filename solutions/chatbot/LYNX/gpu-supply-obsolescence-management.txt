 GPU Supply & Obsolescence Management 

Today’s graphics processors are excellent at rendering high quality graphics and can have parallel processing capabilities that bring new opportunities to the mission-critical edge. However, they are also not always manufactured to industrial, military, or aerospace standards or architected to support safety certification. They usually also have a shorter production life cycle than the typical aerospace or military program. 

Lynx Software now secures the edge with a selection of extended temperature screened and Dynamic Temperature Range validated graphics processors and system-on-chip (SOC) processors. Customers can make lot buys from Lynx of these program-ready GPUs and we will manage the inventory in a climate- controlled environment for the life of your program.
