LYNX & INTEL

LYNX & INTEL

WHAT WE'RE DOING TOGETHER

Lynx and Intel have partnered closely to ensure our LYNX MOSA.ic software framework (hypervisor, real-time operating system, Linux, and tools) has been optimized for processors from the Xeon and Atom product families. Our collaboration extends beyond development to include certification work, ensuring SEAL and DO-178C DAL A certifications are in place for particular components:

Elkhart Lake

TigerLake

Ice Lake

In summary, if it’s a “Lake” from Intel – the chances are that Lynx is supporting it with software that harnesses all possible hardware hooks in the underlying processor to deliver strong isolation between applications running in virtual machines and guarantee performance levels no matter what else is happening in the system.
