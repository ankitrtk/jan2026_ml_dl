 Modernizing the Mission-Critical Edge Without Breaking Assurance 

Modernization at the mission-critical edge is a balancing act: introducing advanced AI and accelerated computing while preserving the assurance these systems depend on. Edge platforms must operate predictably in constrained, high-risk environments where failure isn’t an option and certification is non-negotiable. The path forward isn’t faster hardware alone, but architecture designed for determinism, isolation, and long-term trust, so new capabilities can be deployed safely, incrementally, and with confidence.

 Unifying AI and GPU Compute for Mission- and Safety-Critical Deployment 

Enabling GPU compute for safety-critical systems becomes significantly more complex when AI is introduced. LYNX MOSA.ic.AI® provides a unified CPU and GPU software platform that brings determinism, certification readiness, and lifecycle governance to AI and GPU-accelerated workloads. It allows advanced graphics, compute, and AI applications to run predictably within mission-critical environments while reducing integration risk and maintaining system integrity.
