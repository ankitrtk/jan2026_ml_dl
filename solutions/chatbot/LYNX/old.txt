General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, Inc. This modular framework ensured that critical functions remained fast and deterministic, enabling GE-ER to meet the rigorous demands of defense operations while paving the way for future certification and enhanced mission readiness.

 Advancing the Edge of Avionics 

LYNX MOSA.ic Enhances F-35 Lightning II's Mission Systems

Lockheed Martin, in collaboration with Lynx, has elevated the F-35 Lightning II's mission system avionics through the integration of LYNX MOSA.ic. This modular software framework supports the "Technology Refresh 3" (TR3) modernization program, focusing on enhancing performance, reducing costs, and enabling future capabilities. LYNX MOSA.ic powers key components like the Integrated Core Processor (ICP), which processes critical data for communications, sensors, and control systems. This integration ensures the F-35 remains at the forefront of technological advancement, meeting the evolving demands of modern defense operations.

 Accelerating Medical Device Development at the Edge  

Lynx Enables 70% Cost Savings for Infection Control Specialist

An infection control company specializing in medical device sterilization partnered with Lynx to expedite the development of a new embedded product.
