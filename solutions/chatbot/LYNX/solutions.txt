 General Atomics 

 Powering the Future Edge of UAS 

Lynx Drives Safety, Efficiency, and Resilience for General Atomics' Gray Eagle Extended Range Aircraft

General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, Inc. partnered with Lynx to modernize the Gray Eagle Extended Range (GE-ER) Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS), focusing on enhancing safety, cost efficiency, and system resilience. LYNX MOSA.ic™ enabled secure integration of real-time operating systems, commercial OSes, and bare-metal applications on a single platform, ensuring fast, deterministic performance for mission-critical operations. The solution paved the way for future certifications and long-term mission readiness.

 Lockheed Martin 

 Advancing the Edge of Avionics 

Lynx Enhances F-35 Lightning II’s Mission Systems for Lockheed Martin

Lynx collaborated with Lockheed Martin to elevate the F-35 Lightning II’s avionics through the integration of LYNX MOSA.ic as part of the "Technology Refresh 3" (TR3) modernization program. This solution supports the Integrated Core Processor (ICP), delivering critical data for communications, sensors, and control systems. LYNX MOSA.ic enabled a 25-fold performance improvement, reduced production costs, and introduced a future-ready architecture for seamless technology upgrades.

 Ensuring Medical Device Security at the Edge 

Lynx Provides Lifecycle Security for Leading Medical Device Manufacturer

A leading medical device manufacturer relied on Lynx to address lifecycle security challenges for its embedded systems. By utilizing Lynx’s expertise, the company transitioned to a common software platform, supported by a collaborative infrastructure and continuous security monitoring.  CoreAVI 

 Modernizing Avionics with Safety-Critical Graphics 

Lynx Powers HENSOLDT’s Certified Airborne Computer

HENSOLDT partnered with Lynx to develop a scalable, high-performance Certified Airborne Computer, addressing modern avionics' growing demands for processing power and safety-criticality. By leveraging Lynx’s Vulkan-based graphics and compute technologies, HENSOLDT reduced system complexity while enhancing flexibility and performance. This solution enables integrators to modernize legacy systems with state-of-the-art capabilities, streamlining certification and integration processes.
