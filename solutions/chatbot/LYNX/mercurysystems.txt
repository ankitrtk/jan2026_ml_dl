LYNX & MERCURY SYSTEMS

WHAT WE'RE DOING TOGETHER

Like Lynx, Mercury Systems focuses on mission-critical workloads in SWaP-constrained aircraft, vehicles, naval, military, and other edge platforms. Their focus on delivering hardware platforms using the latest Intel processor technology aligns well with our customer base’s needs. Lynx was an early supporter of Mercury’s TigerLake based, VPX form factor board with optimized RTOS and hypervisor technology.
Lynx works closely with Mercury to coordinate product roadmaps to release proven software to the industry as close to board availability as possible.
