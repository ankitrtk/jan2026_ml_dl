Challenges Facing Medical Device Manufacturers

The medical device industry operates under relentless pressure to innovate while meeting strict regulatory requirements. Lynx understands these challenges and is here to help you overcome them.

Cybersecurity Threats
: IoT-connected devices are prime targets for cyberattacks, risking patient data and functionality.

Regulatory Complexity
: Navigating 
FDA
,

, and 
ISO
 standards can slow time-to-market and strain resources.

Lifecycle Maintenance
: Devices require long-term support to remain secure and compliant in a rapidly evolving landscape.

Innovation Pressure
: Accelerating development cycles without compromising safety or compliance is a persistent challenge.

 82% of Healthcare IoT Devices are Vulnerable 

 A recent study found that the majority of IoT-enabled healthcare devices have known vulnerabilities, highlighting the urgent need for proactive security measures. (Source: Cybersecurity News) 

 $6.5 Million Average Cost of a Healthcare Data Breach 

 The financial and reputational impact of breaches underscores the critical importance of robust cybersecurity. (Source: IBM Security) 

 12-18 Months Average Compliance Timeline 

 Navigating FDA and IEC 62304 requirements adds significant time to the development cycle. (Source: Medical Device Online) 

 70% of Medical Devices Face End-of-Life Risks 

 Lack of long-term maintenance strategies leaves devices vulnerable and non-compliant. (Source: Industry Reports)
