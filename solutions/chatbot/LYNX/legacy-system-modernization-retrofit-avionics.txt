 Modernize Legacy Systems with Modular, Certifiable Solutions 

Legacy systems are the backbone of your critical operations, but they often struggle to meet today’s evolving safety, compliance, and performance demands. With Lynx’s retrofit solutions, we help you extend the lifecycle of your platforms, modernize with modular architectures, and secure them against emerging threats. We’re your partner at the edge, ensuring your legacy systems continue to serve your mission now and in the future. Seize the Edge by future-proofing your systems with Lynx and achieve up to 60% cost savings compared to full redesigns.

Additionally, modernizing legacy avionics requires more than code migration, it demands architecture built for longevity. Discover how conformity and abstraction, grounded in disciplined OOP, enable reusable, maintainable software across evolving, multi-vendor defense systems.

 Retrofitting Ensures Reliability and Compliance 

Replacing a legacy system is often impractical and unnecessary. Retrofitting provides a streamlined, cost-effective path to modernizing your systems while avoiding the downtime and expense of a full redesign. With Lynx as your partner, you gain future-ready solutions that fit your current needs and scale with future demands.

Extend System Lifecycles: 
Upgrade capabilities without sacrificing existing investments.

Ensure Compliance: 
Achieve certifications like DO-178C and ISO 26262 with proven methodologies.

Strengthen Security:

Enable Future-Ready Design: 
Leverage MOSA to integrate new technologies seamlessly.

In industries such as aerospace and defense where failure isn’t an option, retrofitting ensures reliability and compliance under the most demanding conditions.
