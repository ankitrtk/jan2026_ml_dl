Seize the Edge: Introducing the New Lynx

After decades of pioneering mission-critical software solutions, we are launching our boldest chapter yet. 

by 
Lynx

Share

Lynx is entering its boldest chapter yet—solidifying its position as the global leader in mission-critical edge solutions. 
After decades of pioneering mission-critical software solutions, we are launching our boldest chapter yet. 
Through strategic growth and targeted acquisitions—including CoreAVI, Thompson Software Solutions and TimeSys—Lynx has expanded its capabilities to empower industries with safe, secure and scalable CPU- and GPU-based edge computing solutions.

Our strategic acquisitions of CoreAVI, Thompson Software Solutions, and Timesys, Lynx now offers expanded capabilities that cater to a broader spectrum of industries, including aerospace, defense, automotive, industrial and medical. These diverse sectors share a common need for safe, secure, resilient and scalable solutions to meet the demands of mission-critical operations at the edge.

We made these acquisitions recognizing the absolute imperative of supporting our customers' success, and, today, Lynx offers a comprehensive suite of solutions. This transformation represents our steadfast commitment to making mission-critical edge applications safe, 
secure and resilient, empowering our customers to deploy innovations at the pace of relevance and drive decisive advantages in their respective markets. With Lynx, they can Seize the Edge!

Our Five Guiding Principles

At the core of our commitment to the brand, lies a vision rooted in five fundamental principles that guide the Lynx approach to edge computing: 

First
: Safe and secure by design. 
Lynx secures the edge with built-in safety and security, ensuring resilience from the ground up in an era of evolving cyber threats
. 

Second
: Innovation. Lynx
 accelerates edge innovation by unlocking the full potential of multicore CPU and GPU compute platforms, enabling rapid deployment while maintaining reliability. 
We remove traditional barriers to innovation, enabling rapid deployment of new capabilities while maintaining the highest standards of reliability. 

Third
: Simplification. We 
simplify edge computing by eliminating legacy constraints and integrating seamlessly across complex workloads.

Fourth
: Partnership. We consider c
ollaboration key to success at the edge. Lynx partners with customers to co-create solutions that adapt to evolving challenges.

Fifth
: Standardization. 
Standardization is the backbone of interoperability. We recognize that edge applications are inherently heterogeneous, spanning diverse hardware architectures and software ecosystems. To seamlessly integrate solutions across different vendors and open-source projects, the use of industry standards, APIs and ABIs is critical. 

At Lynx, we are committed to fostering interoperability, ensuring flexibility and enabling seamless integration through a standards-driven approach that empowers our customers to build scalable and resilient edge systems

We believe our approach positions Lynx as the partner of choice for organizations seeking to transform their edge computing capabilities, whether modernizing legacy systems, deploying next-generation software on multicore CPU or GPU or building new solutions from the ground up. 

Ready to transform your edge computing strategy? Discover how Lynx’s integrated solutions can enhance security, accelerate innovation and streamline deployments. 
Contact us
 today, visit our 
website
, and Seize the Edge!
