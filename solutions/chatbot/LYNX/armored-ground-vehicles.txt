INDUSTRY SECTOR DYNAMICS

The armored vehicles market is dynamic and evolving, with various vital trends driving its development and growth. There are several high-level themes we hear about in discussions with customers, including:

The use of lightweight materials to improve fuel efficiency and reduce costs while still providing a high level of protection

The modular design approach allows vehicles to be customized and adapted for different applications and environments

A path toward autonomous systems, enabling them to be operated with reduced risk to human operator

The use of electric power reduces fuel consumption and improves environmental sustainability

The integration of digital technologies, including the use of advanced sensors and cameras for situational awareness, as well as advanced communication and control systems allows for real-time monitoring and response.

Instead of enclosed systems, these platforms are increasingly being connected to the cloud and/or other vehicles to realize the vision of the connected battlefield, driving a need for ground vehicles to be resilient against cyber-attacks and less vulnerable in a cyber-contested battlespace, which implies:

Ability to send and receive encrypted messages across the vehicle data bus to securely update the firmware of electronic  control units (ECUs)

Virtualization of hardware and software ground vehicle components

Inclusion of Cross Domain Solutions (CDS), also known as high-speed Guard solutions, are integrated software-hardware systems that make secure access to and exchange of sensitive data possible across networks between various levels of security classification, including incompatible security domains.

SOFTWARE CHALLENGES

Delivering this dramatically increased complexity and capability while decreasing development cycles creates challenges for program execution/management offices, chief architects, and P&L owners. The following are solutions to these challenges:

Increasing resilience to cyberattack, both in terms of early recognition of the system being threatened and in terms of providing a path to return the system to a known good state

Delivering systems that can be updated in real-time

Reducing development cycles
