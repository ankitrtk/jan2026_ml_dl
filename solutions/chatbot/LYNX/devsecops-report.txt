DevSecOps

Interested in learning more about the latest security trend in the Aerospace and Defense industries? Fill out the form to the right to receive our 
DevSecOps Survey Report
.

REGISTER HERE

WHY THIS SURVEY

There has been a lot of rhetoric in the public domain about the challenge faced by the 
US defense agencies to maintain their dominance over rivals
 and the need for new approaches for development to be embraced as part of that strategy. Other industries have embraced DevSecOps. We were curious as to whether the military, avionics and federal market sectors, markets that have typically been slow to adopt new technologies due to heightened concerns around safety and security would be broadly adopting DevSecOps, beyond a few high profile software factories such as the Air Force’s 
Kessel Run
.

METHODOLOGY

Lynx Software Technologies commissioned Zogby Analytics to conduct a survey of IT decision-makers in the Aerospace, Aviation, Government/ Public Sector and Military industries to analyze the state of DevSecOps in the A&D industry in regards to how companies grade their digital transformations and how companies like Lynx can help educate in the process. The survey was conducted online from Oct. 14-18, 2022.
