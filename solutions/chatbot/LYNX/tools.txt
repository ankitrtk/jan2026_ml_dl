Key Tools for Your Productivity Success

Our tools are designed to optimize your development and testing workflows, help you reduce costs, meet tight deadlines, and achieve mission success.

Virtual Integration Environment (VIE)

Start development early by creating software on servers with virtual targets for Arm and Intel systems. Decouple hardware dependencies and collapse development schedules.

Embedded Board Farm (EBF)

Test on live hardware remotely with secure access and automation tools, ensuring precise timing and cyclic testing without physical bottlenecks.

 Unblock Hardware Access Limits 

Accelerated Development Timelines

Safety Certification Support

Meet DO-178C and other industry requirements confidently with early defect detection and streamlined testing.

Virtual Integration Environment (VIE)

Lynx VIE enables your teams to decouple software development from hardware dependencies. By supporting virtual targets as integral elements,  your teams can build, debug, and test Linux and LynxOS-178 applications early, reducing delays and ensuring seamless CI/CD integration.

Embedded Board Farm (EBF)

Lynx EBF provides secure, remote access to project hardware, enabling your teams to collaborate globally. With support for Linux, Android, and other embedded OSes, EBF ensures efficient debugging and testing cycles on live hardware, reducing your infrastructure costs.
