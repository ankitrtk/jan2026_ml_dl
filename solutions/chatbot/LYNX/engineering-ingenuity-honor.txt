LynxElement among top recipients in software category

San Jose, California - October 25, 2023 –

Lynx Software Technologies (Lynx), the leader in delivering solutions for the Mission Critical Edge, announced today that its LynxElement unikernel was recognized among the best software of the year in the 2023 Military + Aerospace Electronic Innovators Awards. Votes placed by the engineering community recognized Lynx as a Silver honoree.

LynxElement is the industry’s first unikernel to be POSIX compatible and available for mission-critical commercial use. Offered as part of the LYNX MOSA.ic portfolio of products, LynxElement provides increased density, better security and speed due to its small size. Since a Unikernel contains no dependencies on system functionality like external libraries, tools and other packages necessary for the application to work correctly, it is a much simpler module of software to define and characterize. This greatly simplifies system certification effects and is a key element in enabling software reuse across mission critical systems.

The 2023 Military + Aerospace Electronics Innovators Awards celebrate outstanding innovation in defense and aerospace for the engineering industry. Every year Military + Aerospace Electronics showcases and rewards companies, designers and innovators who have contributed to the advancement of technology through their creative and impactful designs.

“At Lynx our mission has always been to provide industry-leading mission-critical software solutions to clients across the aerospace and defense industry,” said Tim Reed, CEO, Lynx Software Technologies. “We are very grateful to receive this award from Military + Aerospace Electronics for LynxElement, which has been welcomed by the industry looking for the latest in safety- and security-critical solutions. This recognition is a testament to the innovative engineering of our product teams as well as the commitment of the entire organization in delivering on our mission.”
