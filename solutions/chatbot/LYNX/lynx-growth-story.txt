Our 3-Year Growth Story

Lynx has rapidly evolved from a respected RTOS vendor into a global edge computing powerhouse. With over 300 employees, 12 worldwide locations, and trusted deployments across hundreds of aircraft, drones, and space systems, we’re redefining how safety-critical and mission-critical systems operate at the edge.

From 50 to 300+ Employees

Lynx has evolved from a niche RTOS company into a global leader in secure edge computing. What started in 2022 as a 50-person team focused on real-time systems has grown into a worldwide force of over 300 employees, powering innovation across avionics, defense, and beyond.

We’ve built an end-to-end solution for organizations developing and deploying high-integrity edge applications for CPU and GPU systems, and as demand keeps growing, so do we.

That’s 600% growth since 2022. Here's how we got there.

Strategic Acquisitions That Supercharged Our Platform

With the support from investment firm OceanSound Partners, Lynx underwent a series of strategic acquisitions that transformed us from an operating environment software company to a turnkey edge computing solution provider to mission- and safety-critical system providers.

 ACQUISITION 

 CoreAVI 

The industry’s leading provider of safety-critical graphics libraries, CoreAVI uniquely empowers the integration of graphics processors into highly regulated aerospace and defense systems. Following our acquisition of CoreAVI, our solutions are purpose-built to meet the most rigorous industry certification standards, enabling developers to confidently deploy advanced visual and compute capabilities in mission- and safety-critical environments.

By supporting both graphics rendering and GPU compute at the edge, we unlock real-time data processing, AI, and other compute-intensive functions in harsh, resource-constrained settings. This approach accelerates development, reduces cost and complexity, and minimizes certification overhead, delivering high-performance capabilities exactly where they’re needed most.

                          Read More

 ACQUISITION 

 Thompson Software Solutions 

Thompson Software Solutions brings Lynx deep expertise in developing bare-metal and RTOS-hosted flight software for mission and avionics systems, following key standards like ARINC 653, and MIL-STD-1553, and FAA regulations. We combine safety-critical avionics with modern mission systems to enable rapid capability upgrades across aircraft and spacecraft platforms.

We also offer advanced simulation and training solutions using standards like Distributed Interactive Simulation (DIS) for realistic tactical environments. Our rapid prototyping labs and hardware-in-the-loop facilities speed development and testing, while our skilled network architects deliver secure, resilient communications across complex defense networks, including JADC2, with a strong cybersecurity focus.

                          Read More

 ACQUISITION 

 Timesys 

The embedded industry’s leading provider of end-to-end device security and long-term Linux support, Timesys uniquely enables secure product development from design through deployment. With VigiShield Secure by Design, our solutions integrate essential security features, like secure boot, encryption, OTA updates, and hardening, directly into the device architecture, helping teams build resilient systems from the start.

Complemented by Vigiles SBOM generation and CVE monitoring and software maintenance services, we empower developers to manage vulnerabilities, ensure long-term security, and streamline OS/BSP updates. Combined with expert engineering, consulting, and test automation infrastructure, Timesys has a long history of helping companies in industries such as medical device manufacturing, building/energy controls and management, material handling, and EV charging to reduce risk, accelerate development, and ensure robust, secure performance in the field.

From RTOS to Multicore Orchestration Platform

We listened to what our customers needed: open, flexible architectures free from vendor lock-in. So we built a platform that supports:

Bare-metal applications

Multiple operating systems

Open-source and third-party toolchains

And we solved the multicore problem. Where others rely on centralized control, our architecture gives each application its own kernel space and direct access to specific CPU cores. No bottlenecks, no slowdown. This resulted in higher performance, lower power draw, cooler system temps, and true multicore scalability.
