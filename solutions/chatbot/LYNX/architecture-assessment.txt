Software architecture and design

 Software Architecture 
 and Design 

Our partners trust the expertise of our team to provide modern solutions with architecture and detailed design for successful development, test, and deployment of products with all applicable certifications. The capabilities we provide enabled fieldable products trusted by end users.

Strong software architecture isn’t just about functionality, it’s about sustainability. Learn how conformity and abstraction, powered by disciplined OOP, create reusable, maintainable software that adapts to evolving hardware, multi-vendor systems, and decades-long mission requirements. 

            Why Conformity and Abstraction Matter

Avionics and Mission Software

Lynx has rich experience in bare metal and RTOS hosted operational flight programs and mission systems applications. We employ industry standards like ARINC 653, MIL-STD-1553, ARINC 429 and other US Government and Commercial FAA standards for avionics and flight software associated with Integrated Modular Avionics environments. We excel at blending the power of safety critical avionics with modern, high performance mission systems enabling rapid capability insertion and technology upgrades. We have a wealth of knowledge in aircraft and spacecraft flight software achieving safety certifications with high pedigree.

 Open System Architecture 

Our staff were part of the inception and maturation of multiple open standards and know how to leverage each standard. Some of the open standards we have had the most significant influence on are the following:

Open Mission Systems (OMS)

Universal Command and Control Interface (UCI)

Future Airborne Capability Environment (FACE
TM
)

Common Open Architecture Radar Program Specification (COARPS)

Hardware Open Systems Technologies (HOST)

Sensor Open System Architecture (SOSA
TM
)

Labs and Rapid Prototyping

We believe in prototyping early and often meet the evolving demands of our partners. We have built pristine demonstration facilities enabling multi- domain operations across multiple platforms to building, certifying, and operating state of the art aircraft hardware in the loop laboratories (HWILs).

Simulation and Training

Our teams have led multiple Live, Virtual, Constructive events and understand the details of the network and configuration needed for distributed mission operations. Leveraging available tools and standards like Distributed Interactive Simulation (DIS), we provide creative solutions for this critical capability. Our staff were part of the creators of the new F-35 Embedded Training product line. We are passionate about providing meaningful simulation capabilities that maximize real, tactical software enabling rapid transition to fieldable capabilities on platforms.

Cybersecurity & Network Architecture

We have seasoned network architects with experience across all domains of the JADC2 battlespace with cybersecurity at the forefront. Network knowledge is vital to getting data between distributed disparate systems and our team has proven experience in live exercises to solve the toughest problems.

 Maintenance, Sustainment & Data Analytics 

Lynx provides several application services in helping keep your aircraft operation at optimal performance. The services offered range from post flight analysis to real-time monitoring of onboard systems. With data analytics expertise on staff, we can help customers advance to the latest predictive maintenance techniques and meet CBM+ objectives.

Proven Performance

An established history of delivering solutions for critical military platforms, development operations, and sustainment.

 Featured 

 MC-130J Project Virtue Rapid OMS Demonstrations 

Lynx supported the effort to host an Open Mission Systems (OMS) enclave on the MC-130J Special Mission Processor (SMP). TSS engineers supported multi-site testing and airworthiness certification required for military flight authorization. We specialize in integrating multiple systems together with aircraft avionics.

 Featured 

 Digital Transformation and Web Applications 

Lynx has a strong history of supporting DevOps process improvements and web application tooling. Our team's expertise is applied toward many of our projects by setting up and operating a true DevSecOps pipeline being used by multiple teams in many baselines.

 Featured 

 Hypersonics Flight Software Development, Integration & Test 

Lynx is proud to be a part of strengthening American defense systems. Our deep knowledge of embedded flight software leveraging modern software development practices and toolchains has been applied across multiple domains in the battlespace including Land, Air, and Space. We are on the front lines of some of America's most important systems to national security.

 Featured 

 Open Architecture Maturation 

Lynx is part of developing, integrating, flight testing, and fielding multiple technologies onto systems across all domains of the battlespace including subsurface, land, air, space, and cyber. Applying the correct approach to multiple open standards allowing true interoperability in a non-proprietary solution while achieving safety-critical certifications where needed is what we are passionate about.

 Featured 

 Mission Computer Tech Refresh 

Lynx is taking a prominent role in Mission Computer refresh programs. The efforts require porting OFPs from established single-core MIPS and PowerPC solutions to multi-core ARM and x86 processing environments without compromising the level of safety certification and minimizing re- certification impact. The work includes device driver development (e.g. MIL-STD-1553, ARINC 429), standing up lab/test environments with each hardware delivery, and integration with an ARINC 653-compliant RTOS in a mixed-criticality system.

 Featured 

 Research & Development 

Lynx has ongoing Research & Development efforts that allow for rapid insertion of new technologies into very applicable missions to our current and prospective partners. Our team is energized and passionate about prototyping new technologies and capabilities that will make a difference. We are always open to discuss collaboration opportunities with other businesses to promote, mature, and integrate technology faster than our adversaries in a realistic environment.

 Featured 

 C-5 Super Galaxy CNS/ATM 

TSS was proud to support the USAF Warner Robins AIMS Program Office and help the program obtain CNS/ATM certification. The effort included full lifecycle development and avionics design support that leveraged existing C-5 capabilities to modify a legacy platform to achieve program requirements and obtain the certification without major platform redesign.

 Featured 

 LM-100J FAA Certification 

Lynx engineers provided full lifecycle development support to meet DO-178C objectives. The effort included comm radio development and a VHF crosstalk solution necessary to meet the mission and certification objectives. The LM-100J received the FAA Type Certificate Update in November 2019.

 Featured 

 MCTF-MC-130J Commando II 

Support for this project involved integration of the Raytheon AN/APQ-187 Silent Knight terrain- following/terrain-avoidance radar, including cockpit display updates for fusion of video input with aircraft state data. TSS provided full lifecycle development support that met safety-critical airworthiness objectives.
