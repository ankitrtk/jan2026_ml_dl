Emirates Skywards Credit Card FAQs

What are the kinds of Emirates Skywards credit cards available in India?

The types of Emirates Skywards Credit Cards in India are

Emirates Emeralde: Best for High- End Flying Perks

Emirates Sapphiro: Luxury Travel and Lifestyle

Emirates Rubyx: Best for Frequent Flyers

How do I accumulate Skywards Miles on these credit cards?

You get 2.5 Miles, 2 miles and 1.5 miles for ₹100 spent using your Emaralde, Sapphiro and Rubyx credit cards. Miles are awarded for expenditure on travel, lifestyle, and daily spends.

Is airport lounge access available on these cards?

Yes, lounge access is card-specific. The Emarlade card entitles users to unrestricted access to domestic and international airport lounges. Sapphiro offers two free domestic lounge visits every quarter and two free foreign lounge visits annually, whereas Rubyx offers one free domestic lounge visit every three months.

What should I do if I notice an unauthorised transaction on my credit card?

If you detect any unauthorised transaction, immediately contact ICICI Bank Customer Care or Internet Banking to report it.

How do I get an Emirates Skywards Credit Card?

You can apply online from the ICICI Bank website or any ICICI Bank branch. Check if you meet the eligibility criteria and have the documents necessary for a smooth application.

Can I apply for an add-on card for my family members?

Yes, you can apply for up to three add-on cards for your immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, or children 18 years and above). The cards have the same credit limit and benefits as the primary card, including Skywards Miles accrual.

How do I redeem my Skywards Miles accrued through the credit card?

The Skywards Miles are credited to your Emirates Skywards account. You can redeem them for Emirates flights, upgrades, hotel stays, and other partner services.
