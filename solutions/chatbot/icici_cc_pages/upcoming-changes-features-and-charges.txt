Changes to ICICI Bank Credit Card Features and Charges

Airport Lounge Access:

Starting 01 January 2025, you can enjoy complimentary lounge access at domestic airports by spending ₹75,000 on certain ICICI Bank Credit Cards. Spends made in the previous calendar quarter will unlock access for the subsequent calendar quarter. For example, to be eligible for complimentary lounge access in the quarter of January 2025 to March 2025, you need to spend a minimum of ₹75,000 in the quarter of October 2024 to December 2024 and similarly for subsequent quarters. The number of complimentary access varies as per the Card.

Please go through the below illustration to understand the Spend period along with the Lounge eligibility period

FAQs

How to access complimentary airport lounges in India?

Eligible cardholders need to present their valid, unexpired cards, and a valid air ticket or boarding pass for travel on the same or next day at the entrance of the participating Lounges to gain entry. An authorisation amount of ₹2 shall be  charged on each eligible card presented at the entrance to the Participating Lounge. It's important to note that the free access is generally limited to the cardholder, any accompanying guest(s) may incur charges. This programme is provided by a third party and governed by such third party terms and conditions.

How will I qualify to avail complimentary domestic airport lounge access?

Starting Jan 01, 2025, you can enjoy One complimentary airport lounge access by spending ₹75,000 in the preceding calendar quarter. Spends made in the preceding calendar quarter will unlock access for the subsequent calendar quarter. To be eligible for complimentary lounge access in April-May-June, 2024 quarter, you need to spend a minimum of ₹75,000 in the January-February-March, 2024 quarter and similarly for following quarters.

This updated criterion to avail complimentary domestic airport lounge access will be applicable for the cards in abovementioned table.

However, for the other ICICI Bank credit cards, there is no change in the spending criteria.

What is Dynamic Currency Conversion?

DCC is a currency conversion service on a real-time basis. DCC allows you to make credit card payments in Indian currency at international location or to merchants located in India but registered in a foreign nation. Although DCC simplifies the understanding of prices in Indian currency, it involves additional mark-ups or charges levied by merchants that maybe unfavourable for you.

What is Dynamic Currency Conversion charges?

Starting 15 January 2026, DCC fees has been revised on your ICICI Bank Credit Cards. DCC fee is applicable on each transaction performed in Indian Currency at international location or transactions performed in Indian currency with merchants located in India but registered in a foreign nation.

Below shows the revised DCC charges for ICICI Bank Credit cards.

Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card - 1.49%

ICICI Bank Emeralde Metal Credit Card, ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card, ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Credit Card -  2.00%

MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Travel Credit Card - 0.99%

Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card -  1.99%

All other credit cards including MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card and MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card -  3.50%

Example:

When you make a purchase at a store in an international country and use your ICICI Bank credit card to pay in Indian Rupee (INR) on a Point of Sale (POS) machine, a DCC Mark-up fee will be applied to the transaction amount. Similarly, if you make a purchase in India from a merchant registered overseas and use your ICICI Bank credit card to pay in INR, a DCC Mark-up fee will be charged on the transaction amount.

Please check the above table for cardwise DCC Markup fee charged on your ICICI Bank Credit Card

Which MCCs are covered under Rent Payment?

Credit card transactions with Merchant Category Code (MCC) 6513, 7349 are covered under rental payments.

A Merchant Category Code (MCC) is a four-digit number assigned to a merchant/business by the merchant’s acquiring bank. These are defined by networks e.g. Visa, MasterCard.
