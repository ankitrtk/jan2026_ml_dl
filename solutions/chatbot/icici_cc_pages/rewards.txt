Rewards

Earn Rewards Through Your ICICI Bank Credit Card

You can earn rewards on a wide range of purchases using your 
ICICI Bank Credit Card
. You can redeem these rewards for a host of gift items and gift vouchers for yourself and your family.

APPLY NOW

Categories of Reward Points

Domestic Transactions

Earn reward points with your ICICI Bank Credit Card on all domestic purchases. The points earned depends on your card type.

Retail purchases

Gain reward points on select retail spends made using your ICICI Bank Credit Card. Your card type determines the total points accumulated per transaction.

International Transactions

Use your ICICI Bank Credit Card for international purchases to gain reward points. Earning rates vary according to the specific card variant held.

Utilities and Insurance

Accumulate reward points by paying for utilities and insurance with your ICICI Bank Credit Card. Check your specific card type for the applicable earning structure.

Steps to redeem rewards online

Net Banking

ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Login into Net Banking

Go to ‘Cards and Loans’

Click on ‘ICICI Bank Rewards’

Click on ‘Redeem’

ICICI Bank has a partnership with plenty of retail and online stores. You can shop at any of these partner stores and pay for the purchases using the Reward Points.

Login into the ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Go to ‘Cards and Forex’

Select your Card

Click on ‘Redeem now’

ICICI Bank has a partnership with the plenty of retail and online stores. You can shop at any of these partner stores and pay for the purchases using the Reward Points.

Things you should know

Reward Points are valid for 3 years from issuance
Reward Points will lapse if the card is cancelled
Reward Points will be credited within 7 working days from the statement generation date
Reward Points can be redeemed through Net Banking or the ICICI Bank's iMobile app
It is advised to check the terms and conditions of the product or e-voucher before redemption.
Vouchers are sent digitally to the registered e-mail ID and are also visible in the orders section of the rewards platform.
Physical products will be delivered within 15 days from the order placement date.
Complaints regarding non-delivery of physical products or e-vouchers must be raised within 30 days from the date of order placement.
Complaints regarding broken, damaged, defective or missing products must be raised within 5 days of receiving the product along with the evidence.

ICICI Bank Credit Card Rewards FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Credit Card Rewards Programme?

The ICICI Bank 
Rewards Programme
 is the new platform launched by ICICI Bank in August 2022. Under this new programme, you can earn ICICI Bank Reward Points on Credit Card purchases and redeem points against a variety of products on the new ICICI Bank Rewards platform. This programme gives you an easier way to earn, track and redeem your Reward Points.

Do ICICI Bank Reward Points expire?

Yes, you need to use Reward Points earned on ICICI Bank Credit Card purchases within 36 months since they have a lifespan of 3 years from the date of issuance.

What is covered under 'View Rewards'?

In this section, the reward options are separated into different categories for your easy reference. Just select the type of category you wish to redeem from and you will be able to browse through all the relevant offers in that category.

How can I earn Reward Points on my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You can easily earn Reward Points on your ICICI Bank Credit Card. All you have to do is use your eligible ICICI Bank Credit Card for your everyday purchases and watch your Reward Points add up.

To understand the exact mechanism of earning Reward Points on your card, please refer to your Card page at 
Click here
 or call our 24-hour Customer Care at 1800 1080. 

You can also increase your points balance by giving Plus Credit Cards to your loved ones. All transactions made on the Plus Cards add to your rewards tally. You can gift a Plus Card to 3 family members above 18 years of age by paying a minimal fee of ₹199+Goods and Services Tax (GST).

How can I view my Credit Card reward points summary?

Through Net Banking:

Step 1: Login into Net Banking

Step 2: Go to Cards & Loans

Step 3: Click on ICICI Bank Rewards

Points will be visible against the card number

Through ICICI Bank's iMobile app:

Step 1: Login into ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Step 2: Go to Cards & Forex

Step 3: Select your card

Points will be visible above the Redeem Now option.

What are the Terms and Conditions for redeeming products?

Click here
 to view the Programme Terms and Conditions.

How long does it take to process my redemption request?

The e-voucher or gift cards are delivered within a TAT of 30 minutes on your registered email address. The physical products are delivered to the shared communication address within a TAT of 15-20 working days.

Can I request for my gift to be delivered to another address?

Yes, you can enter the communication address manually while placing the redemption order through ICICI Bank Rewards Portal.

What happens if ICICI Bank is unable to deliver the gift?

The product is returned to the origin and points are reversed within 7 working days.

What are the benefits of registering for ICICI Bank Net Banking for rewards programme?

This site helps you to:

Check your points balance

Browse through all the reward redemption options

Redeem rewards instantly

You can use your existing Net Banking Username and Password to log in. 
Login now!

How do I resolve a query regarding the rewards programme?

The site has a detailed FAQs section to address your queries. If there are any specific queries that you have, please write to us through the '
Email Us
' option at www.icicibank.com or call our 
24-hour Customer Care
. We will be happy to attend to your queries.

How do I return unwanted items?

Please note that rewards are not exchangeable, replaceable, transferable or refundable for cash or credit.

Are all my ICICI Bank Credit Cards eligible for redeeming points via Net Banking?

All the cards under ICICI Bank Rewards program are eligible for redeeming points via Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

How do I find out the status of the gifts I have ordered?

We request you to call our Customer Care on 1800 1080 between 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. from your registered mobile number.

To know the Customer Care numbers, we request you to 
click here
 .

Alternatively, you may write to us through the 
'Email Us'
 link at www.icicibank.com at any time to find out the status of your shipment.

Can I change the item I have ordered if the number of reward points being redeemed are the same?

Sorry. You cannot change the item you have selected after the order has been placed.

Does the Points Summary display the points that I have across all my credit cards with the Bank?

The Points Summary displays the points available for redemption in your consolidated rewards account. The card numbers for which the points are displayed are indicated next to the points.

How do I redeem my ICICI Bank Rewards?

You can redeem your reward points either through Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile App

Through Net Banking:

Step 1: Login into Net Banking

Step 2: Go to Cards & Loans

Step 3: Click on ICICI Bank Rewards

Step 4: Click on Redeem

Through ICICI Bank's iMobile app:

Step 1: Login into ICICI Bank's iMobile App

Step 2: Go to Cards and Forex

Step 3: Select your card

Step 4: Click on Redeem now
