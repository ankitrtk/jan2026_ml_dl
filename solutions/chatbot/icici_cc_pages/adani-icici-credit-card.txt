Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are the annual fees for the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card and Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

The annual fee for the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card is ₹750 + Goods and Services Tax (GST) and the annual fee for Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card is ₹5,000 + GST.

How can I apply for an Adani One Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

To apply for an Adani One Credit Card, you can visit the ICICI Bank website and choose the Card you want to apply for, follow the steps and complete your application (with personal, salary and residential details). After submitting your application, complete your Know Your Customer (KYC) verification.

T&Cs.

What are the different types of Adani One Credit Cards?

There are two types of Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards, Platinum and Signature. To learn more about the benefits for travel, lifestyle, shopping and more.

What is the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

The Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card is offered to frequent travellers to provide them with multiple benefits in the Adani ecosystem.

What is the joining fee for an Adani One Credit Card?

The joining fee for Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card is ₹5,000 + GST and for Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card is ₹750 + GST.

What benefits are offered with the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

The benefits offered with the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card include joining benefits worth ₹3,000, Adani managed airport benefits (premium car parking, premium domestic lounge), valet services and porter services.

What benefits are offered with the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

There are multiple benefits available with the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card like flight and hotel vouchers worth ₹5,000 and ₹2,000, 16 domestic lounge access per year, Pranaam services, premium lounge upgrades, porter services, reward points for spending in the Adani Ecosystem and many more.

How can I activate the Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You can activate your Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card using the following options:

ICICI Bank iMobile App:

Log in to the app.

Tap on ‘Cards & Forex’.

Select your Adani One Credit Card.

Go to ‘Manage Card’ and enable card usage.

Net Banking:

Log in to ICICI Bank Net Banking.

Navigate to ‘Cards’ > ‘Credit Cards’ > ‘Manage’.

Select ‘Manage Credit Card Usage’ and enable transactions such as tap-and-pay.

How do I redeem Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card reward points?

Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card reward points can be redeemed exclusively through the Adani One app or website. Points may be used for bookings such as flights, hotels, duty-free shopping and other services within the Adani ecosystem, subject to applicable redemption terms.
