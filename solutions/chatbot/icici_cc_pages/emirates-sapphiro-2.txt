Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card FAQs

What is the Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card?

A premium travel credit card by ICICI Bank and Emirates Skywards. It rewards you with Skywards Miles on spends, free lounge access, travel privileges, and an exclusive Emirates Silver Tier status.

What are the joining and annual benefits of the Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card?

Cardholders receive 5,000 Skywards Miles and a complimentary Emirates Skywards Silver Tier membership as joining benefits.

How to avail complimentary international airport lounge access?

Enjoy 2 complimentary accesses to international airport lounges with ICICI Bank Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card. Complimentary benefits are applicable only for the primary cardholder.

For an ICICI Bank Emriates Sapphiro Credit Card customer who has applied for the card on or after 15 November 2024.

Priority Pass Membership

Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for the primary cardholder with ICICI Bank Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card

Access 1,400+ lounges in 145+ countries worldwide

Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.

For complete list of international airport lounges visit 
www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges

For an ICICI Bank Emriates Sapphiro Credit Card customer who has applied for the card before 15 November 2024.

DreamFolks Membership

Access 650+ lounges in 100+ countries.

Present your DreamFolks Membership Card at the participating international lounges.

Click here (PDF, 1.12 MB)
 for the complete list of international airport lounges.

What type of lounge access is provided?

The primary cardholder receives complimentary access to domestic lounges twice a quarter and international lounges twice a year.

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026.

Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter

Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section

Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking

Choose ICICI Bank Complimentary tickets offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards

Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’

Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page.

How can I avail discount on Emirates flight bookings using my ICICI Bank Emirates Sapphiro Credit Card?

To avail upto 10% discount on Emirates flight bookings, please follow the below steps

Visit 
www.emirates.com
 or open the Emirates App.

Click on “Book Now”

Enter promo code “INICI26” before searching for flights.

Complete your booking using your Emirates Skywards ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card at the time of payment.

How are Skywards Miles earned?

Earn up to 2 Skywards Miles per ₹100 spent on most categories, including dining, travel, and entertainment.

How do I redeem the Skywards Miles?

Miles in your Emirates Skywards account can be redeemed for flights, upgrades, hotel stays, and more with Emirates and its partners.

How can I maintain the Emirates Silver Tier benefit in the subsequent years?

To retain the Silver Tier status in subsequent years, an annual spend of ₹5 lakh is required.

Who is this card best suited for?

You are a frequent Emirates flyer, want to earn Skywards Miles on your spending, and are comfortable with reward redemption being limited to Emirates.
