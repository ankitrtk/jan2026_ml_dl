Coral Credit Card Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card free?

No, the card has a joining fee of ₹500 + GST and an annual fee of ₹500 + GST.

How can I get the annual fee waived off for the ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card?

If you spend over ₹1.5 lakh, you can get your annual fee waived off for the next year.

Can I make online purchases with the ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card?

Yes. You can easily make online purchases across India and in select overseas stores using the ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card.

What is CVV in a Credit Card? How do I find it in my ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card?

Flip your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card. The three-digit security code next to the signature panel is your CVV (Card Verification Value). You can also check it by logging in to your ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking.

Which bank’s Credit Card should I opt for?

The ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card is an excellent choice. It has a low joining fee and several benefits for movie and travel enthusiasts.

What is the age limit to apply for a Coral Credit Card?

You must be between the age group of 21–65 years to be eligible to apply for an ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card.

How do I know the payment due date of my ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card?

You can know thepayment due date of your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card through the monthly statement, ICICI Bank's iMobile app, Net Banking or by calling our Customer Care on 1800 1080.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your Coral Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

Please note that the 
airport lounge access on Credit Card

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Coral Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period.

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed Terms & Conditions, please

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to cardholders?

Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with counterfeit card liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the card provides an account takeover cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please 
click here

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

A 1% fuel surcharge waiver is eligible for transactions between ₹400 to ₹4000 on HPCL Fuel Stations when the transaction is conducted on ICICI Bank Merchant Services Swipe Machine. This benefit applies to both the primary cardholder and the plus (supplementary) cardholder, helping them save on regular fuel expenses.

What are the criteria for availing domestic railway lounge access?

Upgrade your travel experience with complimentary access to select railway lounges with your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card. Get  1 complimentary access to select railway lounges in India every quarter. Simply present your ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card to enjoy the privileges

Over and above the permitted number of complimentary access, each subsequent access will be charged as per rates laid out by each lounge service provider.
