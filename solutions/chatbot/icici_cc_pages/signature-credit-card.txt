Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card FAQs

What are the exclusive benefits of an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

The Adani One Signature Credit Card has exclusive benefits of 2 Pranaam, 8 Porter and 8 car parking (Premium and Valet) services annually, at Adani managed airports.

What is an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

The Adani Card, specifically the Adani One Signature Credit Card, is a premium credit card offered by ICICI Bank that provides various airport, reward and entertainment benefits.

How to avail complimentary international airport lounge access?

Enjoy 2 complimentary access to international airport lounges with Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card. Complimentary benefits are applicable only for the primary cardholder.

Priority Pass Membership

Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for the primary cardholder with Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card

Access 1,400+ lounges in 145+ countries worldwide

Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.

For complete list of international airport lounges visit 
https://www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Adani One Signature Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026.

Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter

Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section

Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking

Choose ICICI Bank Complimentary tickets offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards

Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’

Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page.

How can I apply for an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

To apply for an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card, visit the Bank’s website or a branch. You can complete the application process online by entering your details and following the prompts.

What are the main Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card benefits?

Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card benefits include discounts, access to airport benefits, reward benefits and exclusive offers on enhancing your overall experience.

Can I apply for an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card online?

Yes, you can easily apply for the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card

online through the Bank’s website. Simply follow the steps provided to complete your application.

What is the minimum spending requirement for an Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

To enjoy benefits such as waiving the annual fee, you need to meet a minimum spending requirement of ₹6 lakh in a calendar year with your Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card.

What types of rewards can I earn with the  Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

With the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card, you can earn Adani Reward Points on various spends, including domestic, international and utility expenses, allowing you to maximise your rewards.

Is there a joining fee for the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

Yes, the Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card has a joining fee of ₹5,000 plus taxes, which grants you access to all the card's benefits and features.

How do I activate my Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card?

Once you receive your Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card, you can activate it by following the instructions provided in the Welcome Kit or by logging into the ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking.

What are the different types of Adani Credit Cards?

There are two types of Adani Credit Cards - Platinum and Signature. To learn more about the benefits for travel, lifestyle, shopping and more 
click here

What is the joining fee for Adani One Credit Cards? 

The joining fee for an Adani One Signature Credit Card is ₹5,000 + GST and ₹750 + GST for an Adani One Platinum Credit Card.

What are the benefits of Adani One Credit Cards?

Adani One ICICI Bank credit cards offer unparalleled benefits, enhancing your experience across the Adani One ecosystem*, including Adani-managed airports, Trainman, gas, electricity, and more.

How to apply for a Credit Card online

To apply for a Credit Card online, visit the ICICI Bank website (Credit Card page) and select the card. After submitting the application form, complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) verification and get your card.

Who is eligible for flight voucher?

A customer who has paid their joining or annual fee and completed spends of  ₹25,000 (not including joining/annual fee, interest or bank levies) within 60 days of card activation. Customers who have availed card without joining/annual fee shall be ineligible for flight voucher.
