FAQs on Business Credit Card

Why Business Credit Card?

Business Credit Cards are specifically designed for entrepreneurs, proprietors and MSMEs to manage business finances more efficiently. proprietors and MSMEs to manage business finances more efficiently. Unlinke personal credit cards, they help you manage company expenses separately, build your business credit profile and access higher credit limits to support growth.

Who is the card best suited for Business Cards?

Business Credit cards are specifically designed for entrepreneurs, proprietors and MSMEs to manage business finances more efficiently .

Where can I use my Business credit card ?

You can use your Business Card to make purchases on categories such as utility payments, tax, fuel, digital marketing expenses, CSR costs, Advertising, software and license purchases, insurance, travel and entertainment etc.

Are international transactions allowed?

Yes, many ICICI Bank Business Credit Cards support international usage. Foreign currency mark-up charges may apply as per the card’s terms and conditions.
