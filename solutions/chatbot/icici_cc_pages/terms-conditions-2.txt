Balance Transfer Terms & Conditions

DEFINITIONS

"Facility" means transfer at the sole discretion of ICICI Bank, of such amount of the Card member's outstanding balances on the Credit Cards of other banks, to the Card provided or agreed to be provided by ICICI Bank, not exceeding the available Credit Limit.

"EMI BT" means the Facility in the event the Card member converts the mode of repayment of the Facility into Equated Monthly Instalments.

"Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI)" means the equated monthly instalment of the amounts payable by the Card member to ICICI Bank in respect of the Facility, comprising of the principal amount of the Facility and interest thereon.

"Flexi BT" means the Facility under which the Card member transfers his amount at a low interest rate (specified by ICICI Bank, at the time of availing of such Facility) for a period specified by ICICI Bank. The Card member can also choose to repay the amount post this specified period at the normal interest rate applicable for the overdue amount. The amount will be added to the total amount due of the Card Account.

"Card member" means the individual to whom a Credit Card has been issued by ICICI Bank and who has availed the Facility.

"Card Account" means the account opened in the name of the Card member and maintained by ICICI Bank for the purpose of usage of the Credit Card as per the terms and conditions contained in the Primary Terms and Conditions.

"Primary Terms and Conditions" mean the terms and conditions governing the Credit Card issued by ICICI Bank, as is available on 
Click here

"Credit Limit" means the limit up to which the Card member is authorised to spend on their Credit Card.

"Cash Limit" means the maximum amount of cash or equivalent of cash as defined or prescribed by ICICI Bank, that the Card member can withdraw on his Card Account. Cash Limit forms a subset of the Card Member's Credit Limit / Purchase Limit.

USAGE OF THE FACILITY

Under the Facility, ICICI Bank shall at its sole discretion, permit the Card member to transfer all or part of his or her outstanding balances on Credit Cards issued by other banks or entities, to his/her Card Account. Such transfer shall be carried out through the following 2 ways:

A demand draft sent to the mailing address of the Card Member, last registered with ICICI Bank, in favour of the other bank or entity issuing the Credit Card for crediting the card account of the Card member with such other bank / entity for the approved Facility amount.

NEFT in favour of the other bank/entity issuing the Credit Card for crediting the card account of the Card member with such other bank / entity for the approved Facility amount

ICICI Bank shall not be liable for any overdue payments or service charges that may be incurred by the Card Member in respect of his other Credit Card post the Facility being granted / agreed to be granted by ICICI Bank. The Card Member shall remain liable to continue to make payments to the other Credit Card issuing bank / entity until the transferred amount of the Facility is received by the other Credit Card issuing bank / entity. Additionally, the Card member shall also receive the confirmation in a future ICICI Bank Credit Card statement providing that his/her card account with the other Credit Card issuing bank / entity has been credited. The Facility shall be available to the Primary Card member as well as the Supplementary Card member, subject to the available Cash Limit and or Credit Limit and will be provided to the Card member requesting the Facility.

The Cash Limit and/or the Credit Limit on the Card will be blocked by the amount of the Facility granted to the Card member. The card member shall be required to pay a non-refundable processing fee on the Facility up to the percentage specified by ICICI Bank to the Card Member. Service tax shall be applicable on the processing fee and interest amount as per ICICI Bank's prevailing rates. Such amount shall be reflected in the ICICI Bank Credit Card statement for the particular month.

The Card member understands that by clicking on the ‘I Agree’ button, he/she is consenting to the Terms and Conditions herein, and the same shall be admissible as an electronic record under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. Furthermore, upon clicking on ‘I Agree’ button, the Card member is giving consent to share and retrieve his/her data to and from credit rating agency/credit bureau for his/her credit evaluation, before approval of the funds being sanctioned by ICICI Bank for usage of this facility.

REPAYMENT

In case of the Facility being EMI BT, the Card Member shall repay the Facility and interest thereon in EMIs. The interest shall be at such rate which has been indicated by ICICI Bank to the Card Member at the time of making the offer for the Facility and also as communicated to the Card Member thereafter. The interest calculation will start from the time the request regarding EMI BT has been confirmed by the Card member through the service call made to the Card member. The amount of the EMIs together with other details of the Facility (including rate of interest) shall be also communicated to the Card member at his/her mailing address.

The amount of EMIs due for a particular month shall subsequently be reflected in the ICICI Bank Credit Card statement of that particular month. ICICI Bank shall be entitled / is authorised by the Card member to debit the Card Account of the Card member on a monthly basis for the amount of EMI due for that particular month, as has been intimated to the Card member through the Statement. Upon repayment by the Card member, the Cash Limit / Credit Limit shall be reinstated to the extent of the amount of EMI repaid by the Card member. The entire amount of EMI will be added to the Minimum Amount Due of the Card member.

In case of the Facility being Flexi BT, the amount of the Facility shall form part of the Total Amount Due and/or EMI Amount Due and shall be repayable as total amount due. For this Facility, only the transferred principal will be charged at an interest rate specified by ICICI Bank at the time of availing of such facility for the specified period. All other fees, charges and transactions will attract interest from the date of such charges/transactions. Post the expiry of this specified period, if the Facility amount is not settled by the Card Member, it will also attract the standard overdue interest rate as given in the “Most Important Terms & Conditions”.

FORECLOSURE OF THE FACILITY

In case of the Facility being EMI BT, if the Facility is foreclosed or terminated before the tenure of the repayment of the Facility, the amount of the Facility outstanding at the time of foreclosure/termination together with all interest thereon and all other monies in respect of the Facility shall become repayable by the Card member immediately.

The Card member may foreclose the Facility at any time by contacting ICICI Bank's 24 Hour Customer Care Centre to avail of the foreclosure. The Card member shall pay such foreclosure charges as may be indicated by ICICI Bank in the communication sent to the Card member at the time of granting the Facility.

ICICI Bank shall, without prejudice to all rights and remedies, have the right to call upon the Card member to repay the Facility, all interest thereon and all other monies in respect of the Facility upon occurrence of an event of default in payment of EMI for two consecutive months and the Card Member shall be liable to repay all such amounts upon such demand.

CANCELLATION OF THE FACILITY

The Card member may cancel the Facility within 15 days of granting the Facility by contacting ICICI Bank's 24 Hour Customer Care Centre. Such cancellation shall be subject to return of the demand draft at Credit Card Operations, ICICI Bank Ltd., ICICI Bank Tower, C – Wing, Autumn Estate, Near Chandivali Studio, Chandivali Farm Road, Opp. Mhada Colony, Chandivali, Andheri (East), Mumbai 400072 and non-encashment of the demand draft by the Card Member or the beneficiary.

In cases where the Facility is availed through NEFT transfer, the Card member shall not have the right to cancel the Facility.

The Card member's having been declared as an insolvent

Any proceedings for misconduct having been initiated against the Card member

The Card member's failure to furnish any information or documents that may be required by ICICI Bank

The Card member's entering into any composition with his/her creditors

The Card member's defaulting on any of the terms and conditions of any other loan or facility provided by ICICI Bank to the Card member

The existence of any other circumstances which, in the sole opinion of ICICI Bank, jeopardise ICICI Bank's interest. Upon the occurrence of any event of default as specified above, ICICI Bank shall be entitled to exercise rights and remedies available to it under these terms and conditions as also the Primary Terms and Conditions.
