About Credit Card Payment

Multiple payment channels

Choose from 
iMobile app
, Internet Banking, UPI, NEFT, Scan to Pay, Auto Debit, ICICI Bank ATMs, or even Cash Deposit at branches to pay your dues.

UPI-based convenience

Make payments using any UPI/BHIM app, including non-ICICI Bank accounts. Use VPA format:

ccpay.<mobile><last 4 card digits>@icici.

Scan and pay QR option

Use the Scan to Pay feature with UPI-enabled apps by scanning the ICICI Bank QR code. No ICICI Bank account required.

NEFT from any Bank

Pay using Internet Banking linked to the accounts of other banks (if they support NEFT transactions). When you register your ICICI Bank Credit Card as the payee for fund transfer, enter the 15-digit number (in case of American Express Credit Card) or the 16-digit number (in case of Visa/Mastercard Credit Card) as the account number. In addition to this, you need to enter ‘ICIC0000004’ as the Indian Financial System (IFS) Code. For details, please

Auto debit setup

Avoid missed due dates by enabling auto debit from your ICICI Bank Savings Account. Submit a written request to activate this facility on your card’s due date

ATM-based payments

Swipe your ICICI Bank Debit Card at any ICICI Bank ATM. Choose ‘More Options’ to pay your Credit Card bill directly and securely.

Cash deposit at branch

Pay via cash at any ICICI Bank branch. A fee of ₹100 + 18% GST is applicable per payment transaction.

Secure transactions only

Always use authorised ICICI Bank payment channels for Credit Card dues. Avoid third-party platforms to ensure your payments are secure and correctly credited.

ICICI Bank Credit Card bill FAQs

How can I pay my ICICI Bank Credit Card bill?

You can pay using the following authorised modes:

ICICI Bank Internet Banking

iMobile app

Scan to Pay (UPI)

ATMs

Auto Debit

NEFT (for other bank accounts)

Click to Pay (for non-ICICI Bank customers)

Cash Deposit at ICICI Bank branches (₹100 + GST applicable)

Ensure you use only authorised payment modes to avoid delays.

Can I pay my Credit Card bill using another bank account?

Yes, you can pay from any other bank using the NEFT facility or platforms like Click to Pay and Scan to Pay (UPI-based). Ensure correct card number and IFSC (ICIC0000004) for NEFT transactions.

When should I pay my Credit Card bill?

Ideally, pay before the due date to avoid interest and late payment charges. Bills are typically due 15–20 days after the statement generation date

What is the ideal time to pay the Credit Card bill?

The best time to pay is well before the due date. Early payment helps avoid charges and boosts credit health. Paying in full is always recommended.

Should I pay my Credit Card bill in full?

Yes, paying the total outstanding amount helps avoid interest charges and keeps your credit score strong. If unable to pay in full, pay at least the ‘Minimum Due’.

What is the ‘Minimum Due’ on a Credit Card?

It’s the minimum amount payable by the due date to avoid late fees. It typically includes interest, fees and a small part of the outstanding principal.

Can I pay my bill immediately after a purchase?

Yes, you can pay anytime. However, the charge will reflect in the next billing cycle. Early payments can help with better spend management and avoid overspending.

How is a Credit Card payment processed?

Here’s the simplified journey:

Initiate the transaction using your card.

Card details are shared with the recipient’s bank.

The bank routes the request via a payment network.

Card issuer verifies and approves/declines the transaction.

Once approved, the transaction is completed.

How long does it take for my Credit Card bill payment to reflect?

Credit Card bill payment usually reflects instantly or within a few hours after a  successful transaction. In some cases, it may take up to one working day depending on the payment mode used.

Are there any convenience fees for Credit Card bill payments?

ICICI Bank does not levy convenience charges for Credit Card bill payment made through its digital platforms. However, third-party payment channels may have separate fee structures.

How can I resolve payment failures or disputes?

If your Credit Card payment fails or there is a discrepancy, check transaction status through the ICICI Bank Net Banking or iMobile app. For unresolved issues, contact ICICI Bank customer care with transaction details.

How do I get a payment receipt or invoice for my Credit Card bill?

After successful Credit Card bill payment, you can download the payment receipt from the confirmation screen or access it later through transaction history in ICICI Bank Net Banking or iMobile app.
