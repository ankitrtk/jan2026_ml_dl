Manchester United Signature Credit Card FAQs

Who should use the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card? 

The ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card is an ideal Credit Card for football enthusiasts. You can get a Manchester United-Branded Holdall and Football, a signed Manchester United Jersey and many other discounts on ManU experiences.

Can I make an online purchase with the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card? 

Yes, you can easily make online purchases across India and at select overseas stores using the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card.

What are the benefits that I can get with the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card?

With the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card, you get discounts and Rewards on your ManU experiences. You also get complimentary airport lounge access, monthly discounts at BookMyShow, INOX and many more benefits.

What is the age limit for the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card? 

You must be between the age group of 21 to 65 years to be eligible to apply for the ICICI Bank Manchester United Signature Credit Card. 

What are the key variants of ICICI Bank Credit Cards? 

The key variants of ICICI Bank Credit Cards are Platinum, Coral, Rubyx, Sapphiro and Emeralde. You can know more about several other Credit Cards tailored for specific needs like travel, lifestyle, shopping and more, like Amazon Pay Credit Card, MakeMyTrip Credit Card, Adani One Credit Card and Emirates Skywards Credit Card by exploring the ICICI Bank website. 

How can I see my Credit Card number online?  

You can see your ICICI Bank Credit Card number by logging into the ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking.

How can I redeem my Credit Card points?  

You can experience seamless redemption of your Rewards with ICICI Bank. First, log into ICICI Bank Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. In the Rewards section, you can redeem your ICICI Bank Rewards across multiple categories such as electronics, lifestyle, luxury, shopping and travel.  

How can I apply for a Credit Card online?  

You can apply online by visiting the ICICI Bank website and selecting a Credit Card that best suits your needs. Make sure to check the eligibility criteria. Fill out the application form with your details. You can also visit any ICICI Bank branch, where our team will help you with the application process. Remember to keep your documents like Identity Proof, Address Proof and Income Statements ready with you.  

What is the welcome gift for Manchester United Cardholders?

As a welcome gift, Manchester United Credit Cardholders receive a Manchester United-Branded Holdall and Football. This exclusive merchandise adds to the excitement for fans of the club.

What does the top monthly spender receive?

The top spender (across all Cardholders) every month receives a signed Manchester United jersey, branded football or t-shirt.

What benefits do the top 18 spenders receive?

The top 18 spenders across Cardholders are rewarded with an exclusive fully-paid Manchester United Experience. This includes a private stadium and museum tour in Manchester, England, an opportunity to watch part of a First Team squad training session and a visit to the Manchester United megastore.

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Manchester United Signature Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

 Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant.  Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’

What is included in the private stadium tour?

The private stadium tour includes a guided visit to the historic Manchester United stadium and museum, offering an immersive experience of the club's legacy and achievements.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your Manchester United Signature Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

Please note that the airport lounge access benefit is available only for primary Cardholders. For more details on the criteria,

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹150 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets through BookMyShow and INOX. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders can avail of a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions of up to ₹4,000 per transaction when the card is swiped at any HPCL fuel pump using ICICI Bank Point of Sale (POS) machine.
