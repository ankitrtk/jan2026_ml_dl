ICICI Bank Plus Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Plus Card?

The ICICI Bank Plus Card is an add-on Credit Card linked to a primary 
ICICI Bank Credit Card
, designed to provide flexibility and benefits to family members of the primary cardholder.

Who is the ICICI Bank Plus Card suitable for?

This card is ideal for families who want to share a single credit line while giving individual members the convenience and freedom of using their own card.

Are transactions on the Plus Card secure?

Yes, the card uses advanced encryption technology to ensure secure transactions. Additionally, Plus Cardholders can authenticate transactions with OTPs and transaction alerts are sent to their registered mobile numbers. Cardholders also receive transaction details in the consolidated monthly statements, offering complete peace of mind.

How are reward points managed for the ICICI Bank Plus Card?

All reward points earned through transactions on the Plus Card are consolidated into the primary cardholder's reward points account.

What can I redeem my reward points for?

You can redeem your reward points across a variety of categories, including vouchers, travel, electronics and lifestyle products, through the ICICI Bank rewards portal.

How many Plus Cards can I apply for under one Credit Card?

You can apply for up to 3 Plus Cards under a single primary Credit Card. This allows you to share your credit line with up to three family members while keeping expenses consolidated in one statement.

How do I apply for the ICICI Bank Plus Card?

You can apply online through the ICICI Bank website or visit any ICICI Bank branch. Ensure that you have the required documents like proof of identity, proof of income and proof of address for a smooth application process.

How does the ICICI Bank Credit Card shared limit work for multiple cards?

The ICICI Bank Credit Card shared limit applies when a primary cardholder adds a Plus (Add-on) Card. The add-on card shares the primary card’s approved credit limit. Both cards can be used separately, but all transactions are adjusted against the same combined limit.
