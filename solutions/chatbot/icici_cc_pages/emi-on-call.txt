EMI On Call

EMI On Call: Easy post-purchase EMI on Credit Card

Get ready to experience next-level convenience in shopping, with EMI On Call. This solution by ICICI Bank lets you convert the purchases on your ICICI Bank 
Credit Card
 into EMIs (Equated Monthly Instalments). You can make purchases instantly and pay for them at your convenience in the form of EMIs, over a period ranging from 3 months – 24 months.

CONVERT NOW

Benefits of EMI on Call

Flexible tenure options

You can choose from a range of tenures for your EMIs, from 3 months – 24 months.

Low interest rate

Convert your transactions into EMIs by paying interest of up to 1.5% per month, on a monthly reducing balance basis.

Low one-time processing fee

You need to pay a one-time processing fee of up to 2% of the transaction value at the time of EMI conversion.

24/7 Service

Convert your transactions into EMIs anytime, anywhere.

Features of the EMI On Call facility

Transactions of ₹1,500 and more on an ICICI Bank Credit Card can be converted into EMIs through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app, Net Banking, WhatsApp Banking and Customer Care
Transactions of ₹3,000 and more on an ICICI Bank Credit Card can be converted into EMIs through the 
3D Secure page
 or 
OTP page
Convert your transactions into EMIs and pay interest at up to 1.5% per month on a monthly reducing balance basis and enjoy a lower rate of interest than the revolving rate you would normally pay on your Credit Card
A one-time processing fee of up to 2% of the transaction value is charged at the time of EMI conversion
Purchase of gold, jewellery and fuel and gambling and cash transactions will not be eligible for EMIs
Transactions older than 30 days cannot be converted into EMIs

EMI On Call FAQs

What is the ‘EMI On Call’ facility?

After making a purchase with your Credit Card, you can choose to pay for it in the form of EMIs (Equated Monthly Instalments) through ICICI Bank's iMobile app, WhatsApp Banking, Net Banking, the OTP page or using your phone. ICICI Bank’s EMI On Call facility helps you pay the instalments conveniently over a tenure that ranges from 3 months – 24 months.

What types of transactions can be converted to ‘EMI On Call’?

All retail transactions of ₹ 1,500 and above can be converted into EMIs, with some exceptions:

Transactions older than 30 days cannot be converted into EMIs

Certain categories of transactions like cash, gambling, fuel, gold and jewellery purchase, etc. are not eligible for the ‘EMI On Call’ facility. In particular, jewellery & gold transactions cannot be converted into EMIs as per the guidelines issued by RBI from time to time.

What are the charges applicable on availing the EMI On Call facility?

This facility offers EMI payments with interest payment at a rate of up to 1.5% per month on a monthly reducing balance basis. A one-time processing fee of up to 2% of the transaction value is charged at the time of EMI conversion. A foreclosure fee of up to 3% and advance EMI interest and GST as applicable shall be chargeable if you opt for pre-closure of the EMIs.

How can I pay the EMIs through ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Customers wondering how to pay EMI through Credit Card do not need to make a separate payment for EMIs. The EMI amount is included in the monthly ICICI Bank Credit Card statement and can be paid along with the total outstanding amount as per the billing cycle, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

What if I convert my Credit Card transaction into EMI after the statement is generated?

You can deduct the converted amount from the statement bill and pay the differential amount.

Will converting to EMI affect my credit score?

Converting to EMI does not negatively impact your credit score as long as you make timely EMI payments. However, late payments can affect your credit score.

Is No Cost EMI available on an ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Yes, No Cost EMI may be available on select transactions, subject to merchant tie-ups and ICICI Bank terms and conditions.

Who is eligible for No Cost EMI on Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card?

All customers who hold an active Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card are eligible for No Cost EMI, subject to applicable merchant terms and transaction criteria.
