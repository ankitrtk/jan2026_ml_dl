MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card FAQs

Are there any foreign transaction fees on the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Yes, foreign transaction fees may apply when you use the Card for international purchases. However, the forex markup is only 0.99% + GST.

Which transactions do not qualify for earning myCash on the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Rent payments, cash advances, late payment costs, joining / annual fees, EMI transactions, Balance Transfers and Personal Loan transactions on the Card are not eligible for myCash Rewards.

How to avail Complimentary International Airport lounge access?

Enjoy 1 complimentary accesses to international airport lounges with MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card. Complimentary benefits are applicable only for the primary cardholder.

Priority Pass Membership

Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for the primary cardholder with MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card

Access 1,400+ lounges in 145+ countries worldwide

Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.

For complete list of international airport lounges visit 
https://www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges

How can I redeem myCash earned through the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You can redeem your myCash on the MakeMyTrip website, mobile site or app during checkout. Select 'myCash & Gift Cards' on the payments page, choose the ‘myCash’ option, enter the amount to redeem, click 'Use & Apply' and then click 'Complete Payment' to complete the transaction.

Can I encash or transfer myCash earned through the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card to my bank account?

No, myCash earned through the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card cannot be encashed or transferred to a bank account.

How to apply for an MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Click here
 to visit the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card page

Scan the QR code on your mobile phone

Download the MakeMyTrip app and apply for the Card.

Who can claim the lounge access benefit of the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card lounge access benefit is available to the primary Cardholder. With this benefit, you can enjoy complimentary entry to select airport lounges, offering a premium travel experience before your flight.

What are some of the most attractive benefits of the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Some of the most attractive benefits of the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card are unlimited myCash Rewards that never expire, exclusive MakeMyTrip vouchers and complimentary lounge access for the primary Cardholder. You’ll also enjoy the flexibility of two payment networks - MasterCard and RuPay, making this Card a versatile and rewarding choice for frequent travellers.

Is DigiLocker sufficient for applying for the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Yes, DigiLocker can be used to submit your documents when applying for the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card. It provides a secure and digital platform to store and share essential documents, such as proofs of identity, address and income, making the application process faster and more convenient. Simply upload the required documents to DigiLocker and share them during your application.
