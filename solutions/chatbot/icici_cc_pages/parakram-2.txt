Parakram Credit Card FAQs

Who should use the Parakram Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

The Parakram Credit Card from ICICI Bank is best suited for Defence personnel.

What are the benefits of the ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card comes with benefits such as reward points on CSD purchases, airport lounge access, insurance benefits and fuel surcharge waiver.

How many reward points can be earned on everyday purchases with the Parakram Credit Card?

Earn 1 reward point for every ₹100 spent and 5 accelerated reward points for every ₹100 spent at grocery stores, department stores and Canteen Stores Departments (CSD), with a maximum of 1,000 reward points earned per month.

Can I make an online purchase with the ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card?

Yes. You can easily make online purchases in India as well as at select overseas online stores using the ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card.

What are the lounge benefits that the Parakram Credit Card offers?

The Parakram Credit Card comes with 1 airport lounge access per quarter.

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹ 75,000 or more on your Parakram Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

Please note that the airport lounge access benefit is available only for primary Cardholders. For more details on the criteria, click here. To view the complete list of lounges, 
click here.

Which Credit Card is ideal for Defence personnel?

The ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card is an excellent choice for Defence personnel. It offers reward points on almost every purchase.

What are the insurance benefits of the Parakram Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card has personal insurance cover of ₹2 lakh and air accident insurance cover of ₹20 lakh.
