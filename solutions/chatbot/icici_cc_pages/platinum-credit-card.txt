MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card FAQs

Does the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card provide any offers on MakeMyTrip?

Yes. It does. The Credit Card offers a MakeMyTrip holiday voucher of up to ₹3,000.

Is the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card free?

No. The Credit Card has a one-time joining fee of ₹500+ GST.

What is CVV in a Credit Card? How do I find it for my MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

Flip your Credit Card. The three-digit security code next to the signature panel is your CVV. You can also check it by logging in to your ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

How can I know the due date of my MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

You can check the due date of your Credit Card through your monthly statements, ICICI Bank's iMobile app, iMobile, Net Banking or by calling 1800 1080.

What is myCash?

If you hold the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card, you will earn MakeMyTrip myCash on certain transactions. These rewards can be redeemed through the MakeMyTrip platform against bookings made on the MakeMyTrip platform

What are the criteria for availing domestic airport lounge access?

To enjoy domestic airport lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your MakeMyTrip Platinum Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter.

What are the criteria for availing railway lounge access?

Enjoy 1 complimentary railway lounge access in a quarter to participating lounges in India

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider.

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders can avail of a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions of ₹400 to ₹4,000 at HPCL fuel outlet when the Card is swiped on ICICI Merchant Services swipe machines in India. This benefit applies to both the primary Cardholder and the Plus (supplemental) cardholder, helping them save on regular fuel expenses. The waiver is automatically applied when the eligible transaction is completed using the Credit Card.

What fraud protection benefits are available to Cardholders?

Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with Counterfeit Card Liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the Card provides an Account Takeover Cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The 
Credit Card
 comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please 
click here

What are the criteria for availing domestic railway lounge access?

Upgrade your travel experience with complimentary access to railway with your MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card. Get 1 complimentary access to select railway lounges in India every quarter. Simply present MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card to enjoy the privileges

Over and above the permitted number of complimentary access, each subsequent access will be charged as per rates laid out by each lounge service provider.

The access to railway lounges will be applicable only for primary cardholders.

Click here (PDF, 95.3KB)
 for a complete list of lounges.

Where can I find the list of lounges available for MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card Holders?

You can check participating lounge websites to clarify if the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card is accepted.

Where can I find the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card lounge access list?

You can find the 
MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card lounge access list
 on the official MakeMyTrip website page dedicated to the card. The complete list of eligible lounges is also available in the downloadable document in the tabs ‘Domestic lounges’ and ‘International lounges’ provided on the page.

What are the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card charges?

The charges for the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card are detailed in the Fees and Charges section. Applicable charges may vary based on card usage and the specific 
terms and conditions.
