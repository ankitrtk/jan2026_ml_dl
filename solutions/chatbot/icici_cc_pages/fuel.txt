Fuel Credit Card FAQs

Is there an option to apply for an ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card online?

You can apply for a Fuel Credit Card online by visiting the ICICI Bank website and selecting a card that best suits your needs. Make sure to check the eligibility criteria. Fill out the application form with your details. You can also visit any ICICI Bank branch and a Bank representative will help you apply for a Credit Card. Keep your documents like proof of identity, proof of address and Income statements handy.

What is the minimum due for an ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card?

The minimum due is the smallest amount you need to pay by the due date to avoid late fee. It is around 5% of your total outstanding balance plus any Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) instalments, interest, fees or overdue amounts. 

Will paying the minimum due save me from a penalty? 

Yes. Paying only the minimum due will help you avoid a penalty. However, it will lead to interest charges on the remaining balance.

How can I use an ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card for an in-store purchase? 

To use your ICICI Bank Credit Card for an in-store purchase, just swipe or tap your card on the card machine and enter your Personal Identification Number (PIN). 

How can I pay the bill for my ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card? 

You can pay your ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card bill through Net Banking, ICICI Bank's iMobile app, the iMobile app, National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT), Unified Payments Interface (UPI), by setting up the Auto Debit facility for your account or by visiting an ICICI Bank branch or an Automated Teller Machine (ATM).

How can I know the due date of my ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card?

You can check the due date of your ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card through the monthly statements, the iMobile app, Net Banking or by calling 1800 1080.

What are ICICI Bank Credit Card reward points?

If you are a cardholder, you will earn ICICI Bank reward points on various transactions. These points can be redeemed through the Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. You can use them to purchase products, vouchers, E-Gift Cards and more.

What is a fuel surcharge waiver on a Credit Card? What are the other benefits of a Fuel Credit Card?

A fuel surcharge waiver is a Fuel Credit Card benefit from ICICI Bank, waiving off the additional fee or surcharge that is typically added to fuel purchases. You can avail this benefit if you have a Fuel Credit Card from ICICI Bank. Other benefits of a Fuel Credit Card include cashback, reward points on fuel purchases and exclusive discounts on other utilities, making this fuel Credit Card an excellent choice for savings on fuel expenses.

What are Fuel Credit Cards?

If you are an individual seeking financial flexibility, ICICI Bank Credit Cards are exactly what you need. You get benefits like cashback, reward points and exclusive access to premium services, all while experiencing the joy of hassle-free payments. ICICI Bank provides a diverse range of Credit Cards keeping in mind the varying needs of every individual.

What is the lowest Credit Card limit?

The lowest Credit Card limit varies depending on the card issuer. Call 1800 1080 for more details.

How can I see my ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card number online?

To find your ICICI Bank Credit Card number, log in to ICICI Bank's iMobile app and view your details.

How can I redeem Credit Card reward points?

First, log in to ICICI Bank's iMobile app. Go to the ‘Services’ section > Accounts Services > View Reward Points. From the dropdown, select your Credit Card number and then click on ‘Submit’. You will be able to view your reward points. Click on ‘Redeem Now’. You can redeem your ICICI Bank reward points across multiple categories such as Electronics, Lifestyle, Luxury, Shopping and Travel.

How can I increase the limit of ICICI Bank Fuel Credit Card through ICICI Bank's iMobile app?

You can increase your credit limit through ICICI Bank's iMobile app by navigating to the ‘Cards or Forex or Paylater’ section. Select your ‘Card Number’. Go to ‘Manage Card’. Click on ‘Manage Credit Limit’ (under More options). Select the ‘Desired Credit Limit’ and click on ‘Submit’. In case of any queries, call 1800 1080.

How can I check my Credit Card details online? 

To check your 
ICICI Bank Credit Card
 details, log in to  ICICI Bank's iMobile app. Here, you can view all the details related to your Credit Card.

What is a petroleum Credit Card?

A petroleum Credit Card is the same as a Fuel Credit Card. This Credit Card is designed to offer savings on fuel purchases. ICICI Bank’s Fuel Credit Cards provide cashback, reward points and fuel surcharge waivers when used at petrol pumps. These Cards partner with fuel companies and are perfect for daily commuters looking to reduce monthly fuel costs while earning rewards.

Can I use a Fuel Credit Card at any fuel station?

You can apply for a Fuel Credit Card from ICICI Bank and use it at most fuel stations, but the best benefits are usually applicable only at specific partner outlets. Using the Card at pumps other than the partner pumps might not offer rewards or surcharge waivers, so read the terms and conditions carefully before applying for a Card or using it.

Are there Credit Cards specifically for diesel as fuel?

Cashback and Reward points on most Fuel Credit Cards apply to diesel purchases also. They also offer surcharge waivers on fuel spends, regardless of fuel type. However, always check the Card’s terms and partner fuel stations to reconfirm whether diesel purchases are eligible for benefits and rewards.
