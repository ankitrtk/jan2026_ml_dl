Domestic Lounge Programme

Every journey now feels exceptional with a premium Domestic Airport Lounge Programme that provides access to an exclusive network of 80+ exquisite domestic airport lounges across India. Step into an environment designed for comfort, where you can relax, rejuvenate and enhance your travel experience.

Enjoy curated amenities including refined ambience, gourmet refreshments, comfortable seating and a perfect escape from the airport crowd. Whether you are travelling for business or leisure, the lounge experience is designed to enhance every stage of your journey.

Access to domestic lounges is available through your eligible ICICI Bank Credit Card, ensuring a seamless and elevated travel experience.

Note: We have updated the eligibility criteria for our domestic lounge access to enhance your travel experience. To avail this benefit, you must spend a minimum of ₹75,000 or more on your eligible Credit Card within a calendar quarter. The eligible spend entitles you to access lounge benefits in the subsequent calendar quarter.

Domestic airport lounge access benefit is exclusively available to primary cardholders.

Emeralde Private Metal Credit Cards, Diamant Credit Cards and Times Black Credit Cards provide complimentary unlimited domestic lounge access.

For the customers with dual Credit Card variants of Rubyx and Sapphiro (Mastercard & Amex / Visa & RuPay), the benefit will be equally distributed amongst both the variants.

International Lounge Programme

Get access to a global network of 1,800+ international airport lounges across 146 countries with your Priority Pass Membership Card. Designed for comfort and convenience, these lounges provide premium seating, curated dining options and an elegant space to unwind before your flight.

With seamless worldwide access, your Priority Pass Membership Card delivers a reliable and elevated standard of service across leading international airports. Whether you are travelling for business or leisure, the lounge experience is designed to enhance every stage of your journey.

Note: The Priority Pass Membership Card is eligible for complimentary airport lounge access outside India. Customers are required to present their valid Priority Pass Membership Card at the lounge to avail the complimentary international (global) lounge access benefit. The Priority Pass Membership Card remains valid until the expiry date of the associated Credit Card. Any lounge visit exceeding the complimentary access entitlement will be chargeable. The international (global) airport lounge access benefit is exclusively available to primary cardholders except Emeralde Private Metal, Diamant and Times Black Credit Cards.

The applicable charges for additional international lounge access will be billed to the Credit Card within 60 days from the month of usage and will appear in the subsequent statement.

The Priority Pass Membership Card is included in the Welcome Kit provided along with the eligible Credit Card.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the ICICI Bank Credit Card Experience Programme?

The ICICI Bank Credit Card Experience Programme is an exclusive programme for select ICICI Bank Credit Card customers. By opting for this programme, you get handpicked privileges and offers from top brands.

Where can I find privileges or experiences specific to my card? 

To find an experience specific to your card, go to the Credit Card Experience Programme Page > Eligible Cards. You will find the privileges or experiences specific to your card.

How to keep track of all the latest offers and privileges?

You will receive regular updates about the latest offers and privileges on your registered mobile number and e-mail ID. If you want to know more about any offer or need any other information related to the Credit Card Experience Programme, you can check the Credit Card Experience Programme page or offers page on our website.

How do I avail the BookMyShow offer?

To avail your offer, you can follow the below steps on BookMyShow:

1. Select the movie you want to watch

2. Click on ‘Book tickets’ 

3. Select the location and venue

4. Select the number of seats and proceed 

5. Click on ‘Pay’

6. Accept the T&Cs 

7. Click on the ‘Unlock Offers’ or ‘Promo Code’ button

8. Click on the ICICI Bank Credit Card option 

9. Choose the offer applicable to your Credit Card. (For example: Up to ₹ 500 off on the second ticket or 25% off up to ₹ 100 on the second ticket)

10. Enter the entire Credit Card number 

11. Make the payment when you see the ‘offer applied’ message. 

What movie offers are available for ICICI Bank Credit Card customers?

You will get discounts on platforms like BookMyShow and INOX.

What travel benefits do I get with my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You will get travel benefits like complimentary lounge access at airports and railway stations, discounts and offers on travel booking sites, Uber vouchers and more.

Which Credit Card is the best for travel benefits?

You can opt for ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card. It provides complimentary international and domestic airport lounge access, welcome vouchers from Uber & EaseMyTrip, air accident insurance cover up to ₹ 3 crore and more.

Are there any dining offers I can avail with my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

There are limited time culinary offers on Swiggy, Zomato and EazyDiner that you can avail with your ICICI Bank Credit Card.

Which ICICI Bank Credit Card is most suitable for Football enthusiasts?

The Manchester United Credit Card by ICICI Bank is perfect for Football enthusiasts. It comes with several amazing offers and an opportunity to win a fully paid trip to Old Trafford.

How can I win a trip to Old Trafford using my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You can use the Manchester United Credit Card by ICICI Bank and get a fully paid trip to Old Trafford by becoming one of the top 18 spenders.

Which ICICI Bank Credit Card offers fuel savings?

ICICI Bank offers a wide range of Credit Cards such as ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card and ICICI Bank Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) Super Saver Credit Card with the benefit of fuel surcharge waiver. 

Can I use this PIN for cash withdrawals?

Yes, you can use the same PIN for cash withdrawals through Automated Teller Machines (ATMs). Please note that terms of the Cash Withdrawal facility will be as communicated at the time of delivery of your card. For more information on the same, please contact our Customer Care.

Can I use my Chip and PIN Credit Card overseas?

Yes, you can use your Chip and PIN Credit Card overseas. Please note that in a few countries, Chip and PIN cards are not the market norm and all the point of sale (POS) terminals may not ask for the PIN. In such a scenario, you can continue to use your card by swiping the magnetic stripe.

Do I need to register my new Chip and PIN card for 3D Secure for online transactions?

Yes, you will need to register your new Chip and PIN card for 3D Secure (Verified by VISA or MasterCard SecureCode or American Express Safekey) for online transactions at www.icicibank.com

How do I convert my existing magnetic stripe credit card to Chip and PIN Credit Card?

Please call our Customer Care to convert your existing magnetic stripe Credit Card to Chip and PIN Credit Card.

What is a Chip and PIN Credit Card?

ICICI Bank 
Chip Credit Cards
 come with an embedded micro chip which provides additional security against counterfeiting or duplication of the card. It also provides an extra layer of security in the form of a Personal Identification Number (PIN). You will be required to enter your 4-digit PIN on the terminal for transactions at merchant outlets.

Will I receive a separate PIN for my Add-on cards?

Yes, you will receive a separate PIN at your mailing address for each Add-on card that has been issued to you.

Would every POS terminal ask for a PIN?

All merchants in India are progressively moving towards Chip and PIN configuration on their terminals. If the terminal is already configured for a Chip and PIN card, it will prompt for a PIN for transactions on the Chip and PIN card. If the terminal is not configured for a Chip and PIN card, you can continue to use your card without entering the PIN.

When will I receive my Credit Card PIN?

Your 4-digit PIN will be sent to you separately at your mailing address and should reach you within 7 working days of receiving your Credit Card.

For Ferrari Platinum Cardholders, your 4 digit PIN is sent through SMS on registered mobile number. In case your mobile number is not registered, the PIN is sent separately at your mailing address.

Alternatively, to generate a PIN number for your ICICI Bank Credit Card please follow the below steps.

Pin generation through ICICI Bank's iMobile app:

Login to Net Banking with your user ID and Password > My Accounts > Credit Cards > Generate Credit Card PIN Online

(to be used only for Primary card PIN generation, for add-on card same is not applicable)

Pin generation through ICICI Bank's iMobile app:

Login to ICICI Bank's iMobile app > Click on Smartkeys & Services > Click on Card Services > Generate credit card PIN. Enter the required details and Submit.

Customer Care:

Dial our Customer Care from your registered mobile number and follow the instructions to generate the PIN.

click here
.

Please note:

1. After 3 incorrect PIN attempts your PIN will be blocked and you have to generate a new PIN.

2. After 9 incorrect PIN entry attempts your card will be blocked automatically and you will need to call our Customer Care for issuance of a replacement Credit Card.

How do I avail the Buy 1 Get 1 movie ticket offer using ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

Here is the process to avail the Buy 1 Get 1 movie ticket offer:

Visit the BookMyShow website

Select the movie, cinema, showtime and choose at least two tickets

Proceed to checkout and open “Avail Discounts & Offers”

Select the “ICICI Bank Credit Card Offer”

Enter your card details to apply the discount and complete the transaction

How many free movie tickets can I get per month with ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

The number of complimentary movie tickets depends on the specific ICICI Bank Credit Card variant.

Cardholders can get up to 2 free movie tickets per month with ICICI Bank Sapphiro Credit Card, ICICI Bank Emerald Credit Card and ICICI Bank Emerald Private Metal Credit Card. Cardholders of ICICI Bank Diamant Credit Card can get up to 4 free movie tickets per month, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
