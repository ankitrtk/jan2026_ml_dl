Priority Pass Terms and Conditions

Priority Pass ICICI Bank Terms & Conditions.

The International Lounge Access Programme is an airport lounge access programme that allows access to affiliated airport lounges globally with a valid Membership Card.
Membership to the International Lounge Access Programme is provided on select ICICI Bank Credit Cards on a complimentary basis as specified.
Present your Membership Card at the participating lounge to avail complimentary international lounge access benefit.
Except ICICI Bank Diamant, Times Black, Emeralde Private Metal, Emeralde, and Emirates Emeralde Credit Cards all other applicable ICICI Bank Credit Cards shall be liable to pay for each lounge visit over and above the complimentary visits subject to per person, per visit charge.
Additional visits, including those by accompanying guests, shall be debited to the member’s Credit Card by ICICI Bank within 60 days from the month of usage and will be reflected in the subsequent month.
Member’s additional lounge visit charges will be debited after converting the charges in Indian Rupees. The foreign exchange rate applicable will be the rate prevailing on the date of debit plus applicable GST.
Guest visits are complimentary for ICICI Bank Diamant Credit Card members.
ICICI Bank will be able to provide a proof of the lounge visit only if received from the partner.
Usage of each individual lounge shall be governed by its own Terms & Conditions.
The Bank is not responsible for the quality of service provided within any of the lounges.
The lounge access shall be available to the Card members on the condition that the said ICICI Bank Credit Card continues to remain valid and in good standing.
The Membership Card will be blocked if the ICICI Bank Credit Card is blocked on account of any fraud/default.
Presentation of a blocked Membership Card at the lounge shall not be entertained.
The Membership Card is not a payment Card nor a proof of creditworthiness and attempts to use it as such could constitute fraud.
Lost, stolen or damaged Membership Cards are to be notified immediately to ICICI Bank.
The Card member should intimate ICICI Bank immediately upon loss of the Membership Card, to facilitate blocking of the same and ICICI Bank is not liable for any charges on the Membership Card between the time of loss of the Membership Card and reporting of the same to ICICI Bank.
The officials are not bound to announce flights and ICICI Bank shall not be held liable for any Card member / accompanying guests missing flights while in the lounge.
ICICI Bank shall not be held liable for any loss of possessions by the Card member while within the lounge.
The Membership Card is not transferable and is valid up to the date of expiry mentioned on the Card or until such time as the said ICICI Bank Credit Card is valid. Any lounge visits made by the Card member, including any guests, using an invalid Card, shall be charged to the Card member.
The Card member must abide by the rules & regulations of each participating lounge.
Access to the lounge may be restricted on account of space constraints and will be at the discretion of the lounge operator.
The participating lounges may choose to enforce a maximum stay period, beyond which the Card member may be charged. Participating lounges may reserve the right to enforce a maximum stay policy (usually 3-4 hours) to prevent overcrowding. This is at the discretion of the individual lounge operator who may impose a charge for extended stays.
The provision of free alcoholic drinks (where local law permits) is at the discretion of each lounge operator and in some cases may be limited. In such cases the Card member is responsible for paying any charges for additional consumption directly to the lounge staff.
Telephone facilities (where available) vary from lounge to lounge and are provided at the lounge operator's discretion. Free usage is normally limited to local calls only. Fax, shower, internet, and WiFi charges (where applicable) are at the discretion of each lounge operator and the card member is responsible for paying these directly to the lounge staff.
Entry to lounges is strictly subject to Card members and any guests being in possession of a valid flight ticket for the same day of travel. Airline, airport and other travel industry employees travelling on reduced-rate tickets may not be eligible for lounge access. Outside the US, flight tickets must be accompanied by a valid boarding pass for a departing flight, i.e., outbound passengers only. Please note some lounges in Europe are located within designated Schengen areas of the airport which means that access is only provided to these lounges if members are travelling between Schengen countries. (Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland).
Entry to lounges is subject to Card members and any guests (including children) behaving and dressing in an orderly and correct manner. Any infants or children causing discomfort to other members may be asked to vacate the lounge facilities. The partner is not liable for any loss suffered by the Card member and any guests, where a lounge operator has refused admission because the Card member and/or guests have not complied with these conditions.
All participating lounges are owned and operated by third party organisations. The Card member and accompanying guests must abide by the rules and policies of each participating lounge/club. Access may be restricted due to space constraints, but this will be wholly at the discretion of each individual lounge operator. The partner has no control over the facilities offered, the opening / closing times or the personnel employed by the lounges. The administrators will use every endeavour to ensure the benefits and facilities are available as advertised but do not warrant nor guarantee in any way that said benefits and facilities will be available at the time of the Card member’s visit.

The partner will not be liable for any loss to the Card member or any accompanying guests, arising from the provision or non-provision (whether in whole or in part) of any of the advertised benefits and facilities. All children accompanying the Card member (where permitted) will be subject to the full guest fee unless otherwise stated in the lounge listing.

ICICI Bank shall remain indemnified from any liabilities / damages / costs associated with the injury / death of any member or damage / destruction of any property arising out of use of any lounge.
ICICI Bank is not responsible for the goods / services obtained / availed at the airport lounges.
Any dispute or claim regarding the goods and services or offers must be resolved by the Card members with the partner directly without any reference to ICICI Bank.
In addition to these rules and regulations, the Card member shall be bound by the Terms & Conditions prescribed by the respective lounge operators and partners.
