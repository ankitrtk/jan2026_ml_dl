Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card FAQs

How can I apply for an Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

To apply, visit ICICI Bank’s website, complete the application process by entering your details and following the prompts or visit any branch.

What is the minimum spending requirement for the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card to enjoy benefits?

To enjoy annual fee waiver and other benefits, you need to meet a minimum spending requirement of ₹3 lakh in a calendar year.

What are the types of rewards that I can earn with the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

Earn Adani Reward Points on various spends including domestic, international and utility expenses, allowing you to maximise your rewards.

Is there a joining fee for the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

Yes, joining fee of ₹750 plus GST.

How do I activate my Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

Activate your card by following the instructions provided in the welcome kit, by logging into the ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking.

About Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards

With Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards, you can get lifestyle benefits, reward points and the joy of hassle-free payments. If you are someone who seeks financial flexibility, Adani One Credit Cards have a wide range of advantages to offer you.

What are the types of Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

There are two types:

Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card

Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card

What is the Joining Fee for Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards

Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card - ₹5,000 + GST

Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card - ₹750 + GST

What is the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

The Adani Platinum Credit Card is ideal for frequent flyers and comes with welcome vouchers, reward points, entertainment, airport or travel benefits.

What are the travel benefits offered with the Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

Enjoy 8 domestic lounge access, 4 porter services and 2 premium car parking services per year at Adani managed airports, for utmost convenience during travel.

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026. 

 Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section 

 Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking 

 Choose ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount Offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards 

 Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’ 

 Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page. 

How to apply for a Credit Card online?

Visit ICICI Bank’s website, go to Credit Cards and select your preferred Card. After submitting the application, complete the KYC verification and get your card.

What are the annual charges for Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

Adani One ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card - ₹750 + GST

Adani One ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card - ₹5,000 + GST

What are the main benefits of Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card benefits include discounts on fuel purchases, access to airport lounges and exclusive offers on movie tickets, enhancing your overall experience.

Can I apply for an Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Card online?

Yes, you can easily apply online through ICICI Bank’s website.

Who is eligible for Flight Voucher?

A customer who has paid their joining or annual fee and completed spends of  ₹10,000 (not including joining/annual fee, interest or bank levies) within 60 days of card activation. Customers who have availed card without joining or annual fee shall be ineligible for flight voucher.
