MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card FAQs

What are the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card benefits for new and renewal cardholders? 

When you pay the joining fee of the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card, you receive a MakeMyTrip voucher worth ₹1,000 that is valid for one year. Upon renewal, you get a ₹1,000 MakeMyTrip hotel voucher that is valid for three months. Finally, you also receive a complimentary one-year MMTBLACK Gold membership with added benefits and discounts. These benefits make your travel bookings even more rewarding.

Why have I received two MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card is offered as a dual proposition with both Mastercard and RuPay variants. You receive both Cards to provide enhanced network flexibility, ensuring seamless usage across a wider range of merchants and platforms.

How do I complete the KYC verification for my MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Provide your PAN and Aadhaar details and then complete video KYC with an ICICI Bank employee by answering a few questions and showing your original PAN card. The Video KYC process is available 24/7 for your convenience.

Does the myCash earned through MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Cards expire?

The myCash earned through the new MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Cards does not expire once credited to the primary Cardholder's MakeMyTrip wallet. However, myCash earned through the older MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature and Platinum Credit Cards expires within 12 months of the credit date

Can I apply for a MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card if I already have an ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Yes, you can apply for a MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card even if you already hold another ICICI Bank Credit Card. Your application will be processed based on the bank's internal policies. If approved, you will retain ownership of your existing ICICI Bank Credit Card and also get the new Credit Card.

What benefits does the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card offer?

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card offers several exclusive benefits including the MMTBLACK Platinum membership, savings of up to ₹3,600 annually on movie tickets booked on BookMyShow and the opportunity to earn 40,000 myCash points during travel. You will also receive MakeMyTrip holiday vouchers worth ₹2,500 and 1,500 myCash points on paying the joining fee. The joining fee for this Card is ₹2,500.

What are the features of the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card?

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card offers a range of travel benefits, including a myCash benefit worth ₹500. Cardholders receive a holiday voucher worth ₹3,000 and complimentary domestic airport and railway lounge access. The nominal joining fee for this Card is ₹500 and you can avail benefits worth up to ₹20,000 (T&Cs apply).

What makes the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card unique?

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card stands out with its unlimited myCash rewards that never expire and the ability to earn up to 6% myCash on MakeMyTrip bookings and 1% myCash on other retail purchases. It also provides complimentary lounge access at both domestic and international airports. The Card includes benefits like discounted movie tickets, a minimal joining fee of ₹999, and a one-year MMTBLACK Gold Membership, among other perks.
