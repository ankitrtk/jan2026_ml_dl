INOX Credit Card Offer Term and Conditions

The Offer details which are provided herein, is provided by a third party

This Offer is valid only on select 
ICICI Bank Credit Cards
. The offer can be availed only when payments are made using the eligible ICICI Bank Credit Card

Minimum 2 tickets need to be booked for availing the offer. This offer can be availed up to two times in a month. Limited ticket quota on first come, first-serve basis.

Maximum discount of 25% per ticket up to INR 150 on purchase of minimum two tickets up to two times in a month for below cards:

ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Cards

ICICI Bank MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Cards

ICICI Bank Ferrari Signature Credit Cards

InterMiles ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Cards

Any additional amount has to be borne by the customer.

Avail of 25% discount up to INR 100 on purchase of minimum two tickets up to two times in a month for below cards:

ICICI Bank Coral Credit Cards

InterMiles ICICI Bank Coral Credit Cards

ICICI Bank Ferrari Platinum Credit Cards

ICICI Bank MakeMyTrip Platinum Credit Cards

ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Cards

Any additional amount has to be borne by the customer.

The offer on dual credit cards can be availed up to once every month on the following dual cards:

ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Cards

InterMiles ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Cards

InterMiles ICICI Bank Coral Credit Cards

Discount is applicable only on the ticket price. Convenience fee is levied on each ticket (as applicable)

Please send your queries to 
contact@inoxmovies.com
 if the discount has not been completed

Users will have to buy movie tickets through www.inoxmovies.com to avail of the offer on tickets purchased

Offer is valid only for customers going directly to www.inoxmovies.com and not through any other website

Offer is valid at the Inox Box Office (except for Inox Siliguri Orbit, Siliguri City Centre, Panchkula NH22, Raipur City Centre, Bhiwadi Genesis Mall, Hind Inox Kolkata, Jorhat ABS Mall and Atria Mall, Worli, Mumbai)

The current purchase process at www.inoxmovies.com will remain unchanged till the time where the user opts for the ‘ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount’ offer. After this stage, the website will recognise the user and the offer related to the same, and will reduce the bill amount and inform the transacting user accordingly.

In case you have selected the ‘ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount’ offer, but the transaction doesn’t go through for some reason, please wait for 20 minutes before trying again to avail of the discount.

‘ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount’ offer is applicable at all Inox cinemas in India for which tickets can be booked through www.inoxmovies.com .

This offer cannot be combined with any offers/ discounts/ promotions available at www.inoxmovies.com .

Movie tickets are subject to availability at the respective Inox cinemas

Tickets once bought online shall be considered sold and cannot be cancelled, refunded or exchanged in any form whatsoever

In case of Box Office bookings, once the booking is completed, the confirmation e-mail/ SMS received from www.inoxmovies.com needs to be exchanged with the cinema representative at the Box Office or you can pick up copies of the tickets from the kiosk

User(s) shall not be entitled to compensation/ benefits in any form whatsoever in lieu of the offer being availed of

Inox Leisure Ltd. and ICICI Bank reserve the right to disqualify any User(s) from the benefits of the Programme. In case of any fraudulent activity, prosecution will be carried out according to the purpose of availing of the benefits under the Programme

Inox Leisure Ltd. and ICICI Bank reserve the right to modify/change all or any of the terms applicable to the Programme without assigning any reasons or without any prior intimation whatsoever

Inox Leisure Ltd. and ICICI Bank also reserve the right to discontinue the Programme without assigning any reasons or without any prior intimation whatsoever

Inox Leisure Ltd.’s general rules and regulations are applicable

ICICI Bank Users are not bound in any way to participate in this Programme. Any such participation is voluntary and the same is being made purely on a ‘best-effort basis’

Inox Leisure Ltd. and ICICI Bank reserve the right at any time without prior notice to add, alter, modify all or any of these terms and conditions or replace, wholly or in part, this Programme with any other Programme, whether similar to this Programme or not or to withdraw it altogether

In addition to the above, this offer on the website is also subject to www.inoxmovies.com and ICICI Bank’s General Terms of Use. Please refer to www.inoxmovies.com Terms and Conditions on the homepage

User(s) shall be required to give personal information and Card details online for tickets purchased at www.inoxmovies.com

In case of any dispute, the decision of Inox Leisure Ltd. will be final

The ICICI Bank offer is valid till Sep 30, 2023.
