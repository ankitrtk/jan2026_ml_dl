Instant EMI on ICICI Bank Retail Credit Cards – Terms and Conditions

Terms and conditions relating to Instant EMI on ICICI Bank retail Credit Cards

Updated on 30 August 2025

A processing fee of 2.99%, capped at a maximum of ₹299 + GST is applicable on all Instant EMI transactions w.e.f. 01 September 2025 at all merchant outlets or stores and online or e-commerce merchants.

Interest rate up to 20% and a processing fee of up to ₹999 can be charged for Instant EMI conversion of transactions related to two-wheelers.

Interest rate up to 11.40% and a processing fee of up to 2% of the loan amount can be charged for Instant EMI conversion of transactions related to ‘Apple for Life’ and ‘OnePlus Easy Upgrades’.

‘EMI’ or ‘Equated Monthly Instalment’ means the monthly instalment payable by the Card Member to ICICI Bank comprising of principal amount, interest and any or other charges, if applicable.

‘Instant EMI’ refers to the conversion to EMI upon the Card Member’s request. These are for transactions carried out using the retail 
Credit Card
 on the EDC (Electronic Data Capture) terminal installed at a merchant outlet and or for such online transactions, where the option for EMI payment is available.

Instant 
EMI on Credit Card
, without prejudice to the obligation of the Card Member to make immediate payment on the incurring of the charge, is a facility by which the Card Member may seek to avail the option of paying for certain purchases through Equated Monthly Instalments (EMIs) as informed to the Card Member by ICICI Bank’s Customer Care. The same is indicated in the Statement sent to the Card Member which shall be subject to the following terms:

If any portion of the billed and outstanding charges are not permitted to be paid in Instant EMI and if such portion is not paid by the Payment Due Date, the same will bear and carry an interest, Goods and Services Tax and late payment fee at the rate and in the manner stated in Clause VI;

The Instant EMI facility is available to the Card Member at ICICI Bank's sole discretion and this facility will be available for a certain period and at Merchant Establishments as decided by ICICI Bank. The rate of interest for Instant EMI will be specific to the Card Member and Merchant, as decided by ICICI Bank and may vary from time to time. The transaction amount, the tenure for repayment and other payment particulars will be specific to the Card Member and Merchant, as decided by ICICI Bank.

The Instant EMI facility is not applicable on ICICI Bank EMI Card, Corporate Credit Card, 
Business Card
 and American Express Card. ‘ICICI Bank’ and ‘I-man’ logo are trademarks and properties of ICICI Bank Limited. Misuse of any intellectual property or such other ancillary or related content with respect to this product / offer / programme is strictly prohibited.

Purchases made from categories relating to fuel, jewellery, watches, etc., may not be eligible for EMI conversion.

The Card Member shall be charged an interest rate on a monthly reducing basis, for availing the Instant EMI facility. The interest rate applicable will be communicated to the Card Member at the point of sale and will also be mentioned on the charge-slip.

Repayment

The card member shall repay the facility and interest thereon in EMIs. The interest shall be at such rate, which has been indicated by ICICI Bank to the Card Member at the time of making the offer for the facility and as communicated on the charge-slip. The interest calculation will start from the time the transaction has been booked for the facility. The EMI amount along with other details of the Facility (including rate of interest) shall also be communicated to the Card Member at his/her registered e-mail/mailing address.

The EMI amount due for a particular month shall subsequently reflect in the Credit Card Statement of Accounts (the ‘Statement’) of that particular month. The Card Member hereby authorises ICICI Bank to debit their Card Account monthly for the EMI due for that particular month, as has been intimated to the Card Member through the Statement. Principal and interest will be mentioned separately in the Statement. The Card Member shall pay the EMI and all other charges by way of cash, cheque, draft, online or through payment instructions to ICICI Bank and the cash limit or credit limit shall be reinstated to the extent of the EMI amount repaid by the Card Member.

The Card Member shall be required to pay the entire EMI amount for a particular month on the due date, as indicated in the Statement and the same shall not be permitted to be carried forward / included in the next Statement. If the payment is not made by the due date as specified, it shall be construed as a default by the Card Member who shall become liable to pay the amount together with late payment charges.

Goods and Services Tax will be applicable on processing fee, interest and foreclosure charges as per government mandate, w.e.f. 01 July 2017.

Prepayment of the Facility

The Card Member may close the facility (prepayment of the outstanding amount) at any time by contacting ICICI Bank's Customer Care to avail the foreclosure. Foreclosure charges of up to 3% on the outstanding principal amount plus next month’s interest shall be levied in the Card Account. The Card Member must contact ICICI Bank’s Customer Care for complete prepayment of the facility. ICICI Bank shall, without prejudice to all rights and remedies, have the right to call upon the Card Member to forthwith repay the Facility, all interest thereon and all other monies with respect to the facility, in case of a default in EMI payment for more than two consecutive months and the Card Member shall be liable to repay all such amounts on demand.

Cancellation of the Facility

Effective Date: 01 May 2025

To cancel the Credit Card EMI facility, the Card Member must call ICICI Bank Customer Care on 1800 1080 within 15 days from the date of the transaction. The eligibility conditions for cancellation are as follows:

A full refund has been received from the merchant as a single transaction, within 15 days of the original transaction

A partial refund is received as a single transaction within 15 days of the transaction date and the difference between original transaction amount and refund amount is less than ₹1,500.

Additional Points:

Foreclosure charges will be levied if the EMI option is opted for at the time of purchase and the customer later opts to cancel the EMI facility.

Upon discontinuation of the EMI facility, the deferred EMI interest payment amount, as confirmed at the time of availing the facility, will not be returned to the Card Member.

For the EMI availed via Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Credit Card transactions on third-party apps, the Card Member must call ICICI Bank Customer Care on 1800 1080 and provide the RRN (Retrieval Reference Number) of the original transaction to initiate cancellation in case of Merchant Refund.

Foreclosure charges will be applicable, even if the Card Member initiates EMI cancellation. 

 Particular Affirmative Covenants

The Card Member has understood ICICI Bank's method of calculating EMIs payable.

The Card Member has understood that by availing the facility, he or she shall not be, unless otherwise stated by ICICI Bank in writing, eligible for any reward points whatsoever, whether awarded under the ICICI Bank Reward Points Scheme or any other reward points scheme on the Card, that may be announced by ICICI Bank from time to time.

The Card Member shall not be eligible for any reward points whatsoever on cancellation of the Instant EMI facility.

The Card Member shall renew the Card when the Card expires during the tenure of the facility. If the Card Member does not renew the Card as stated above, ICICI Bank shall be entitled to call upon the Card Member to forthwith repay the facility without prejudice to all rights and remedies against the Card Member.

The Card Member shall notify ICICI Bank in writing, of any change in the Card Member's address within a week of such change.

The Card Member is deemed to have accepted these Terms & Conditions while requesting for the facility.

Repayment of the entire amount of the facility to ICICI Bank by the Card Member shall release the credit limit or cash limit for which the Card Member is eligible, by virtue of holding the Card.

The Card Member shall pay all taxes, duties, levies (including Goods and Services Tax) with respect to the facility.

Liabilities of the Card Member

ICICI Bank reserves the right to request the Card Member to make an advance payment of one or more EMIs against the grant of the facility. ICICI Bank shall have the right to adjust the amount of the advance EMIs towards the balance amount of the facility, at its sole discretion.

Events of Default 

If one or more of the events specified in this Clause (‘Events of Default’) occur or shall have occurred, ICICI Bank may foreclose the facility through a written Notice to the Card Member.

The card member's breach of any of the Terms and Conditions along with covenants contained herein or having made any misrepresentation to ICICI Bank

The Card Member has been declared as an insolvent

Any proceedings for misconduct have been initiated against the Card Member

The Card Member's failure to furnish any information or documents that may be required by ICICI Bank

The Card Member entering any arrangement with their creditors

The Card Member defaulting on any of the Terms and Conditions of any other Loan or Facility provided by ICICI Bank to the Card Member

The existence of any other circumstances, which, in the sole opinion of ICICI Bank, risks ICICI Bank's interest.

Upon the occurrence of any Event of Default as specified above, ICICI Bank shall be entitled to exercise rights and remedies available under these Terms & Conditions and the primary Terms & Conditions.
