ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Cards

Parakram Credit Cards are crafted to empower the Defence Personnel of India.

The Credit Card has 2 variants - ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card and ICICI Bank Parakram Select Credit Card. They offer exclusive benefits like complimentary Domestic lounge access, Personal and Air Accident Insurance and a fuel surcharge waiver. Reward Points accumulated can be used for groceries, department stores and Canteen Stores Department (CSD) purchases.

Parakram Credit Cards FAQs

Who should use the Parakram Select Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Parakram Select Credit Card is best suited for Defence Personnel and Paramilitary Personnel.

Who should use the Parakram Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

The Parakram Credit Card from ICICI Bank is best suited for Defence Personnel.

What rewards can be earned through everyday purchases with the Parakram Select Credit Card?

Earn 2 ICICI Bank Reward Points for every ₹100 spent and 10X Reward Points on grocery stores, department stores and Canteen Stores Department (CSD), with a maximum of 1000 Reward Points per month.

What rewards can be earned through everyday purchases with the Parakram Credit Card?

Earn 2 ICICI Bank Reward Points for every ₹100 spent and 5X Reward Points on grocery stores, department stores and Canteen Stores Department (CSD), with a maximum of 1000 Reward Points per month.

Can I make an online purchase with the ICICI Bank Parakram Credit Card? 

Yes. You can easily make online purchases PAN India as well as select overseas stores using the ICICI Bank Parakram Select Credit Card.

What are the main types of Credit Cards? 

The main Credit Card types are Platinum, Coral, Rubyx, Sapphiro and Emeralde. You can learn more about several other Credit Cards for travel, lifestyle, shopping and more, such as the Amazon Pay Credit Card, MakeMyTrip Credit Card, Adani One Credit Card and Emirates Skywards Credit Card, by exploring the ICICI Bank website. 

What is the lowest Credit Card limit? 

The lowest Credit Card limit varies depending on the card issuer. Call ICICI Bank’s Customer Service at 1800 1080 to get your queries resolved. 

How to check my Credit Card number online? 

To find your ICICI Bank Credit Card number, log in to the iMobile app or Internet Banking and view your details. 

How to redeem Credit Card points for rewards? 

You can experience seamless redemption of Rewards Points with ICICI Bank. First, log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking or the iMobile app. In the ‘Rewards’ section, you can redeem your ICICI Bank Reward Points across multiple categories such as Electronics, Lifestyle, Luxury, Shopping and Travel. 

How to apply for a Credit Card online? 

You can do it online by visiting ICICI Bank’s website and selecting a card that best suits your needs. Make sure to check the eligibility criteria. Fill out the application form with your details. You can also visit any ICICI Bank branch in person, where a representative will help you throughout the process. Don't forget to keep your documents like ID proof, address proof and income statements handy.
