Pre-Approved Credit Card FAQs

What is a Pre-Approved ICICI Bank Credit Card?

A Pre-Approved ICICI Bank Credit Card is an offer provided by ICICI Bank to eligible customers based on their creditworthiness and banking history. It does not require additional documentation or credit checks, making the approval process faster and easier.

Does a Pre-Approved Credit Card offer affect credit score?

A Pre-Approved Credit Card offer does not affect your credit score unless you choose to accept the offer and activate the card. 

How can I redeem a Pre-Approved Credit Card offer on the ICICI Bank's iMobile app?

To redeem your Pre-Approved Credit Card offer, log in to the ICICI Bank's iMobile app, navigate to the Credit Card section and check for available offers. You can then click on the offer, fill in any required details and submit your request for activating the offer.

Benefits of a Pre-Approved ICICI Bank Credit Card?

A Pre-Approved ICICI Bank Credit Card offers a range of lifestyle, travel and security benefits, from attractive discounts on shopping, dining and movies, to complimentary airport lounge access and fuel surcharge waivers. The card also allows you to earn ICICI Bank Reward Points and access 24/7 concierge services. Additionally, the card’s advanced chip technology ensures enhanced security, protecting you against fraud and unauthorised transactions.
