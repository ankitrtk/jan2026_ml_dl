Credit Card Benefits

Did you know that your 
ICICI Bank Credit Card
 comes packed with exclusive benefits like complimentary airport lounge access, reward points, fuel surcharge waivers, discount coupons and other such credit card benefits? You can also track your spending, set transaction limits and convert your purchases into EMIs - with a few clicks. Here, we will walk you through how to unlock and make the most of these powerful features.

ICICI Bank Credit Cards Features

Pay Without PIN

You can pay instantly by tapping your card on the cashier's Point of Sale (POS) terminal. This terminal should support contactless payment. This is one of those Credit Card features that makes it easy and convenient to pay for your purchases.

Scan and Pay

Another popular credit card benefit is that you can link it with your Unified Payments Interface (UPI)-enabled app. To make payments, all that you will need to do is scan its QR code.

Easy Bill Payments

One of the main Credit Card advantages is that you can set up auto-pay after registering your credit card and enabling this feature via ICICI Bank Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. This will help ensure that you never miss any of your credit card payments, saving you from penalties.

Convenience of ICICI Bank's iMobile app

By registering your credit card on the iMobile app, you will be able to keep track of your card status and ensure timely payment of bills.

Things You Should Know

How to Maximize Your Credit Card Benefits?

For example,

Mr. Rajiv spends ₹30,000 on flight tickets and earns accelerated reward points on such travel bookings.
They then convert this amount to a 3-month EMI via mobile banking, which will help you in managing your cash flow.
They use their Card to fill fuel worth ₹5,000 in their car and enjoys a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on this purchase.

All these Credit Card features are available through digital platforms, making it easy to track and control your credit card spending.

Customizing Your Credit Card Usage

To avoid overspending, Mr. Rajiv sets a monthly transaction limit of ₹50,000.
When traveling abroad, they temporarily enable international transactions for added convenience.
They regularly redeem reward points for gift vouchers and cashback.
This level of customization ensures your credit card adapts to your lifestyle, offering both flexibility and control, all with enhanced security.

Credit Card Benefits FAQs

Can I convert large purchases into EMIs?

Yes, ICICI Bank credit cards allow EMI conversion of purchases above a certain value, offering flexible payment options.

Is there any advantage to using a credit card over a debit card?

Yes, credit card advantages include building a credit history, earning rewards and access to short-term credit.

What are the advantages of a contactless credit card?

Contactless cards offer faster, more secure transactions while reducing the need to touch payment terminals.

Can I get lounge access through my credit card?

Yes, you can. Premium lounge access credit cards provide complimentary domestic lounge access, while international lounge access credit cards offer access to select international airport lounges as part of their top credit card benefits.

What are the milestone benefits of credit cards?

Milestone benefits reward you with vouchers, bonus points, or fee waivers when you reach a certain annual spend.

What are the security features of a credit card?

Security features include OTP verification, card lock or unlock and zero-liability fraud protection.

Is there a benefit to having multiple credit cards?

Yes, using multiple cards can optimise rewards and benefits across different spending categories.

Do credit cards have features to prevent overspending?

Yes, you can set daily or monthly limits and enable alerts to avoid crossing your budget. This is one of the most important Credit Card advantages.
