7 Different Types of Credit Cards in India

2 mins Read

               | 1 Year 11 Months Ago

APPLY NOW

KNOW MORE

Credit Cards are convenient and powerful financial tools, offering various benefits tailored to meet the diverse needs of cardholders. Understanding the different types of Credit Cards available in India can help you choose the one that best suits your lifestyle and financial goals.

You can make an informed decision that aligns with your spending habits and financial objectives by exploring these options. Rewards Cards, for instance, provide points for every purchase, which can be redeemed for various products and services. Cashback Cards offer a percentage of your spending back as cash, making them ideal for everyday purchases. Travel Cards, on the other hand, provide benefits such as air miles, lounge access and travel insurance, catering specifically to frequent travellers. From rewards and cashback cards to travel and lifestyle cards, each type is designed to offer specific advantages. This guide provides detailed insights into the major Credit Card types, highlighting their features and benefits.

Types of Credit Cards

Here is a list of the various types of Credit Cards provided by ICICI Bank:

Regular Credit Cards

The first type is Regular Credit Cards, which are ideal for routine spending or beginners. You can earn many benefits like cashback, reward points and fuel surcharge waivers. If this is your ideal card, then the ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card is a good option, providing discounts on dining and movie tickets.

Premium Credit Cards

The second type of Credit Cards

are the Premium Credit Cards that are mostly designed for users searching for enhanced benefits such as higher rewards, lounge access and better lifestyle privileges. If your goal of securing a Credit Card is complimentary airport lounge access and many other exclusive points across categories like shopping, dining, etc., then go for a Rubyx Credit Card from ICICI Bank.

Super Premium Credit Cards

Super Premium Cards cater to high-spending individuals, offering luxury benefits such as concierge services, international lounge access and premium travel rewards. The ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card provides wellness benefits, unlimited lounge access and no over-limit charges, making it ideal for frequent flyers and luxury lifestyle seekers.

Co-branded Credit Cards

Co-branded Credit Cards are launched in partnership with leading brands, offering brand-specific benefits like discounts, accelerated rewards and exclusive offers. ICICI Bank 
Amazon Pay Credit Card
, for example, provides cashback on Amazon for Prime users and attractive rewards elsewhere, perfect for online shoppers and loyal brand customers.

Commercial or Business Credit Cards

Business Credit Cards
 help manage corporate expenses efficiently. They offer higher credit limits, employee card options and detailed expense tracking. ICICI Bank Business Advantage Black Credit Card offers fuel and travel benefits, cashback on business spends and easy integration with expense management tools, ideal for SMEs and entrepreneurs.

Shopping Credit Cards

If you are an avid shopper, then applying for Shopping Credit Cards can be your best choice, as it offers maximum rewards on retail purchases, that includes both online and in-store shopping. You can get your hands on exclusive discounts, EMI options and reward points. The ICICI Bank Platinum Chip Credit Card is a smart option, making it great for budget-conscious shoppers.

Rewards Credit Cards

Rewards Credit Cards are designed to offer points, cashback or other incentives for using the card. These rewards can be accumulated and redeemed for various products, services or discounts.

Points System:
 Each time you use the card, you earn points based on your spending. These points can be redeemed for gifts, vouchers or travel bookings

Cashback:
 Some cards offer a percentage of your spending back as cash, which can be used to pay off the Credit Card bill or transferred to your bank account

Flexibility:
 Rewards can often be used for various options, from flights and hotel stays to shopping and dining.

Travel Credit Cards

Travel Credit Cards
 cater to frequent travellers, offering benefits like air miles, free lounge access and travel insurance.

Air Miles:
 Every rupee spent on travel-related expenses like flight tickets, hotel bookings or dining earns air miles. These miles can be redeemed for availing discounted or even free air tickets

Lounge access:
 Complimentary access to airport lounges is a significant perk, providing comfort and convenience during travel

Travel Insurance:
 Many Travel Credit Cards come with travel insurance, which covers trip cancellation, lost luggage and medical emergencies abroad.

Fuel Credit Cards

Fuel Credit Cards
 are tailored for those who frequently spend on fuel, offering benefits such as fuel surcharge waivers and reward points for fuel purchases.

Fuel Surcharge Waiver:
 Cardholders can save on the surcharge typically added to fuel transactions, making fuel purchases more economical

Reward points on fuel:
 Earn points for each fuel purchase, which can be redeemed for fuel or other rewards

Partner stations:
 Some cards offer additional benefits when used at partner fuel stations, enhancing savings and convenience.

Cashback Credit Cards

Cashback Credit Cards provide direct cashback on your purchases, making them ideal for everyday spending.

Percentage back:
 Cardholders receive back a percentage of their spending as cash. This cashback can vary based on the spending category, such as groceries, utilities or online shopping

Statement Credit:
 The cashback earned is usually credited to the card account, reducing the overall bill

Monthly limits:
 Some cards may limit the amount of cashback you can earn each month, so it is essential to understand the terms.

Lifestyle Credit Cards

Lifestyle Credit Cardsoffer a variety of benefits across different categories, including dining, shopping, entertainment and wellness.

Dining discounts:
 Enjoy discounts and offers at partner restaurants, making dining out more affordable

Shopping offers:
 Special deals and discounts at various retail stores and online marketplaces

Entertainment benefits:
 Access to exclusive events, movie ticket offers and discounts on entertainment-related expenses

Wellness perks:
 Discounts on gym memberships, spa treatments and health-related services.

Student Credit Cards

Student Credit Cards
 are specifically designed for students, offering features that help them manage their finances and build a credit history.

Lower limits:
 These cards typically have lower credit limits, reducing the risk of overspending and making them manageable for students

Simplified application:
 The application process is often simpler, with fewer requirements than standard Credit Cards.

Secured Credit Cards

Secured Credit Cards
 are an excellent option for those with no credit history or a poor credit score. They require a security deposit, which acts as collateral.

Credit building:
 Using a secured Credit Card responsibly helps build or rebuild credit scores

Deposit-based:
 The credit limit usually equals the security deposit made, reducing the issuer's risk

Transition to unsecured:
 Many issuers offer the option to transition to an unsecured Credit Card after demonstrating responsible use over time.

Choosing the right Credit Card

Selecting the right 
Credit Card
 depends on your spending habits, financial goals and lifestyle needs. Here are some tips to help you choose the best card:

Assess your spending:
 Identify where you spend the most money and choose a card that offers the best rewards or benefits in those categories

Compare fees:
 Look at the annual fees, interest rates and other charges associated with the card to ensure it aligns with your budget

Read the fine print:
 Understand the Terms and Conditions, including reward redemption processes, caps on earnings and any exclusions.

Conclusion

Credit Cards offer a range of benefits that can cater to various financial needs and lifestyle preferences. You can select a card that aligns with your specific requirements by understanding the different types of Credit Cards available in India. The right Credit Card can provide financial advantages and enhance your spending power.

Analyse your options carefully, consider your spending habits and choose a card that supports your financial goals and manages your overall financial situation. As the financial arena continues to evolve, staying updated about the latest features and benefits offered on different Credit Cards can help you make the most of them. Whether you seek rewards for your spending, cashback on everyday purchases, travel perks or ways to build your credit, there is a Credit Card type aligned perfectly to your goals.
