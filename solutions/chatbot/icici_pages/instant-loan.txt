About Instant Personal Loan

Advantages of Instant Personal Loan

An instant Personal Loan provides access to funds in a few clicks with a 100% digital process. Applicants can complete the journey online, from entering loan details to final disbursement. The facility offers instant approval and quick fund transfer. No collateral is required to apply, making it suitable for urgent financial needs. Eligible applicants can also benefit from loan amounts of up to ₹50 lakh. Additionally, there are no foreclosure charges after 24 EMIs.

Fees & Charges of Instant Personal Loan

A processing fee of up to 2% + GST is applicable on the instant personal loan. All applicable charges are displayed before loan approval to ensure transparency. Applicants can review the fees under the “Fees & Charges” section before proceeding with the application. The final charges depend on the loan terms and eligibility. This helps applicants clearly understand the cost structure while applying for an instant loan online through a fully digital process.

Instant Personal Loan Eligibility

Instant Personal Loan eligibility is based on basic age and income criteria set by the bank. Salaried Indian citizens aged between 20 and 65 years can apply for this loan. For self-employed individuals, the eligible age range is between 23 and 65 years. Applicants must also meet other bank-defined requirements related to income and credit profile. It is recommended to review the detailed eligibility criteria before applying. Eligibility details can be checked online, and qualified applicants can proceed through a fully digital application process.

Instant Personal Loan FAQs

What is an Instant Personal Loan?

An Instant Personal Loan from ICICI Bank is a quick, unsecured Loan that offers up to ₹50 lakh as the loan amount. Eligible existing customers can apply online and receive funds instantly.

Can I foreclose my Loan with ICICI Bank?

Personal Loan foreclosure facility is extended to all customers availing a Personal Loan with ICICI Bank. Customers can foreclose their Personal Loan after the payment of at least one EMI. However, foreclosure charges (and taxes) will be applicable as per the 
service charges
 but no foreclosure charges will be applicable after the payment of 24 EMIs.

What is the maximum loan amount I can get?

Eligible customers can avail of an Instant Personal Loan of up to ₹50 lakh, depending on their credit history and repayment capacity.

What is the repayment tenure for an Instant Personal Loan?

You can choose a flexible repayment tenure ranging from 12 to 72 months, allowing you to manage your EMIs conveniently.

What are the advantages of an Instant Personal Loan over a regular loan?

An Instant Personal Loan offers a 100% digital process, instant approval and disbursement. It is unsecured, requires no collateral and allows eligible customers to apply online easily.

How long does it take to get an instant loan?

An Instant Personal Loan is processed quickly with instant approval and disbursement. Eligible customers can apply online and receive funds in a few clicks after completing the digital process.

Can I get a top-up on my existing Instant Personal Loan?

A personal loan top-up option is available under related loan services. Eligibility depends on existing loan conditions and bank assessment during the application process.

Can I change the EMI date for my Instant Personal Loan?

EMI date changes are subject to bank approval. Customers can submit a request through service channels and modifications are processed in accordance with internal policy.

What is the minimum and maximum Instant Personal Loan I can take?

The Instant Personal Loan offers loans up to ₹50 lakh for eligible applicants. The final loan amount depends on eligibility, credit profile and bank assessment.

Do I need to check my credit score before obtaining an Instant Personal Loan?

Checking your credit score before applying is recommended. It helps assess eligibility for an instant loan application and improves understanding of the chances of approval.

How does a CIBIL score affect Instant Personal Loan approval?

Credit score or commonly known as the CIBIL score plays an important role in loan evaluation. A strong credit score supports faster approval and improves eligibility for an Instant Personal Loan online application.
