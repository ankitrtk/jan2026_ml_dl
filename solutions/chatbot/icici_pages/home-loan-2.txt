FAQs for NRI Home Loan

What is the NRI Home Loan process in India?

The NRI Home Loan process in India with ICICI Bank starts online by  entering basic personal and income details, reviewing loan eligibility, paying the processing fee and uploading KYC and income documents. After verification, ICICI Bank provides a provisional sanction. It is  followed by final approval based on further assessment by the ICICI Bank.

How long does the NRI Home Loan process take?

The timeline for the NRI Home Loan process depends on the submission and verification of documents. ICICI Bank offers a well-defined journey in which customers receive provisional sanction after completing the application steps.

 Final approval timelines may vary based on document validation, eligibility checks and completion of all required formalities.

What is the maximum amount I can borrow for an NRI Home Loan?

ICICI Bank provides NRI Home Loan depending on your profile, eligibility and property value. Loans support the purchase of new and resale properties  and construction of property with attractive rates and tenures up to 30 years for Home Loans, 15 years for home improvement loans and 20 years for land loans.

How to repay NRI home loan?

EMI for NRI home loan is repaid using NRE/NRO account only. 

Is a PAN card or an Aadhaar mandatory for NRI/OCI?

PAN is mandatory for NRI customers. OCI customers can submit Form 97 in absence of PAN card.

Aadhar card is not mandatory for NRI and OCI customers. However, Aadhar card may be used as proof of address for Indian address.

Can NRIs and OCIs apply for an ICICI Bank Home Loan?

Yes, NRIs and OCIs can apply for Home Loans to buy or construct a home in India.

What is the maximum tenure for an NRI Home Loan?

Maximum tenure is upto 30 years for Home Loan.

What documents are required for salaried NRIs?

You will need a valid passport, NRI status proof, proof of address, income proof (salary slips, bank statements), and an overseas credit report if applicable.

Can an NRI or Resident Indian be a co-applicant for an NRI Home Loan?

Yes, both NRIs and Resident Indians can be co-applicants.

How do I calculate my eligibility for a Home Loan as an NRI/OCI?

You can use the eligibility calculator for precise information.

Do I need an NRE/NRO account?

NRE/
NRO account
 is required for repayment.

Customers from which countries are required to submit overseas credit report?

An Overseas Credit Report ( not older than 45 days )must be submitted for NRI/OCI customers belonging to USA, UK, Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, Ireland, South Africa, Oman. For other countries, it is not mandatory.
