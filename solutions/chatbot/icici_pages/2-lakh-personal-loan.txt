About ₹2 Lakh Personal Loan

How can you calculate the EMI for a ₹2 lakh personal loan?

The EMI for a ₹2 lakh Personal Loan can be calculated using the 
Personal Loan EMI calculator
 available on ICICI Bank’s website.

Tips for Managing Your ₹2 Lakh Personal Loan

Plan your EMI according to your monthly income.

Avoid delayed payments to maintain credit discipline.

Track outstanding balance regularly.

Limit additional borrowing during tenure to manage repayment effectively.

Eligibility Criteria for a ₹2 lakh Personal Loan

Eligibility for a ₹2 lakh Personal Loan is assessed as per defined criteria.

For salaried individuals:

Age between 20 and 58 years

Minimum monthly net salary of ₹30,000, subject to profile evaluation

Minimum 2 years of total work experience

For self-employed individuals:

Minimum age 23 years and maximum age at loan maturity 65 years

Business vintage of at least 2 years in the current business and total experience of 3 years

Loan amount up to ₹50,00,000, subject to eligibility

The minimum salary requirement may vary depending on employer type, required loan amount, tenure and relationship with ICICI Bank. The above points indicate the basic eligibility criteria. The actual eligibility is determined by the bank once you apply for the loan and approval is subject to internal assessment.

FAQs on Personal Loans of ₹2 Lakh

Who can apply for the ₹2 Lakh Personal Loan?

Any individual salaried or self-employed, who meets 
ICICI Bank’s basic eligibility criteria
 and needs a short-term loan can apply. The loan is designed for borrowers requiring a small amount for short-term needs.

What documents are required for a ₹2 lakh loan?

Standard identity proof (e.g. Aadhaar, PAN), address proof, bank statements, and income verification (as applicable) are required. Documentation is minimal to keep the process quick and simple.

What is the repayment tenure & interest rate for a ₹2 lakh personal loan?

Tenure and interest rate will depend on your profile and the bank’s lending norms at the time of application. EMI amounts will be proportionate to the small loan size, offering an affordable repayment schedule.

How quickly is the ₹2 lakh personal loan disbursed after approval?

On approval, the loan amount is disbursed promptly, often within 24–48 hours to your registered bank account.
