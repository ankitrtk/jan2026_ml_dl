Planning a foreign business trip?

Use a Corporate Forex Prepaid Card to eliminate the hassles of currency conversion and the risks of carrying cash in a foreign land. The Corporate Forex Card offers a convenient and secure way to manage your travel expenses. It can be loaded with several popular currencies such as USD, EUR, CAD, GBP, AED, SGD and more. Once loaded, the Card can be used just like a Debit Card to pay for all your travel expenses in the local currency of the place you are visiting.

 FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Corporate Forex Prepaid Card?

The ICICI Bank Corporate Forex Prepaid Card is a digital-first Prepaid Card specifically designed for businesses. It allows business owners or employees to manage international business expenses in multiple foreign currencies efficiently, with no joining or annual fees.

How do I load foreign currencies onto my Corporate Forex Prepaid Card?

You can conveniently load foreign currencies onto your Card through the InstaBIZ app. The app offers a seamless, secure experience for instant loading and management of funds.

What should I do If I lose my Corporate Forex Prepaid Card?

In case of loss, you can instantly block your Card through InstaBIZ. Alternatively, you can contact Customer Care at 1800 1080 to report the loss. Your Card is protected with coverage for losses up to ₹5,00,000.

How to avail the International SIM ?

Eligible cardholders can use their Card number to check the validity of this benefit

After logging in, eligible cardholders can copy the unique code from the offer tile and click on the link to visit the e-SIM or SIM card vendor's platform

On the vendor's platform, eligible cardholders have to select their preferred call plan details (as mentioned in the above table)

Eligible cardholders will receive an e-mail with a link to complete the KYC process by uploading the necessary details (as mandated by relevant telecommunications authorities)

After KYC is completed, an option to select an e-SIM or a physical SIM card will be available in the drop-down menu on the SIM card vendor's platform

For an e-SIM, a QR code will be sent via e-mail to activate the e-SIM. A list of compatible phones will be available on the platform

For a physical SIM card, Cardholders must enter their delivery address and pay a delivery charge of ₹100 for the vendor to ship the SIM card to the cardholder's address.

How long does it take to reload my Corporate Forex Prepaid Card?

You can reload your Corporate Forex Prepaid card through InstaBIZ App. While online reload requests can be processed quickly, the exact time may vary depending on transaction verification and processing. ICICI Bank also specifies daily limits for online reloads to ensure secure transactions.

Are there any charges for cross-currency transactions?

Yes, a cross-currency fee is applicable when a transaction is made in a currency not loaded on the card. For ICICI Bank Forex cards, this is around 3.5% plus applicable taxes, as per the schedule of charges.

Where can I use the Corporate Forex Prepaid Card abroad?

The Corporate Forex Prepaid card can be used globally at merchant outlets, online platforms and ATMs that accept card networks. It supports transactions in multiple currencies and can be used like a debit card to pay in the local currency of the country you are visiting.
