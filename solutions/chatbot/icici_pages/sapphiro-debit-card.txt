Sapphiro Business Debit Card FAQ’s

What is ‘tap to pay’ available among the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card benefits?

The ‘tap to pay’ feature makes payments seamless and quick by allowing you to make payments with a single tap on the POS machine.

Do I get a 25% discount on online or offline movie tickets with the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card?

Both. You can avail 25% discounts on tickets purchased online like BookMyTrip and even while purchasing tickets offline at INOX.

How many complimentary airport lounge visits do I get each quarter with the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card?

You get a maximum of four complimentary airport lounge visits each quarter using the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card.

What are the card security measures offered with the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card?

With the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card, you can avail security measures like 24-hour loss reporting, worldwide cover, and a free loss reporting telephone line.

What is the surcharge on fuel purchases with the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card?

Zero surcharge is available on fuel purchases nationwide except at select outlets.

What are the transaction limits for the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card?

The domestic transaction limit of the ICICI Bank Sapphiro debit card is ₹1,50,000, and the international transaction limit is ₹2,00,000.
