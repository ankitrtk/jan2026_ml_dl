About Tap and Pay Debit Cards

Eligibility Requirements

To use the Tap and Pay feature, you must be an ICICI Bank Debit Cardholder with an active ICICI Bank's iMobile app account. Ensure your card carries the contactless symbol for easy payments.

Transaction Limit

With Tap and Pay, you can make payments up to ₹5,000 without needing to enter a PIN. It's a fast, secure, and convenient way to pay for small purchases.

Activation Process

Activate Tap and Pay by logging into your ICICI Bank's iMobile app or through Net Banking. In the app, go to 'Debit Card' under 'Transaction Settings' and enable the Tap and Pay option.

Lost or Stolen Card

If your card is lost or stolen, immediately contact ICICI Bank to suspend your card and prevent unauthorized transactions. It’s essential to report any issues as soon as possible for your security.

Tap and Pay FAQs

What is Tap and Pay?

Tap and Pay is a feature on your ICICI Bank Debit Card that allows you to make contactless payments up to ₹5,000 without entering a PIN. Simply tap your card on the machine to pay.

How do I activate Tap and Pay?

To activate Tap and Pay, log into your ICICI Bank's iMobile app, go to 'Debit Card' under 'Transaction Settings,' and enable the Tap and Pay option. You can also activate it through Net Banking.

What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?

Immediately report a lost or stolen card to ICICI Bank’s customer care to suspend the card and prevent unauthorised transactions. Contact details can be found on the bank’s website or through your ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

Is Tap and Pay secure?

Yes, Tap and Pay transactions are secured. The system ensures that accidental payments do not result in double charges, and your payment is protected by encryption and card verification.

Is there any fee for using Tap and Pay?

No, there are no additional fees for using the Tap and Pay feature. It’s a convenient and fee-free way to make quick payments with your ICICI Bank Debit Card.
