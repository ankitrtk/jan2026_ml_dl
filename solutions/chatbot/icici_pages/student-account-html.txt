About NRI Student Account

Eligibility Criteria

Open to NRI students with a valid passport and student visa. Maintain a minimum monthly average monthly balance of INR 15,000.

Easy Account Opening

Apply at any ICICI Bank branch in India or overseas. Submit an attested passport, visa, university ID, and address proof. Funding must come from your KYC-compliant source.

International Money Transfers

Send funds home or pay fees using Money2India, Wire Transfer, or Money2World. Supports multiple currencies for fast and secure remittances.

Global Banking Access

Get 24/7 access to the Internet and Phone Banking. Use the international ATM-cum-Debit Card to withdraw local currency or shop securely while studying abroad.

FAQs

What is a Student Card, and how is it useful abroad?

The ICICI Bank Student Card is an international ATM-cum-Debit Card issued with the NRI Student Account. It allows you to withdraw local currency overseas, shop globally, and manage expenses securely, offering ease and convenience while studying abroad.

Who is eligible to open an ICICI Bank NRI Student Account?

NRI students aged 18 years and above who are pursuing education overseas can apply. A valid passport, student visa or I-20 form, and university admission proof are required at the time of account opening. 
Click here
 for document list.

What documents are needed to apply for the account?

You’ll need self-attested copies of your passport (first and last four pages), a valid student visa or I-20 form, address proof, and a university admission letter or student ID. If applying from outside India, these must also be attested by a banker, notary, or embassy official.

Can I transfer money internationally from this account?

Yes. ICICI Bank supports global transfers via services like Money2India, Money2World, and Wire Transfers. These options allow quick, secure fund transfers in major currencies like USD, GBP, AED, AUD, and more.
