About Money2India UK

Who Can Use M2I UK?

Residents of the United Kingdom aged 18 or above can use M2I UK to send money to any bank account in India. It's ideal for individuals supporting family, managing property expenses, or sending gifts.

Simple Registration Process

Start by signing up on the M2I UK website or mobile app. Your details will get auto verified*. Once verified, you're ready to send money in just a few steps.

(* If we are unable to verify your identity then we will require KYC documents to complete the registration)

Flexible Payment Options

You can fund your transfers using a UK Debit Card or via local bank transfer, offering convenience and flexibility for every transaction.

Access Anytime, Anywhere

Enjoy seamless transfers through both the M2I UK website and mobile app, available 24x7. Track transactions, manage beneficiaries, and access customer support on the go.

Transparent & Locked Exchange Rates

Send money with confidence using confirmed exchange rates, visible before you initiate the transaction with zero fees on all transfers.

Robust Security Features

Your peace of mind matters. M2I UK uses 2-factor authentication, end-to-end 256-bit encryption, and advanced firewall protection to safeguard every transaction.

FAQs

How do I transfer money using Money2India UK?

To transfer money via Money2India UK, simply 
Sign Up
 on our website or Mobile App. Once registered, add a recipient in India, confirm the best exchange rate, and transfer funds using your UK bank account or debit card. Once funds are received at our end, the money is transferred to the recipient’s bank account in India.

Where can I transfer money using Money2India UK?

Money2India UK exclusively facilitates the transfer of money from any bank in the UK to any bank in India, ensuring convenient and secure transactions. This platform allows you to send money to various banks across India, but it does not support transfers to bank accounts in any other country. Additionally, it is important to note that you can only send funds to individual recipients, not to other entities, including but not limited to business current account, charity accounts, etc.

Am I required to hold a personal account with ICICI Bank UK to use Money2India service?

No, you do not need to hold a personal account with ICICI Bank UK to use the Money2India UK platform. You can initiate transfers from a personal account held in any bank in the UK to any bank in India through the Money2India platform. 

However, if you already have a UK Personal Bank account with us, you won’t be able to register on the Money2India UK platform, as there's no need for you to sign up separately on Money2India UK. You can use our "Money transfer to India" service through 
internet banking
 or our 
mobile application
 of your personal account held with ICICI Bank UK. This service provides the same functionality as Money2India UK for sending money to India. This is done to enhance your convenience and avoid the need for additional sign-ups on Money2India UK separately.

I already hold an active bank account with ICICI Bank UK. Am I still required to register on Money2India UK?

If you have a UK Personal Bank account with us, you don’t need to register separately on Money2India UK. You can conveniently access our "Money transfer to India" service through our 
internet banking
 or our 
mobile application
. This service offers the same functionality as Money2India UK for sending money to India.

I hold an active NRI account with ICICI Bank India. How can I register and use Money2India UK services?

Visit our 
Money2India UK Sign Up page
, register yourself and start transferring money to India.
