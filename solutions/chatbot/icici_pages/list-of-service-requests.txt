Requests for NRI Customers

ICICI Bank service requests FAQs

How to change address at ICICI Bank in Current Account?

To update or change the address in your ICICI Bank 
Current Account
, please visit the branch and submit your request.

Steps: 

Visit your home branch or nearest ICICI Bank branch.

Submit a request letter signed as per the Mode of Operation (MOP) for the account.

Provide a valid proof of address in the account holder’s name as per KYC guidelines.

The branch representative will verify your details and initiate the address update request.

Documents required:

Request letter signed as per MOP

Valid proof of address in the account holder’s name (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID)

Processing Time: 2 working days

How to change address at ICICI Bank in Demat Account?

To update or change address in your ICICI Bank Demat Account, please submit physical request at your nearest demat-enabled branch.

How can I request or update address for NRI Account in ICICI Bank?

Address change for your ICICI Bank NRI (Non-Resident Indian) account can be placed conveniently through 
Net Banking
 or the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app.

This allows you to update your communication address linked to your NRE/NRO account without visiting a branch.

1. Net Banking

Login to 
Net Banking
 using your credentials.

On the top right side of the page, click Customer Service → Service Request.

Under Account Modification Related, select Request for Change in Communication Address.

Enter your updated address details and upload valid proof of address.

Review the details and submit your request.

Important:

A valid 
Debit Card
 linked to the account is mandatory to place any request through Net Banking.

Ensure that the proof of address uploaded is in the name of the account holder or a close relative (as per RBI guidelines).

Documents Required: 
Click here
 for the list of acceptable documents for NRI proof of address change. 

2. ICICI Bank's ICICI Bank’s iMobile app

Unlock your ICICI Bank's ICICI Bank’s iMobile app using your fingerprint or 4-digit PIN.

On the dashboard, go to Services → Service Request.

Select Communication Address Change Request.

Choose the Account Number for which the address is to be updated.

Upload your valid proof of address in the name of yourself or a close relative.

Enter Grid Card details for security verification.

Click on Activate and raise the request for processing.

Documents Required: 
Click here
 for the list of acceptable proof of address documents.

Processing Time: Your address will be updated within 3 working days from successful verification.

How to change the Personal Loan address digitally in ICICI Bank?

To update or change address in your ICICI Bank Personal Loan Account, you just need to visit the branch and place the request with the help of a branch employee, who will raise the request. Currently, there is no digital process to change the Personal Loan address.

Documents Required: While visiting the branch, kindly carry 
Stay Connected Form
 with valid proof of address (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence and Passport)

Processing Time : 3 Working days

What documents are required for Funds Transfer Abroad in ICICI Bank?

For transferring funds abroad from your ICICI Bank Account, specific documents are required depending on the purpose and nature of the transaction.

Mandatory Documents:

Form A2 – 
Retail Outward Remittance Application

(Applicable for requests submitted at ICICI Bank branches)

Supporting documents for:

Purpose of remittance (e.g., education, travel, investment, gift)

Source of funds (salary slips, bank statement, etc.) as applicable

Form 15CA or 15CB (applicable for Non-LRS transactions)

How to Submit:

Visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch and submit the above documents.

Ensure all details match your KYC-registered information.

The request will be processed after successful verification.

For more information, 
click here
.

How can I request an ICICI Bank statement through the service request form?

You can easily request an ICICI Bank statement through the raise requests section on the Net Banking platform. Simply log in to your ICICI Bank Account, select the 'Service Request' option, and choose the 'Statement Request' form. Once completed, you can download your statement in PDF format directly from the platform or choose to have it emailed to you.

Where can I download the ICICI Bank cheque book request form PDF?

To request a cheque book, you can download the ICICI Bank cheque book request form PDF directly from the raise requests section on the ICICI Bank website. Alternatively, you can raise a service request for a cheque book through Net Banking. After submitting the request, the cheque book will be delivered to your registered address.

Who can open an NPS account? 

Indian citizens aged 0-85 years can enroll

What are the types of NPS accounts?

Tier I (retirement account) and Tier II (voluntary savings account). 

Are NPS contributions eligible for tax benefits?

Yes, contributions qualify for tax benefits under Section 80C and 80CCD of the Income Tax Act.

What is CCIL limit?

CCIL limit is Forex trading exposure limit.

Who can apply for CCIL?

Eligible corporate or authorized users can apply for CCIL.

Is approval required to apply CCIL?

Yes, subject to verification.

What is CCIL deal limit?

It is the approved transaction limit for Forex deals.

Can CCIL deal limit be increased?

Yes, subject to eligibility and documentation.

Can CCLI deal cancellation be reapplied after cancellation?

Yes, it can be applied again anytime.

Is there a fee for CCIL deal limit cancellation?

Charges depend on bank policy.

Is linking of ICICI Bank Savings Account to Forex Card mandatory?

Yes, it is required to link for loading funds.

Can multiple accounts be linked to Forex Card?

Usually one primary account is allowed.

How long does linking of ICICI Savings Account to Forex Card take?

It is processed instantly or within 1 working day.

How do I activate my Forex Card?

It can be activated via Net Banking and ICICI Bank’s iMobile app. 

Is OTP required to activate Forex Card?

Yes, for security authentication. 

Can I activate Forex Card internationally?

Yes, if international usage is enabled.

Who can use the secondary Forex card?

The authorized cardholder.

Is there a separate PIN required for secondary Forex Card? 

Yes.

Can I block only the secondary card?

Yes, independently.

How long does outward remittance transfer take?

Usually 1–3 working days.

What details are required to link ICICI Prudential policy with ICICI Bank Savings Account?

Policy number and date of birth. 

Can ICICI Prudential policy premium be paid online?

Yes after linking ICICI Prudential policy with ICICI Bank Savings Account.

Is OTP verification required to link ICICI Prudential policy with ICICI Bank Savings Account?

Yes.
