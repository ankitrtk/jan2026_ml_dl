How to renew a Fixed Deposit?

You can easily renew your Fixed Deposit (FD) with ICICI Bank using any of the below methods:

Auto-renewal
: Opening an FD in the auto-renewal mode will automatically renew it for the same period at the interest rate applicable on the day of maturity.

Manual renewal
: If auto-renewal was not selected at the time of opening the FD, you can still manually 
renew your FD using Net Banking
 or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. Select 'Deposits', select the relevant FD and then click on the option to renew.

Fixed Deposit - FAQs

What is a Fixed Deposit?

An FD is an investment offered by banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) that provides assured returns based on the interest rate at the time of account opening. Interest rates vary depending on the deposit amount and tenure.

What types of Fixed Deposits does ICICI Bank offer?

Traditional Fixed Deposit: Interest is paid regularly (monthly or quarterly) as chosen during the account setup.

Cumulative Fixed Deposit: Interest is compounded quarterly and paid as a lump sum on maturity.

What is the ‘reverse sweep’ feature?

The ‘reverse sweep’ feature from ICICI Bank automatically transfers funds from a linked Fixed Deposit (FD) to your Savings Account when the balance in your Savings Account is low. This ensures that the transactions in your Savings Account continue, without having to break your FD manually. It’s a convenient way to access funds during emergencies while keeping your FD intact as much as possible.

Can I opt for auto-renewal for my FD? 

Yes, auto-renewal can be selected at the time of opening the FD or before maturity. The FD will renew for the same tenure as the original and at the interest rate applicable at the time of renewal.

Can I open an FD without visiting a branch? 

Yes, existing ICICI Bank customers can open an FD through Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. They can also open an FD (of amount below ₹50,000) at any ICICI Bank ATM. New customers need to visit a branch.

What is the tenure of Fixed Deposits? 

Fixed Deposits can be opened for flexible tenures, ranging from 7 days to 10 years.

What is the monthly income FD?

The monthly income FD allows you to get your FD interest in the form of monthly payouts. The principal (FD amount) is paid to you once the FD matures. It is an easy way to get consistent income every month.
