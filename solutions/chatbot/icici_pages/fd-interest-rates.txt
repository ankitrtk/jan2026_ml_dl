FD interest rates FAQs

Can I earn monthly interest on my Fixed Deposit?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers a monthly income option on Fixed Deposits, allowing you to receive monthly interest payouts. To calculate the monthly FD interest amount, you can use our 
FD Interest calculator
. For detailed information, contact your relationship manager.

Is the interest on a Fixed Deposit taxable?

Yes, the interest earned on your ICICI Bank 
Fixed Deposit
 is taxable based on your income bracket and is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as per Income Tax laws. You can also opt for a 
Tax Saver Fixed Deposit
 to save taxes up to ₹46,800 under the old tax regime.

How much interest can I earn on my Fixed Deposit?

ICICI Bank provides competitive Fixed Deposit interest rates ranging from a minimum of 2.75% to a maximum of 6.50% per annum. These secure and lucrative options make ICICI Bank a reliable choice for FD investments.

What are form 15G and form 15H?

Forms 15G and 15H can be submitted to avoid TDS deductions on interest income, provided you meet the eligibility criteria. Customers must have a PAN to claim tax exemptions and can submit these forms online via Net Banking or the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app.

When is TDS deducted on Fixed Deposit interest?

TDS is deducted when the interest income exceeds ₹50,000 for General Residents and ₹1,00,000 for Resident Senior Citizens. However, filing forms 15G or 15H can prevent TDS deductions if eligible, but the interest income remains taxable.

Will filing form 15G or 15H exempt me from tax on my interest income?

Submitting the forms only avoids TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) if your total income is below the taxable limit. If you do not submit the requisite form, the bank will deduct TDS which you will then have to claim as a refund later. If your taxable income is above the taxable limit, do not submit form 15G or 15H.
