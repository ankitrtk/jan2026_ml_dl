Multiple Bill Registration

Customers can register multiple bills of the same biller at the same time in 3 easy steps. Bills are auto-registered and bill details are fetched. Post logging in to CIB, navigate through Transfers & Bill Pay > Bill Payment > Multiple bill registration Search the biller, download the file in default excel format (Do not copy paste or change format) Fill bill details in the excel file, review & upload.

Bulk Bill Payment

Bulk bill payment feature facilitates fetch and payment of multiple bills of the same biller at the same time. Post logging in to CIB, navigate through Transfers & Bill Pay > Bill Payment > Bulk Bill Payment Search the biller, select an account, download the file format from the hyperlink available, upload the .txt file after filling the details. Click on ‘Proceed’. Next, select approver(s). Post the approval, payment gets completed.

Single Bill Payment

Post logging in to CIB, navigate through Transfer and Bill Payments > Bill Payments > Single bill payments > Due bill section Select the due bills from the list & proceed Review your bills’ details. Next, select approver(s). Post the approval, payment gets completed. Additionally, Auto-pay can be enabled for multiple bills of the same biller, thus making bill payments extremely convenient.

Bill Payment on ICICI Bank's Corporate Internet Banking (CIB) FAQs

Where can I view/download receipt post a Bill Payment? 

To download the receipt post payment, navigate through the following path:

Transfers & Bill Pay > Bill Payments > Single Bill Payments > Payment history

What happens when my bill payment is not successful, but amount is debited? When will the refund be processed? 

For failed transactions, amount debited will be refunded in 4-7 working days.

Can I set-up an Auto Pay for my bills? 

Yes, you can set-up Auto-Pay for your bills. You can avail Early Pay discount by setting up Auto-pay for eligible bills on early pay date. Eligible billers – Adani Electricity (Mumbai), TATA Power (Mumbai), Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL) & TP Ajmer Distribution Ltd (TPADL).

Can LIC premium payment be done through CIB?

Yes , LIC premium can be paid through CIB . Refer the below path

Login to CIB> Transfer & Bill pay> Bill payments > Single bill payment > LIC/Other Insurance > Select LIC

Enter your policy number & Date of birth to fetch your premium details for payment.

How do I set-up an Auto-Pay? 

Following are the two scenarios in which Auto Pay can be set up:

During a Transaction 

Post bill fetch, Auto-Pay can be enabled for the bill. You can also set a transaction limit for auto-debit.

Note: If Bill outstanding is greater than the Auto Pay Limit, then the bill amount will not be auto-debited and you will require to pay manually.

From ‘Manage Biller’ section 

1) Navigate to ‘Manage Biller’ section on Single bill payment landing page. 

2) Select the bill for which Auto Pay is required to be enabled. 

3) You can also set a transaction limit for auto-debit.

How to invest in mutual funds using CIB?

To Invest in mutual funds, please follow the below path 

Login to CIB > Transfer & Bill pay > Bill payment & Presentment > Click on “Add Biller”> click on “Look up” to get the list of mutual fund billers available

Select your biller & enter your mutual fund details to register. Post completion of registration you can invest in the mutual fund.
