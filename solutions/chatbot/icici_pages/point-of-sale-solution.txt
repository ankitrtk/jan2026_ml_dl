Point-of-Sale Terminal FAQs

What is a POS terminal and how does it work?

A Point-of-Sale terminal or POS machine allows merchants to accept digital payments through cards, UPI, and more. Customers can simply scan the UPI QR code on the screen, or Swipe or Tap their card, and the transaction is instantly processed.

Who can use a POS machine?

Any merchant whether small shops, retail outlets, restaurants, hospitals, or large businesses can use POS machines to accept cashless payments.

What is the difference between a regular POS and a Dual-Screen POS?

Both have the same core features, but the Dual-Screen POS has an additional customer-facing screen that enhances transparency, enables branding, and improves customer trust.

Do I need a current account with ICICI Bank to get a POS?

Yes, a current account with ICICI Bank is required for settlements of POS transactions.

What types of payments can be accepted on POS?

Customers have an option to scan and pay through UPI, or swipe or tap using all major cards.

Does POS support features like EMI, Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC), and pre- authorization?

Yes, merchants can offer EMI facilities, Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) for international cards, and pre-authorization for industries like hotels and travel.

Can POS integrate with my billing or ERP system?

Yes, POS devices can be integrated with ERP/billing systems to streamline operations and reduce manual entry errors.

Is there a cost involved, and how can I get a POS machine? 

Yes, POS machines come with a rental plan or a one-time payment subscription plan.

To get your POS machine, you can either contact your Relationship Manager, visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch, or call our customer care at 1800 1080.

How quickly will I receive the money after a customer pays?

Consolidated settlement happens on a T+1 day basis.
