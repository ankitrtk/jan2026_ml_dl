About BizPay Card

How Does the BizPay Card Work?

The BizPay Card ensures broader acceptance with access to numerous merchant outlets and e-commerce platforms. You can also conveniently withdraw cash using the Cash at POS service when making purchases.

Loading funds is simple: add money through Immediate Payment Service (IMPS), National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT), Unified Payments Interface (UPI), or ICICI Bank's Corporate Portal, giving you complete control over your card balance.

Rest assured, the BizPay Card prioritises security, ensuring safe transactions with advanced features like OTP and PIN validation.

This pre-funded card is perfect for various business expenses, such as fuel, vendor payments, and office expenses, simplifying your business transactions.

To avail services of ICICI Bank BizPay Card

Contact your Relationship Manager

FAQs

What is the maximum annual spending limit on the BizPay Card?

The BizPay Card offers an annual spending limit of up to ₹50 lakh.

How can I load funds onto my BizPay Card?

You can load funds through IMPS, NEFT, UPI, or directly via ICICI Bank's Corporate Portal.

Is the BizPay Card secure for transactions?

Yes, the BizPay Card is equipped with advanced security features, including OTP validation for online transactions and PIN-based validation for in-store purchases.

Can I withdraw cash using the BizPay Card?

Yes, you can withdraw cash at any of the 14,000 ICICI Bank ATMs and other bank ATMs.

Who do I contact for assistance regarding the BizPay Card?

For assistance, you can contact our customer care at 18001080.
