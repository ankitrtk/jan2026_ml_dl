FASTag FAQs

ICICI FASTag Fees and Charges

FASTag is a chip that is similar to the RFID tags which car companies place in a sticker on the windshield. It has all your details so when you pass through a toll, you can pay the FASTag charges by scanning. Paying FASTag charges at any toll plaza directly is contactless. You can open a prepaid 
FASTag account
 put in money so that the account is usable. You can keep reloading money in this account as long as you need to if you are going on a trip or trips that require you to pass through tolls. There will be FASTag lanes demarcated for every toll across the country.

What is the cost of an Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag?

The price for an Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag typically includes a one-time fee for the tag, along with a refundable security deposit. The exact price may vary based on your vehicle type and location.

What are the standard FASTag fees and charges for new customers?

Standard FASTag charges include tag issuance fee, refundable security deposit and minimum wallet recharge amount. Charges vary depending on vehicle category and applicable policies at the time of purchase.

What is the refundable security deposit, and when do I get it back?

The refundable security deposit is collected during FASTag issuance. It may be refunded after account closure and tag deactivation, subject to verification and applicable terms.

Are there any hidden charges in FASTag usage?

FASTag usage generally involves toll deductions and applicable issuance or deposit charges. Customers should review official FASTag fees and charges to understand applicable costs.

What are the charges for a new Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag?

Charges for a new Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag may include issuance and security deposit. Customers should refer to the applicable charges section.

What is the Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag?

Amazon Pay ICICI Bank FASTag enables electronic toll payments and allows eligible users to manage toll transactions digitally.
