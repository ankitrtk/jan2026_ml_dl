Which type of taxes fit your profile?

Income Tax

Tax levied on annual personal income.

ITR (Income Tax Return)

Annual filing to declare income, calculate tax liability, and claim refunds.

Advance Tax

Tax payable in instalments during the financial year.

State Taxes

Taxes collected by individual state governments on income, goods or services.

GST (Goods & Services Tax)

Comprehensive tax on supply of goods and services.

Customs Duty

Tax on cross-border movement of goods.

Self-Assessment Tax

Balance tax computed after income calculation

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)

Tax deducted from income at the time of payment

How can you pay your taxes?

Direct Tax

Payment mode: Internet Banking

Step 1 – Go to the online e-Filing portal of the Income Tax Department

Step 2 – Click on ‘e-Pay Tax’ and log in with your PAN

Step 3 - Select ‘Tax Type’ as per your requirement

Step 4 - Choose the ‘Assessment Year’ and fill in other details

Step 5 - Enter the amount details under the ‘New Payment’ section

Step 6 – Select Net Banking as the payment mode and follow the path: Net Banking > ICICI Bank > Pay Now

Step 7 – Click on “Retail User”> Log in to “Internet banking” to complete the payment

Step 8- Post payment completion, customer can login to online e-Filing portal to download challan.

Payment mode: Debit Card

Step 1 – Go to the online e-Filing portal of the Income Tax Department

Step 2 – Click on ‘e-Pay Tax’ and log in with your PAN

Step 3 - Select ‘Tax Type’ as per your requirement

Step 4 - Choose the ‘Assessment Year’ and fill in other details

Step 5 - Enter the amount details under the ‘New Payment’ section

Step 6 – Select Debit Card as the payment mode and follow the path > ICICI Bank > Pay Now

Step 7 – Complete your payment using ICICI Bank Debit Card

Step 8- Post payment completion, customer can login to online e-Filing portal to download challan.

Payment mode: Pay at the Bank Counter

Step 1 – Go to the online e-Filing portal of the Income Tax Department

Step 2 – Click on ‘e-Pay Tax’ and log in with your PAN

Step 3 - Select ‘Tax Type’ as per your requirement

Step 4 – Choose the ‘Assessment Year’ and fill in other details

Step 5 - Enter the amount details under the ‘New Payment’ section

Step 6 - Select the payment mode as ‘Pay at Bank Counter’

Step 7 - Select Cheque/ Cash or DD and download the challan

Step 8 - Visit any ICICI Bank Branch to complete the payment

Step 9- Post payment completion, customer can login to online e-Filing portal to download challan.

Income Tax e-Filing

Payment mode: Internet Banking

Step 1 – Login to the Income Tax e-filing portal and go to File Income Tax Return.

Step 2 - Select the appropriate Assessment Year and ITR Form (e.g., ITR 1 / ITR 4).

Step 3 - Review and verify pre-filled details such as income, deductions, and tax paid.

Step 4- Validate and submit the return using OTP or e-Verification and download the acknowledgment.

Indirect Tax

Payment mode: Internet Banking

Step 1 – Log in to the ICEGATE payment page on the ICEGATE 2.0 (CEP) portal of the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)

Step 2 - Select ‘Unpaid Challan’

Step 3 - Select the payment mode as Internet Banking and complete the payment

Step 4- Post payment completion, customer can login to ICEGATE 2.0 (CEP) portal to download challan.

GST (Goods and Services Tax)

Payment mode: Internet Banking

Step 1: Go to the GST portal > Services > Payments > Create Challan

Step 2: Enter your GST Identification Number (GSTIN)

Step 3: Fill in the required details

Step 4: Select the payment mode as ‘e-Payment’

Step 5: Click on ‘Generate Challan’

Step 6: Re-enter GSTIN for verification

Step 7: Select Net Banking > ICICI Bank > Make payment

Step 8: Choose to pay as a “Retail User”

Step 9: Complete the payment as directed

Step 10- Post payment completion, customer can login to GST portal to download challan.

Payment mode: Pay at the Bank Counter

Step 1: Go to the GST portal > Services > Payments > Create Challan

Step 2: Enter your GSTIN

Step 3: Fill in the required details

Step 4: Enter the deposit details

Step 5: Select the payment mode as Over the Counter > ICICI Bank

Step 6: Choose from Cheque/ Cash/ DD

Step 7: Click on ‘Generate Challan’

Step 8: Re-enter GSTIN for verification

Step 9: Review all the details and download the challan

Step 10: Visit any ICICI Bank Branch to complete the payment before the challan expiry date

Step 11: Post payment completion, customer can login to GST portal to download challan.

Who is required to register under GST?

Any business with an annual turnover above the prescribed threshold (Rs. 20–40 lakh, depending on the state and sector) must register under GST.

How can I track the status of my GST payment?

You can view the payment status and download receipts via the GST portal under the 'Services > Payments > Track Payment Status' section.

What is ICICI Bank’s Simplified GST Payment Solution?

It’s a smarter, faster way to pay GST—offering one-time registration, single login, and seamless multi-challan payments via Net Banking.

How is Simplified GST solution different from regular GST payments?

Unlike standard flows that require repetitive logins, this solution lets you register your GSTIN once and pay multiple challans in one go, saving time.
