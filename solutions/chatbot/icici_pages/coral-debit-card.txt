Coral Debit Card FAQs

How do I get a Debit Card from ICICI Bank?

You can get an ICICI Bank Debit Card by opening a Savings Account or Current Account with us.

Do my Debit Card payments impact my credit score?

Since your Debit Card payments are debited from the balance in your Account, they do not impact your credit score.

How are contactless payments a part of the benefits of the Coral Debit Card?

The Coral Debit Card can be used to make contactless payments from a distance of 4 cm from the merchant terminal (POS machine).

When do I pay the annual fees and joining fees for my Coral Debit Card?

You have to pay joining fees for the first year and annual fees starting from the second year onwards.

What is the payWave technology?

The payWave technology built into the Coral payWave Debit Card helps you make contactless payments from a distance of up to 4 cm from the POS terminal. There is no need to hand over your Card to the person at the payment counter.

Can I get discounts on movie tickets through the Coral Debit Card?

Yes, you can get discounts on movie tickets purchased on BookMyShow as well as at INOX multiplexes.

How many times do I get the airport lounge access per quarter across India?

With the Coral Debit Card, you get airport lounge access up to two times per quarter, subject to a spends of ₹ 10,000 in the previous quarter.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹10,000 or more on your Coral Debit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.
