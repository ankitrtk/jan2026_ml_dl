 Basic information 

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is an Education Loan?

An Education Loan helps students fund academic expenses such as tuition fees, books, travel, accommodation, and other study-related costs. ICICI Bank offers loans for domestic and international studies with competitive interest rates and flexible repayment options. Learn more: 
Education Loan

                            Who can apply for an Education Loan?

Any Indian citizen who has secured confirmed admission to a recognised institution in India or abroad is eligible to apply.

                            What expenses are covered?

The loan covers tuition fees, exam fees, books, laptop (if required), travel tickets, living expenses, and hostel charges.

                            Is the loan available for foreign studies?

Yes. ICICI Bank provides loans for undergraduate, postgraduate, professional, and PhD programmes abroad.

                            How to read interest rate clauses in a loan agreement?

When reviewing an ICICI Bank Education Loan agreement, check whether the interest rate is fixed or floating, the applicable rate range, and whether the loan allows switching between fixed and floating rates. If the interest rate is revised, ICICI Bank allows options such as change in EMI, change in tenure, change in both, or switching from floating to fixed rate before the reset date. Applicable conversion fees are specified in the transaction documents.

                            Does ICICI Bank provide Education Loans for studies in countries such as the US, UK, and Australia?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers Education Loans for students pursuing higher studies abroad at recognised institutions. The bank states that it supports international studies and covers eligible academic expenses for foreign education, subject to course, institute category, and applicant profile.

                            Can an ICICI Bank Education Loan help cover pre-departure expenses for studies abroad?

ICICI Bank Education Loans cover a range of study-related expenses such as tuition fees, travel tickets, living expenses, hostel charges, books, and other academic costs. Students planning overseas education should discuss all eligible expenses with ICICI Bank while finalising the total loan requirement.

Eligibility & Application

                EXPAND ALL

                            What are the eligibility criteria?

Applicants must be Indian citizens aged 16 years.

The maximum age is 65 years at the time of loan closure (on a case-to-case basis), with confirmed admission in recognised institutions.

                            Which courses are eligible?

Graduate, postgraduate, diploma, professional, and technical courses recognised by NAAC / NBA / AICTE / UGC are eligible.

                            Can I apply for a loan before admission?

Yes, you may apply for a pre-admission sanction.

                            Can I apply without an earning co-applicant?

Applicants with work experience may apply with a non-financial co-applicant.

T&C apply.

                            How can I download my statement/repayment schedule?

Log in to iMobile Pay → Loans → View Statement.

                            What if I disagree with the sanctioned amount?

Raise a request via customer care or visit nearest branch.

                            How does a co-applicant affect an Education Loan application?

A co-applicant is an important part of the Education Loan assessment process. ICICI Bank considers the co-applicant’s profile, including credit history, while evaluating the loan. A strong co-applicant profile may support loan approval and the overall terms offered. Eligible co-applicants can include parents, siblings, spouse, grandparents, and certain other close relatives, depending on the loan structure.

                            Can international students qualify for ICICI Bank Education Loans in India?

Yes, students can avail NRI education loan. As NRI education loan has its own eligibility criteria.

                            What income criteria apply to co-applicants or guarantors for an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank requires the co-applicant to have a regular and verifiable source of income. The bank’s Education Loan eligibility content states that documents displaying regular income are mandatory for the co-applicant. Depending on the profile, this may include salary slips, bank statements, or Income Tax Returns for self-employed co-applicants.

                            How does ICICI Bank assess future employability for Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank does not publicly describe a separate employability score, but its eligibility criteria indicate that course type, institution category, and applicant or co-applicant profile are important factors in assessment. Recognised professional, technical, and job-oriented courses, along with admission to recognised institutions, are more directly aligned with the bank’s eligibility framework.

                            How do merit or scholarship status influence Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank highlights confirmed admission to a recognised institution and a good academic record as important parts of the Education Loan application. While the bank does not publicly state a separate scholarship-based eligibility rule, merit and scholarship support can strengthen the student’s overall profile and may reduce the total loan amount required.

                            How is parental income evaluated for Education Loan eligibility?

At ICICI Bank, parents can act as co-applicants, and the bank requires documents showing regular income from the co-applicant. This means parental income is evaluated through verifiable income documents such as salary slips, bank statements, or ITR, depending on the profile. The bank does not publicly list a separate liabilities formula, but the parent’s overall financial profile forms part of the eligibility assessment.

                            What can support an Education Loan application when collateral is not provided?

For a collateral-free Education Loan, ICICI Bank does not require you to pledge assets. Instead, the bank evaluates the applicant’s academic performance, university ranking, course type, future income potential, and the credit history of the applicant and co-applicant. The co-applicant’s income and credit profile also play an important role in supporting the application.

                            How long does ICICI Bank take to process an Education Loan application?

ICICI Bank states that Education Loan processing can take a few working days after all required documents are submitted. For the most accurate estimate, students should complete documentation early and check directly with ICICI Bank.

                            How does ICICI Bank verify admission and fee details for an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank requires education-related documents as part of the Education Loan application. These documents typically include admission-related records and course cost details, which support assessment of the course, institute, and funding requirement. Processing starts after all required documents are submitted, so complete and accurate academic documentation helps the loan review move forward.

                            How should I document Education Loan funding needs for dissertation or research-related study costs?

ICICI Bank requires education-related documents as part of the loan application, along with course and cost details. If your course includes dissertation or research-related expenses, you should include the official academic records, admission documents, and institute-issued fee or expense details that support the funding requirement. Complete documentation helps ICICI Bank assess the course, institute, and total eligible cost.

                            Should I tell ICICI Bank if I have a scholarship or stipend while applying for an Education Loan for studies abroad?

Yes, students should share complete funding details, including any scholarship or stipend, while applying for an ICICI Bank Education Loan. This helps present the total education funding requirement clearly along with admission, course, and fee documents.

                            Can I apply for an ICICI Bank Education Loan for MBBS or medical studies abroad?

ICICI Bank offers Education Loans for students pursuing higher studies in India or abroad at recognised institutions. Students applying for MBBS or medicine abroad should keep the admission letter, fee schedule, academic documents, and co-applicant documents ready and then apply through the ICICI Bank Education Loan page or branch.

                            Are there age limits for Education Loan applicants?

Yes. ICICI Bank states that applicants must be Indian citizens aged 16 years or above. The maximum age is considered on a case-by-case basis, with 65 years being the age limit at the time of loan closure. Applicants must also have confirmed admission to a recognised institution.

                            How can I arrange a co-applicant for an ICICI Bank study abroad Education Loan?

ICICI Bank evaluates the applicant and co-applicant profile as part of the Education Loan process. In eligible cases, applicants with work experience may apply with a non-financial co-applicant. Students should keep the co-applicant’s identity, address, and income documents ready wherever applicable when submitting the loan application.

Service requests and account management

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is the Education Loan EMI Calculator?

The Education Loan EMI Calculator is an online tool that instantly shows your monthly EMI based on your loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure.

It helps you understand how much you will pay every month and plan your finances before applying for the loan.

                            What is the Education Loan Eligibility Calculator?

The Eligibility Calculator helps you check how much education loan you can qualify for based on your co-applicant’s income, credit profile, existing EMIs and institute category. It gives an approximate eligible loan amount even before you apply.

                            How does the moratorium period work for an ICICI Bank Education Loan?

ICICI Bank states that simple interest is charged during the moratorium period. Students should review the moratorium structure, total tenure, and EMI planning carefully before finalising the loan so they understand the full repayment impact.

                            How is interest handled during the moratorium period for an ICICI Bank Education Loan?

ICICI Bank states that simple interest is charged during the moratorium period and that repayment can begin after the course is over. When planning repayment, students should review the moratorium structure along with tenure and EMI scenarios to understand the effect on the overall repayment obligation.

                            How should students think about prepayment while planning an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank states that no prepayment or foreclosure charges apply on Education Loans. Students can therefore plan part payments or early closure as a way to reduce the outstanding balance and overall interest burden. When reviewing EMI scenarios, this flexibility should be considered as part of repayment planning.

                            What factors can affect an Education Loan application decision?

ICICI Bank’s Education Loan guidance shows that loan assessment can depend on factors such as applicant profile, co-applicant profile, institute category, type of course, credit history, and required documentation. Incomplete documents, weak repayment profile, or mismatches in required academic and financial records can affect the application outcome. Students should check eligibility, keep documents ready, and provide accurate information during the application process.

                            How are Education Loan disbursements managed for later semesters?

ICICI Bank’s Education Loan covers tuition fees, exam fees, books, laptop if required, travel tickets, living expenses, and hostel charges. For multi-year courses, students should stay in touch with the bank and institution regarding fee schedules and document requirements for future disbursements. If additional funding is needed beyond the approved structure, students should contact ICICI Bank for case-specific guidance.

                            How do ICICI Bank Education Loan eligibility criteria compare with other lenders?

ICICI Bank’s published eligibility criteria focus on holistic approach. Confirmed admission to a recognised institution, an eligible course, and a co-applicant with regular income. Since lender policies can differ, students should compare factors such as collateral requirements, repayment options, course coverage, and documentation.

                            How does co-borrower employment stability affect Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank requires the co-applicant to have a regular and verifiable source of income. This means employment stability matters because a stable income profile supports the bank’s repayment assessment. A co-borrower with consistent income and complete supporting documents is generally better placed to strengthen the application.

                            How can I check whether additional Education Loan support is available after sanction?

If you need additional funding, it is best to contact ICICI Bank directly through customer care, the nearest branch, or the Education Loan support channels available in the bank’s loan section. The bank can guide you based on your existing loan, repayment track, and updated requirement.

                            What repayment grace period does ICICI Bank typically allow on an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank states that repayment for eligible instant Education Loans can start after the course is over. The total repayment tenure inclusing course period can be up to 15 years. This structure gives students time to complete their studies before full repayment begins.

                            Does ICICI Bank list any insurance options that protect Education Loan repayment?

ICICI Bank offers an optional insurance with Education loan. This totally depends on the borrowers choice if the want to avail it or not.

                            How quickly can ICICI Bank disburse an Education Loan after approval?

ICICI Bank states that processing can take a few working days after all required documents are submitted. Disbursement begins after the required documents, signatures, and bank formalities are completed. The ICICI Bank pages reviewed here do not make a comparison with other lenders, so the most reliable expectation is to keep documentation complete to support faster processing.

                            How does ICICI Bank handle Education Loan disbursement for studies abroad?

ICICI Bank Education Loans for foreign studies are disbursed based on the approved loan amount, documentation, and the institution’s payment requirements. Students should confirm the exact disbursement process and payment mode with ICICI Bank at the time of sanction and before fee remittance.

                            Where can I confirm charges linked to an ICICI Bank Education Loan disbursement for foreign studies?

Students should review the applicable Education Loan charges and confirm any payment-related charges directly with ICICI Bank before disbursement. This is especially important for foreign-study payments where the institution’s payment mode and timing need to be aligned with the loan process.

They are free to use and available 24×7.

                            Why should I use these tools before applying for an Education Loan?

Using calculators helps you:

Plan EMIs realistically

Choose the right loan amount

Understand interest and tax impact

Compare different loan options

Make a confident, informed decision

These tools ensure you choose a loan structure that fits your financial comfort.

                            Can I export or download an amortization schedule from the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator?

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator is an online tool that instantly shows your monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount. Based on the currently available features, the calculator does not show a dedicated export or download option for a full amortization schedule. However, you can record the EMI, total interest and total payable figures displayed for each scenario you model. For a detailed repayment schedule specific to your sanctioned loan, please contact ICICI Bank directly.

                            What are common mistakes to avoid when using an Education Loan calculator?

Common errors include entering only tuition fees and ignoring other eligible education-related expenses such as hostel, travel, examination charges and study materials overlooking the moratorium period, during which simple interest is charged on the outstanding principal using an incorrect interest rate, even though rates vary based on loan type, applicant profile, institute category and course type ignoring fees such as the processing fee and administrative charges and not comparing multiple scenarios before deciding on the loan amount and tenure. Using the calculator carefully helps you plan more realistically.

                            How do co-applicant details affect Education Loan affordability calculations?

ICICI Bank Education Loan eligibility and interest rates depend on the applicant or co-applicant's profile and CIBIL score along with factors such as loan amount, course type, and institute category. You can refer to the Education Loan calculator.  For a more detailed affordability assessment that includes co-applicant details, please contact ICICI Bank directly.

                            Does ICICI Bank offer a publicly accessible Education Loan calculator?

Yes. You can access the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator and related tools — including the eligibility calculator, interest calculator, loan vs self-funding comparison tool and tax benefit calculator — from the Education Loan section on the ICICI Bank website. These tools help you estimate your monthly EMI, total interest payable, total payable amount, potential tax savings under Section 80E, and eligibility. Information about calculators offered by other banks is not available on this page.

                            What are the limitations of an Education Loan calculator?

An Education Loan calculator is useful for planning, but its results depend on the inputs you enter. The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator estimates monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount based on loan amount, interest rate and tenure. It does not replace the final sanctioned repayment schedule. Actual outcomes may differ due to moratorium-period interest, exact disbursement timing, fees such as processing fee and administrative charges, and any future rate reset. It is best used as a planning tool before speaking with the bank.

                            How to compare APR across different Education Loan offers?

To compare the overall cost of different Education Loan offers, consider the stated interest rate, loan tenure, and applicable fees. ICICI Bank's Education Loan interest calculator shows the total interest you will pay across the loan tenure, and the EMI calculator estimates your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and overall cost of borrowing. ICICI Bank also states that there are no prepayment or foreclosure charges on Education Loans.

                            How to calculate interest savings from prepaying an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank states that there are no prepayment or foreclosure charges on Education Loans. You can estimate the reduction in total interest by using the Education Loan interest calculator, which shows the total interest you will pay across the loan tenure.

                            What disclosures must lenders provide about interest computation?

On its Education Loan pages, ICICI Bank discloses published interest rate ranges, factors affecting the rate, calculator-based estimates for EMI and total interest payable, and that simple interest is charged during the moratorium period. The reviewed pages do not provide a separate list of disclosure obligations beyond these published details.

                            What are common strategies to minimize the total interest paid on an Education Loan?

Use ICICI Bank's interest calculator and EMI calculator to compare loan tenure and repayment scenarios, because these tools show total interest payable, EMI, and overall cost of borrowing. Since ICICI Bank states there are no prepayment or foreclosure charges on Education Loans, prepaying when possible can help reduce total interest. Students from the Economically Weaker Section may also reduce their interest burden during the moratorium period through the Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme.

                            How should students compare Education Loan options from different lenders?

Students should compare Education Loan options on the basis of interest rate, processing fee, repayment tenure, moratorium structure, covered expenses, collateral requirement, tax benefits, and ease of application. ICICI Bank’s Education Loan pages highlight competitive interest rates, flexible repayment options, calculators, tax benefits under Section 80E, and options for both domestic and international studies. Comparing these factors can help students choose the option that best suits their course and repayment needs.

                            What should students compare when reviewing Education Loan eligibility across banks?

Students should compare loan amount, collateral requirement, repayment flexibility, course coverage, documentation, and digital tools such as eligibility calculators. For ICICI Bank, the best reference point is the bank’s stated Education Loan eligibility criteria and FAQs.

                            How should I choose the right ICICI Bank Education Loan for a vocational or short course?

When evaluating an ICICI Bank Education Loan for a vocational or short course, you should check whether the course and institute are eligible, understand the total expenses to be funded, and review whether a secured or collateral-free loan is more suitable for your profile. ICICI Bank states that loan terms depend on the course, institute category, applicant and co-applicant profile, and loan type. You can also use the EMI calculator to estimate repayment before applying.

                            How should I plan for currency fluctuations when using an ICICI Bank Education Loan for studies abroad?

ICICI Bank Education Loans help fund overseas education expenses, but students should plan their total requirement carefully because tuition, travel, accommodation, and living costs for foreign studies can change over time. Applicants can use the Education Loan EMI calculator and discuss the required loan amount with ICICI Bank before disbursement planning.

                            What planning support should students consider when taking an ICICI Bank Education Loan for studies abroad?

ICICI Bank helps students plan their borrowing through tools such as the Education Loan EMI calculator, loan vs self-funding comparison tool, and tax benefit calculator. These tools can help students understand EMI, total interest, and repayment impact before applying for an Education Loan for overseas studies.

                            How can I manage my ICICI Bank Education Loan repayment if I start working abroad after my studies?

ICICI Bank offers flexible repayment options for Education Loans, and repayment can continue over a tenure of up to 15 years.  Students should review their EMI plan in advance using the Education Loan EMI calculator and stay in touch with the bank for repayment servicing support.

                            What are the repayment terms and tenure for an ICICI Bank Education Loan?

The repayment tenure for an ICICI Bank Education Loan can go up to 15 years. Repayment can start as late as after the course is over. During the moratorium period, simple interest is charged. students can use the Education Loan EMI calculator to understand how tenure affects monthly EMI, total interest payable, and the overall repayment amount.

Security Custody & Safety

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is the online application secure?

Yes. ICICI Bank uses encrypted and RBI-compliant systems to safeguard data.

                            Are my documents safe during upload?

Yes, file upload uses secure encrypted channels.

                            How does nationality or residency status affect Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank states that Education Loans are available to Indian citizens who have secured confirmed admission to a recognised institution in India or abroad. This means Indian citizenship is a core eligibility requirement for the applicant. ICICI Bank also offers NRI Education loan, both loans are for education purpose and there is different eligibilty criteria.

                            What minimum academic requirements are considered for Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank requires the student to have secured confirmed admission to a recognised institution for an eligible course. The applicant must have completed 10+2. The bank also highlights that a good academic record supports the application. While no specific minimum marks are publicly stated, admission to a recognised institution is the main academic requirement.

                            What credit score is typically required for unsecured Education Loans?

ICICI Bank does not publicly specify a fixed minimum credit score for unsecured Education Loans. However, the bank’s Education Loan content shows that the applicant and co-applicant profile are important, and its credit score guidance explains that credit history can affect approval, interest rate, loan amount, and whether collateral or a guarantor may be needed. A stronger credit profile improves the chances of approval for a collateral-free loan.

                            What documentary gaps can affect Education Loan eligibility?

ICICI Bank’s Education Loan guidance shows that incomplete or missing documents can affect application progress. Common gaps may include missing identity proof, address proof, academic records, admission documents, co-applicant income proof, gap certificate where required, or property papers for secured loans. Submitting complete and accurate documents helps avoid delays and strengthens the eligibility assessment.

                            How can you improve Education Loan eligibility if the co-applicant is self-employed with irregular income?

ICICI Bank requires verifiable income proof from the co-applicant, and its document guidance notes that ITR is required for self-employed individuals. If the co-applicant has irregular income, keeping complete ITR records, business financial documents, and bank statements ready can help support the application. Where needed, a stronger co-applicant profile or secured loan structure may improve the overall eligibility assessment.
