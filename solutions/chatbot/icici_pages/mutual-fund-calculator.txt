Key Input Requirements

To use the mutual fund return calculator, you need three essential inputs:

Investment Amount: Your planned one-time or monthly contribution
Investment Period: Number of years for the investment
Expected Return Rate: Anticipated annual return percentage

About Mutual Fund Calculator

How does a Mutual Fund Calculator help investors?

The calculator supports both new and experienced investors by simplifying return estimation. It helps evaluate multiple 
types of mutual fund
 returns—absolute, annualised, total, trailing, point-to-point, and rolling. Its intuitive design allows even non-experts to project returns across fixed-tenure periods and make informed decisions for both short- and long-term goals.

How does the mutual fund return calculator work?

The ICICI Bank MF Calculator works on a straightforward algorithm. Whether you're investing via a one-time lump sum or a 
Systematic Investment Plan
 (SIP), it calculates your potential returns instantly based on your inputs, without the need for complex manual calculations.

Mutual Fund Calculator FAQs

What is a Mutual Fund Calculator?

A Mutual Fund Calculator is an online tool that helps estimate the potential returns on your investments. By entering values such as investment amount, duration, and expected rate of return, you can view the maturity amount, helping in better financial planning.

Does a Mutual Fund Calculator assure accurate Future Returns?

No, the calculator provides an estimated return based on the inputs you provide.

Please note:

Actual returns may vary due to market volatility and fund performance.

It is a planning tool and does not assure results.

Use it to get indicative values for better investment decision-making.

Why is the Expected Rate of Return Important in a Mutual Fund Calculator?

The expected rate of return is a key factor in calculating future value.

Here’s why it matters:

It forms the basis for growth projections. 

Even a small change in return rate impacts long-term outcomes significantly. 

It allows you to compare scenarios and plan goals better.

How much money is required to start investing in Mutual Funds?

You can start investing in mutual funds with as little as ₹500 per instalment through a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP).

This makes mutual fund investments accessible and flexible for all types of investors.

How does a Mutual Fund Calculator help compare SIP and Lumpsum investments?

The calculator allows you to evaluate how returns differ between investing a fixed amount monthly (SIP) and investing a larger amount at once (Lumpsum).
