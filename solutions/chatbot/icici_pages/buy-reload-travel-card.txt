How to Reload & Refund Your Forex Prepaid Card

You can avail the ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card online with minimal documentation and get it delivered to the requested address within 4-5 working days. You can also avail the same through any of the ICICI Bank Forex Servicing Branches.

How to renew your Forex Prepaid Card?

Forex Servicing Branch 

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch with your request.

Send an e-mail to the Branch team from your registered e-mail ID (which is registered against your existing Forex Prepaid Card) mentioning old card number.

Mention your complete delivery address and include a scanned copy of your passport (front and back). This should be the same as that present against your existing Card. If your passport has expired, include the scanned copy of the renewed passport; however, do ensure that it has the old passport number mentioned.

Your PAN Card can also be accepted as a valid ID proof if it was updated against your existing Forex Prepaid Card.

The existing Card will be closed automatically and all details and balances will be transferred to the new Card. You will be able to view the Card in iMobile and Net Banking.

The physical Card will be delivered to your address within 5 days, depending on the location.

About Forex Prepaid Card: Reload, Refund and Renew

Why choose ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card?

With ICICI Bank’s 
Forex Prepaid Card
, you can carry multiple currencies on a single Card, saving time as well as money on exchange fees. Your transactions are secure with the embedded microchip and the Card PIN. Enjoy the convenience of reloading funds on the Card online, anytime. Competitive exchange rates ensure that you get the most value. ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card is thus the ideal travel companion for both business and leisure trips.

Load up to 15 different currencies on your ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card, including

United States Dollar (USD)

Great British Pound (GBP)

Euro (EUR)

Canadian Dollar (CAD)

Australian Dollar (AUD)

Singapore Dollar (SGD)

and more!

How to buy or reload ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card ?

Purchase or reload your Forex Prepaid Card Card by logging into Internet Banking or the iMobile app. If you're not an ICICI Bank account holder, visit any ICICI Bank Forex Branch to apply. Reload anytime, anywhere, for continuous access to funds.

Get help with your Forex Prepaid Card

For queries, please call Customer Care on 1800 1080, if in India. International customers can use our 24-hour toll-free numbers specific to their country. Select ‘Option 4’ for Forex Prepaid Cards and speak to our Customer Care Executive.

How much time will it take for the Card to be delivered?

It may take up to 5 days, depending on your location. You can ask the Branch team to provide the tracking number (AWB) on the next working day after placing your request, so you can check where the Card has reached.

Do I need to activate the new Card or transfer balances from the existing Card to the new Card?

An activated Card will be delivered to you. Balance(s) from the existing Card will also be transferred automatically when the Branch team initiates the renewal request.

What is the difference between a personalised and non-personalised Card?

A personalised Card has the Cardholder’s name printed on it. Non-personalised Cards do not have names printed on them. A personalised Card will be delivered to your address within 5 days, while a non-personalised Card can be handed over by the Branch team instantly. Personalised Cards have a validity of 7 years, whereas validity of a non-personalised Card depends on that of the stock of Cards available at the Branch, since older Cards have a validity of 5 years or less.

If my passport has expired and I have renewed my passport, how should I request for my Card to be renewed?

Provide the scanned copy of your latest passport. The old passport number must be present and visible in the scanned copy.

Will my existing transaction settings and limits be applicable to the new Card?

No. You need to enable / disable settings and set limits as per your preference through Net Banking or iMobile for your renewed Card.

Forex Cards FAQ’s

What will be the validity of my Forex Prepaid Card post renewal?

The validity will depend on the type of renewal. If you are opting for home delivery, you will receive a personalised Card (with your name printed on it) which will have a validity of 7 years. If you opt for a quick non-personalised Card from the Branch, then its validity will be that of the stock of Cards available at the Branch.

If my passport has expired, how do I update it against my renewed Card?

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch and place a request for Card renewal. After your Card is renewed, ask the Branch to update the passport and any other details that you wish to update, against the new Card.

What are the valid documents that can be used to request for Card renewal?

Passport and PAN Card are valid documents. Use the document that is updated in the Bank’s records against your existing Card.

How do I reload a Forex prepaid card?

You can reload a Forex prepaid card by logging into Net Banking, visiting a branch or using the ICICI Bank Net Banking. Enter card details, select currency and confirm payment.

What is the reload fee for a Forex prepaid card?

Reload fee depends on currency and card type. Applicable charges are specified in the Forex Card schedule of charges.

What is the process to reload a Multi-Currency Forex Card?

Select the desired currency, enter the reload amount, confirm the exchange rate and complete payment through authorised online or branch channels.

Is a Forex prepaid card top-up available after purchase?

Yes, Forex prepaid card top-up is available multiple times during validity, subject to regulatory and card limits.

Can I reload my Forex prepaid card using online payment methods?

Yes, Forex Card online payment options, such as ICICI Bank Net Banking or ICICI Bank iMobile app can be used to reload, subject to applicable charges.
