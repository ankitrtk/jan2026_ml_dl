NRI Banking

Digital Banking

Bank at your convenience

Enjoy seamless, secure banking from anywhere using ICICI Bank’s Internet Banking or the iMobile App. Access your account, transfer funds, pay bills and more—anytime, anywhere. 

Benefits of Digital Banking

24x7 Access

Bank at any time, from any location, with round-the-clock access to your account.

Secure Transactions

Robust security features including OTP, PIN, and card grid authentication ensure your banking is protected.

Wide Range of Services

From fund transfers and bill payments to opening deposits and UPI payments—do it all in a few taps or clicks.

Easy Setup

Get started in minutes with your User ID and Password or registered mobile number and Debit Card details.

Things You Should Know

No Registration Charges

Signing up for Internet Banking or iMobile App is absolutely free.

SMS Charges May Apply

Standard operator charges may apply when activating via SMS.

Transaction Limits

Daily limits apply for fund transfers via Internet Banking and iMobile; limits can be modified online.

Charges for Certain Transactions

Some services such as Demand Draft requests or tax payments may carry nominal charges.

Device Binding for Security

iMobile App binds to your device to enhance login security.

Grid Card Authentication

Required for completing financial transactions for added safety.

FAQs

How do I activate Internet Banking if I forgot my User ID or Password?

Visit the Internet Banking login page and click on “Get User ID” or “Get Password” to recover your credentials.

Is it safe to use iMobile for financial transactions?

Yes, iMobile uses device binding, MPIN, and grid card authentication to secure your account.

Can I pay bills using Internet Banking?

Yes, you can pay utility bills, credit card bills, and more directly through Internet Banking.

What should I do if I change my mobile number?

Update your mobile number at any ICICI Bank branch or by Calling our customer care. 

Is iMobile available for both Android and iOS?

Yes, the iMobile App is available on Google Play and the App Store.

Do I need to visit the branch to activate Internet Banking or iMobile?

No, you can activate both services online using your User ID, password, or Debit Card details (iMobile).
