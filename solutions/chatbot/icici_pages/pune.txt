About Personal Loans in Pune

What is a Personal Loan in Pune?

ICICI Bank offers a Personal Loan in Pune to help meet planned or unexpected expenses. Eligible salaried and self-employed individuals can apply online for quick processing. The loan is unsecured and comes with competitive interest rates and flexible repayment tenure. Whether you need funds for medical emergencies, weddings, travel, or debt consolidation, ICICI Bank provides a structured repayment plan tailored to your eligibility.

Benefits of a Personal Loan in Pune

You can manage education costs, emergency needs, home renovation, travel and more with quick processing and flexible EMIs.

Personal Loan Interest Rates and Tenure Options in Pune

ICICI Bank offers interest rates starting from 10.85 per cent to 16.65 per cent, with tenures ranging from 12 to 72 months.

Eligibility Criteria for a Personal Loan in Pune

Your income, credit score, job stability and debt-to-income ratio influence your loan eligibility and sanctioned amount.

Personal Loan FAQs

What is the maximum loan amount I can get in 
Pune
?

Eligible customers can avail 
Personal Loans
 up to ₹50 lakh based on income, profile and credit history.

What is the minimum income required to apply?

A salaried individual must have a minimum monthly income of ₹30,000.

Do I need collateral for a Personal Loan?

No. ICICI Bank Personal Loans are fully unsecured and require no guarantor.

How soon will I get the funds?

Upon successful verification, disbursal is typically completed quickly.

Can I foreclose my loan early?

Yes, prepayment charges apply as per the fee structure.

What is the minimum CIBIL score for an instant Personal loan in Pune?

A higher CIBIL score enhances approval chances for a pre-approved Personal Loan in Pune. ICICI Bank also evaluates income, existing EMIs and repayment ability to arrive at your loan eligibility before sanctioning the loan.
