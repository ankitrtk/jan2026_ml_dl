Programme Eligibility

Eligibility

Programme Eligibility for individuals (Any one of the below):

Relationship value of ₹5 Cr

(Deposits + Account balance + MF Value + Demat holdings)
Deposits + Account balance = ₹50 L

*Net monthly salary credit of ₹8 L in ICICI Bank Salary Account

Programme Eligibility for family banking (Any one of the below):

Family relationship value of ₹7.5 Cr

(Deposits + Account balance + MF Value + Demat holdings)
Deposits + Account balance = ₹75 L

If customer does not meet average programme criteria for 12 months, then the account will be re-classified.

If programme eligibility criteria are not met, customer needs to maintain monthly average balance of ₹15,000 in Savings Account to avoid non-maintenance charge.

Non-maintenance charges are waived off for:

Family Savings Account linked to ICICI Bank Salary Account

ICICI Bank Private Savings Account FAQs

What happens if MAB in Savings Account is not maintained?

Non-maintenance charges will be applicable, which is 6% of the shortfall amount or Rs. 500 whichever is lower.

This charge will be waived if customer enrols and fulfils the respective programme eligibility criteria.

What happens if programme threshold is not maintained?

Non-maintenance charges will not be levied if ₹15,000 MAB is maintained.

If customer does not meet average programme criteria for 12 months, then the account will be re-classified.
