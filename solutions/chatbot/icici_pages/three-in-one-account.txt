Ways to trade

Stocks

Cash
:​​​​ Trading in the cash segment is buying stocks for a time horizon that could be from one day to years. 

Systematic Equity Plan (SEP)
: SEP helps you avoid timing the market and averages out the investment cost. Invest in Stocks, Gold, or Index ETFs at intervals. 

Commodities

Trade in commodities throughout the weekdays.

Market hours (non-agricultural)
: 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. IST

Includes bullion, metals, and energy products. Closed on weekends and Exchange holidays.

Futures and options

Futures
: Get higher exposure to hedge your cash and derivatives transactions.

Future plus:
 Take intraday buy or sell positions in Futures and square off before market close.

Options
: Trade in index or stock contracts with limited downside risk.

Shares as margin:
 Pledge your shares online to get higher trading limits in F&O.

Unique features to assist with trading

‘One click equity’ convenience 

Diversify with theme-based baskets of stocks and ETFs curated by our research Team. Invest, track and exit equity portfolios with just one click. No lock-in period. Choose lump sum or 
Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)
 mode as per your preference

Buy more with margin funding

Purchase stocks using Margin Trading Facility (MTF) under the pay later option. Benefit from attractive interest rates starting at 9.85%* with the ICICI direct prime plan. 

*Interest rates subject to change. 

Advanced trading tools

Use Good Till Triggered (GTT) orders to set trigger-based buy or sell requests with extended validity. Define stop-loss and target conditions together for added control and convenience. 

Smarter monitoring features

Track your portfolio and stocks effectively with smart alerts. Use execution algorithms for order placement through automated instructions based on price, time, and volume combinations.

3-in-1 Account FAQs

What is a 3-in-1 Account?

A 3-in-1 Account is a unique offering by ICICI Bank and ICICIdirect, combining a 
Savings Account
, Trading Account, and Demat Account into one seamless platform. It enables easy, secure, and integrated online investing and trading.

What are the advantages of a 3-in-1 Account?

Key benefits include:

One-time account setup for savings, trading and demat needs

Trade in equities, F&O, mutual funds, IPOs and insurance in one place

Accessible across web, mobile app and Call-N-Trade

Multiple brokerage plans to suit investor needs

Buy Now, Pay Later
: Invest even when funds are short with margin support

What are the features of a Trading Account?

Seamless trading
: Buy or sell without writing cheques or dealing with settlement hassles

Secure funds
: Manage your own linked Savings and Demat Accounts—no transfer to broker pool accounts

Wide product range
: Trade across NSE/BSE, access Margin, BTST, derivatives, Mutual Funds, and IPOs

Precise execution
: Place trades for exact amounts without deviation

Real-time tracking
: Use portfolio tracker, watchlists and SMS alerts to stay updated

Advanced research tool
: Use 
iLens
 to screen stocks based on custom trends or criteria

How do you start online investments?

To start investing:

Open a 3-in-1 Account (Savings, Demat and Trading)

Ensure a sufficient balance in your linked Savings Account

Choose a suitable brokerage plan (e.g., ICICIdirect Prime plan)

Start investing online through the ICICIdirect platform

Account opening charges: Nil for Trading and Demat Accounts

Demat AMC:
    1. 1st Year: Nil

    2. From 2nd Year: ₹700 + taxes per year
