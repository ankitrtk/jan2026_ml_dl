About ICICI Bank UK Personal Current Account

What is ICICI Bank UK Personal Current Account?

The Personal Current Account is a current account offered by ICICI Bank UK in GBP, specifically designed for Indians migrating to the UK. You can open this account before leaving for the UK, ensuring access to overseas banking as soon as you arrive.

HomeVantage Current Account is now Personal Current Account

Eligibility Criteria

To open a Personal Current Account, applicants must be 18 years or older and possess a valid Indian passport.

Account Linking Feature

Easily link your NRI accounts in India and the UK with the One View feature for seamless management of finances across both countries.

Instant Account Opening

Open an account instantly with ICICI Bank's digital service, even before you arrive in the UK, for a smooth transition to your new home. Here’s how:

Download the ICICI Bank UK App or visit the 
signup page
.

Fill in the application form and complete KYC (keep your original passport handy).

After submission, your account can be opened instantly*.

Generate your Internet and Mobile Banking password after account activation.

Receive your welcome letter and contactless debit card at your address.

* Subject to Checks. For opening Joint accounts, please visit any of our nearest ICICI UK branch

FAQs

How do I apply for a ICICI Bank UK Personal Current Account?

Apply online through the ICICI Bank UK iMobile app or website, complete KYC with a valid passport, and submit the application for instant account opening.

What documents are required to apply for the Personal Current Account?

You need a valid Indian passport and address proof in India (Aadhaar number).

Can I transfer money from India to my Personal Current Account?

Yes, you can transfer funds to your ICICI Bank UK Personal Current Account. within RBI's permissible outward remittance limits.

Are joint accounts available for the Personal Current Account?

Visit an ICICI Bank UK  branch to open a joint Personal Current Account.

How can I contact ICICI Bank for inquiries regarding my application?

For inquiries, you can email ukservice@icicibank.com with your details and preferred contact time. Alternatively, you can also speak to our customer care at 0344 412 4444 (if calling from the UK) / 1800 419 6588 (if calling from India).

What is the difference between the HomeVantage Current Account and the Personal Current Account?

There is no difference in features or benefits. The HomeVantage Current Account has simply been renamed to the Personal Current Account. Everything else about the account remains the same.
