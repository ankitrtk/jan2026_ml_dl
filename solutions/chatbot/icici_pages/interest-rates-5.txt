Education Loan Interest Rates

ICICI Bank offers Education Loans with competitive education loan interest rates to help students manage higher education expenses in India and abroad. Student loan interest rates may vary depending on factors such as course type, institution, loan amount, repayment tenure and applicant eligibility. Education Loans may cover tuition fees and other eligible academic expenses with flexible repayment options and structured EMI plans. Customers can apply online with simple documentation and choose suitable repayment tenure based on financial requirements and repayment capacity.

As of the quarter ending March 2026, ICICI Bank Education Loan interest rates range from 9.00% to 13.00% per annum, with a mean rate of 10.54% per annum. Secured Education Loans for select domestic institutes start as low as 8.50% per annum, while Unsecured Education Loans for abroad studies start at 10.25% per annum. The applicable rate depends on the course, institution category, loan security, applicant profile and co-applicant credit history.

 Education Loan Interest Rates FAQs

How does Education Loan interest work?

ICICI Bank states that education loan interest rates are linked to current market rates. The final rate depends on factors such as course type, institute category, loan amount, repayment period, and applicant or co-applicant profile. The bank also provides guidance on floating rate reset options for borrowers whose rates are revised during the loan tenure.

This helps keep the rates transparent and affordable. Such rates, coupled with flexible repayment options, help students focus on their studies without financial stress.

Are there any tax benefits that can be availed on an Education Loan?

ICICI Bank education loan borrowers may claim tax benefits on the interest paid under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act. The bank also provides an education loan tax benefit calculator to help estimate potential tax savings. This published benefit applies to the interest component of the education loan. You can reduce your taxable income by the interest amount paid that financial year and thus save on tax. It is a smart way to ease the financial load while repaying your loan.

What is the current Education Loan interest rate at ICICI Bank?

ICICI Bank offers competitive Education Loan interest rates, which depend on factors like loan amount, borrower profile and the course being applied for. You can check the latest rates in the Education Loan interest rate section of our website.

How are Education Loan rates determined?

Education Loan rates depend on factors like the loan amount, course type, repayment period, the applicant’s academic background and the credit history of the applicant and or co-applicant or guarantor. A few loans have fixed rates, while others may change with time (floating rates).

Are interest rates lower for premier institutions?

Yes, ICICI Bank may offer low-interest Student Loans for courses at premier educational institutes. Make sure that you provide all the documents supporting your admission so that the bank can verify them and approve the loan with a lower interest rate.

Does ICICI Bank offer low-interest Education Loans?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers low-interest Education Loan options, especially for students with strong academic records or those studying at top-ranking colleges and universities.

How can I calculate my Education Loan interest?

You can use ICICI Bank’s 
Education Loan interest calculator online
. You need to enter your loan amount, tenure and interest rate to get a quick estimate of your EMIs and payable interest.

What are the benefits of Education Loans with low interest rates?

Low interest rates mean a lower total repayment amount. This makes your 
Education Loan
 much easier to manage in the long run.

How do ICICI Bank’s Education Loan interest rates compare to others?

ICICI Bank offers competitive Education Loan interest rates. Additionally, you also get perks like flexible repayment, tax benefits and loans for courses in India as well as abroad.

Are ICICI Bank’s Education Loan interest rates better compared to others?

ICICI Bank offers competitive Education Loan interest rates. Additionally, you also get perks like flexible repayment, tax benefits and loans for courses in India as well as abroad.

What is Education Loan interest rate?

Education Loan interest rate is the percentage of the principal amount you pay on the money borrowed from the bank for funding your studies (Education Loan).

What is the average interest rate for an Education Loan?

The average Education Loan interest rate in India is around 11.27% per annum with banks like ICICI Bank offering rates that may depend on the applicant’s credit profile, course and academic record.

How does the Education Loan interest rate affect my loan repayment?

A higher Education Loan interest may imply larger EMIs and total repayment. Choosing a lower rate can help reduce your repayment burden over time.

Can I switch to a different interest rate type during the loan tenure?

Yes, ICICI Bank allows you to switch between fixed and floating interest rates but a small conversion fee may apply. Check with the bank before the rate conversion.

Do Education Loan interest rates vary for different courses?

Yes, Education Loan interest rates can differ based on the course type, institution or study location since overseas courses often have higher rates.

How are ICICI Bank Education Loan interest rates determined?

ICICI Bank Education Loan interest rates depend on factors such as the loan amount, course type, repayment period, institute category, applicant and co-applicant profile, and credit history. Rates may also differ for premier institutions and strong academic records. "

What factors influence the terms offered on an ICICI Bank Education Loan?

The terms offered on an ICICI Bank Education Loan depend on factors such as the course type, institute category, loan amount, applicant and co-applicant profile, academic record, and credit history. Premier institutions and strong academic records may support more competitive pricing within the published interest rate framework.

How can I understand the effective annual interest rate on my ICICI Bank Education Loan?

ICICI Bank offers competitive interest rates linked to current market rates. The effective annual cost of your loan depends on the interest rate and any applicable fees such as the processing fee of the loan amount plus GST and administrative charges of the loan amount. To estimate the overall cost, use the Education Loan EMI calculator to see your total interest payable and total payable amount, then add the applicable one-time fees. For the exact rate applicable to your profile, contact ICICI Bank, as rates vary based on loan amount, course type and credit history.

How do fixed vs. floating interest rates work for Education Loans?

ICICI Bank offers both fixed and floating interest rate options on Education Loans. You can switch between fixed and floating rates during the loan tenure for a small conversion fee. If the interest rate is revised for your loan account, you may opt to change your EMI, change your tenure, change both EMI and tenure, or switch from floating to fixed rate before the reset date. Applicable conversion fees are specified in the transaction documents.

How does credit score affect Education Loan interest rates?

Credit history is one of the key factors that determine the interest rate on an ICICI Bank Education Loan. Rates depend on factors like loan amount, course type, repayment period, and the applicant's credit history. A strong credit profile of the applicant or co-applicant may help secure a more favourable rate. Students with strong academic records or those at top-ranking colleges may also benefit from preferential rates.

What role does collateral play in determining interest rates?

Collateral plays important role in determining the interest rate of an education loan as it reduces the lender's risk. Collateral can be a propoerty, FD with bank etc.

How do interest rate caps or floors work in student loans?

ICICI Bank publishes Education Loan interest rate ranges that show the applicable bands for different loan types. You can check the interest rate in https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/loans/education-loan/interest-rates

How do concessional loans for medical or technical studies affect interest?

ICICI Bank states that Education Loan rates can differ based on course type, institution, or study location. The bank also offers lower interest rates for students at premier institutes and for students with strong academic records or those at top-ranking colleges. For exact rate applicability to a specific course, please refer to the current Education Loan interest rate page and loan documents.

How can students evaluate Education Loan interest rates in India?

On ICICI Bank’s Education Loan interest rates page, the bank states that rates are based on loan amount, course type, repayment period, and applicant's credit history. Students should compare the applicable interest rate, processing fee, repayment flexibility, moratorium options, and any charges before choosing a lender. For an accurate estimate of repayment burden, students can also use the Education Loan EMI calculator.

What fees and processing charges apply to an Education Loan?

For fees and processing charges please click on the link: 
https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/loans/education-loan/interest-rates

Is Education Loan Interest Repayment on a monthly basis?

Yes, Education Loan interest repayment is generally done through monthly EMIs after the applicable repayment or moratorium period based on the loan agreement and repayment structure.
