Home Loan Processing Fees & Service Charges

ICICI Bank provides clear details on Home Loan processing fee, applicable Mortgage Loan charges and other related costs. The processing fee for a Mortgage Loan is levied as per policy terms and may vary based on the loan amount and eligibility assessment. Customers can review the complete processing charges for the Mortgage Loan before applying.

Charges
Mortgage Loans
Home Loan Processing Fee (Non-Refundable)
Up to 2% of Loan Amount.

The processing/login fee is a one-time non-refundable fee and is collected by the Bank for the purpose of appraising the Application for the Facility and the same is independent of the outcome or result of such appraisal. The processing/login fee is payable at the time of submitting of the Application, by way of Cheque or Demand Draft favoring the Bank and/or such other mode as may be acceptable to the Bank.

FAQs about Home Loan Processing Fees

How is the Home Loan processing fee calculated?

The processing fee of a Home Loan can be defined as a fixed percentage of the Loan Amount that has been applied for. It is payable during application and is non-refundable regardless of loan approval status.

What is the Home Loan processing fee for ICICI Bank?

ICICI Bank charges a Home Loan processing fee of 0.5% of the sanctioned Loan Amount plus applicable taxes and a special processing fee for customers with an Instant Home Loan offer. The fee is payable at the time of submission of the application.

What are the Mortgage Loan registration charges?

Mortgage Loan registration charges depend on the applicable state government stamp duty and registration fee rules, which may vary from state to state. For more details, please refer to the service charges mentioned on the page.
