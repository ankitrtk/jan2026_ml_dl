Manchester United Platinum Credit Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card is a Credit Card created specifically for football enthusiasts.

What are the benefits of the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card offers benefits such as fuel surcharge waivers, complimentary airport lounge access and movie discounts. In addition, top spenders get Manchester United merchandise and a fully-paid Manchester United Experience.

How can I apply online for the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card?

You can visit the ICICI Bank website and select the Manchester United Platinum Credit Card. After completing the application process, the card will be issued within a few working days if all the eligibility criteria are met.

What is the joining fee for this Credit Card?

The joining fee for the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card is ₹499 + GST.

What are the travel benefits of the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card offers complimentary airport lounge access four times annually.

What are the annual benefits of the ICICI Bank Manchester United Platinum Credit Card?

You can get a waiver on your annual fee if you have spent more than ₹1,25,000 in the previous year.

What are the exclusive Manchester United benefits that I can get with this card?

Top spenders every month get a signed Manchester United jersey, a fully-paid Manchester United Experience which includes a private tour of the Old Trafford stadium, a visit to the Manchester United megastore and more.

What is the welcome gift for Manchester United Cardholders?

As a welcome gift, Manchester United Credit Cardholders receive a Manchester United-branded football. This exclusive merchandise adds to the excitement for fans of the club.

What does the top monthly spender receive?

The top spender (across all Cardholders) every month receives a signed Manchester United jersey, branded football or t-shirt.

What benefits do the top 18 spenders receive?

The top 18 spenders across Cardholders are rewarded with an exclusive fully-paid Manchester United Experience. This includes a private stadium and museum tour in Manchester, England, an opportunity to watch part of a First Team squad training session and a visit to the Manchester United megastore.

What is included in the private stadium tour?

The private stadium tour includes a guided visit to the historic Manchester United stadium and museum, offering an immersive experience of the club's legacy and achievements.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your Manchester United Platinum Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Manchester United Platinum Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026. 

 Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section 

 Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking 

 Choose ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount Offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards 

 Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’ 

 Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page. 

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets through BookMyShow and INOX. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders receive a 1% surcharge waiver on fuel purchases (applicable to transactions up to ₹4,000) when the card is swiped at an HPCL fuel pump using an ICICI Bank Point of Sale (POS) terminal.
