FD Calculation for Residents and NRIs

Information for Resident Customers

Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) applies to interest earned, as per prevailing rates.

Check the latest 
Fixed Deposit interest rates

Minimum FD amount: ₹10,000

Tenure options:

Cumulative FD: 6 months – 10 years

Quarterly Interest Payout FD: 3 months – 10 years

Monthly Interest Payout FD: 3 months – 10 years

Short-term: 7 days – 185 days.

Information for NRI Customers

The FD Calculator provides indicative maturity amounts for NRE and NRO Term Deposits with premature withdrawal facilities

It cannot be used for calculating FCNR Account Deposit maturity values

Check the latest interest rates for FCNR Deposits

The Calculator covers deposits up to ₹3 crore

For deposits over ₹3 crore, check NRE or NRO interest rates

Senior citizen rates do not apply to NRE and NRO deposits

NRO FDs are subject to TDS

Minimum FD amount: ₹25,000

Tenure options:

NRE FD: 1 year – 10 years

NRO FD: 7 days – 10 years.

Formula to Calculate FD Maturity Amount

The maturity amount of an FD is determined by using the compound interest formula, as most banks compound interest quarterly. Below is the formula used: FD is determined using

A = P (1 + r/n)^(n*t)

where: 

A = Maturity amount 

P = Principal amount (Initial deposit) 

r = Annual interest rate (in decimal, e.g., 6.6% = 0.066) 

n = Number of times interest is compounded per year (i.e., if interest is compounded quarterly, n=4)

t = Tenure of the FD in years

Let us understand it with an example. When an investor invests ₹1,00,000 in an FD at an interest rate of 6.60% per annum for 3 years, in which the interest is compounded quarterly, they get a total amount of ~₹1,21,699 at maturity*.

*The estimated maturity amount is calculated using ICICI Bank’s FD Calculator. Final values may vary subject to ICICI Bank’s policies.

FAQs

What is an FD Calculator?

The ICICI Bank FD Calculator is a user-friendly tool designed to help customers estimate the interest earned on their Fixed Deposit (FD). By inputting the investment amount, tenure, and applicable interest rate, customers can easily calculate the potential returns on their deposit.

How does an FD Calculator help you?

ICICI Bank’s FD Calculator is a simple and efficient tool to estimate your Fixed Deposit returns. The calculator needs only basic information from you, i.e., the FD amount and the FD tenure. The rate of interest is automatically filled. The calculator quickly calculates the expected returns on the FD and helps you save time when comparing various FD plans to choose one.

It also eliminates the need for time-consuming manual calculations and ensures more precise results. It helps in better financial planning by providing a clear picture of how your investment will grow over time. 

How to Use the FD Calculator?

Using the FD Calculator is simple:

Choose your customer type (Normal or Senior Citizen).

Select the FD type (Cumulative or Interest Payout: Monthly or Quarterly).

Input the FD amount and tenure (in Years or Months or Days or Days only)

The calculator will display the interest rate, interest amount, maturity date, and final maturity value.

How does the FD Interest Calculator work?

Interest is calculated based on the FD tenure and the prevailing interest rate at the time of opening the FD.

Cumulative FD: Interest compounded, i.e., reinvested into the principal quarterly

Quarterly Payout FD: Simple interest paid at the end of each quarter

Monthly Payout FD: Interest paid at a discounted value monthly

Short-term FD: Interest calculated and paid on simple interest basis.

What is the minimum amount required to open an FD?

The minimum amount to 
open a Fixed Deposit
 with ICICI Bank is ₹10,000. For a Recurring Deposit, the minimum opening amount is ₹500 and subsequent deposits can be in multiples of ₹100 per month.

What is the tenure of ICICI Bank Fixed Deposits?

ICICI Bank offers flexible tenures from 7 days – 10 years.

Who can invest in ICICI Bank Fixed Deposits?

Eligible applicants include:

Individuals:

Residents

Non-Resident Indians

Minors with guardianship.

Entities:

Sole Proprietorship Firms

Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs)

Partnership Firms

Limited Companies

Trust Accounts.

How can I open a Fixed Deposit?

You can open an ICICI Bank FD anytime, anywhere through ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking. You can also open an FD by visiting any ICICI Bank Branch or ATM or by calling our Customer Care on 1800 1080.
