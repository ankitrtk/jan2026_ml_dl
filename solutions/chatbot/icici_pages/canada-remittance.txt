FAQs

What is Canada Remittance?

Canada Remittance allows you to send money instantly from any Canadian bank account to any bank in India. It’s a secure, fast, and cost-effective way to transfer funds with no hidden charges.

Are there any fees or hidden charges for transferring funds?

No, there are no hidden charges. Our transfers are completely transparent, and we offer competitive exchange rates with no additional fees. You only pay for the processing fees if applicable.

What documents do I need to complete the transfer?

To complete the transfer, you’ll need a valid proof of identity (such as a passport or driver's license) and details of your Canadian bank account. You'll also need the recipient's bank account information in India.

Is the transfer process secure?

Yes, all transfers are fully secured with high-level encryption to ensure your money is protected. We follow industry-standard security measures to guarantee safe, timely, and reliable transfers.

How long does it take for the money to reach India?

Transfers are typically processed instantly. However, processing times may vary slightly depending on the bank's procedures. Rest assured, our platform ensures a quick and reliable transfer process.
