FAQs on NRI Accounts

What is a Savings Account?

An ICICI Bank Savings Account is considered as one of the best to manage and grow your savings. Get access to funds at competitive interest rates along with several bank and digital services.

What are the interest rates for ICICI Bank Savings Account?

ICICI Bank ensures competitive returns on your deposited funds. With a focus on financial growth and increased savings, we offer attractive interest rates of 2.50%. By opening a Savings Account with ICICI Bank, you can enjoy the benefits of reliable and secure banking.

How to apply for an NRI Account?

You can apply for an NRE/NRO Account either through our
 Assisted Journey
 with the help of a Relationship Manager or through a 
Do-It-Yourself (DIY) journey
 online.

RM assisted journey

Sign up for an NRE/NRO Account: Fill your details to receive a call.

Live Assistance: Get help over a call to fill the account opening form and KYC documentation.

Account Opened: Your account is opened after successful scrutiny of KYC documents.

Activation: Activate your account with the first fund transfer from your own overseas account.

APPLY NOW

Do-It-Yourself (DIY) journey

Get Started: Choose the NRE/NRO account program that suits you.

Complete Your Application: Complete and submit the digital form with your details (Keep your self-signed copies of Passport, visa, and address proof ready).

Account Opened: Your account is opened after successful scrutiny of KYC documents.

Activation: Activate your account with the first fund transfer from your own overseas account.

*Only available for customers in USA.

What is the minimum balance required to be maintained in ICICI Bank Savings Account?

You need to maintain the minimum balance as per the product programme, 
click here
 to know the charges for various programmes.

How is the minimum monthly average balance (MAB) calculated?

The Monthly Average Balance (MAB) is calculated on a simple average of end of the day balances for a calendar month. The MAB is calculated by adding the closing ledger balance of the account of each day from the start of the month to the end of the month. Then the total sum is divided by the number of days in that month.

Can I get a chequebook after opening a Savings Account?

Your chequebook will be issued only upon request after you have successfully completed your KYC verification. You can request for a chequebook by logging in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking or the iMobile App.

iMobile app: Go to Services > Chequebook Services > Issue Chequebook

Internet Banking: Go to Bank Accounts > Accounts > Chequebook request

Will I receive a Debit Card after opening a Savings Account?

Yes. You will receive a Debit Card as a part of your welcome kit at your registered address once your account is successfully opened or bank also provides the option of non personalized kits that are given by the branches across the counter.

How do I register for Internet Banking and Mobile Banking services?

Once your Savings Account is successfully opened, you can log in to the iMobile App, set your 4-digit passcode and start exploring. For Internet Banking, you can generate your password with the User ID which was provided to you after opening the Savings Account.

You can update mobile number through ICICI Bank branch:

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch along with your original photo ID proof. Once the request is placed, your mobile number will be updated in 2 working days. To locate the nearest ICICI Bank Branch, visit 
https://maps.icicibank.com/mobile

We have discontinued mobile number registration/ de-registration/ modification through our Customer Care and Internet Banking due to security reasons

How to generate Internet Banking password for my Savings Account?

Internet Banking:

You can generate your password at www.icicibank.com > Click on ‘Internet Banking’ (Bottom of the page) > Get started > I want my Password.

Customer Care:

You may place a request by calling our Customer Care between 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. from your registered mobile number.

ICICI Bank Branch:

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch along with original photo ID proof. To locate the nearest ICICI Bank Branch, visit 
https://maps.icicibank.com/mobile
.

What is Savings Account portability?

You can request to transfer your Savings Account from one ICICI Bank Branch to another through Internet Banking, iMobile app, Customer Care or by submitting a written request at any ICICI Bank Branch.

What is FATCA declaration?

FATCA stands for Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act. As per regulatory guidelines, all banks have to collect FATCA from every customer who opens a Savings Account. FATCA came into being to combat tax evasion and to ensure strict adherence to tax rules. Its main objective is to identify and prevent offshore tax avoidance by the US citizens or residents.

How do I access my Virtual Debit Card on the iMobile app?

Click ‘Debit Card’ on the Home Page

Get the details of your Virtual Debit Card

Select your Card and click on ‘Manage All Services’.

Why do I have to add a nominee for my account?

Adding a nominee ensures your assets are managed correctly, in case of an unfortunate scenario. The nominee acts as a custodian and must provide a valid ID proof if needed.

What is the process to convert a minor account to a major account?

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch with the originals and copies of the following documents to make this transition seamless:

Identity / Address proof: Valid Passport / Aadhaar Card / Driving Licence / Voter ID / NREGA job card.

Recent passport size colour photograph.

PAN Card/ Form 97.

What is a Dormant Account?

An Account is classified as Dormant, if inactive for over 2 years that includes inability to transact or update details.

How to reactivate a Dormant Account?

Visit an ICICI Bank Branch and complete the Re-KYC form.

Provide address proof and PAN Card/Form 97.

Carry out a transaction.

My debit card has expired. How can I receive my renewed debit card?

If your debit card has expired, a renewed card is issued automatically and sent to your registered communication address, applicable charges will be levied as per the bank 
service charges
.

What should I do if my debit card was not used for a long time before expiry, how can I get the renewed card and will there be any charges?

If the debit card was not used for at least one year prior to its expiry, the renewal request will not be raised automatically. In such case, you will need to place a manual reissue request, applicable charges will be levied as per the bank 
service charges
.
