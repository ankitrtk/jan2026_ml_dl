About Personal Loans in Chennai

What is a Personal Loan in Chennai?

ICICI Bank offers a Personal Loan in Chennai to help you manage planned or urgent expenses such as medical bills, home renovation, weddings, or travel. Eligible salaried and self-employed applicants can apply online for a Personal Loan in Chennai with competitive interest rates and flexible repayment tenure. The loan is unsecured, meaning no collateral is required. With a transparent application process and digital application, ICICI Bank aims to make Personal Loan finance [1] in Chennai simple and convenient.

Why Choose a Personal Loan in Chennai?

Living in Chennai? With ICICI Bank’s extensive branch network and efficient loan process, you get fast approvals, attractive interest rates, flexible EMIs and easy access to funds, right in your city.

Unsecured & Hassle free Personal Loan in Chennai

Since this is an unsecured loan, there is no need for collateral or a guarantor. Documentation is minimal, and the application, online or in-branch, is designed for quick disbursal, helping you access funds when you need them most.

Eligibility Criteria for a Personal Loan in Chennai

Interest rates typically range from 10.85% to 16.65% per annum, depending on your profile. Loan tenures are flexible, generally from 12 to 72 months. Minimum eligibility includes a stable income (₹30,000 per month for salaried) and valid KYC documents.

Personal Loan FAQs

Who can apply for a Personal Loan in Chennai?

Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and business owners who meet income, residence and documentation requirements.

What is the maximum loan amount available?

Eligible applicants can borrow up to ₹50 lakh, depending on income, repayment capacity and credit profile.

What is the repayment tenure?

EMI tenure generally ranges from 12 months to 72 months, depending on loan amount and the borrower’s preference.

Do I need collateral or a guarantor?

No. ICICI Bank’s 
Personal Loans
 are unsecured and do not require any collateral or guarantor.

How quickly can funds be disbursed?

Upon approval and document verification, funds are credited promptly, usually within a few days, either digitally or via branch disbursement.
