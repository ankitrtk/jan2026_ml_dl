Purchasing a home is not just a financial transaction, it is the beginning of a new chapter in your life where memories are created and dreams are realised.

ICICI Bank recognises the importance of owning a home. The Bank is dedicated to being your trusted partner in this journey by offering Home Loans customised to make your home ownership dreams a reality.

Home Loan of up to Rs 50 lakh

ICICI Bank understands that your home is more than just a place; it is an emotion. This is why ICICI Bank provides 
Home Loans
 of up to Rs 50 lakh, making it easier for you to choose your dream home. With an easy application process, minimal documentation and instant provisional sanction, your journey towards home ownership is now hassle-free. 

Eligibility criteria* for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan

Age: Applicants should fall between 21 to 65 years of age

Income: A consistent source of income is required to ensure repayment capability

Credit score: A good credit score is important for loan approval

Our team of experts will guide you through the eligibility process to ensure a seamless experience.

Please note that other factors may also influence your eligibility.

*Terms and Conditions apply

How to apply for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan

ICICI Bank offers a convenient digital application process that simplifies your Home Loan application journey. 

You can follow the steps given below to apply for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan:

Enter your details:
 Enter your personal and contact details

Check your eligibility
: Use our online tools to determine your loan eligibility

Pay the processing fee: 
Initiate your application by paying the nominal processing fee

Upload documents:
 Submit the necessary documents online for verification

Get provisional sanction:
 Get instant provisional sanction to kick-start your home-buying journey.

50 Lakh Home Loan EMI FAQs

What will be the EMI for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan?

The EMI for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan will depend on the interest rate and tenure. ICICI Bank offers a range of interest rates. You can use our EMI Calculator to determine the exact EMI amount based on the tenure decided by you.

What is the EMI for a Rs 50 lakh loan for a tenure of 5 years?

The EMI for a Rs 50 lakh loan for a tenure of 5 years at an illustrative interest rate of 9 % will be Rs 1,03,792. You can use our 
EMI Calculator for Home Loan
 to get an accurate estimate based on the prevailing interest rate.

What is the monthly interest for a Rs 50 lakh Home Loan?

ICICI Bank offers competitive interest rates. Your monthly interest can vary according to the interest rate applicable on your Home Loan and the Home Loan tenure chosen by you. The interest rate depends on various factors such as age, income, credit score and more.

Can I get a Home Loan of Rs 50 lakh?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers Home Loans of up to Rs 50 lakh, making it easier for you to finance your home. Our eligibility criteria ensure that you can apply for a loan that suits your financial situation.
