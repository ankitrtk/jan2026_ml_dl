Things you should know

Programme Eligibility

NRI Smart Star Savings Account - Maintain monthly average account balance of ₹15,000
NRI Smart Star Select Account - Maintain Deposits* + Account Balance of ₹5 lakh OR Deposits* + Account Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹50 lakh
NRI Smart Star Premium Account - Maintain Deposits* + Account Balance of ₹25 lakh OR Deposits* + Account Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹1 Cr

*Deposits include Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits

^AUM -Assets under Management

Non-Maintenance Charges

If programme eligibility criteria are not met, customer needs to maintain minimum monthly average balance (MAB*) of ₹15,000 at Customer ID^ Level to avoid non-maintenance charge.

*MAB is the simple average of day-end balances for a calendar month

^Customer ID includes all your savings account cumulative balances (except Fixed deposits/Recurring deposits)

Eligibility

NRI minors aged 10 to 18 years can open and operate the account independently

Customer ID Level Balancing

Includes the monthly average balance in NRE/NRO Savings Account and

Month-end balance in Fixed Deposits (FDs) & Recurring Deposits (RDs) under the same Customer ID

About NRI Smart Star

What are the eligibility criteria?

NRI minors aged 10 to 18 years can open and operate this account independently. This ensures young NRIs can manage their finances early, promoting financial responsibility. The account offers seamless access to banking services, including Debit Card, Net Banking Access and a Cheque Book, for convenient transactions. 

Please note: This account cannot be offered if the minor is an NRI and the guardian is a resident Indian.

What is the process to apply?

Eligible NRI minors can apply by submitting proof of NRI status (passport & visa), date of birth proof, and parent/guardian's KYC documents. The account can be opened by visiting the nearest branch. An initial deposit is required as per the minimum balance criteria.

What all do I get in the account?

This account provides comprehensive banking services , including a Silver Chip Debit Card with Reward Points and zero liability protection. Additionally, customers get Net Banking and a Cheque Book, ensuring seamless transactions globally. The account balance across NRE/NRO savings, fixed deposits, and recurring deposits at the Customer ID level contributes to MAB calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who can open an NRI Smart Star Account?

NRI minors aged 10 to 18 years can open this account and operate it independently. They must provide necessary KYC documents, including proof of NRI status and date of birth proof.

Can the Smart Star account be operated by a minor?

Yes, Minors above the age of 10 years can open and operate the account all by themselves.

How can I apply for this account?

NRI minors can apply by submitting proof of NRI status, date of birth proof, and parent/guardian KYC documents at an ICICI Bank branch or through online banking. An initial deposit is required as per MAB guidelines.
