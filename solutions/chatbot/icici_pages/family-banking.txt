Family Banking

With Family Banking, you and your loved ones can unlock upgraded benefits as per the highest programme existing amongst the Family Member Accounts. Family Banking is available for Savings, Salary and NRI customers.

Benefits & Features

Upgraded Banking Benefits

All family members get to enjoy premium benefits of the highest existing programme within the family.

Premium Debit Card

Debit Card with airport lounge access for all family members.

Higher Insurance*

Family members will be covered with upgraded insurance benefits.

View & Transact Right

Option for an assigned family member to ‘view & transact’ on behalf of other family members, basis their consent. To avail this feature, visit any ICICI Bank branch.

Family Relationship**

 All family members can collectively maintain the programme threshold, 1.5 times the programme amount as a family to get waiver on non-maintenance charges and continue enjoying the programme benefits.

Priority Servicing

Benefits of priority services are applicable to family members of Wealth Management and Private Banking customers.

*Same as primary member

**At family level across balances, deposits & investments. Individual account balances and transactions are not visible to other family members, unless ‘view & transact’ option has been availed by the family.

Eligibility and Conditions

Eligibility

Conditions

Deposits and Account balances of:

₹3 lakhs for Select Banking
₹15 lakhs for Wealth Management
₹75 lakhs for Private Banking

OR

 Deposits, Account balance, MF Value and Demat holdings of:

₹37.5 lakhs for Select Banking
₹75 lakhs for Wealth Management
₹7.5 crore for Private Banking

Eligibility for Salary Family: Active Salary Accounts with ICICI Bank (Power Pay, Select Banking, Wealth Management, Private Banking).

Family members should hold a ICICI Bank Savings Account (Select Banking, Wealth Management or Private Banking) or a ICICI Bank Salary Account (PowerPay, Select Banking or Family 360 Banking, Wealth Management or Private Banking)
A minimum of 2 and a maximum of 10 members for Savings Account or 6 members for Salary Account can form a Family Banking relationship under a single group ID with ICICI Bank
Family members include parents, spouses, children, grandparents, grandchildren, in-laws and siblings. In case of Salary family, the relationships include parents, spouse and children.

Family Banking FAQs

What are the minimum and maximum number of family members who can form a Family ID?

A minimum of 2 and a maximum of 10 family members (including Primary member) can form a Family ID for Select Banking, Wealth Management and Private Banking families.

A minimum of 2 and a maximum of 6 family members (including Primary member) can form a Family ID for Salary Family.

Can two Savings Accounts be linked under a Family ID?

Yes, two Savings Account holders can form a Savings Account Family ID.

Which accounts are eligible to be linked under the Family ID?

Only Savings Account under Select Banking, Wealth Management and Private Banking Programmes and Salary Accounts under Power Pay, Select Banking, Wealth Management and Private Banking are eligible for Family Banking.

Can a new member be added to the Family ID later?

Yes, a new member can be added to the Family ID later. The Family Banking Form must be filled in and signed by the new entrant and primary member of the family.

Can I upgrade the Family ID to a higher programme at a later stage?

Primary member of the family can request for Family upgrade post which all members in the Family ID will be upgraded to respective programme.

Which Account holder can I nominate as the Primary Member?

Any member of the Family ID is eligible to become the Primary Member, except for minor, student and NRI Account holders.

How can one create a Family ID for Salary Family Banking?

If the Primary Member holds a Salary Account with ICICI Bank and wants to extend the same benefits to their family members, then they can create a Family ID by raising a request.

How many accounts can be added to the Family ID under Salary Family Banking?

6 members can be added to the Family ID under Salary Family Banking.

Who can be added to the Family ID under Salary Family Banking?

Parents, spouse and children can be added to the Family ID under Salary Family Banking.

What are the criteria for getting insurance benefits under Salary Family Banking?

To avail insurance benefits for the family members, there should be at least one salary credit in the last three months in the Primary Member’s Salary Account, prior to the date of loss.

What happens if salary stops getting credited to the Primary Member’s Account?

If salary stops getting credited to the Primary Member’s Account for the last 3 months, but the Program Eligibility Criteria is maintained, then the Family ID will be maintained. 

Can I add multiple family references in ICICI Bank Family Banking?

Yes, you can add multiple family references under ICICI Bank’s Family Banking program. This allows you to manage various family members’ accounts and banking services more effectively.
