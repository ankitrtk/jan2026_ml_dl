Why you should follow Safe Banking practices

Protect your money 24/7

Stay alert against frauds like phishing, parcel scams and investment traps. Awareness is your first line of defense.

Secure digital banking

Use strong passwords, avoid unknown links and transact only on trusted devices to keep Net and Mobile Banking safe.

Protect your data

Banks will never ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV, passwords or card details. Do not share them with anyone, even if they claim to be from the Bank to prevent unauthorized access.

Parcel Fraud: Beat the fear to beat the cheats

That “urgent” call about a parcel you never ordered is not your courier service, it is a scam.

Stay safe with these tips:

Police authorities will not contact you via video calls or video apps

Always verify with your local police

Never share confidential information

Never click links from unknown sources

Investment fraud: Beat the greed to beat the cheats

That magical investment promising sky-high returns is a trap.

Stay alert with these tips:

Do not trust unsolicited advertisements or investment groups

Never click links from unknown sources

Do not link your Bank Account with unknown apps

Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor
