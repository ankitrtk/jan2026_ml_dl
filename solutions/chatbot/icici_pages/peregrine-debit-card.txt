Peregrine Business Debit Card FAQs

What makes the ICICI Bank Peregrine Debit Card unique?

Designed for consumers of Private Banking, this premium debit card offers unique deals on dining, travel, and shopping. It offers higher transaction limits, complimentary access to airport lounges, fuel fee waivers, and reward points.

What daily transaction and withdrawal restrictions exist on this card?

From ATMs, you can withdraw up to ₹1,50,000 daily; spend up to ₹7,50,000 daily at retail stores and online throughout India. The daily purchasing limit for foreign transactions is ₹2,00,000.

Will I earn rewards by using this card?

Using your Peregrine Debit Card, you get 2 ICICI Bank Reward Points for every ₹200 spent on qualified transactions. The rewards program does not consider payments, including mutual funds, utilities, government services, and wallets.

* 
How many airport lounge visits do I get each quarter with the ICICI Bank Peregrine Business Debit Card?

For Current Accounts opened on or before 31st July 2025, you get a maximum of 6 domestic airport lounge visits each quarter. For Current Accounts opened on or after 1st August 2025, you get a maximum of 6 domestic airport lounge visits each quarter, with minimum spends of Rs. 10,000 quarterly or meeting program eligibility of deposits & balances of Rs. 50 Lakhs quarterly  or Relationship value of ₹5 Cr (Deposits + Account balance + Investments + Demat holdings) quarterly. Spends cycle: Spends incurred between 1st - 31st of the 3 months of a calendar quarter will be considered and lounge access will be provided starting 5th of the next month following the quarter end.

Does this card have any exclusive offers?

Yes! Exclusive Peregrine Debit Card deals include dining, travel, lodging, and shopping. To explore the best offers, simply visit www.icici.bank.in.

Will there be alerts for transactions done through the card?

Indeed, every transaction performed at a merchant using your card will get instant SMS alerts on your registered telephone number. This provides protection and facilitates the simple tracking of your expenses.

Is this card suitable for international use?

Yes, you can use this card anywhere. Perfect for regular travellers or overseas business needs, it allows daily purchases up to ₹2,00,000 and international cash withdrawals up to ₹1,50,000.
