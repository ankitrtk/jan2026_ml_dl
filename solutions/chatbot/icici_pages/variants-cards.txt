Need help finding a card?

Cards FAQs

What types of cards are offered by ICICI Bank?

ICICI Bank provides a variety of Cards, including Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Prepaid Cards, Travel Cards and Commercial Cards. Every Card is designed to meet specific financial needs and offer unique benefits.

How can I apply for an ICICI Bank Credit Card?

Applying for an ICICI Bank Credit Card is easy. Visit our website, select your preferred Credit Card and fill in the required details. You can also apply through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app or visit any ICICI Bank branch.

Note: Only pre-approved customers are eligible to apply through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

What are the benefits of ICICI Bank Debit Cards?

ICICI Bank Debit Cards offer secure cashless payments, online banking convenience and exclusive offers on shopping, dining and travel. They’re also linked directly to your account for easy management of your account balance.

Can I use ICICI Bank Travel Cards internationally?

Yes, ICICI Bank Travel Cards are designed specifically for international use, pre-loaded with up to 15 major currencies. This makes it convenient for travellers to make secure payments when abroad.
