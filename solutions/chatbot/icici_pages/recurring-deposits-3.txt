About Recurring Deposits

What is a Recurring Deposit (RD)?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) with ICICI Bank is a savings solution designed for those who want to grow their wealth systematically. Invest small amounts monthly and enjoy attractive interest rates.

Benefits of Opening a Recurring Deposit with ICICI Bank

Low initial deposit
: Start with just ₹500 per month.

Flexible tenures
: Choose from 6 months – 10 years.

Goal-based savings
: Plan your goals with no penalties using iWish.

Attractive returns
: Earn competitive interest rates on your deposits.

Easy loan facility
: Avail loans against your RD balance.

Flexible tenure options

Recurring Deposits allow you to save for periods ranging from six months – ten years. You can extend your investment tenure in multiples of three months, offering you complete control over your savings plan.

Hassle-free setup

Opening a Recurring Deposit is quick and easy. Use the iMobile app, Net Banking, Customer Care, or visit a branch to start saving today.

iWish – Goal-based savings

Enjoy greater flexibility with iWish, our goal-based savings tool:

Save as per your convenience with no penalties for missed payments.

Set and achieve your financial goals with ease.

Documents Required to Open a Recurring Deposit

Provide any of the following documents as identity and proof of address:

Passport

Aadhaar

Voter ID

Utility Bills (as communication proof)

Ensure you submit originals for verification along with photocopies.

Taxation on Recurring Deposits

Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) is applicable on interest accrued on recurring deposits. Deductions are made on September 30, March 31, and at maturity as per prevailing laws.

Recurring Deposit FAQs

What is a recurring deposit?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is an investment product where you deposit fixed amounts monthly and earn attractive interest over a chosen tenure.

Is the interest earned on my RD taxable?

Yes, TDS is applicable on accrued interest as per law. Tax is deducted on September 30, March 31, and on maturity.

What are the minimum and maximum tenures for RDs?

The tenure ranges from 6 months – 10 years, with increments in multiples of 3 months.

How much interest can I earn through an RD?

Interest rates are similar to fixed deposits. 
Click here
 to check the latest rates.

Who is eligible to open an RD with ICICI Bank?

Resident Indians can open an RD account.

How do I manage my RD account?

You can manage your RD using ICICI Bank's iMobile app, Net Banking, Customer Care, or by visiting a branch.
