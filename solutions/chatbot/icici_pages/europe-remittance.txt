FAQs

How long does it take to transfer money from Europe to India?

For an ICICI Bank recipient account: Funds are transferred presumably within 30 minutes, if no unforeseen issues occur, when you choose the Fixed Rate option. This service operates 24x7 to ensure your recipient receives funds quickly.

Other bank transfers may take 1-2 business days.

What are the transfer charges?

Minimal charges apply, with no hidden costs.

Can I choose my exchange rate?

Yes, you can opt for Fixed or Floating exchange rates.

With Fixed rate option, the exchange rate is confirmed as soon as you make the payment using a payment gateway. When you select Fixed rate transfer, the Bank’s exchange rate prevailing at that time is shown to you and the same rate is applied for converting your funds into Indian Rupee. Always note the exchange rate mentioned there – “You get what you see”.

With Floating rate option, once you initiate a transaction and get a transaction number starting with G2NXXXXXXXX, you are required to transfer the funds to Money2India Europe's account via a Bank transfer. While initiating the transfer request, an indicative exchange rate is shown with the indicative total amount you will get in INR for your Euros. When the Bank has received your funds, the actual exchange rate prevailing at the time of conversion would be applied and the actual amount transferred in INR may be different from the amount that was shown to you at the time of initiating the transfer.

How do I track my transfer?

Log in to the Money2India Europe portal or app to check your transaction status anytime.

What are the available funding options?

You can fund your transfer directly from your German Bank Account.

Do I need an ICICI Bank account to use Money2India Europe?

No, account holders of all banks can use this digital platform.

Which all countries can Money2India Europe be used in?

Post Brexit, new customer onboarding is available only for customers living in Germany. Only existing customers of Money2India Europe in Austria, Belgium, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, France, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, onboarded prior to Brexit continue to be served.
