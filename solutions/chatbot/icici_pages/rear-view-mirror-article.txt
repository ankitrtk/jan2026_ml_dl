YOUR GOLDEN YEARS ARE CLOSER THAN THEY APPEAR!

Ever read the message on the side-view mirror of your car? It says, "Objects may be closer than they appear". What if we said, retirement too may be closer than it appears?

If life is like a long drive, full of potholes and expressways... Then retirement is the stretch where you can finally shift into auto-pilot. When you sit back and enjoy the view with your life's co-passengers.

SO ARE YOU PREPARED TO HIT THE ROAD

TOWARDS RETIREMENT?

Let the fuel in your tank take you further.

No matter what your income level is, start investing early. Your wealth has the capacity to grow exponentially, over time. How? With the power of compounding i.e. reinvesting your interest earned from various investments, to earn a greater interest on it.

Don't put your assets into one compartment.

Diversify your investments. Create a portfolio that ranges from Mutual Funds to Stocks to FDs, to increase the returns while mitigating risks. Systematically recurring these investments will feed your corpus.

Don't let problems press the brakes on your plans.

Generate a contingency fund to prepare for all scenarios. Consolidate important information and passwords into 
iLocker
, to make them accessible to your family during an emergency.

Shift into a gear that's right for you.

One size doesn't fit all when it comes to investments. Actively optimise your portfolio based on your lifestyle, investment & retirement personality. Maximise tax benefits as you go along.

Remember that you're in the driver's seat.

You and only you are in control of your fiscal future. Keep track of your wealth with a single-view monitoring. Our expert advisors are on-call if you need any assistance.

Turn the road to retirement into a bump-free highway, With ICICI Bank’s Golden Years Programme!
