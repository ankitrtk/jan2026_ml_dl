FAQs

What is Branch Banking for NRIs?

Branch Banking allows NRIs to access banking services through ICICI Bank’s extensive network of branches across India. You can manage accounts, perform transactions, and meet your branch manager when in India.

What services can I access through ICICI Bank branches?

You can avail a wide range of services, including:

Account opening and management

Cash deposits and withdrawals

Cheque services and demand drafts

Account linking and nominee updates

In-person assistance from relationship managers

Submission of service request forms

Repatriation-related queries and support

How can I find the nearest ICICI Bank branch or ATM?

You can search for a branch/ATM online by location or required facility using our Branch Locator. 
Click here
 to find the nearest ATM/branch.

How can I update my contact details while in India?

You can update your contact details:

By visiting the nearest ICICI Bank branch

Or contacting customer support

Can I track bank documents sent to me while abroad?

Yes. Use iTrack via Internet Banking or iMobile app to track dispatched bank items.

Where can I find the holiday list of a particular branch?

You can view the holiday list for any ICICI Bank branch by visiting the branch holiday section online. This helps in planning your visit on working days.

Where can I view ICICI Bank’s NRI FD interest rates?

You can check the latest interest rates for NRE, NRO, and FCNR Fixed Deposits on the ICICI Bank website. Rates vary depending on the currency and tenure. 
Click here
 for the latest NRI FD Rates.

What are the service request options available for NRI customer and their service channels?

ICICI Bank offers a wide range of NRI service requests to help you manage your accounts efficiently:

Account linking and updation

Fixed Deposit creation or closure

Repatriation and fund transfer requests

Address or contact detail changes

Request for TDS certificates, account statements and more

You can initiate these through Internet Banking, the iMobile app or the NRI Service Request page 
Click here
.

What customer support options are available globally for NRIs?

ICICI Bank offers multiple contact channels to assist customers worldwide:

Toll-free numbers in countries like the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore and more

Email support via 
nri@icicibank.com

Dedicated phone banking and NRI relationship managers

Branches in India and key global locations for in-person support
