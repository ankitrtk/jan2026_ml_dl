RTGS

Transfer High-Value Funds Instantly with RTGS

ICICI Bank’s Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) service enables fast, secure, and real-time transfer of large-value funds across banks in India.

Transfer via RTGS

What are the benefits of RTGS?

Real-Time Settlement

Funds are credited to the beneficiary within 30 minutes.

Ideal for High-Value Transfers

Minimum transfer amount is ₹2 lakh perfect for large payments.

Available Anytime, Anywhere

Use Internet Banking or iMobile to transfer from anywhere 24x7.

No Online Charges

RTGS via digital channels is free of transaction charges.

How does it work?

Internet Banking & iMobile app

Log in to Internet Banking or the iMobile app

Add a beneficiary using account number and IFSC

Choose RTGS as the transfer mode

Enter amount and confirm with Grid Card Authentication (GCA).

Funds are credited in real time (within 30 minutes)

Did you know?

RTGS is perfect for urgent, high-value transfers such as property payments, business settlements, or bulk transfers. You can also schedule your transaction up to 1 working day in advance for better planning and control

RTGS FAQs

What is RTGS?

Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) is a payment system that allows real-time and individual settlement of high-value fund transfers through the RBI network.

What are the timings for RTGS?

RTGS is available 24x7, including weekends and holidays, via digital channels.

Are there any charges for using RTGS

RTGS via digital platforms is free. However, transactions at branches incur a nominal charge.

What is the minimum amount required for RTGS?

The minimum transaction amount is ₹2 lakh.

What details are needed for an RTGS transfer?

You need the beneficiary's name, bank name, account number, and IFSC code.

Can I schedule RTGS transactions in advance?

Yes, you can schedule RTGS transactions up to 1 working day in advance.

What if I enter the wrong account number?

Funds are credited solely based on the account number. ICICI Bank recommends double-checking details before confirming a transaction.

What if the funds are not credited to the beneficiary?

If the transaction fails, the funds are returned to your account within an hour by the beneficiary bank.

Is RTGS available at all bank branches?

No, only RTGS-enabled branches offer this service. You can check the list on the 
RBI website.

How is RTGS different from NEFT?

Unlike NEFT (which settles transactions in batches), RTGS settles transactions individually and in real-time, making it faster for large-value transfers.
