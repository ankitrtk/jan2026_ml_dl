Expressions Coral Debit Card FAQs

How can I get an Expressions Coral Debit Card? 

You can upgrade the Card via iMobile app or Internet Banking or you can visit any ICICI Bank Branch where a branch representative will guide you. 

Do I get any offers or vouchers while upgrading or enrolling for the Expressions Coral Debit Card?  

Yes, you will get the joining benefits which are dynamic in nature and you will earn vouchers on getting this Card. 

In what ways can I activate my Expressions Coral Debit Card? 

You can activate your Expressions Coral Debit Card through the iMobile app, Internet Banking, Customer Care (1800 1080) or by visiting any ICICI Bank Branch. 

What are the most notable Expressions Coral Debit Card features?  

The high transaction limits, movie and dining discounts, spend-based complimentary airport lounge access and Reward Points on every spend are some of the features of the Expressions Coral Debit Card. 

Can I customise the display picture on my Expressions Coral Debit Card? 

You can choose the image displayed on your Expressions Coral Debit Card from a gallery of <200+> images or you can upload your picture.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹10,000 or more on your Expressions Coral Debit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.
