Eligibility Criteria for a ₹10 Lakh Personal Loan

To apply for a Rs. 10 lakh Personal Loan, you must meet specific criteria:

Age: Generally, applicants should be between 20 and 58 years of age

Employment: Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and business owners are eligible

Income: A steady income but the minimum amount may vary across lenders

Credit Score: A low CIBIL Score may affect Personal Loan approval and securing favourable interest rates. A good credit score is crucial.

FAQs on Personal Loans of ₹10 Lakh

Why should I choose a ₹10 lakh Personal Loan?

A ₹10 lakh Personal Loan is unsecured, meaning you do not need collateral. It offers flexibility, quick access to funds and repayment tenures of up to 6 years.

What are the eligibility criteria for a ₹10 lakh loan?

Applicants must typically be between 20 and 58 years old, have a stable income, and maintain a good credit score. Salaried, self-employed professionals and business owners are eligible.

What documents are required for a ₹10 lakh loan?

Identity proof, address proof, bank statements and income proof such as salary slips or income tax returns are generally required.

How much EMI will I pay on a ₹10 lakh loan?

EMIs depend on interest rates and tenure. For example, at 10.75 percent interest for 5 years, the approximate EMI is ₹21,544.
