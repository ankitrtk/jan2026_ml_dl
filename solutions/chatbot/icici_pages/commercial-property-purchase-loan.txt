About NRI Commercial Property Purchase Loan

What is an NRI Commercial Property Purchase Loan?

An NRI Commercial Property Purchase Loan enables NRIs/OCIs to finance the purchase of commercial property in India for self-use. It offers repo-linked interest rates, flexible repayment tenures up to 15 years, attractive terms, minimal documentation, and online sanction, helping buyers secure office or retail premises easily.

Benefits of NRI Commercial Property Purchase Loan

Easy access to funds for purchasing commercial properties.

Flexible repayment tenures.

Attractive interest rates for NRIs.

Online application process with minimal paperwork.

Can I use the loan for any type of commercial property?

Yes, you can use the loan to purchase office spaces, retail properties, or industrial properties in India.

How long is the loan tenure?

The loan tenure can be up to 15 years, depending on your eligibility. Longer tenures provide more flexibility in repayment.

FAQs on NRI Commercial Property Purchase Loan

Can an NRI/OCI apply for a Commercial Property Purchase Loan in India?

Yes, both NRI and OCI individuals can apply for a Commercial Property Purchase Loan in India.

What are the eligibility criteria for this loan?

The loan is available for NRI/OCI individuals. The applicant must have a valid passport, NRI status proof (work visa/residence permit/OCI card), and the required KYC documents. Specific income and business proof will be needed depending on your employment status (salaried or self-employed).

What is the tenure and repayment option for the loan?

The loan offers flexible tenures of up to 15 years, allowing easy EMIs. You can also avail of a balance transfer facility from an existing loan with another lender.

Can I get a top-up on my existing loan?

Top up loan over and above originally sanctioned amount is available. You can also transfer existing loan from other financers and avail top up on the same. (Subject to further assessment)

 Do I need a ICICI Bank NRE/NRO account for availing the loan?

Yes, NRE/NRO account is mandatory for repayment.

Customers from which countries are required to submit overseas credit report?

An Overseas Credit Report ( not older than 45 days )must be submitted for NRI/OCI customers belonging to USA, UK, Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, Ireland, South Africa, Oman. For other countries, it is not mandatory.
