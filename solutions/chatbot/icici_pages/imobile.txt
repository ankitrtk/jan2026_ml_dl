FAQs

Is iMobile free to use?

Yes, ICICI Bank’s iMobile app is free to download and use. Standard data charges may apply as per your mobile network.

Can I use iMobile without internet?

No, internet connectivity is required to access iMobile banking services.

How secure is the iMobile app?

The app is protected with two-factor authentication, PIN or fingerprint login and advanced encryption for safe banking.

Can I access iMobile on any smartphone?

Yes, iMobile is available on both Android and iOS platforms.

What if I forget my iMobile PIN?

You can reset it by verifying your registered mobile number and debit card credentials.
