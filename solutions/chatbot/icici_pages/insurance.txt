About the product

Life Insurance

Offers a financial safety net for your family through term, ULIP, and endowment plans. Helps build long-term wealth and plan for key milestones.

General Insurance

Protects health, assets and travel through comprehensive policies. Covers medical expenses, vehicle damage, home protection and more.

Pradhan Mantri Social Security Schemes

Government-backed schemes:

PMSBY: ₹2 lakh accidental death cover at ₹12 per year

PMJJBY: ₹2 lakh life cover at ₹330 per year 

Convenient Online Purchase

Buy insurance online at your convenience from ICICI Bank’s secure platform.

Trusted Partnerships

Insurance FAQs

How to download my insurance policy?

For queries or complaints related to ICICI Lombard, contact ICICI Lombard on 18002098888 (Toll-Free) or write to customersupport@icicilombard.com. For more details, you may visit the ICICI Lombard website, 
www.icicilombard.com
.

For queries or complaints related to ICICI Prulife, you may contact ICICI Prulife on 1800222020 (Toll-Free) or write to lifeline@iciciprulife.com. 

For more details, you may visit the ICICI Prulife website,
www.iciciprulife.com
.

What affects Health Insurance premiums? 

Premiums depend on your age, sum insured, and health profile.

Why do insurers ask for health details? 

Health details help assess risk and ensure fair pricing and claims processing.

Can I buy multiple insurance products? 

Yes. You can combine life, general, and government insurance for full coverage.
