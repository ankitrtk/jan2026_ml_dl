Demat Account Service Request Forms

You can download the 
Demat Account opening form
 and submit it at our demat-enabled branches along with the required KYC documents.

There is no fee for opening a 
Demat Account
 with ICICI Bank. However, a nominal fee will be levied based on services availed by you. Please refer to our 
service charges
 for details.
