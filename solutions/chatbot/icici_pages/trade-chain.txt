About

Easy customer eligibility

TradeChain is available for registered business customers with Corporate Internet Banking access. It enables secure, paperless trade transactions for domestic businesses that want efficient working capital management.

Simple digital documentation

Most trade transactions on TradeChain require minimal paperwork. Customers can submit and manage trade documents digitally from LC issuance to Bill presentation and financing making the process faster and fully auditable.

Flexible trade lifecycle

Clients can manage Letter of Credit transactions on one platform. From raising requests to tracking real-time status, presenting bills under LC and requesting finance, the entire journey stays seamless and paperless.

Always-on transaction access

TradeChain operates 24x7 for transaction initiation and tracking. Processing times may vary depending on transaction type, but real-time alerts and updates keep customers informed at every step.

FAQs

What is TradeChain?

TradeChain is a digital end-to-end Blockchain solution for managing domestic trade transactions under a Letter of Credit. It covers the entire trade cycle from submitting LC requeststo presenting documents and initiating financing requests all digitally.

Who can be onboarded on TradeChain?

Existing ICICI Bank customers who have a Current Account with the Bank can be onboarded.

How are clients onboarded on the TradeChain platform?

Clients need to provide a simple Onboarding Form to avail the platform. For more details, please contact your nearest branch or connect with your Account Manager.

Is physical document submission required on TradeChain?

No, there is no requirement for physical document submission. The entire process is digital.

How can customers track their transactions on TradeChain?

Clients receive an email at each stage for all transactions. The same can also be tracked on the TradeChain platform.
