Corporate Credit Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Card?

The ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Card addresses organisational financial needs as well as personnel requirements during business travel. It is designed to offer businesses a complete option for handling travel and entertainment (T&E) costs.

What distinguishes the ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Card from other cards?

The ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Card distinguishes itself from others regarding the benefits offered. It includes up to 50 days of interest-free credit, direct savings via a cash rebate programme, improved spending visibility for tracking and compliance and automated electronic uploads into your Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system.  

In what ways does the Corporate Credit Card India assist in employee cost control?

It gives companies tools for improved tracking and compliance, helping them monitor and manage employee T&E costs. 

Is there a fuel surcharge applicable with the Corporate Credit Card?

Yes, the card offers a 1% of the transaction amount or ₹10 fuel cost waiver. Whichever is higher. 

Are there any Corporate Credit Card joining or membership fees?

When you apply for a corporate credit card, a joining fee is not applicable for either the platinum or gold variant. However, an annual fee of ₹999 + GST is applicable for the platinum card's annual membership.

Can our ERP system be connected with the Corporate Credit Card?

Yes, the card offers electronic uploads and automation into your Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system, so it helps with smooth spending control. 

How is the cash rebates programme set up?

The cash rebate programme directly saves money for your company through refunds on qualified transactions made with the Corporate Credit Card.

Where can I apply for the Corporate Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

You can apply for the Corporate Credit Card through the official ICICI Bank website or ask your Relationship Manager for help.

What are the fees and charges for ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Cards?

Fees vary by Corporate Credit Card type and usage. Refer to the 
Fees and Charges
 page for detailed information on applicable fees and other charges.

Who is eligible for an ICICI Bank Corporate Credit Card?

Corporate Credit Cards are issued to authorised employees of eligible organisations, subject to company approval and internal assessment by ICICI Bank.
