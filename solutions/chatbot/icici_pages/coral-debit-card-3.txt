Coral Business Debit Card FAQS

How do I get the Coral Business Debit Card?

You can apply online on the ICICI Bank website or at any branch. Contact the bank's customer service if you prefer to switch from your existing debit card to the Coral Business Debit Card.

Should I pay an annual and joining fee for the Coral Business Debit Card?

The debit card attracts an annual fee of ₹699 + GST from the second year. You are charged ₹699 + GST as a joining fee.

How do I earn my Coral Business Debit Card reward points?

You earn reward points on all qualifying purchases made using the card, both domestically and internationally. These points can be redeemed for Coral Business Debit Card discount offers, gifts, exclusive discounts and among other special rewards.

Can I use the Coral Business Debit Card overseas?

Yes, you can withdraw cash and make purchases abroad using the Coral Business Debit Card.

What are the security features of the Coral Business Debit Card?

To facilitate secure transactions, the card has advanced security features, such as EMV chip technology, secure PIN protection and 24/7 fraud monitoring.

Are there any transaction limits on transactions I can make with the Coral Business Debit Card?

Yes, there are transaction and withdrawal limits, but they are higher than those of regular debit cards.

How do I track my Coral Business Debit Card expenses?

You can track your transactions easily through ICICI Bank's mobile banking app, net banking, or monthly statements to get detailed information about your spending.

Is the Coral Business Debit Card contactless-enabled?

Yes, the Coral Business Debit Card is contactless-enabled, allowing quicker and easier transactions with a tap.

One of the key Coral Business Debit Card benefits is the convenience of contactless payments, which enhance both speed and security for business transactions.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹10,000 or more on your Coral Business Debit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.
