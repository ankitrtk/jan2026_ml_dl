Personal Loan for Home Renovation FAQs

Is there a loan available for home renovation?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers Personal Loan for Home Renovation   specifically aimed at home remodelling, repairs and upgrades.

What is a Personal Loan for Home Renovation?

A Personal Loan for Home Renovation is a Personal Loan by ICICI Bank intended to finance home upgrades, repairs and remodelling. With loan amounts up to ₹50 lakh and flexible repayment terms, it is the perfect solution for enhancing your home without financial strain.

What are the eligibility criteria for personal loan for home renovation?

To be eligible, the applicant must be an Indian citizen in the age group of 20–58 years, with a minimum monthly income of ₹30,000. The minimum salary requirement will differ depending on the profile of the applicant – for example: the type of employer, whether the applicant has an existing relationship with ICICI Bank etc.

Why choose ICICI Bank for this loan?

ICICI Bank offers attractive interest rates, flexible repayment options and a hassle-free application process.

What is the maximum tenure for a personal loan for home renovation?

The loan tenure ranges from 12 to 72 months, allowing borrowers to select a plan that suits their financial capabilities.

How much can I borrow for a personal loan for home renovation?

You can get a 
Personal Loan
 ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹50 lakh, depending on your income, credit history. Click here to check your 
Personal Loan eligibility
.

What is the interest rate of a personal loan for home renovation?

ICICI Bank offers attractive interest rates based on your credit profile and repayment tenure. Specific rates can be discussed with the Bank's representatives.

Is any collateral required for personal loan for home improvement?

No, ICICI Bank Home Renovation Loans do not require any collateral. Therefore, no security or guarantor is required.

How quickly can I get home improvement loans?

Personal Loans can be availed for home improvement. Personal Loan disbursement process is a hassle free digital process where in a customer can apply for Personal Loan digitally through ICICI Bank iMobile app or Net Banking. If the customer has a pre-approved Personal Loan offer, the loan is usually disbursed within 2 hrs without any documentation. However, otherwise, the loan is processed within one working day.

Are there any tax benefits of a home improvement loan?

No, there are no tax benefits of a Personal Loan for home improvement.
