About Car Insurance

What does car insurance cover?

Natural calamities: Any kind of loss happened due to fire, explosion, self-ignition, lightning, earthquakes, floods, typhoons, hurricanes, storms, cyclones, hailstorms, frost, landslides and rockslides.

Man-made calamities: Damage due to man-made issues like burglary, theft, riots, strikes, malicious acts, terrorism, and transit damage by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator or air.

Personal accident cover: Up to ₹15 Lakhs coverage for the driver while traveling, mounting or dismounting from the vehicle, along with optional coverage for co-passengers.

Third party legal liability: Protection against legal liabilities for permanent injury or death of individuals and damage to surrounding property.

No claim bonus (NCB): Transferable if you haven’t made claims on your previous policy, valid within 90 days of renewal, with proof of entitlement

Policy Exclusions

Exclusions in Car Insurance Coverage

Normal wear and tear and vehicle aging

Depreciation or consequential loss

Mechanical or electrical breakdowns

Damage to tyres and tubes (limited to 50% of replacement cost if vehicle is damaged simultaneously)

Usage contrary to policy limitations

Any kind of damage occurred due to drivers without a valid license or somebody under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Loss due to war, mutiny or nuclear risk.

Features and Advantages

Roadside Assistance: Get help on the spot for vehicle breakdowns or emergencies.

Cashless Service: Enjoy hassle-free claims without upfront payments at 13000 network garages.

Instant Policy: Receive your insurance coverage immediately upon purchase.

Renewal: Easily renew your policy online to maintain continuous coverage.

Depreciation Cover: Protect against depreciation loss on your vehicle during claims.

Retain Existing Policy: Keep your current policy benefits when switching providers.

Allowance: Receive daily allowances for transportation or lodging during claims processing.

Car Insurance FAQs

What Does Car Insurance Cover?

Car insurance of ICICI Lombard sourced by ICICI Bank provides protection against:

Natural Calamities

Man-made Calamities

Personal Accident Cover: Up to ₹15 Lakhs coverage

Third Party Legal Liability

No Claim Bonus (NCB): valid within 90 days of renewal 

Is Zero Depreciation worth it? 

Yes. It ensures no value reduction on replaced car parts. 

Can I switch insurers and retain my No Claim Bonus?

Yes, provided you haven’t made any claims. Submit your NCB proof. No Claim Bonus (NCB): valid within 90 days of renewal 

What documents are required to make a claim? 

 You’ll need the claim form, RC, DL, insurance copy, and an FIR if needed. 

Is roadside assistance included? 

Yes, if you’ve opted for the add-on cover.

Why do I need to insure my vehicle?

Your vehicle is probably one of the most expensive things you own. Insurance protects your vehicle and helps you cope with the financial loss caused by accidents, damage or theft. Another reason is that while driving, you are responsible for the safety of:

Your passengers

Your fellow drivers

Other people's property

Pedestrians

Yourself

Insurance helps cover the costs of potential damages or injuries in case of an unforeseen accident or theft. In India, it is mandatory to have at least third-party motor insurance before driving on the roads.

What are the benefits of buying a Car Insurance online?

When you buy Motor Insurance online, you get an instant policy, as there is no documentation or paperwork involved. In addition, you have the advantage of choosing from multiple payment options e.g. Credit Card (Visa, Master Amex card), Net banking, Debit Card, etc.
