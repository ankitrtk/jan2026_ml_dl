Business Debit Cards FAQs

What is an ICICI Business Debit Card?

The Business Debit Card is a handy tool for organizations, business owners and authorised personnel to make business payments. The ICICI Bank Business Debit Card is linked to a business account and not a personal one. Debit cards are also used as a company ATM card and enable easy cash withdrawal from the company account. You can use the card for online payments on merchant websites and at retail shops.

What are the eligibility criteria for an ICICI Business Debit Card?

A business owner or an authorized signatory can apply for a Business Debit Card for an existing business account in ICICI Bank.

Is the ICICI Bank Business Debit Card free?

Business Debit Cards can have enrollment fees, renewal fees, ATM cash withdrawal fees and foreign exchange fees for international transactions. Kindly confirm the current fees with your local ICICI Bank branch or customer care.

What is the process for applying for a Business Debit Card in ICICI Bank?

You can apply in person at your local ICICI Bank branch and submit the application form along with the necessary supporting documents or apply online through the Bank's portal.

Can you fix a spending limit for your ICICI Business debit card?

Yes, spending limits can be fixed for the Business Debit Card based on the cardholder's requirements.

Can I use my ICICI Business Debit Card abroad?

Yes, you can perform international transactions with the ICICI Business Debit Card. However, it is advisable to check with ICICI about foreign transaction charges and whether a specific payment network abroad, such as Visa or Mastercard, is facilitated.

How can I report a lost or stolen ICICI Business Debit Card?

For a lost or stolen Card, report it immediately through a phone call to ICICI Bank's customer care. You can report a loss through the portal or the ICICI Bank's iMobile app to block your Card from further unintended usage.

How can I check my balance and transaction details for my ICICI Business Debit Card?

You may view your balance and transaction history through ICICI website or ICICI Bank's iMobile app or opt for an SMS alert on transactions.
