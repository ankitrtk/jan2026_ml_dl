Loan Against Property FAQs

Loan Against Property FAQs

          Search FAQs

 Basic information 

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

ICICI Bank Loan Against Property is a secured loan where you mortgage your residential, commercial, or industrial property with ICICI Bank to raise funds for personal or business needs.

                            Who can apply for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, business owners, and NRIs who own eligible property in India and meet ICICI Bank’s eligibility criteria can apply.

                            What can ICICI Bank Loan Against Property be used for?

ICICI Bank Loan Against Property can be used for business expansion, education expenses, medical emergencies, weddings, travel, debt consolidation, or other personal or business requirements.

                            What types of properties are accepted under ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

ICICI Bank Loan Against Property is available against residential, commercial, or industrial properties with a clear and marketable title.

                            How much loan can I get under ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

The loan amount depends on the property’s market value, your income profile, repayment capacity, and ICICI Bank’s assessment.

Eligibility and documentation

                EXPAND ALL

                            What are the eligibility criteria for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Eligibility is based on age, income stability, credit history, repayment capacity, and ownership of eligible property.

                            Can NRIs apply for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, NRIs who own property in India can apply for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property, subject to ICICI Bank’s NRI eligibility and documentation norms.

                            What documents are required for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Documents may include KYC documents, income proof, bank statements, and property ownership documents, as required by ICICI Bank.

                            Is property valuation mandatory for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, ICICI Bank conducts a property valuation before approving the Loan Against Property.

                            Can I add a co-applicant to ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, a co-applicant such as a spouse or family member can be added, subject to ICICI Bank’s assessment.

                            How are interest rates decided for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Interest rates are based on the borrower’s profile, loan amount, tenure, and ICICI Bank’s prevailing lending rates.

                            Are interest rates fixed or floating for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

ICICI Bank Loan Against Property is generally offered at floating interest rates linked to applicable benchmarks.

Interest rate, fees and charges

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is there a processing fee for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, ICICI Bank charges a processing fee along with applicable taxes, as per its schedule of charges.

                            Are prepayment or foreclosure charges applicable on ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Prepayment or foreclosure charges, if applicable, are disclosed by ICICI Bank at the time of loan sanction.

                            Does ICICI Bank disclose all charges upfront?

Yes, ICICI Bank provides complete transparency on interest rates, fees, and applicable charges before loan approval.

Repayment and tenure

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is the maximum tenure for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

ICICI Bank Loan Against Property offers flexible repayment tenure of up to 15 years, subject to eligibility.

                            How is EMI calculated for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

EMIs are calculated based on the loan amount, applicable interest rate, and chosen tenure.

                            Can I make partial prepayments on ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, partial prepayment is allowed, subject to ICICI Bank’s applicable terms.

                            What happens if I miss an EMI payment?

Missing an EMI may attract penal charges and can negatively impact your credit score.

                            Can the tenure be changed after sanction of ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Tenure modification requests may be considered by ICICI Bank based on eligibility and repayment history.

Security, custody and safety

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is ICICI Bank Loan Against Property a secured loan?

Yes, ICICI Bank Loan Against Property is a secured loan backed by the property mortgaged with the bank.

                            Does ICICI Bank retain original property documents?

Yes, ICICI Bank retains custody of original property documents until the loan is fully repaid.

                            Are customer data and transactions safe with ICICI Bank?

Yes, ICICI Bank follows RBI-regulated security standards to safeguard customer data and transactions.

                            Can the mortgaged property be sold during the ICICI Bank Loan Against Property tenure?

The property can be sold only after clearing outstanding dues and obtaining a No Objection Certificate from ICICI Bank.

Service request and account management 

                EXPAND ALL

                            How can I track my ICICI Bank Loan Against Property application status?

You can track your ICICI Bank Loan Against Property application through Net Banking, the iMobile App, or ICICI Bank Customer Care.

                            What is the processing time for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

The processing time depends on documentation and verification and is generally completed within a few working days after submission of complete documents.

                            What is the ICICI Bank Loan Against Property disbursement process?

After approval and completion of legal and technical checks, ICICI Bank disburses the Loan Against Property amount to the borrower’s account as per agreed terms.

                            Can I request changes after sanction of ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, service requests such as repayment account change or tenure modification can be raised, subject to ICICI Bank policies.

Calculator and tools

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is there an EMI calculator for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, ICICI Bank provides an online EMI calculator to estimate monthly instalments for Loan Against Property.

                            Can I check my eligibility for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property online?

Yes, ICICI Bank’s digital tools and branch support help customers estimate eligibility and EMIs.

                            Are property-value-based calculators available for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers digital tools to assess eligibility based on property value and borrower profile.

Customer Services

                EXPAND ALL

                            How can I contact ICICI Bank for ICICI Bank Loan Against Property queries?

You can contact ICICI Bank via Customer Care, Net Banking, the iMobile App, or by visiting an ICICI Bank branch.

                            Does ICICI Bank provide end-to-end assistance for Loan Against Property?

Yes, ICICI Bank supports customers throughout the Loan Against Property lifecycle, from application to closure.

Closure

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I close ICICI Bank Loan Against Property before the tenure ends?

Yes, early closure of ICICI Bank Loan Against Property is allowed after clearing all outstanding dues.

                            Are foreclosure charges applicable on ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

Foreclosure charges, if applicable, are as per ICICI Bank’s prevailing loan terms.

Miscellaneous

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is the maximum loan amount under ICICI Bank Loan Against Property?

The maximum loan amount depends on property value, income profile, credit history, and ICICI Bank’s assessment.

                            Does ICICI Bank Loan Against Property affect my credit score?

Yes, timely repayment can improve your credit score, while delays or defaults may negatively impact your credit profile.
