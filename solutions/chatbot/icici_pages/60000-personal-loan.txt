About ₹60,000 Personal Loan

Eligibility Criteria for a ₹60,000 Personal Loan

To get a Rs. 60,000 
Personal Loan
, you must meet the following eligibility criteria:

For salaried individuals:

Age: Between 20 to 58 years

Net salary: Minimum monthly income of Rs. 30,000

Total years. in the job/profession: A minimum of 2 years in the current employment

Years in the current residence: Residing at the current address for at least 1 year.

For self-employed individuals:

Age: Minimum 28 years for self-employed individuals, 25 years for doctors and a maximum age of 65 years

Minimum turnover: Rs. 40 lakh for non-professionals and Rs. 15 lakh for professionals

Minimum profit after tax: Rs. 2 lakh for a Proprietorship firm/Self-employed individuals and Rs. 1 lakh for non-professionals

Business stability: At least 5 years in the current business for non-professionals and 3 years for doctors

Existing relationship with ICICI Bank: Minimum 1 year liability relationship (Current or Savings Account) or Asset Relationship (loan) either live or closed in the last 36 months, with a satisfactory repayment track as required.

FAQs on Personal Loans of ₹60,000

Who is eligible for the ₹ 60,000 Personal Loan?

Any individual meeting ICICI Bank’s standard eligibility criteria whether salaried or self-employed and requiring a small loan amount for immediate needs can apply.

What documents are required for a ₹60,000 loan?

Standard KYC documents: identity proof, address proof, and (if required) proof of income or bank statements. Documentation remains minimal for quicker processing.

What are the EMI and repayment options for a ₹60,000 personal loan?

EMI amount and repayment tenure will depend on applicable interest rate and borrower’s profile. Given the modest loan size, EMIs are expected to remain small and manageable.

How quickly is the ₹60,000 personal loan disbursed after approval?

Upon approval, the loan amount is typically credited promptly often within 24–48 hours, directly into your account.

Can I get a ₹60,000 Personal Loan without a credit score?

Credit assessment is generally required. Approval depends on income profile, repayment history and internal eligibility norms. For select customers with a pre-approved offer from ICICI Bank, the bank already knows the credit score based on existing banking relationships. In such cases, the credit score may not be checked again. For all other customers, the bank will check the credit score when they apply for a Personal Loan to determine loan eligibility.

How long does it take to disburse a ₹60,000 Personal Loan?

Disbursal timelines depend on documentation completeness and internal approval procedures. For the ICICI Bank customer with a pre-approved Personal Loan offer, disbursement is completed within 2 hrs. For customers without a pre-approved offer, it may take around one working day to do a credit assessment and loan disbursement, subject to the customer's eligibility and internal assessment.

What is the minimum salary required for a ₹60,000 Personal Loan?

Minimum salary requirements depend on eligibility criteria and internal assessment policies. However, you can check your eligibility online in a few clicks by applying for a Personal Loan. You can use the ICICI Bank eligibility calculator to get an estimate of the loan amount you are eligible for.

What are the tax benefits on a ₹60,000 Personal Loan?

Personal Loans do not qualify for any tax benefits
