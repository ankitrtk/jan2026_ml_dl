FAQs

What is zero depreciation cover? 

 It ensures full claim value on parts without depreciation deduction (cars < 5 yrs).

How to avail roadside assistance? 

Call the helpline; assistance is dispatched within 60 mins.

Can I transfer NCB if I change insurer?

Yes, no claim bonus is fully transferable with proof.

What documents are required during claim? 

Policy copy, FIR (if theft), repair estimate and Registration Certificate (RC) book.

Can I get a no-claim bonus on my two-wheeler insurance? 

Yes, you can avail a no-claim bonus on renewal if you have not made any claims during the policy term.
