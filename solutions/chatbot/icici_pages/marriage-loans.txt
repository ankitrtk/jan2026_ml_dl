Personal Loan for Marriage FAQs

What is a Marriage Loan?

A Marriage Loan, also known as a Personal Loan for weddings, is designed to cover wedding expenses. It provides a lump sum amount that can be repaid in monthly EMIs over a flexible tenure. If you want to estimate your payments beforehand, tools like the ICICI Marriage Loan calculator can help you plan better.

How to calculate EMI for a Marriage Loan?

Use the ICICI Bank 
Personal Loan EMI calculator
 by entering the below. The tool will give you an accurate monthly EMI.

Loan amount

Interest rate

Loan tenure

Which Loan is best for wedding expenses?

A 
Personal Loan
 for wedding or marriage is ideal due to flexible usage, competitive interest rates and minimal documentation.

Can women apply for a Marriage Loan?

Yes, women can apply for Marriage Loans, provided they meet the eligibility criteria.

What is the interest rate on a Personal Loan for marriage at ICICI Bank?

Interest rates for a Personal Loan for wedding or marriage depend on multiple factors such as credit profile, income stability and internal assessment. The interest rate on a Personal Loan for marriage offered by ICICI Bank may start from 9.99%* p.a. as per the borrower’s eligibility. Final interest rates are subject to prevailing policies and are confirmed at the time of Loan approval. To confirm the latest interest rates, 
click here
.

What are the eligibility criteria for applying for a Personal Loan for marriage?

Personal Loan for marriage eligibility depends on factors such as age, income stability, employment type and credit profile. Eligibility is assessed through internal evaluation and verification. Meeting basic criteria does not assure approval, as final eligibility is determined during the Loan assessment process.

How much can I borrow under a Personal Loan for marriage?

You can avail a Personal Loan for wedding or marriage up to ₹50 lakh. The eligible amount is determined during the application process based on internal assessment and applicable lending guidelines. 

Are there any restrictions on how I can use a Personal Loan for marriage?

A Personal Loan for marriage offers flexibility in usage and can be used for various wedding-related expenses. There are no specific usage restrictions, provided the Loan is used for lawful personal purposes and in line with the Loan agreement terms.

Can I foreclose or prepay a Personal Loan for marriage?

Yes, foreclosure and part-payment options are available for a Personal Loan for marriage, subject to applicable terms and charges. Prepayment charges are applicable after the first EMI is paid and no foreclosure charges are applied after the completion of 24 EMIs.
