About Two-Wheeler insurance

What Does Two-Wheeler Insurance Cover?

Natural Disasters: Covers damage from natural disasters like floods, storms and earthquakes

Man-Made Disasters: Coverage against theft, riots, terrorism and accidents

Personal Accident Cover: Offers personal accident coverage up to ₹15 lakh for owner or driver and optional cover for co-passengers

Third-Party Liability: Protects against legal liabilities for injuries, fatalities or property damage.

For detailed information, please refer to the Policy Wordings.

**No Claim Bonus (NCB) can only be availed if you renew your policy within 90 days post-expiration. This benefit reduces your premium and applies solely to the 'Own Damage' portion of the policy. In case you switch from another insurer, you may carry over your NCB by presenting legitimate evidence like a renewal notice or confirmation letter stating your NCB eligibility. Furthermore, when choosing a policy duration of two years or longer, your NCB remains valid throughout that span, given zero claims occur during the term.

Important Policy Exclusions to Note

General Wear and Tear: The policy will not cover regular wear and tear or ageing of the vehicle

Depreciation: Any reduction in value resulting from depreciation or subsequent losses will not be included in the coverage

Mechanical or Electrical Malfunctions: Issues related to the breakdown of mechanical or electrical parts are not covered

Tyre and Tube Damage: Expenses associated with replacing tyres and tubes will only be partially reimbursed (up to 50%) when the vehicle also sustains simultaneous damage

Usage Beyond Limits: Using the vehicle outside the stipulated terms mentioned in the policy can lead to the invalidation of claims arising out of such usage

War and Nuclear Perils: Losses due to war, rebellion or exposure to nuclear materials are explicitly excluded from the scope of this insurance policy

Unlicensed Driving: Damages caused by unlicensed or underage drivers

Influence of Drugs or Alcohol: Damages caused by driving while under the influence of drugs or alcohol. 

For full details, refer to the Policy Wordings.

Two-Wheeler Insurance FAQs

What does the policy cover?

Covers theft, accident damage, fire, natural calamities and third-party liabilities. 

What is not covered? 

Normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, and driving under influence or without license. 

Can I renew an expired policy? 

Yes, without inspection for bikes below certain engine capacity (T&C apply). 

What documents are needed for claims? 

Claim form, RC, license, policy copy, and FIR (if applicable). 

Is there a discount for installing anti-theft devices? 

Yes, if approved by ARAI.

What is the accident cover for unnamed passengers?

Upon payment of an additional premium, you can extend coverage to co-passengers or pillion riders, ensuring they are financially protected against fatalities or incapacitation arising from accidents. 

What are the benefits of buying a Car Insurance online?

When you buy Motor Insurance online, you get an instant policy, as there is no documentation or paperwork involved. In addition, you have the advantage of choosing from multiple payment options e.g. Credit Card (Visa, Master Amex card), Net banking, Debit Card, etc.
