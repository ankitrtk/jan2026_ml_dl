Private banking for business FAQs

I am an existing ICICI Bank Current Account holder. Can I enrol myself for Private Banking?

 Yes, you can get yourself enrolled into the program and continue enjoying benefits by maintaining the eligibility criteria.

What is the eligibility to enter the programme?

Relationship value of ₹5 crore (Deposits + Account balance + Investments + Demat holdings)

Deposits + Account balance = ₹50 lakh

Cumulative balance = ₹5 crore (Deposits + Account balance + Mutual Fund + Demat holdings)

Monthly Trade Transactions = >$75K

Will I be charged anything if I don’t maintain the required eligibility criteria?

A non-maintenance charge is applied if the customer does not maintain any of the below:

Quarterly Average Balance of ₹50,000

Deposits + Account balance = ₹ 50 L 

Does this account support international transactions?

Yes, this account supports international transactions.

Does this programme offer merchant/trade services?

Yes, merchant/trade services are embedded in this programme.

How do I contact customer service for any query or issue?

You can call us at 1800 1080 for any queries or visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch.

Can I be upgraded or downgraded after enrolling in this programme?

Yes, you can be upgraded or downgraded according to the eligibility criteria.

Can I link multiple accounts in this programme?

Yes, you can link multiple accounts in this programme.

Can this programme provide family office services?

Yes, this programme provides family office services.
