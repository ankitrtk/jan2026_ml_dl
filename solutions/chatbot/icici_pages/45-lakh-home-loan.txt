45 Lakh Home Loan EMI - Home Loan Up to 45 Lakh

2 mins Read 

               | 2 Years 4 Months Ago

APPLY NOW

KNOW MORE

Home ownership is a significant turning point in your life that involves important financial choices. Among these, understanding the Equated Monthly Instalments (EMIs) for a Home Loan is critical. For those considering a Home Loan of around ₹45 lakh, comprehending the Home Loan EMI of ₹45 lakh is essential for effective financial planning. ICICI Bank offers resources and tools to help prospective homeowners understand and manage their Home Loan EMIs, ensuring a smooth transition into home ownership.

Detailed EMI calculations for different tenures

EMI calculations vary depending on loan tenure and interest rates.

Let's explore the specifics for a Home Loan of ₹45,00,000 at an illustrative interest rate of 9%:

10-Years Tenure:

Principal amount: ₹45,00,000

Interest amount: ₹23,40,492

Total amount payable: ₹68,40,492

Monthly EMI: ₹57,004

15-Years Tenure

Principal amount: ₹45,00,000

Interest amount: ₹37,15,559

Total amount payable: ₹82,15,559

Monthly EMI: ₹45,642

20-Years Tenure:

Principal amount: ₹45,00,000

Interest amount: ₹52,17,040

Total amount payable: ₹97,17,040

Monthly EMI: ₹40,488

These figures highlight how tenure influences the EMI and total interest payable. Borrowers can use 
ICICI Bank’s Home Loan EMI Calculator
 to assess different scenarios and make optimal decisions about the length of their loan.

ICICI Bank Home Loan eligibility

A successful loan application depends on meeting certain eligibility criteria:

Age:
 20 to 65 years for salaried individuals and 21 to 70 years for self-employed individuals.

Income:
 The bank stipulates a minimum salary requirement, ensuring that borrowers have the capacity to repay the loan.

Employment stability: 
Stability in employment or business is critical, especially for self-employed applicants.

Credit score:
A good 
credit score
 (above 750) is preferred for better loan terms.

Nationality: 
The loan facility is primarily available to Indian citizens, including Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).

Borrowers can instantly check their eligibility using 
ICICI Bank’s Home Loan Eligibility Calculator
. 

Planning your Home Loan

EMI Calculator:
 The online EMI calculator of ICICI Bank helps you to estimate your monthly outgo and plan accordingly.

Financial Health Assessment: 
A thorough assessment of your finances, including income, expenses and existing debts is key to choosing the right loan amount.

Customisation of Loan Options: 
The bank offers flexibility in terms of loan tenure and interest rates, allowing borrowers to tailor their EMIs according to their financial situation.

Understanding the ‘Home Loan EMI of ₹45 lakh’ is a fundamental part of purchasing a home. ICICI Bank’s diverse 
home loan
 solutions, including user-friendly tools like the EMI calculator, are designed to simplify this process for you. With the right knowledge and planning, you can confidently navigate the financial aspects of buying your dream home.

FAQs on 45 Lakh Home Loan EMI

How does the tenure of the loan affect my EMI?

The loan tenure directly impacts your EMI. A longer tenure generally means a lower EMI, but it also increases the total interest payable.

Can I prepay my Home Loan? Are there any charges?

Yes, ICICI Bank allows prepayment of Home Loans which can reduce your total interest cost. The bank provides clear guidelines on any applicable prepayment charges.

How does my credit score affect the Home Loan interest rate?

A higher credit score can help you secure a lower interest rate as it indicates good creditworthiness. It is advisable to maintain a healthy credit score for favourable loan terms.

Are there any tax benefits on Home Loans?

Yes, borrowers can avail tax benefits on both the principal and interest components of the Home Loan under various sections of the Income Tax Act.

How do I choose between fixed and floating interest rates?

The fixed rate remains constant throughout the loan tenure, offering predictability in EMIs. The floating rate changes with market conditions, which can increase or decrease your EMI. The choice depends on your risk appetite and market analysis.

What are the implications of early loan repayment?

Early repayment can significantly reduce the total interest payable. ICICI Bank offers clear guidelines on any charges applicable for prepayment, allowing borrowers to plan their finances effectively.

Can joint applicants apply for a Home Loan?

Yes, ICICI Bank accommodates joint Home Loan applications, which can increase the overall loan eligibility with better loan terms.

What role does the property location play in loan approval?

The location and valuation of the property are crucial in determining loan approval as they affect the loan-to-value ratio and overall risk assessment.
