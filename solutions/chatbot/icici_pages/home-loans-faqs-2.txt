 Basic information 

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can an NRI/OCI apply for a Home Loan in India?

Yes, NRIs and OCIs can apply for Home Loans to buy or construct a home in India.

                            How can an NRI/OCI customer apply for a Mortgage Loan?

You can apply online through our Express Home Loan platform. 
Click here
 to start the application process. Our team will get in touch with you for further processing.

                            What is the maximum loan amount for NRI Home Loan?

Maximum amount of home loan which customer can avail depends on financial eligibility of the customer and property valuation as determined by the ICICI Bank.

                            Can a Resident Indian (RI) or a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) be a co-applicant for an NRI mortgage loan?

Yes. An RI or NRI individual can be a co-applicant for an NRI mortgage loan.

                            What types of properties are financed by ICICI Bank?

ICICI Bank finances ready-to-move, under-construction, resale residential properties, and house construction in India.

                            Who can be a co-applicant in NRI loan?

Any individual who is a blood relative. Son, daughter, sister, brother or spouse can be a co-applicant. Co-applicant can be a RI or NRI/OCI. However, all property owners must be included as applicants in the loan.

                            Is there any validity period for expiry of Sanction Letter for an NRI/OCI mortgage loan?

Yes. Sanction Letter is valid for a period of 6 months or 180 days from the date of issue.

                            When does the disbursement in a loan happen?

Your loan will be disbursed after you have selected a property, submitted all necessary property related documents to initiate legal & technical verification and you have paid your own contribution towards purchase of your home. Disbursement would be done directly into builder’s account in case it is a builder purchase property. In case of an under-construction property, disbursement would happen as per stage of construction. For LAP & Top Up, disbursement would happen in applicant’s NRO account only

Eligibility and documentation

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is a Person of Indian Origin eligible to apply for a Home Loan?

Yes. PIOs can apply for a Home Loan in India

                            I am an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) customer, but I do not hold an OCI card.  Can I apply for mortgage loan?

Yes, an OCI customer can apply for a mortgage loan. In the absence of OCI card, OCI declaration in bank’s format must be submitted. 
click here
 for OCI format.

                            Is PAN card mandatory for NRI/OCI?

PAN is mandatory for NRI. OCI can submit Form 60 in absence of PAN

                            Is Aadhaar mandatory for NRI/OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) to apply for mortgage loan in India?

No. Aadhaar is not mandatory for NRI/OCI customers. However, if Aadhaar is available, it can be submitted as Indian address proof.

                            What are the documents required by salaried NRI/OCI customers?

List of documents required:

Valid Passport copy (For NRIs, both front & back page)
Valid visa (Residence visa/work permit/Permanent citizen card)
OCI card (For OCI customers only. In the absence of OCI card, OCI declaration in bank’s format to be submitted. For OCI format  
click here
Valid overseas address proof
Valid Indian address proof (Applicable only if Indian address is provided as mailing/communication address)
PAN card copy (In absence of PAN number, Form 60 is required by OCI)
Last 1 month salary slips
Last 3 months overseas bank statement with salary credit
Last credit report (applicable for USA, UK, Canada, Singapore, Hong Kong, UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, New Zealand, Australia, Ireland, South Africa, Malaysia)

                            I am an NRI customer do I need to submit residential address proof in India?

If you are an NRI, Indian address proof is mandatory only if you provide Indian address as mailing/communication address. Below documents can be accepted as Indian address proof:
Valid Passport
Aadhaar card
Valid Driving Licence
Voter ID

                            Is valid visa mandatory as an NRI status proof to apply for a mortgage loan?

Yes. Valid residence visa/work permit/permanent citizen card or equivalent is mandatory to establish NRI status.

                            Is overseas address proof mandatory to apply for NRI mortgage loan?

Overseas address proof is required only if overseas address is opted as communication address by customer in his loan application.

                            Is Power of Attorney (POA) mandatory in an NRI/OCI mortgage loan?

POA is mandatory only if any applicant or co-applicant cannot come to India to complete disbursement formalities. The POA holder may also submit post disbursement related services in any of the asset servicing branches on the customer’s behalf.

                            Who can be my Power of Attorney (POA) holder?

POA holder can be anybody from your family, friends or neighbours residing in India.

                            Is there a POA document in ICICI Bank format or as an NRI/OCI customer I can execute a general POA?

POA document must be executed only in ICICI Bank format signed by all NRI applicants. 
click here
 for POA format.

                            Is it mandatory to execute a POA document when applying for a mortgage loan?

No. POA execution is not mandatory at the time of applying for a mortgage loan. The customer must execute a POA before disbursement only.

                            What is the ROI applicable for a Home Loan for an NRI/OCI customer?

To check rate of interest, 
click here

Repayment and tenure

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is the maximum tenure for an ICICI Bank NRI Home Loan?

The loan tenure is up to 30 years depending on borrower profile.

                            Can I link my NRE account to my existing loan account for repayment of EMI?

Yes. Repayment for NRI/OCI customer can be linked to either NRE or NRO account.

                            Can I make part-prepayments?

Yes, ICICI Bank allows part-prepayment as per applicable loan terms.

Property related

                EXPAND ALL

                            What security is required for the loan?

The residential property financed is mortgaged in favour of ICICI Bank.

                            Does ICICI Bank verify property documents?

Yes, ICICI Bank conducts legal and technical verification before loan disbursement.

 Track My loan

                            Which post disbursement services can be availed online without visiting India?

For post disbursement related services, you can log on to our Non logged in section (NLI) and submit your request.

Closure

                EXPAND ALL

                            Is foreclosure allowed?

Yes, ICICI Bank allows foreclosure of NRI Home Loans, subject to applicable conditions.

Miscellaneous

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I transfer my existing NRI Home Loan to ICICI Bank?

Yes, ICICI Bank allows balance transfer of existing NRI Home Loans, subject to eligibility.

                            Can a loan be taken for house renovation? 

Yes, ICICI Bank offers 
home improvement loans
 for renovation of residential property.

                            Can an NRI/OCI avail a Top-up along with Balance Transfer? 

Yes, an NRI/OCI customer can avail Top-up along with balance transfer.

Yes. An NRI/OCI customer can avail Top-up loan over and above the existing Home Loan. If you are in India, you can visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch. If you are an NRI salaried customer currently overseas, you can apply online through Express Home Loan (EHL). To apply 
click here

                            I am an NRI/OCI customer and already have a mortgage loan with another bank along with a top-up, I would like to do a balance transfer of my loan and also avail additional top-up. Is that possible?

Yes. Basis income eligibility & property valuation, you can avail an additional top-up over and above the balance transfer with Top-up.

                            Do I get a tax benefit on the loan being an NRI/OCI customer?

Yes, if you are filing your IT returns in India as an NRI/OCI, you can get the tax benefits on the principle & interest components on your home loan under Income Tax Act, 1961. As the benefits could vary every year, please check with your tax advisor.
