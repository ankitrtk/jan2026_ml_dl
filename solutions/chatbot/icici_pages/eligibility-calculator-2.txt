Car Loan Eligibility Criteria

ICICI Bank offers comprehensive car loan eligibility solutions designed to match your financial profile, making it simpler than ever to determine how much you qualify for. Explore the eligibility criteria based on your income, credit score, and existing debts, and take the first step towards securing a seamless car loan with flexible repayment options and competitive interest rates.

New Car

Loan up to 100% on road price

Used Car

 Loan up to 100% financing for your used car with a maximum tenure of 84 months onwards. (depending on profile)

How to Calculate Car Loan Eligibility

Understanding your Car Loan eligibility before applying helps you plan better and choose a suitable loan amount. Here is how you can calculate it:

Determine Your Net Income: Start by identifying your net monthly income after taxes and deductions. This forms the basis of your eligibility assessment.
Calculate Existing EMIs: Add up all your current monthly debt obligations, including any ongoing loan repayments or Credit Card dues.
Apply the 50% Rule: Your total monthly EMI obligations, including the proposed Car Loan EMI, should ideally not exceed 50% of your net monthly income.
Use the Formula: Eligible Loan Amount = (50% of Net Monthly Income − Existing EMIs) × Loan Tenure in Months.

Key Factors Impacting Car loan Eligibility

The key factors that impact your Car Loan eligibility with ICICI Bank are:

Salary/Income: Your net monthly income determines your borrowing capacity. A stable and sufficient income strengthens your Car Loan eligibility.
Credit Score (CIBIL): A CIBIL score of 700 or above is preferred, as it reflects responsible repayment behaviour and improves your chances of approval.
Employment Stability: Permanent employment with a consistent work history is preferred for salaried individuals, as it demonstrates reliable income and repayment capacity.
Age: You must be between 20 and 65 years (Salaried) and 21 to 70 years (Self-employed) of age to be eligible for an ICICI Bank Car Loan.
Loan-to-Value (LTV): ICICI Bank offers financing of up to 100% of the on-road price for new cars, subject to your overall eligibility and profile.

Car Loan Eligibility FAQs

What CIBIL score is required to be eligible for a car loan?

The minimum CIBIL score for an ICICI Bank car loan generally starts at 750 or above, though eligibility may vary based on the applicant’s overall credit profile.

Can I get a car loan with a 30k/40K salary?

While a salary of ₹30,000 or ₹40,000 may qualify for a 
car loan
, eligibility ultimately depends on factors like credit score and existing debts. 

What employment duration is required for salaried individuals applying for an ICICI Bank Car Loan?

To check Car Loan eligibility for an ICICI Bank New Car Loan, salaried individuals should have stable employment with a regular source of income. Permanent employment status is generally preferred, along with repayment capacity and a good credit profile 

What income criteria must salaried individuals meet for an ICICI Bank Car Loan?

For ICICI Bank Car Loan eligibility, salaried applicants generally need a minimum monthly income of ₹30,000. The final eligibility may also depend on factors such as credit score, existing financial obligations and repayment capacity. 

What income criteria must self-employed individuals meet for an ICICI Bank Car Loan?

To check Car Loan eligibility, self-employed applicants should demonstrate stable income and financial capacity to repay the loan. Income assessment may include business stability, financial records, tax returns and overall repayment capability. 

Can I get an ICICI Bank Car Loan without income proof?

Income proof is required to complete an ICICI Bank Car Loan eligibility check, as it helps assess your repayment capacity. However, eligible existing customers may receive a pre-approved Car Loan offer with simplified documentation. Final eligibility depends on your profile, banking relationship, credit history and internal assessment criteria. 

How much salary is required for a ₹10 Lakh Car Loan?

The salary required for a ₹10 lakh Car Loan depends on factors such as loan tenure, EMI affordability, credit score and existing obligations. At ICICI Bank, salaried applicants require a minimum monthly income of ₹30,000 to apply. 

What is the minimum salary to qualify for a car?

There is no fixed minimum salary to qualify for a Car Loan. ICICI Bank assesses your Car Loan eligibility based on your net monthly income, existing financial obligations, credit score, and overall repayment capacity rather than a fixed salary threshold.

How much loan can I get with a ₹70,000 per month salary?

With a monthly salary of ₹70,000, your Car Loan amount depends on your existing EMIs, credit score, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio. ICICI Bank evaluates your overall repayment capacity to determine the loan amount you qualify for.

Is 750 a good CIBIL score for a Car Loan?

Yes, a CIBIL score of 750 is considered a strong score for a Car Loan eligibility check. ICICI Bank prefers a score of 700 or above, so a score of 750 reflects responsible credit behaviour and strengthens your application.
