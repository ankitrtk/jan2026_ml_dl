Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who can use the CNB platform?

CNB is designed for corporate and business customers, including Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs), large enterprises, institutions, and business entities who require online banking for managing multiple Business Accounts and transactions.

How is CNB different from regular Net Banking?

CNB is built for Business Banking needs, offering advanced features like:

Multiple Account management

Payments and collections

Customized approvals and access control

API integration for scalable capabilities

How do I access Corporate Net Banking (CNB)?

To access Corporate Net Banking, visit ICICI Bank website and navigate to corporate login.

Where can I get more information about Corporate Net Banking (CNB)?

Call: Call our customer care at 1800-1080

Email: Write to us at corporatecare@icici.bank.in

Online: View the CNB online demo hosted on ICICI Bank website.

What is the process for user ID activation?

Most transactions can be carried out digitally without submitting physical documents at branch through trade online platform

What is to be done when the customer gets the message "Access denied"?

Customer should check for invalid login credentials - if their password is incorrect or account is locked. For incorrect password, please input the correct login password. If you are unable to remember the same, please reset your password by following the process of online password reset.

What are the various modes of fund transfer available through Corporate Net Banking (CNB)?

CNB offers one-to-one Funds Transfer to ICICI Bank and non-ICICI Bank Accounts. You can transfer funds to multiple beneficiaries using bulk file upload through CNB.  You can also make tax payments and bill payments through CNB.

Can I use Corporate Net Banking (CNB) for international fund transfers?

No. You can only transfer funds within India using CNB. Please visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch to make a request for an international fund transfer.

What is the process for User ID activation?

Your User ID can be activated by resetting your password.

Can we integrate Corporate Net Banking (CNB) with our ERP?

Yes. We can help you in integrating your payments, collections and reconciliation APIs with your ERP system. Host 2 host integration facility is also available through CNB.

Can I track the status of my transactions on CNB?

Yes, CNB provides real-time transaction tracking, downloadable reports and instant SMS or email confirmations, along with reverse MIS reports for easy reconciliation.

Does CNB offer mobile access?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers InstaBIZ, a dedicated mobile app for CNB users that allows Account management, payments, approvals and tracking on-the-go with advanced login security through mobile binding features
