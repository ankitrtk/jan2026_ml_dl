Documents Required for Personal Loan in India

Personal Loan Documents

ICICI Bank makes your loan process seamless with the minimal documents required for a Personal Loan.  and a bank statement or a salary slip. For self-employed applicants, the documents required may differ. Be sure to check the documents needed before applying for Personal Loan.

APPLY PERSONAL LOAN

FOR SALARIED

FOR SELF-EMPLOYED

List of Documents for Salaried

Proof of identity and residence: Passport or Driving Licence or Voter ID or Job card issued by NREGA/Letter from National Population Register/ Proof of possession of complete Aadhaar number (Any One)

Latest 3 months bank statement (where salary or income is credited)

Salary slips for last 1 month

Passport size photographs

Go Green Initiative

As part of the ‘Go Green’ initiative - an endeavor to reduce use of paper and make our contribution to this noble cause, the below mentioned documents related to your Personal Loans will be sent on your e-mail ID registered with us, with effect from 05 February 2017.

No due certificate

Refund Letter

Pending Dues Letter

Also, we wish to inform you that if your e-mail ID is not available, communication will be sent through physical letters.

If your e-mail ID is not registered with us, we request you to visit your nearest loan servicing branch or call our Customer Care and update your e-mail ID.

Document Category

Identity & Residence Proof

Income Proof

Office Address Proof
Salaried customers may enter their office address during the application process. For self-employed applicants, office address proof may be required.

FAQs

Can I foreclose or prepay my Personal Loan?

Yes, you can prepay the entire loan outstanding anytime during the loan tenure after 12 instalments, subject to payment of standard pre-closure charges of 3% + GST.

Part prepayment is allowed. For prepayment of your Personal Loan, please visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch. We request you to carry the following documents to complete the process:

Request letter signed by the loan applicant

PAN Card (if the mode of payment is cash and the amount is more than ₹50,000)

Payment can be made through cash, cheque or demand draft.

What is the procedure to prepay my Personal Loan?

For prepayment of your 
Personal Loan
, please visit the nearest ICICI Bank Asset Servicing Branch. We request you to carry the following documents to complete the process:

Request Letter signed by the loan applicant

PAN Card (if the mode of payment is cash and the amount is more than ₹50,000)

Payment can be made through cash, cheque or demand draft.

How much time will it take for my loan to be approved for Personal Loan?

Loans are approved within 48 hours* of submission of complete documentation.

How do I repay the Personal Loan?

You can repay the loan in Equated Monthly Instalments (EMIs). If you have an ICICI Bank Account, you can mandate us to directly debit the same through auto debit mandate or if you have an alternate bank account, then through NACH mandate.

What are the documents required for a Personal Loan? 

The documents required for a Personal Loan include identity proof, address proof and income-related documents. The list of exact Personal Loan documents may vary depending on whether the applicant is salaried or self-employed. All submissions are reviewed as part of the verification and internal assessment process.

What is a pre-approved Personal loan?

A pre-approved Personal Loan is a loan offer extended to selected customers based on their credit profile and relationship with ICICI Bank, subject to internal eligibility criteria and verification at the time of application.

Is there no documentation process for a pre-approved Personal Loan?

Personal Loan documents, such as identity and income, are verified automatically using existing records, Net Banking or account aggregator data. There is no need to submit physical documents for a pre-approved Personal Loan. The application is approved based on eligibility checks and ICICI Bank’s internal assessment.

How fast is the Personal Loan documentation process?

The Personal Loan documentation process depends on the applicant's profile, the accuracy of submitted documents and their verification. Submitting accurate details as per the Personal Loan checklist helps avoid delays. Processing timelines are subject to internal review and compliance procedures.

Do I have to submit my original KYC documents?

Applicants are required to submit copies of KYC documents for a Personal Loan. Original documents may be requested for verification purposes. The list of required documents for verification is handled in accordance with regulatory and internal guidelines.

Are there any documents I should submit after the sanction or before the disbursement of my Personal Loan?

In some cases, additional documents may be required after sanction or before disbursement of a Personal Loan. These may include mandate setup, confirmations or other formal declarations. Any such requirement is communicated during the process, as part of the applicable Personal Loan documents list.

Can you avail ICICI Bank Personal Loan without documentation?

ICICI Bank offers pre-approved Personal Loan to eligible existing customers without any physical documentation. For applicants who are not ICICI Bank customers, the loan can be applied digitally with minimal documentation. Providing the necessary details from the Personal Loan checklist ensures a smooth process and helps in faster verification.

Which proof of income is accepted for a Personal Loan application?

Proof of income required for a Personal Loan depends on the applicant's employment type. Salary slips and bank statements are considered for salaried individuals, while audited financial statements and bank records may be reviewed for self-employed applicants. These documents are evaluated along with other 
application details
 during the assessment process.
