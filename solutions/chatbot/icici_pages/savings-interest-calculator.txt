About the Savings Interest Calculator

What is a Savings Account Interest Calculator?

A savings account interest calculator is a digital tool that helps you estimate the interest you may earn on your balance. It considers your deposit amount, tenure, and applicable interest rate to give you an accurate projection in seconds.

How does the calculator work?

The calculator uses a simple formula based on your daily end-of-day balance. It factors in the current interest rate and the time your funds remain in the account to give you a total interest earned.

Why use this calculator?

It helps with financial planning, gives instant results and supports responsible money management, making it a must-have tool for every saver.

How is interest credited?

Interest is calculated daily and credited quarterly at the end of March, June, September, and December based on your daily closing balance.

Benefits of ICICI Bank Savings Account

FAQs on Savings Account Interest Calculator

How often is interest credited?

Interest is credited quarterly at the end of March, June, September, and December.

Can the interest rate change?

ICICI Bank’s savings interest rates are fixed, but may change at the bank’s discretion. Customers are notified accordingly.

Is the calculator accurate for all account types?

This calculator is designed for ICICI Bank Savings Accounts. Variations may apply for other account types.

Can I use the calculator for future planning?

Yes, you can use the tool to project your earnings over any custom tenure by entering the desired savings amount and duration.
