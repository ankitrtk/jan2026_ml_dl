FASTag has revolutionised toll payments in India by expediting the procedure and improving convenience. Uploading important documents such as your vehicle's Registration Certificate (RC) is necessary to keep your FASTag updated and active. If you don't finish this step, your FASTag can be deactivated, making it difficult to make toll payments.

In this blog, we will guide you on the significance of submitting your vehicle’s RCl. We will also provide steps on 
how to upload RC in a FASTag 
Account.

What is RC (Registration Certificate)?

The Registration Certificate, also known as the RC, is a document provided by the Regional Transport Office (RTO). It acts as proof that your vehicle has been lawfully registered in your name and is permitted to be operated on roads in India. The RC provides information about your vehicle. Some of the details include:

License plate number

Chassis number

Number of the engine

Address of the owner

Registration date

Type of vehicle

Significance of RC for your FASTag Account

Submitting your vehicle’s RC plays a vital role in ensuring the seamless operation of your FASTag Account for smooth functioning. Here’s why it is important:

Verification of Ownership

RC confirms the ownership of the vehicle. It helps connect your FASTag to the vehicle accurately.

Compliance with Government Rules

According to the government rules, every vehicle using FASTag must have an RC along with all the related documents for verification purposes.

Prevention of Fraud

Ensuring that you upload the RC document ensures that your vehicle’s FASTags are safeguarded against fraudulent activities like tag misuse or cloning.

Avoid Deactivation

Neglecting to upload your RC document could result in your FASTag Account getting deactivated and causing inconvenience when making toll payments.

How to Upload RC to Your FASTag Account?

Here are the steps to upload your RC (Registration Certificate) for your FASTag Account:

Via iMobile App

Login to 
iMobile app

Click on “Bill Pay, Recharge & FASTag” and select “FASTag”

Click on the 3-dots CTA on the vehicle whose RC needsto be uploaded

Click on “Update Vehicle Number”

Add the requested details along with the front and back images of the RC document and click on Submit.

Via FASTag portal:

Login to the FASTag Customer Portal

Visit the official 
ICICI Bank FASTag portal
 and log in with your credentials.

Access Tag Account Summary

On the Home Page, scroll down and select View Tag Account Summary.

Navigate to Vehicle Management

Click on Vehicles and then select Manage Vehicle.

Upload the Registration Certificate (RC)

Click on Upload and enter your Vehicle Number.

Attach the scanned copy of the front & back side of your Registration Certificate (RC).

Click on Upload to complete the process.

When uploading your Registration Certificate (RC) for your FASTag Account, ensure the following:

Document Clarity

The RC should be visible without any blurs, smudges, or distortions.

All details, including vehicle number, owner's name, chassis number, and registration validity, should be legible.

Accepted Formats

The document should be in PDF, JPEG, or PNG format (check the FASTag portal for specific format requirements).

File size should not exceed the maximum limit allowed on the portal.

Other Important Considerations:

Ensure that the entire document is visible, and no part of it is cropped.

The document should not be password-protected or encrypted.

If uploading a scanned copy, ensure it is scanned at a high resolution for better readability.

If uploading a photograph, take the picture in good lighting without glare or shadows.

Following these guidelines will help prevent any rejection or delays in verifying your RC for your FASTag Account.

Tips for a Smooth RC Upload Process:

There are a few things to keep in mind while uploading your RC:

Ensure Document Clarity:
 Always make sure that the RC you are uploading is clear and accurate to ensure all information is clear and easy to read.
Use Trusted Platforms: 
Get your RC from government platforms such as DigiLocker to ensure its authenticity.
Stay Updated: 
Make sure to upload the latest version of your RC if it's been updated or replaced (for example after a change in ownership) to prevent any discrepancies.
Check for Confirmation: 
Make sure to check your email or FASTag dashboard to see if your RC verification is complete.

Conclusion

Uploading your RC to your FASTag Account is very important and is helpful when you travel through national highways. It promotes smooth toll transactions and makes your journeys stress-free. By following the steps mentioned in the blog post, you can complete this process conveniently from the comfort of your home.

Don't delay updating your FASTag with your vehicle’s registration certificate to ensure travel on the highways without any interruption. It's important to keep your FASTag Account active as it not only saves time but also makes it easier for you to pass through toll stations seamlessly.

FAQs

How do I request a change from chassis number to vehicle number in my FASTag?

You can log in to the FASTag customer portal and upload the updated Registration Certificate. Alternatively, you can do the same Via iMobile App or WhatsApp Banking.

How can I find my FASTag Customer ID?

You can find your FASTag ID by logging into the FASTag customer portal, checking SMS alerts received during activation, or contacting customer care using your registered mobile number.
