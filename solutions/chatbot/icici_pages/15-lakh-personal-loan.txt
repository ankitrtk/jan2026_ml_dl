Eligibility Criteria for a ₹15 Lakh Personal Loan

To qualify for an 
online personal loan
 , applicants must meet the following 
eligibility criteria for personal loan
:

Profession: Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and non-professionals

Age: 20 years to 58 years for salaried individuals, 28 years to 65 years for self-employed professionals and 25 years to 65 years> for doctors

Income: Minimum Rs. 30,000 for salaried individuals, minimum turnover of Rs. 15 lakh for self-employed professionals and turnover of at least Rs. 40 lakh for non-professionals

Experience: At least two years of job experience for salaried applicants, at least five years for business owners and at least three years for doctors

Duration of stay at the current residence: At least one year

Minimum profit: At least Rs. 2 lakh for self-employed individuals and proprietorship firms and Rs. 1 lakh for non-professionals, after tax.

FAQs on Personal Loans of ₹15 Lakh

What is the repayment tenure for a ₹15 lakh personal loan?

Interest rates change as per bank’s policy. Click here for the updated interest rates. Tenure ranges from 12 to 72 months depending on your repayment capacity.

How do EMIs work for a ₹15 lakh loan?

EMIs depend on your interest rate and tenure. Longer tenures reduce
monthly EMI but increase total interest payable. For example, at
10.75% interest rate per annum:

60 months EMI: ₹32,3

	72 months EMI: ₹28,245

How can I increase my chances of approval for a ₹15 lakh personal loan?

Maintain a good credit score, lower your debt-to-income ratio, pay bills on time and ensure stable employment or business continuity.

Can I get a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan without a credit score?

Credit assessment is generally required. Approval depends on income profile, repayment history and internal eligibility norms. For select customers with a pre-approved offer from ICICI Bank, the bank already knows the credit score based on existing banking relationships. In such cases, the credit score may not be checked again at the time of applying for the loan. For all other customers, the bank will check the credit score when they apply for a Personal Loan to determine loan eligibility.

How long does it take to disburse a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan?

Disbursal timelines depend on documentation completeness and internal approval procedures. For an ​​​​ICICI Bank customer with a pre-approved Personal Loan offer, disbursement is completed within 2 hrs. For customers without a pre-approved offer, it may take around one working day to do a credit assessment and loan disbursement, subject to the customer's eligibility and internal assessment.

What is the minimum salary required for a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan?

Minimum salary requirements depend on eligibility criteria, required loan amount and tenure and other internal assessment policies. However, you can check your eligibility digitally in a few clicks by applying for a Personal Loan. You may also refer to the 
ICICI Bank eligibility calculator,
 which can provide an indicative, broad-level eligibility estimate.

What are the tax benefits on a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan?

Personal Loans do not qualify for any tax benefits.

Can I take a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan for debt consolidation?

Yes, subject to eligibility assessment and internal approval norms.

What is the processing fee for a ₹15 lakh Personal Loan?

The processing fee is applicable as per policy terms at the time of application.
