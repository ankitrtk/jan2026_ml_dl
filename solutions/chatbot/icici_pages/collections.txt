About

What are ICICI Bank’s collection solutions for corporates? 

ICICI Bank offers a comprehensive suite of corporate collection solutions designed to simplify receivables across cash, cheque, digital, and recurring payment modes. These services help businesses collect & reconcile payments efficiently, securely, and at scale. 

How do ICICI Bank’s collection services help improve reconciliation? 

With features like payer-tagging, virtual accounts, and instant alerts, ICICI Bank’s solutions automate reconciliation, minimize errors, and reduce manual intervention in accounting processes. 

Can businesses receive real-time payment updates? 

Yes, ICICI Bank provides instant fund update notifications and custom MIS reports, enabling real-time tracking of collections and better cash flow visibility. 

Are doorstep cash and cheque pickups available? 

Absolutely. Businesses can opt for secure, hassle-free doorstep cash and cheque pickup services across multiple locations, backed by a vast branch and logistics network. 

 Is there a solution for high-volume recurring collections? 

Yes, ICICI Bank offers automated debit solutions including NACH, eNACH, and Auto Debit for managing large-scale recurring collections with minimal manual input. 

 How can I monitor my collections? 

You get access to custom MIS reports via email, H2H, or APIs, along with dashboards and automated reports that give clear insights into payment status and trends. 

Are ICICI Bank’s collection services suitable for small and large enterprises? 

Yes, the suite is scalable and can be customized to suit businesses of all sizes and industries, from SMEs to large corporates. 

FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Collection solutions used for? 

It is used to efficiently manage incoming payments through cash, cheque, digital, or recurring modes, helping businesses improve collections and reconciliation. 

How do I get started with ICICI Bank’s collection services? 

Simply contact your relationship manager or visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch to explore customized solutions based on your business needs. 

Can I integrate ICICI Bank’s collection services with my ERP? 

Yes, integration is possible via APIs and H2H connectivity, enabling smooth data exchange and automation within your existing ERP. 

What types of businesses can benefit from these services? 

ICICI Bank’s Collection Suite is ideal for manufacturing firms, NBFCs, e-commerce companies, utility service providers, and any enterprise with a high volume of receivables. 

Are there any tools for monitoring and reporting? 

Yes, businesses can leverage real-time dashboards, scheduled MIS reports, and customizable analytics for effective decision-making and compliance tracking. 

Are there MIS and reconciliation reports available?

The suite supports UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, debit cards, credit cards, and auto-pull, providing flexibility and convenience to both businesses and their customers. 

How secure are doorstep collection services? 

ICICI Bank follows stringent operational and security protocols to ensure cash and cheque pickups are safe, timely, and traceable. 

How quickly are funds credited? 

Most digital and doorstep collections are processed and reflected on the same day, helping businesses maintain a strong liquidity position.
