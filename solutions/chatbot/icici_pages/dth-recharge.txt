DTH Recharge

DTH (Direct-To-Home) Recharge Online with ICICI Bank

ICICI Bank makes it easy to explore and execute DTH Recharge

Get access to a variety of choices from top service providers to fit your needs and price range. ICICI Bank's online portal facilitates effortless browsing and selection, regardless of your preference for monthly, quarterly or yearly subscriptions.

You may verify information before recharging, with the help of an easy-to-use navigation system, guaranteeing that your choice is well-informed. Use ICICI Bank's online DTH recharge service to handle your entertainment demands with ease as well as for seamless transactions.

PAY NOW

Features and benefits of the online DTH Recharge from ICICI Bank

Easy accessibility

ICICI Bank's online platform allows you to recharge your DTH whenever and wherever you choose, removing the necessity for in-person visits.

Safe transactions

Use the reliable online banking platform from ICICI Bank to make payments securely.

User-friendly Management

Simplify your entertainment demands by easily managing your DTH subscriptions and payments.

No convenience fee

Enjoy no convenience fee on your recharges for DTH.

Steps to recharge your DTH via ICICI Bank

Via ICICI Bank’s iMobile app

Via Net Banking

Log in to ICICI Bank’s iMobile app. Move to the ‘Bill Pay and Recharge’ section

Select ‘DTH’ and choose ‘Operator’

Enter the registered mobile number or Subscriber number

Enter the Recharge Plan amount

Proceed to payment and click on ‘Confirm’

The recharge will be processed within a few minutes. You will receive a confirmation message on the screen, as well as an SMS confirming the successful recharge.

Login to ICICI Bank Internet Banking. Select ‘Payments & Transfer’ and click on ‘Pay Bills’

Next, click on ‘DTH’ and choose ‘Operator’ from the available Billers list

Enter Customer Identification details such as registered mobile number/subscriber number

Input the recharge amount of your selected DTH Recharge Plan

Enter your OTP to complete your transaction

Get an instant confirmation from your DTH operator about your recharge on your registered phone and via SMS.

DTH Recharge FAQs

Does ICICI Bank support all operators for DTH Recharge Plans?

Yes, ICICI Bank supports all major DTH providers including 
Airtel
, 
Tata Play
, 
Dish TV
 and 
Videocon
, providing a variety of recharge options for each.

How much time does it take to process a DTH recharge?

DTH recharges made through ICICI Bank are completed instantly. Once you receive payment confirmation, the recharge appears in your account.

What is a monthly DTH pack?

The monthly DTH pack differs from provider to provider, but it usually gives you a choice of channels for a month, depending on your budget and subscription choices.

Which DTH provider has the best plan?

The best DTH plan is determined by a number of criteria, including price, preferred channels and extra services by providers such as Videocon, Tata Play, Airtel and Dish TV.

What is the minimum and maximum recharge amount allowed?

Recharge amounts may vary depending on the DTH operator plan selected. You can choose from the available plans displayed during DTH recharge.

Can I recharge someone else’s DTH connection?

Yes. You can recharge any valid DTH subscriber ID by entering the correct details through ICICI Bank Net Banking or the iMobile app.
