About Insta Overdraft

What is a Bank Overdraft

A Bank Overdraft allows businesses to withdraw funds beyond their account balance up to a sanctioned limit. Interest is charged only on the amount used.

What is the interest charged

Interest is charged only on the utilised amount. The annual percentage depends on your banking profile.

What is the maximum limit that can be sanctioned

Existing ICICI Bank customers can get up to Rs 50 lakh. New to bank customers can get up to Rs 25 lakh.

What documents are required

For pre approved customers, no documents are required. Others need to provide their bank statement. Partnerships and companies may need MOA or Board Resolution.

FAQs

What is the tenure of the OD facility?

The tenure is 12 months. It can be renewed based on eligibility with a renewal processing fee of 2% plus taxes.

Are there any pre payment or foreclosure charges?

There are no pre payment or foreclosure charges on InstaOD.

What determines the sanction limit?

Sanction depends on account usage and transactional history over the last 12 months.

Whom can I contact for assistance?

You can visit any ICICI Bank branch or call Customer Care on 1800 1080.

What types of entities can apply?

Individuals, proprietors, partnerships, private limited companies and public limited companies can apply.
