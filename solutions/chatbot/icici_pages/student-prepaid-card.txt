Student Prepaid Card

The ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card offers students a convenient and secure way to manage expenses without the need for a Savings Account. With contactless payments, no requirement of minimum balance and an annual spending limit of ₹10 lakh, students can use it for expenses like fees, travel, shopping and much more. Available for students aged 10 years and older, the Card is widely accepted at ATMs, POS machines and online platforms.

How to load the ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card?

Via ICICI Bank’s iMobile app / Net Banking

Via Other Banks’ Apps or Websites

Via UPI Apps

1. Log in 

2. Go to the section for Fund Transfers

3. Add a beneficiary using the below steps:

a. Select ‘Other bank’ as the beneficiary bank

b. Type 9595 followed by the 9-digit reference number as the account number (i.e. 9595XXXXXXXXX). The reference number can be found below the Card Number in the Card kit 

c. Re-enter the above number as the account number

d. Enter IFSC code: ICIC0000104

e. Enter the payee name

4. Select the above payee from the list of beneficiaries in the fund transfer section 

5. Enter the amount and remarks for the transfer

6. Choose transfer type: NEFT/IMPS

7. Select the source Account from which you want to load the funds on the Card

8. Click ‘Proceed’ and enter the OTP received for validation 

9. Once OTP validation is completed, the transaction will be successful and the funds will be loaded on the Card.

Note:

● The 9-digit reference number can be found below the Card Number in the kit that accompanied the Card.

● To load the Card again, Step 3 will not be required.

No Savings Account Required

There is no need for students to have a Student Minor Account or their parents' Savings Account. The ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card is a standalone product that does not require an associated Savings Account.

Faster Card Activation

Enjoy the convenience of digital Card activation within 5 hours after successfully completing the KYC process.

Safe and Secure

The ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card provides PIN-based security for transactions at Point of Sale (POS) terminals and OTP validation for online transactions.

Contactless or Tap and Pay Payments

Experience the ease of contactless payments with the ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card. Simply tap and pay at POS machines, making your transactions quick and effortless.

Dedicated Customer Care Service

Reach out to us at 1800 1080 or 022-50405238 for any queries or concerns.

FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card?

It’s a reloadable Prepaid Card for students, offering secure and contactless payments without needing a bank account or minimum balance.

Who can apply for this card?

This card is available for students aged 10 and above, offering them a safe and easy way to manage their expenses.

How can funds be loaded onto the card?

Funds can be loaded on the Card via ICICI Bank’s iMobile app, ICICI Bank Net Banking, other banks’ mobile banking apps or Net Banking portals and UPI apps. The Prepaid Card should be added as a beneficiary to transfer funds on it.

What are the Card’s transaction limits?

The Student Prepaid Card has an annual spending limit of ₹10 lakh.

How can I apply for the ICICI Bank Student Prepaid Card?

Contact your ICICI Bank Relationship Manager or visit any ICICI Bank branch.
