Things you’ll need

to apply

To apply for a Gift Card, you need to have an active ICICI Bank Savings or Current Account.

Note: Account should not be due for Re-KYC.

No physical documents required.

About Gift Card

The perfect gift, every time

ICICI Bank Gift Cards are designed to make gifting simple and thoughtful. Whether for personal occasions or corporate purposes, they offer unmatched convenience, flexibility and personalisation.

What makes ICICI Bank Gift Cards special?

ICICI Bank Gift Cards are widely accepted at over 10 lakh Visa-enabled stores and websites. Recipients can thus explore a range of products and services and choose what they like.

Gift Cards are a convenient gift option for occasions like birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and festivals. They can also be used in a corporate setup as welcome gifts for employees, festive gifts, gifts for channel partners, etc.

Why choose ICICI Bank Gift Cards?

Save time and effort: Eliminate the hassle of choosing specific gifts

Flexibility: The recipient gets to choose a gift of their choice

Exclusive offers and discounts: Make every rupee count with value-packed deals

Widely accepted: Can be used online as well as offline across a vast network of merchants

Convenience: The Gift Card holder can check the Card balance anytime, anywhere.

How to Purchase a Gift Card

Corporate Customers:

Reach out to your Relationship Manager

Other Customers:

Visit any ICICI Bank branch

Fill out a simple application form

Load the Card with an amount of your choice between ₹500 and ₹10,000.

Alternatively, 
click here
 to provide your details.

For any support or queries, please contact our Customer Care on 1800 1080.

Redeeming your Gift Card is easy

Present the Gift Card to the recipient 

The recipient can reset the Card PIN through the following methods: 

Download and log in to the 'Pockets by ICICI Bank' app (mobile application) -> Click on 'Prepaid Card' -> Select your Prepaid Card -> Click on 'Set Prepaid Card PIN' -> Enter the PIN and submit. 

Register on inrprepaid.icicibank.com/customer/login.jsp . Go to 'My Profile' -> 'My Setup' -> Click on 'RePIN'. Enter the PIN, transaction password and submit. 

Call Customer Care on 022-50405238 or 1800 1080.

Use the Card at Visa-enabled outlets in India, by swiping the Card and entering the PIN  

Alternatively, shop online by entering the Card details with OTP validation.  

Manage your Gift Card with ease

Through SMS:

The recipient can send an SMS to 5676766/9215676766 from their registered mobile number to manage their Card, as given below: 

To check Card balance: PCBAL<space>Last 4 digits of the Card number

For example: PCBAL 1234 

To reactivate a temporarily blocked or dormant Card: PCACTIVE<space>Last 4 digits of the Card number<space>Expiry date of the Card in MM/YY 

For example: PCACTIVE 1234 12/28 

Through Pockets by ICICI Bank:

Reset PIN: Easily reset or generate a PIN for new or existing Gift Cards 

Set Spending Limits: Control your expenses by defining transaction limits 

Block / Unblock Cards: Secure your Card if misplaced and unblock it anytime 

Enable or disable transactions: Manage usage settings with a single tap. 

Gift Card FAQs

What are Gift Cards used for?

Gift Cards are versatile and can be used for birthdays, festivals and corporate purposes such as employee or channel partner gifting

What is the fee for an ICICI Bank Gift Card?

Each Gift Card costs ₹100 + GST and you can load it with any amount between ₹500 and ₹10,000.

What is the validity of the Gift Card?

ICICI Bank Gift Cards are valid for over a year, ensuring recipients have ample time to redeem them.

Can I manage my Gift Card online?

Yes, you can reset the PIN, block/unblock the Card and enable/disable transactions through SMS or Pockets application by ICICI Bank.
