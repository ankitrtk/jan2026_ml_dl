About Forex Services

How can I buy foreign currency?

You can buy Forex online using Net Banking or the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app, or visit any ICICI Bank Forex branch.

Where can I use my Forex Card?

Your Forex Card can be used at ATMs, online platforms and POS terminals worldwide.

How can I locate the nearest Forex branch?

You can locate a branch through the branch locator on the ICICI Bank website.

What are Foreign Exchange Services?

Foreign Exchange services involve the purchase and sale of different global currencies. ICICI Bank provides these services to facilitate international travel, education payments and business transactions, ensuring you can buy Foreign Exchange online with ease.

How does Foreign Exchange (Forex) work?

Forex Exchange involves the conversion of one currency into another at the prevailing market rate. When you buy Forex, the bank settles the transaction by delivering the foreign currency in various forms, such as foreign currency notes or a digital Forex Card.

Card variant update

Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card: Enjoy premium benefits and higher limits for your global travels.

Forex services related FAQs

How can I buy ICICI Bank Forex Services online?

Log in to Net Banking, go to Forex Services, select Buy Forex, choose the currency and complete the payment online.

What currencies can I buy online?

ICICI Bank offers multiple currencies such as USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD and JPY. The complete list is available on the Forex Services page.

Are there any fees for buying Forex online?

Yes. Fees may include service charges, GST and other applicable costs, which are shown during the transaction.

Can I cancel or modify my Forex order?

Online orders generally cannot be changed, but Customer Care may assist depending on the order status.

What types of Forex accounts can be opened?

Personal travel accounts, student travel accounts and corporate accounts for international transactions.

How does Foreign Exchange work?

You exchange INR for foreign currency at a set rate and use it abroad for payments or withdrawals.

Can I access Forex services on mobile?

Yes. You can buy, sell and manage Forex through the ICICI Bank website or the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app.

How secure are online Forex Services?

They are safeguarded through encryption, authentication and multiple security checks.

What types of Forex Cards are offered by ICICI Bank?

ICICI Bank offers 
Coral Forex Prepaid Card
, Multi Currency Forex Prepaid Card, Corporate Forex Prepaid Card, Student Sapphiro Forex Card, Student Forex Prepaid Card and Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card.

What is the card rate in Forex?

The card rate is the rate applied for card-based foreign currency transactions and includes margins and service costs.
