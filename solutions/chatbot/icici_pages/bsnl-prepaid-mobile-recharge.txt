BSNL Prepaid Mobile Recharge via ICICI Bank

Recharging your BSNL prepaid mobile is now quick, secure and hassle-free with ICICI Bank Net Banking  or ICICI Bank's iMobile app. Avoid visiting physical stores — recharge your BSNL number anytime, anywhere in just a few clicks.

PAY NOW

Why choose ICICI Bank for BSNL prepaid mobile recharge?

Instant recharge

Recharge your BSNL prepaid mobile instantly through Net Banking or the iMobile app.

24x7 Access

Recharge your mobile anytime, anywhere, without visiting any physical outlet.

Browse latest plans

View and choose from the latest BSNL prepaid plans directly through iMobile or Internet Banking.

Secure transactions

All transactions are encrypted and processed with advanced security measures.

Multiple payment modes

Use your ICICI Bank Savings Account, or ICICI Bank Credit Cards.

How to Recharge BSNL Prepaid Mobile via ICICI Bank

Via iMobile App

Via Net Banking

Log in to the iMobile app.

Go to the ‘Bill Pay, Recharge and FASTag’ section.

Select ‘Mobile Prepaid’ and enter your mobile number.

‘Select Operator and Circle’ get updated automatically category.

Enter the recharge amount or browse available plans.

Confirm payment mode: ICICI Bank Savings Account or ICICI Bank Credit Card

A success message will appear, and you can download your receipt.

Log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking.

Go to ‘Pay Bills’ under ‘Payments & Transfer’.

Select ‘Mobile Recharge’ and enter your mobile number.

Browse and select the desired BSNL prepaid plan.

Review the details and make payment using your preferred mode.

Receive a confirmation message once the recharge is complete.

FAQs on BSNL Prepaid Mobile Recharge

How can I recharge my BSNL prepaid mobile plan online?

You can recharge your BSNL prepaid mobile through ICICI Bank Internet Banking or the iMobile app under the Bill Pay, Recharge and FASTag section.

How can I check the latest BSNL prepaid mobile recharge plans?

Use the ‘Browse Plans’ option available in ICICI Bank's iMobile app under Prepaid or Recharge to view the latest BSNL plans.

How can I recharge instantly using the iMobile app?

Log in to the iMobile app → Go to Bill Pay, Recharge and FASTag → Select Prepaid/ Recharge → Enter your mobile number → Choose BSNL Prepaid → Enter the amount → Confirm and pay.

What is the recharge amount for BSNL’s 6-month validity plan?

Recharge amounts vary by region and plan benefits. You can check the latest plan details through the ICICI Bank website or the iMobile app.

What is the validity of BSNL prepaid mobile recharge plans?

The validity depends on the selected plan. Visit the Browse Plans section on ICICI Bank’s digital channels to check plan-wise validity.

Is BSNL prepaid recharge available 24x7 via ICICI Bank? 

Yes, BSNL prepaid mobile recharge is available 24x7 through ICICI Bank Net Banking and the iMobile app, subject to service availability from the biller.

How long does a BSNL prepaid recharge take to reflect? 

BSNL prepaid recharges usually reflect instantly. In rare cases, it may take a few minutes, depending on the network or the biller system availability.

What should I do if my BSNL prepaid recharge fails? 

If a BSNL prepaid recharge fails but the amount is debited, the transaction amount is usually reversed automatically as per applicable timelines.
