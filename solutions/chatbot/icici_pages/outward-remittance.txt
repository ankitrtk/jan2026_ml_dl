Things you should know

Documents for transfer from NRO Account

Source of funds must be supported by relevant documents depending on the transaction type - like rental agreement for rent income, sale deed in case of sale of property, copy of will for inheritance proceeds. No document is needed for select sources like gifts from relatives or interest earned on accounts.

Purpose Selection for transfer from NRO Account

Repatriation from NRO Account requires selecting the correct Purpose of Remittance and Source of Funds in line with regulatory guidelines, including Form 145 & 146 as applicable.

Documents for transfer from NRE Account

No documents are required for transfers made from NRE Account.

Purpose Selection for transfer from NRE Account

Repatriation from NRE Account requires selecting the Purpose of Remittance.

About ICICI Bank’s outward remittance

Online Overseas Money Transfer Limits:

Online Overseas Money Transfer Limits define the maximum amount that can be remitted based on account type, transaction timing and regulatory guidelines. These limits help ensure compliance while remitting money. When you plan to send money abroad from India, understanding these thresholds helps you plan better and execute international remittances seamlessly.

FAQs

Is tax applicable on outward remittance from an NRE Account?

No, outward remittances from an NRE Account are not subject to tax in India, as these are tax-free funds that are maintained on a repatriable basis. No additional documentation is required for such transfers. However, customers must select the correct purpose of the remittance and any tax liability, if applicable, depends on the destination country's regulations.

How can I send money overseas from my NRE Account/NRO Account ?

ICICI Bank Account holders can transfer money overseas from their NRE/NRO Account in India to any bank around the world, using Internet Banking or iMobile App via Money2World or visit the nearest forex-enabled ICICI Bank Branch. 

In how many currencies can I send money overseas?

Overseas money transfer can be initiated in 21 currencies.

These include USD (US Dollar), EURO (Euro), GBP (British Sterling Pound), SGD (Singapore Dollar), CAD (Canadian Dollar), AUD (Australian Dollar), CHF (Swiss Franc), HKD (Hong Kong Dollar), JPY (Japanese Yen), AED (Arab Emirates Dirham), SAR (Saudi Arabian Riyal), DKK (Danish Kroner), NOK (Norwegian Kroner), SEK (Swedish Kroner), QAR (Qatari Riyal), NZD (New Zealand Dollar), PLN (Polish Zloty), BHD (Bahraini Dinar), OMR (Omani Riyal), THB (Thai Baht) and ZAR (South African Rand).

Who can be the receiver for overseas money transfer from my NRO Savings Account?

Overseas money transfer can be done to:

Your own NRE Account with ICICI Bank under the same/different Customer ID/s (such NRE Account can be selected from the dropdown)

Any Overseas Account

How can I transfer funds from my NRO Account and NRE Account using Money2World?

From NRO Account:

Log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking

Go to Payments & Transfer > Funds Transfer

Select Overseas Transfer using Money2World > Repatriation from NRO SB via Wire Transfer

On the Money2World page, click on ‘Send Money’

Choose destination country (select 
India
 for NRO to NRE) and enter the amount

Select the ‘Purpose of Remittance’, ‘Receiver’ and ‘Source of Funds’

Upload the required documents.

Authenticate via Debit Card Grid Values and OTP.

From NRE Account:

Log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking

Go to Payments & Transfer > Funds Transfer

Select Overseas Transfer using Money2World > Repatriation from NRE SB via Wire Transfer

On the Money2World page, click on ‘Send Money’

Choose the ‘Destination Country’ and enter the ‘Amount’

Select the ‘Purpose of Remittance’ and the ‘Receiver’

Authenticate through Debit Card Grid Values and OTP.
