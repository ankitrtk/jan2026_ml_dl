About Campus Power

What is Campus Power?

Campus Power is ICICI Bank’s unified platform that supports students and parents with education loans, pre or post-admission guidance, and value-added services for both Indian and international education goals.

Financial Services Offered

From education loans up to ₹3 crore to forex cards and remittance, Campus Power offers integrated, easy-to-access solutions under one roof helping you finance your learning journey smartly.

Value-Added Services

Whether it's test prep, counselling, or money management tips, we provide support beyond banking to ensure your education journey is smooth and well-informed.

Who is it For?

Campus Power is built for students, parents, and even institutes. Whether you're planning your journey or mid-way through, our solutions are customised for your current stage.

Why Choose Campus Power?

With a global presence across 18+ countries and partnerships with over 1,200 institutes, we bring expertise, scale, and convenience for every learner's dream.
