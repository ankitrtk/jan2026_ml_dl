About the Product

Life Insurance for All

Affordable protection plan for individuals aged 18–50. ₹2 lakh cover in case of death due to any cause.

Simple Enrolment

Available online and offline with automatic premium debit from an ICICI Bank Savings Account.

Pro-Rated Premiums 

Premium varies based on enrolment month and channel,​￼​ at low and minimum rates.

Annual Auto-Renewal 

No need to reapply every year policy renews automatically unless cancelled. 

Government-Backed Security

Offered under a central government initiative in partnership with ICICI Bank and ICICI Prudential Life Insurance.

Policy Renewal

Auto-renews each year 

Annual premium debited between May 25–31 

To opt out, submit cancellation request before renewal

Termination Conditions 

When you turn 55 years old 

Closure of the Savings Account or insufficient balance 

If multiple policies are taken, only one claim is allowed; others get terminated 

Important Note:

For joint account holders wishing to enroll for PMSBY/PMJJBY, requests must be made at the ICICI Bank branch.

FAQs

Who is eligible for PMJJBY?

Any ICICI Bank Savings Account holder aged 18–50 who gives consent for auto-debit and has Aadhaar-linked KYC.

Is a medical test required? 

No medical test is required. Just provide your health declaration and Aadhaar details.

Is the ₹2 lakh cover valid for any cause of death?

Yes, the cover applies to death due to any reason, including natural or accidental causes.

Can I take this plan through multiple bank accounts?

No. Only one PMJJBY policy is allowed per person. If found with multiple covers, excess premiums will be refunded and cover terminated.

Will I get tax benefits on the premium? 

 Yes. Premiums paid are eligible for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

How can I cancel my existing PMJJBY policy?

To cancel your existing PMJJBY policy, please visit any ICICI Bank Branch or call Customer Care on 1800 1080.
