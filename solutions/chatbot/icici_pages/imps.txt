Send money immediately, even on holidays and outside banking hours.

Things you should know

Beneficiary’s MMID

The beneficiary’s MMID is required for IMPS transfers using mobile number and MMID. This is not required for account number and IFSC based IMPS transactions.

Generate MMID

Generate MMID via Net Banking or SMS: MMID [space] <last 4 digits of your account number> to 9222208888

Chargeable

IMPS may be chargeable for certain account types. Charges vary as per slabs defined by the Bank. Check the latest charges on our website: ICICI Bank IMPS Charges

Transfers

Transfers are processed instantly. In rare cases, it could take up to 2 days.

FAQs

What is IMPS?

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) is a real-time interbank fund transfer system available 24x7.

What is MMID in ICICI Bank?

MMID (Mobile Money Identifier) is a 7-digit code linked to your account, used for IMPS via mobile number.

How can I generate MMID Number in ICICI Bank?

Log in to Net Banking or send an SMS: MMID [space] <last 4 digits of your account number> to 9222208888

Can I use IMPS without beneficiary registration?

Yes. Beneficiary registration is not required if you transfer funds using your Mobile Number + MMID via ICICI Bank's iMobile app. However, for IMPS using Account Number + IFSC, adding the beneficiary is mandatory.

 What if the transaction fails?

In case of failure, the debited amount is normally reversed instantly, but in rare cases, it may take up to 2 days.
