About Campus Power Student

What are Campus Power Solutions?

This section of Campus Power is designed to support students at every stage of their education starting from course discovery to online learning and settling into student life abroad.

Discover Courses and Universities

Search and compare global universities, discover trending destinations, and access rankings all to help you make the right career and course decision from the beginning.

Prepare for Admission

From GRE/IELTS prep to SOP and application help, connect with experienced counsellors to make the admission process smooth and stress-free.

Next Steps After Admission

Find details on Education Loans, tax benefits, visa processing, accommodation options and sim card setup for a seamless transition abroad.

Value-Added Learning Options

Campus Power also features online learning pathways across skill-based domains to help students learn flexibly and build strong professional profiles.

Campus Power Student FAQs

How do I find the right course or university?

Use our university and course explorer to filter by country, stream, duration, and more. We also offer rankings and expert blogs to guide you.

What services are available after I get admission?

You’ll get access to 
education loans
, visa and tax guidance, forex and remittance tools, student SIM cards, accommodation booking, and more.

Is admission counselling available through Campus Power?

Yes, you can book a session with our admission experts for SOP writing, test prep, and country-specific advice.

Can I explore online learning too?

Absolutely. We offer multiple certified online courses to upskill while you prepare for your education journey.
