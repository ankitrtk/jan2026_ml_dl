MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card FAQs

Does the MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card provide any offers on MakeMyTrip?

The MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card offers an exclusive MMTBLACK Platinum Membership and a MakeMyTrip Holiday Voucher up to ₹2,500.

Is the MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card free?

The MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card has a one-time Joining Fee of ₹2,500.

How to avail Complimentary International Airport lounge Access?

Enjoy 1 complimentary accesses to international airport lounges with MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Signature Credit Card. Complimentary benefits are applicable only for the primary cardholder.

What is CVV on the Credit Card? How do I find it on my MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card?

Flip your MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card. The three-digit Security Code next to the signature panel is your CVV. You can also check it by logging in to your iMobile.

How to know the Due Date of ICICI Bank Credit Cards?

You can find your ICICI Bank MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card’s due date in your monthly statement, on the iMobile, through Internet Banking or by contacting Customer Care on 1800 1080.

What is myCash?

If you hold the MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Platinum Credit Card , you will earn MakeMyTrip myCash on certain transactions. These rewards can be redeemed through the MakeMyTrip platform against bookings made on the MakeMyTrip platform

What are the criteria for availing Domestic Airport Lounge Access?

To enjoy Domestic Airport Lounge Access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter.

What are the criteria for availing International airport lounge Access?

Enjoy 1 complimentary access to international airport lounges with Dreamfolks membership card provided with your MakeMyTrip Signature Credit Card.

As a Dreamfolks member, you can relax at more than 650+ comfortable and luxurious airport lounges across 100+ countries around the world, regardless of the airline or class you fly with, or whether or you are a member of an airline frequent flyer club or not. This feature is available for Primary card members only.

Please present your Dreamfolks membership card to the lounge staff at the entrance of the participating International Lounges, to avail the complimentary benefits.

Enjoy 1 complimentary railway lounge access in a quarter to participating lounges in India

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹150 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms and conditions, please 
click here
.

What is the Fuel Surcharge Waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders can avail of a 1% Fuel Surcharge Waiver on all fuel transactions of ₹400 to ₹4,000 at HPCL fuel outlet when the Card is swiped on ICICI Merchant Services swipe machines in India. This benefit applies to both the primary Cardholder and the Plus (supplementary) cardholder, helping them save on regular fuel expenses. The waiver is automatically applied when the eligible transaction is completed using the Credit Card.

What fraud protection benefits are available to Cardholders?

Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with Counterfeit Card Liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the Card provides an Account Takeover Cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please 
click here

Over and above the permitted number of complimentary access, each subsequent access will be charged as per rates laid out by each lounge service provider.

The access to railway lounges will be applicable only for primary cardholders.
