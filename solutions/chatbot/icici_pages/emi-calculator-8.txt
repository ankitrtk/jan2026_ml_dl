Education Loan EMI Calculator

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator helps you estimate monthly EMIs based on loan amount, interest rate and tenure. This Student Loan Calculator instantly shows your monthly outflow and total interest payable across the repayment term.

The Education Loan EMI Calculator helps borrowers and co-applicants compare repayment scenarios before committing to a loan. Whether you are funding undergraduate, postgraduate, engineering, management or medical courses in India or abroad, the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator gives complete clarity on affordability. Use this Student Loan Calculator to plan repayment around your moratorium period and post-course income, and to choose the most suitable loan tenure.

Apply now

About Education Loan EMI Calculator

Why Use the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator?

Accurate EMI estimation in seconds:

The Education Loan EMI Calculator uses an RBI-standard formula to deliver accurate EMI values.

Compare multiple scenarios:

Adjust the loan amount, interest rate or tenure to see how each factor impacts your EMI.

Ideal for higher studies in India or abroad:

Whether you're exploring domestic or international education, the student loan interest calculator helps you plan your long-term finances.

Smart planning for students & parents:

Understand monthly cash flows well before applying for the loan.

Benefits of Using the Education Loan EMI Calculator

Choose the right loan tenure: Use the calculator to determine whether a short-term loan (with higher monthly EMIs) or a long-term loan (with lower monthly EMIs) is suitable for you.

Understand interest accumulation: The calculator helps you see how interest builds over time.

Plan repayment in a stress-free way: Evaluate monthly affordability before applying.

Transparent and user-friendly: No formulas, no spreadsheets- just enter the details and get estimates instantly.

Factors That Affect Your Education Loan EMI

Loan Amount: A higher amount leads to higher EMIs.

Interest Rate: The rate varies based on the institute, course, co-applicant’s profile and collateral offered (if any).

Loan Tenure: A longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases the total interest outflow.

Moratorium Period: During the moratorium, interest continues accruing on the loan. Use the Education Loan interest calculator to view its impact.

Eligibility for an ICICI Bank Education Loan

Nationality: Indian

Age: Minimum: 16 years, Maximum: 65 years at the time of loan closure (on case to case basis)

Academic record: Proven good academic performance

Qualification: Completed 10+2 (12th standard) or a Diploma

For pursuing: Graduation / Postgraduate Degree or Postgraduate Diploma in professional education

University applied to: Listed, recognised and accredited institutes (India and overseas)

Loan sanction is based on: Pre-admission sanction, entrance exam scores (GRE, GMAT, etc.), 12th / H.S.C. results, confirmed admission.

Loan Amount:

Domestic &  International studies: Up to ₹ 3 crore

Co-applicant: Father, Mother, Brother, Sister, Spouse (Husband/Wife), Grandparents, Parents-in-law, Maternal / Paternal Uncle

Security: Property (House or Flat), Fixed Deposit, Existing ICICI Bank Home Loan for cross-collateral.

Comparing Unsecured Education Loan Offers

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator helps students compare collateral-free education loan offers by estimating EMI, total interest payable, and overall repayment cost. While comparing education loans, students should evaluate factors such as interest rate, repayment tenure, moratorium period, processing fee, and prepayment or foreclosure charges instead of focusing only on the headline interest rate. ICICI Bank also provides tools such as the Eligibility Calculator, Interest Calculator, Tax Benefit Calculator, and Loan vs Self-Funding Comparison Tool to support informed borrowing decisions.

Repayment Planning for Unsecured Education Loans

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator helps students estimate monthly EMI based on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure. This allows borrowers to understand future repayment obligations before applying for a loan. Additional tools such as the Interest Calculator and Tax Benefit Calculator can help estimate total borrowing cost and available tax benefits on interest paid. Since simple interest may apply during the moratorium period, planning repayments early can help reduce financial burden later.

Education Loan Options Without Collateral

Students without collateral can still evaluate suitable financing options using the ICICI Bank Eligibility Calculator and Loan vs Self-Funding Comparison Tool. These tools help estimate an affordable loan amount and compare the impact of education loan financing versus self-funding. Scholarships or partial self-funding can also help reduce the required loan amount and overall repayment liability.

Education Loan Repayment Planning Before Graduation

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI Calculator helps students estimate EMI, total interest payable, and overall borrowing cost before completing their course. This enables borrowers to compare different loan amounts and repayment tenures in advance. For eligible instant education loans, repayment can begin after course completion, and the overall tenure can extend up to 15 years including the moratorium period. Early repayment planning can help students select a repayment structure aligned with their expected future income.

Education Loan Calculator FAQs

What does the Education Loan EMI Calculator do?

The student loan calculator estimates your EMI amount, total interest payable and overall cost of borrowing. It also compares this with the cost of self-funding, showing you the opportunity cost, potential investment returns and overall savings if you choose an 
Education Loan
 instead of paying upfront.

How does the loan vs self-funding comparison work?

The calculator assumes that, if you self-fund (i.e. pay for higher education yourself without availing a loan), you withdraw money from your savings or investments. It then shows how much wealth you could have built if that money continued to earn returns. This helps you understand whether borrowing is more cost-effective than liquidating your savings/investments.

Does the calculator include tax benefits under Section 80E?

Yes. The Education Loan calculator estimates your potential tax savings on the Education Loan interest component under Section 80E. These savings reduce your effective cost of borrowing, making a loan potentially cheaper than self-funding.

Why is an Education Loan sometimes better than self-funding?

Availing an Education Loan can be more beneficial, because:

You preserve your long-term savings

Your investments continue to grow during the study period

You receive tax deductions on the interest paid on the loan

You pay the education cost in affordable instalments

You maintain liquidity for emergencies.

Can this calculator help me plan my finances before going abroad?

Yes. By showing EMIs and potential savings through investment growth, it helps you plan cash flows, repayment and total study cost before applying for a loan.

Does the calculator show how much wealth I can build if I avoid self-funding?

Yes. The student loan EMI calculator estimates the future value of your savings if they remain invested instead of being used for tuition. This shows the potential advantage of choosing an 
Education Loan.

Is this calculator only for ICICI Bank customers?

No. Anyone exploring the financial impact of higher education in India or abroad can use the student loan interest calculator.

Why should I calculate and evaluate my repayment before taking an Education Loan?

Calculating repayment before borrowing helps you understand your future EMI, total interest payable, and total payable amount. ICICI Bank’s Education Loan EMI calculator can help you compare tenures and repayment scenarios so that you choose a loan amount that remains manageable after your course is completed.

How does the loan amount affect the EMI on an Education Loan?

The loan amount is one of the main inputs used by the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator. A higher loan amount generally leads to a higher EMI and higher total repayment amount, depending on the interest rate and tenure selected. Students can compare different loan amounts and tenures to understand the repayment impact before applying.

How should students use the EMI calculator when planning Education Loan repayment?

Students should use the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator to estimate EMI, total interest payable, and total payable amount based on the planned loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. Where the study cost changes over time, students should review updated repayment scenarios as the borrowing requirement becomes clearer.

How can students review different borrowing amounts before finalising an Education Loan?

Students can use the ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator to test different loan amounts, interest rates, and tenures. This helps compare repayment outcomes before deciding the final borrowing requirement.

How can I factor in scholarships or grants when calculating my Education Loan EMI?

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator allows you to enter your desired loan amount. If you have received a scholarship or grant, subtract that amount from your total education cost, which may include tuition fees, hostel, travel, study materials and other education-related expenses, and enter only the remaining funding requirement as your Loan Amount. The calculator will then show your monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount based on the reduced borrowing. This helps you plan more accurately and potentially choose a shorter tenure or lower EMI.

How do I calculate EMI for a variable (floating) interest rate Education Loan?

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator computes your monthly EMI based on the interest rate you enter. Since ICICI Bank offers interest rates linked to current market rates and allows you to switch between fixed and floating interest rates, you can model a floating-rate loan by entering your current applicable rate. To understand the effect of a rate change, re-run the calculator with a higher or lower rate and compare the resulting monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount. If your rate changes during the loan, ICICI Bank's rate reset options let you choose between a change in tenure, a change in EMI, or both.

How do I calculate the loan-to-cost ratio for my education financing?

The loan-to-cost ratio is the share of your total education cost that is funded by the loan. Add up all your education expenses such as tuition fees, hostel, travel, examination charges, study materials and other related costs. Then divide your Education Loan amount by this total cost. ICICI Bank also offers zero margin for selected institutes, which may reduce or remove your upfront contribution for eligible expenses. You can use the Education Loan EMI calculator to model different loan amounts and understand the EMI and total repayment impact.

What features make the ICICI Bank Education Loan calculator more useful than a basic EMI estimate?

A basic calculator usually shows only the monthly instalment. The ICICI Bank Education Loan tools go further by showing monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount, and by offering additional tools such as interest calculator, Education Loan vs self-funding comparison tool and tax benefit calculator. These features help students and parents plan cash flows, compare scenarios and understand the broader cost of funding higher education.

Can the ICICI Bank Education Loan calculator suggest an ideal loan amount based on future income?

The ICICI Bank Education Loan EMI calculator does not automatically suggest an ideal loan amount based on projected post-graduation income. However, you can use it to test different loan amounts, interest rates and tenures and compare the resulting monthly EMI, total interest payable and total payable amount. This helps you judge which borrowing level may be more affordable based on your own income expectations. You can also use the eligibility calculator to understand how much Education Loan you may qualify for.

How to compute amortization with changing interest rates?

You can use ICICI Bank's Education Loan EMI calculator, which uses an RBI-standard formula and displays the impact of different variables on EMI. If the interest rate is revised, ICICI Bank allows you to change your EMI, change your tenure, or change both before the reset date. The interest calculator can also be used to review total interest payable across the loan tenure.

How to evaluate the impact of interest rate hikes on monthly EMIs?

Use ICICI Bank's Education Loan EMI calculator to estimate how changes in interest rate affect your monthly EMI. The calculator estimates your EMI amount, total interest payable, and overall cost of borrowing, and it displays the impact of different variables on EMI. If the interest rate is revised, ICICI Bank also allows you to change your EMI, change your tenure, or change both before the reset date.

How can I plan my Education Loan amount between tuition and living expenses?

ICICI Bank Education Loans can cover tuition fees, books, travel, accommodation, hostel charges, and living expenses. When planning your loan amount, start by separating mandatory academic costs such as tuition and exam fees from variable costs such as travel and living expenses. You can then use the ICICI Bank EMI calculator to estimate EMI, total interest, and overall borrowing cost for the amount you intend to borrow.

What should I review before choosing an ICICI Bank Education Loan for studies abroad?

Students should review the loan amount, interest rate, repayment tenure, moratorium structure, covered expenses, and whether the loan is secured or collateral-free. ICICI Bank also provides tools such as the EMI calculator, loan vs self-funding comparison tool, and tax benefit calculator to help plan the decision carefully.

What should students review carefully before taking an ICICI Bank instant Education Loan?

Students should review the loan amount, interest rate, repayment tenure, and applicable charges before taking an instant Education Loan. Using the EMI calculator can help them understand the EMI, total interest payable, and overall cost of borrowing before they commit.

How can I compare an ICICI Bank instant student loan quickly?

Students can compare an ICICI Bank instant Education Loan by checking the loan amount, interest rate, tenure, charges, and whether the loan is unsecured, FD-backed, or otherwise structured. The Education Loan EMI calculator helps estimate EMI, total interest, and borrowing cost for faster comparison.

What should students verify before taking an instant Education Loan?

Students should verify the interest rate, applicable charges, repayment tenure, and total borrowing cost before taking an Education Loan. ICICI Bank provides tools such as the EMI calculator and clear charge disclosure during application so students can understand the loan terms before proceeding.

How can I estimate the total cost of an ICICI Bank instant Education Loan including fees?

Students can use the Education Loan EMI calculator to estimate EMI, total interest payable, and overall borrowing cost. They should also review the disclosed processing fee, GST, and administrative fees during application to understand the full cost before accepting the loan.

How can students borrow responsibly with an ICICI Bank instant Education Loan?

Students should choose a loan amount they can manage, estimate EMI in advance using the Education Loan EMI calculator, and review the total interest and charges before borrowing. Planning repayment carefully helps them use the Education Loan in a more disciplined way.

How to project total interest paid over varying tenures?

Use ICICI Bank's Education Loan interest calculator and EMI calculator. The interest calculator shows the total interest you will pay across the loan tenure, while the EMI calculator estimates your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and overall cost of borrowing based on your loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure. The maximum tenure is up to 15 years including moratorium.

What is an Education EMI Calculator?

An Education EMI Calculator is an online financial tool that helps students estimate monthly EMI obligations based on loan amount, interest rate and repayment tenure.

What happens after 20 years of student loan payments?

The repayment outcome after 20 years depends on the outstanding loan balance, repayment history and the applicable loan terms agreed during loan sanction.

How much interest on a ₹20 lakh Education Loan?

The interest payable on a ₹20 lakh Education Loan depends on the applicable interest rate, repayment tenure and loan repayment structure selected by the borrower.

How do you calculate a student budget for an Education Loan?

Students can calculate an education budget by estimating tuition fees, accommodation, travel, books and other education-related expenses for the selected course duration.

Can I use the Education Loan EMI Calculator for different interest rates?

Yes, the Education Loan EMI Calculator can be used to compare EMI amounts across different interest rates, loan amounts and repayment tenure combinations.
