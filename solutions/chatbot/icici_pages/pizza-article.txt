Is your retirement pizza

well rounded?

Get the recipe right!

So, what is common between a pizza and retirement planning. They both need a perfect recipe, a blend of ingredients and right timing. Let’s get ready to make one.

The dough:

Get your dough right for that perfect pizza base and estimate your retirement corpus required accurately, to give a perfect base to your retirement plan.

Tips to estimate the required retirement corpus:

Calculate your post retirement expenses based on your lifestyle and inflation

Identify your and your family’s retirement goals

Don’t forget the 
emergency funds
 needed for contingencies

The round base:

Spin the dough in air for that perfectly rounded pizza base. Do a well-rounded 360-degree assessment of your assets and liabilities.

Tips to assess your finances:

Tag your current investments, for retirement and other goals

Estimate your employer retirement corpus

Calculate your current and future liabilities

The sauce:

The strongest flavour coming out of your pizza is the sauce! Making your own sauce will give you control on the aroma you desire. Likewise, customising your retirement plan will give you the control over your retirement life.

Tips to customise your plan:

Asses the gap in the retirement corpus

Optimise your EMIs and spends

Do goal-based investments

Get an optimum 
insurance cover

Increase your contribution to the Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF)

Add the toppings:

As you choose toppings as per your taste, choose your investments as per your investment personality, and re-balance them periodically.

The cheese:

Cheese is the glue that holds the pizza together. To get hold of your retirement plan, don’t forget to consolidate your finances.

Tips to consolidate:

Consolidate your investments, transactions and spends with one bank

Limit your card usage to the top 2 
Credit Cards
 that align with your post retirement lifestyle

Get one-view statement of your finances

Regularly track your progress

The seasoning makes

all the difference:

Whether it’s the spicy chilli flakes or a hint of oregano, these will elevate the taste of your pizza.

So here are some seasoning tips:

Add nominees, wherever required

Make your spouse/family member, a second holder

Choose ICICI Bank’s iLocker - to store all your financial details

And with that, you’ve got all the ingredients

to ensure that you enjoy good taste,

in your golden years.
