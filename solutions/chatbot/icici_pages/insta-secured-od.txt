FAQs

What is the Instant Secured OD?

Insta Secured OD is an secured overdraft facility of up to ₹3 crore for existing ICICI Bank customers basis their banking transactions and statistical scoring.

What is the minimum and maximum OD limit under Insta Secured OD?

The minimum overdraft limit is ₹15 lakh, and the maximum overdraft limit is ₹3 crore.

Which entities are eligible for this facility?

Insta Secured OD can be availed by:

Sole Proprietorship Firms

Partnership Firms

Private Limited Companies

How can a customer avail an Instant Secured OD?

Instant Secured OD can be availed through the InstaBIZ app, Corporate Internet Banking, or the ICICI Bank website.
