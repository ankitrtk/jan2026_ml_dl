What are the benefits of NEFT?

24/7 Availability

Transfer funds any time of the day, including weekends and bank holidays.

Zero Charges via Digital Banking

NEFT transfers via Net Banking, ICICI Bank’s iMobile app are completely free.

Wide Coverage

Send money to any NEFT-enabled bank branch across India.

Secure and Efficient

Transactions are processed in RBI-regulated batches for reliability.

How does it work?

Net Banking and iMobile app

Log in to ICICI Bank Net Banking or the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app

Add the beneficiary with account number and IFSC code

Choose NEFT as the mode of transfer

Enter the amount and authenticate the transaction

Funds are credited to the beneficiary within 30 minutes to a few hours

Did You Know?

You can make a part pre-payment of your ICICI Home Loan using NEFT. Visit an ICICI Bank branch to get a Service Request (SR) number. Mention this SR number in the remarks column while initiating the NEFT payment. Only transactions with valid SR numbers will be accepted. Minimum part payment must be at least 1 Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI).

FAQs

What is NEFT?

NEFT is a nationwide payment system managed by the RBI, allowing one-to-one fund transfers from ICICI Bank to any participating bank branch in India.

Is NEFT available 24x7?

Yes, NEFT is available round the clock, including weekends and holidays, as per RBI guidelines.

Are there any charges for NEFT?

There are no charges for NEFT transfers via digital modes. Charges apply only for branch-based NEFT.

How long does NEFT take to credit the beneficiary?

NEFT transactions are usually processed within 30 minutes to 4 hours, depending on RBI settlement batches.

Can I use NEFT to make Credit Card payments?

Yes, you can pay Credit Card dues using NEFT by adding the card number as the beneficiary and entering the issuing bank's IFSC.

What details are needed for NEFT?

You’ll need the beneficiary’s account number, bank name, branch IFSC and the amount to be transferred.

Can NEFT be used for foreign remittances?

No. NEFT is strictly for Indian Rupee transfers within India only.

What happens if I enter the wrong beneficiary account number?

In such cases, visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch or call Customer Care to raise a Service Request.

How can I raise a complaint related to an NEFT transaction?

You can file a complaint via 
ICICI Bank Feedback Portal
or escalate it to the Banking Ombudsman if unresolved.
