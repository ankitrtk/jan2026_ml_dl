Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card FAQs

Is the Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card a luxury Credit Card?

Yes. The Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card is a luxury Credit Card from ICICI Bank. It is packed with plenty of premium benefits.

What is the best metal Credit Card currently available with ICICI bank?

If you are looking for the best metal Credit Card, then the ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card is an excellent choice for you. It has top luxury lifestyle offers & benefits.

Is there a joining fee and Annual fee for the ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

Yes. The Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card has a joining fee and Annual fee of ₹12,499 + GST.

How can Emeralde Private Metal Credit Cardholders claim their joining vouchers?

Step 1: Log into ICICI Bank Rewards

•   iMobile App:

Open iMobile 🡪 Cards & Forex 🡪  Select Credit Card 🡪 Redeem 🡪 Joining Benefit

•   Internet Banking:

Log in 🡪 Cards 🡪 Credit cards 🡪 Select Credit Card 🡪 Redeem 🡪 Joining Benefit

Step 2: On the Rewards site, click the ‘Joining Benefit / Annual Benefit’ tab to open the Benefits Page.

Step 3: Find details for your TAJ Epicure and EazyDiner vouchers.    

Your Voucher Code and / or PIN and steps to redeem are displayed next to each voucher

Follow the on-screen instructions for membership activation and voucher redemption.

For details and T&Cs, 
click here

When will I get the Joining Benefits for my Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

You will receive an e-mail regarding the same within 45 business days after the payment of the Joining Fee.

How to avail complimentary international airport lounge access?

Enjoy unlimited complimentary access to international airport lounges with ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card.

Priority Pass Membership

Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for both the primary cardholder and Plus cardholder with ICICI Bank Emeralde Metal Credit Card

Access 1,800+ lounges in 146 countries worldwide

Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.

For complete list of international airport lounges visit 
https://www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

For lounge visits within India, Primary and Add-on Cardholders can avail unlimited complimentary lounge access through ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card. Please swipe the ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card to avail the complimentary lounge access. To view the complete list of lounges, 
click here

What are the golf privileges and eligibility criteria?

As an Emeralde Private Metal, Credit Cardholder, you are eligible for unlimited round or lesson of golf. The benefit can be booked through the Golftripz website. Simply visit 
https://teetimes.truztee.com/
   and create a Golftripz account using the same e-mail ID as registered against your ICICI Bank Credit Card. Benefits will be credited to your account by the 10th day of the following month, available for use in the next two months.

To view the updated list of golf courses, please 
click here

How do I make the booking for Golf Rounds?

Eligible ICICI Bank Credit Card members can book their Golf Rounds by following the below steps:

Log in to the ICICI Bank Golf platform 
https://teetimes.truztee.com/
 with your registered mobile number.

Choose your preferred golf course and select your preferred date and tee time.

Submit your booking request and await confirmation.

Check your Booking History to view all bookings and details.

You may also call i-Assist, our 24X7 concierge service on 1800 26 70731 (toll free for MTNL/BSNL lines) or 022 6787 2016 (standard call charges applicable), for assistance with online booking. An authorisation amount of ₹2 shall be taken for each eligible Card.

What are the movie ticket benefits available Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

Cardholders are eligible for a Buy-One-Get-One movies, sport events, theatre or concert tickets offer worth up to ₹750 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

What are the key features of the concierge services offered?

The concierge services provide 24/7 support for varied needs such as restaurant reservations, flower and gift deliveries, hotel and flight bookings, car rentals and even movie ticket delivery. Additionally, the service offers emergency auto assistance and medical concierge support, ensuring comprehensive assistance during emergencies. Contact i-Assist for access to these services. To contact the i-Assist team, please call 1800 26 70731 (toll free for MTNL/BSNL) or 022 6787 2016 (standard call charges applicable).

What Air Accident Insurance coverage is provided to Cardholders?

The primary Cardholder is entitled to Air Accident Insurance coverage of ₹3 crore. This benefit applies only when the Card has been used at least once in the 60 days prior to the event. Note that the insurance covers international travel only, provided that the tickets were purchased using the Credit Card. Supplementary Cardholders are not eligible for this insurance coverage. For more details on the terms and conditions of the insurance cover on ICICI Bank Credit Cards.

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders can avail of a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions of up to ₹4,000 at any fuel outlet in India. This benefit applies to both the primary Cardholder and the Plus (supplementary) cardholder, helping them save on regular fuel expenses. The waiver is automatically applied when the eligible transaction is completed using the Credit Card.

What fraud protection benefits are available to Cardholders?

Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with Counterfeit Card Liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the Card provides an Account Takeover Cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please 
click here
 .

What is the age limit for the Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

You must be between the age group of 21–65 years to be eligible to apply for the ICICI Bank Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card.

How can I know the bill due date of my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You can find the bill due date of your Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card on your monthly statement, in the iMobile app, through Internet Banking or by contacting Customer Care at 1800 1080.

Is it safe to transact with the Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

Yes. Since your Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip for high-level security, you can transact safely & securely.

What are the key features of the Mastercard Privileges?

Mastercard Moments is an exclusive programme specially created for Mastercard cardholders. At the heart of this programme is a host of exceptional experiences designed to inspire and enrich the lives of our Mastercard cardholders, with programme members able to enjoy additional benefits.

For more details, 
https://specials.priceless.com/en-in/homePage

How do I find the CVV on my Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card?

Flip your Emeralde Private Metal Credit Card. The three-digit security code next to the signature panel is your Card Verification Value (CVV). You can also check it by logging in to your ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking.
