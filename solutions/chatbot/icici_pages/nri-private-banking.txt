NRI Private Banking FAQ

What is NRI Private Banking?

ICICI Bank NRI Private Banking Programme is a dedicated relationship-based offering designed for Non-Resident Indians. It includes a dedicated relationship manager, priority assistance, competitive Forex and loan rates, and a range of NRI Private Banking Services to manage your global finances seamlessly

Who can open an NRI Private Banking Account with ICICI Bank?

NRI Private Banking Account can be opened by NRIs holding an NRE or 
NRO Account
, subject to eligibility criteria such as holding required deposits and assets under management. A minimum monthly average balance and relationship value threshold must be maintained to enjoy programme benefits.

What are the benefits of NRI Private Banking Services?

NRI Private Banking Services offer a global banking experience with dedicated relationship managers across India, UAE, Singapore, Bahrain and GIFT City. Benefits include preferential remittance and loan rates, family banking, multi-currency facilities, premium cards, lounge access, locker discounts, priority service.

What is the minimum balance required for NRI Private Banking?

To be eligible for NRI Private Banking, customers need a total deposit value of ₹50 lakh or a total relationship value of ₹5 crore, including deposits, demat and investments with 
ICICI Bank
. If eligibility criteria are not met, a minimum monthly average balance of ₹15,000 must be maintained. Services are subject to country-specific regulations.
