                            What is a Corporate Fixed Deposit?

A Corporate Fixed Deposit (FD) is an investment option offered by ICICI Bank. It provides fixed returns based on the interest rate applicable at the time of booking. The rate of interest depends on the deposit amount and tenure selected.

                            What is a Corporate Fixed Deposit?

A Corporate Fixed Deposit (FD) is an investment option offered by ICICI Bank. It provides fixed returns based on the interest rate applicable at the time of booking. The rate of interest depends on the deposit amount and tenure selected.

                            Who can avail a Corporate FD? 

Corporate FDs are available to:

Proprietorships
Partnership Firms
LLPs
Private and Public Limited Companies
Trusts
Societies
Government Institutions

                            Who can avail a Corporate FD? 

Corporate FDs are available to:

Proprietorships
Partnership Firms
LLPs
Private and Public Limited Companies
Trusts
Societies
Government Institutions

                            How often will I receive interest on my FD? 

You can choose to receive interest monthly, periodic or quarterly. This option needs to be selected when opening the FD.

                            What is the minimum and maximum tenure for a Corporate FD 

You can open a Corporate FD for as short as 7 days or up to 10 years.

                            How can I book a Corporate FD? 

You can open an FD through:

Any ICICI Bank branch
Corporate Net Banking
API integration
InstaBIZ app

                            What is the minimum lock-in period? 

The minimum tenure for a Corporate FD is 7 days.

                            What types of Corporate FDs are available, and what are their features? 

There are two types of FDs:

1. Cumulative FD:

Interest is compounded quarterly and paid at maturity
Tenure: 6 months to 10 years
Minimum deposit: ₹10,000

2. Traditional FD (with periodic interest payout):

Monthly Payout FD:
Interest paid monthly
Tenure: 3 months to 10 years

Quarterly Payout FD:
Interest paid every quarter
Tenure: 3 months to 10 years

Period Payout FD:
Interest paid at maturity
Tenure: 7 days to 185 days

Minimum deposit for all types: ₹10,000

                            Can I set up auto-renewal for my FD? 

Yes, you can opt for auto-renewal while opening the FD or anytime before maturity. The FD will renew for the same tenure as the original deposit.

                            What is the difference between Callable and Non-Callable FDs? 

Callable FD: Allows premature withdrawal.
Non-Callable FD: Premature withdrawal is not permitted, except under special circumstances like legal or regulatory orders, or in the case of the depositor’s death.

                            Are Corporate FD returns affected by market conditions? 

No. Corporate FDs offer fixed, guaranteed returns for the entire tenure, unaffected by market fluctuations.

                            Can I choose monthly interest payout? 

Yes. You can opt for a monthly interest payout while opening your FD. Please contact your Relationship Manager or click on the ‘Get in Touch’ tab for assistance.

                            What is the ICICI Bank FD Interest Calculator?

It is a tool to estimate the interest earnings on your deposit based on:

Investment amount
Tenure
Applicable interest rate

                            How does the Corporate FD Calculator help? 

It helps you:

Estimate potential returns
Decide the investment amount
Choose a suitable interest payout option
Plan better with clear return projections

                            What happens if I close the FD within 7 days? 

If you close the FD within 7 days of opening:

No interest is paid
No premature closure charges are applied
TDS obligation if any will be applicable

                            Can I withdraw the FD before maturity? 

Yes, premature withdrawal is allowed for Callable FDs. Non-Callable FDs cannot be withdrawn early unless under exceptional legal or regulatory circumstances.

                            Can I avail an Overdraft (OD) against a Corporate FD? 

Yes, you can avail an OD against a Callable Fixed Deposit.

                            Can I avail an Overdraft Facility against a Fixed Deposit? 

Yes, you can avail an Overdraft Facility against a Callable Fixed Deposit.

                            Can I get any fund-based facility against a Non-Callable FD? 

No, fund-based facilities like Overdraft or Cash Credit are not available against Non-Callable Fds.

                            How can I apply for an Overdraft against a Corporate FD? 

You can apply for an OD facility by:

Visiting your nearest ICICI Bank branch
Getting in touch with your Relationship Manager
