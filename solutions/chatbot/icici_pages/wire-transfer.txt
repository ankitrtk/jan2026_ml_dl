Fast Transfer

Funds are received in Indian bank account within 1-2 working days.

About

Wire Transfer

Wire Transfer is a secure, electronic method for transferring funds between banks globally using the SWIFT messaging platform. It's commonly used by banks worldwide for transferring funds across borders.

Secure & safe

Completely safe and secure transfers through SWIFT messaging platform.

Transfer time

Wire remittances are generally processed within 1–2 working days, if both the payment instructions and funds are received from the sender bank.

FAQs

What is the SWIFT Code of ICICI Bank for retail remittances?

The SWIFT code for ICICI Bank is ICICINBBNRI.

Why is it important to mention the correspondent bank details of ICICI Bank’s nostro account?

By mentioning the correspondent bank details, the remittance gets directed in the intended foreign currency and the risk of getting rerouted by the sending bank/ intermediary bank is avoided.

Where can I find the correspondent bank details of ICICI Bank’s Nostro account details?

Click here
 for list of the details of ICICI Bank's correspondent banks as per the currency selected.

Which currencies are acceptable?

Currently, the following currencies have been enabled for receiving the remittances as inward remittances through SWIFT Wire Transfer.

Currencies with direct Nostro Accounts:

AED, AUD, CAD, CHF, SGD, JPY, USD, NOK, NZD, QAR, DKK, EUR, GBP, HKD, ZAR, SAR, SEK, OMR, PLN, THB, BHD, KWD

Currencies where funds are received in USD through arrangements with correspondent banks.

BGN, BWP, CNY, CZK, HUF, ILS, MXN, RON, RSD, TRY, UGX, ZMW, PEN, BBD, BSD, ISK, JMD, CRC, and CLP

Note: The USD Card Rate prevailing on the date of credit to the customer's account would be the applicable exchange rate.

How can I track my incoming Wire Transfer?

You can track/locate your international Wire Transfer in a simple and convenient way through Net Banking by following these steps:

Log in to 
Retail Net Banking

Navigate: Payments & Transfers

Click: My Transactions > Wire Transactions

Select Account Number > Click on: Track My Wire

View status of transaction or act on hold transaction

You will need the following details to track the same:

SWIFT GPI tracking/reference number (which consists of numbers and letters)

SWIFT copy or payment reference number of sender bank. 

In case the status is not available, you may ask the sender to check with its sending bank.

How can I know about the receipt of my remittance through Wire Transfer?

The Bank sends an e-mail on receipt of the SWIFT message and funds in the Nostro Account, to the registered e-mail ID.
