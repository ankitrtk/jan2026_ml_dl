Agri Infrastructure Fund

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund or AIF provides a medium to long-term debt financing facility for investments in viable post-harvest projects. 

              Read Less

 Know more

How to Get Maximum Benefits?

Interest Subvention

Customer pays 9% interest while subvention is 3% which results in a net interest rate of 6% effectively.

High Project Coverage

This scheme allows up to 25 projects per borrower in different locations. This means a limit of ₹2 crore per project up to 25 projects per borrower

Tenure 

The duration is 7 years (including a moratorium period of up to 2 years)

Convergence with other schemes

Convergence with all schemes of central or state government

FAQs

What are AIF benefits? 

For loans up to ₹2 crore, AIF provides 3% interest subvention with a cap of 9% on ROI and credit guarantee support by way of payment of CGTMSE fee

What is the Loan repayment period?

The loan repayment period is 7 years, including a moratorium period of 6 months to 2 years.

Which are the Eligible Projects under AIF?

Please refer to the following for a detailed list of 
eligible projects.

How many projects can be put up?

An applicant can put up to 25 projects in different locations and each of such projects will be eligible under the scheme for loan up to ₹2 crore.
