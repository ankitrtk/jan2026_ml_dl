About Regular Salary Account

No Minimum Balance and Complimentary Transactions

There is no requirement to maintain a monthly average balance. You also get unlimited complimentary transactions at ICICI Bank ATMs across India.

Complimentary Insurance Coverage

Enjoy Air and Personal Accident insurance benefits without any conditions on debit card spends or activation, based on your Salary Account variant.

Instant Digital Banking Solutions

Use ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking for 24/7 access to services like bill payments, money transfers, FASTag auto-recharge, Credit Card management and loan offers.

Loan and Credit Access

Choose from a range of Gemstone Credit Cards, Personal Loans, Auto Loans (up to 100% on-road price) - all with quick digital processing.

Easy Investment and Savings

Invest online in Fixed Deposits (FDs), Recurring Deposits (RDs), Mutual Funds, NPS, or PPF. You can also open a 3-in-1 Salary, Demat and Trading Account with no account opening fee and an AMC waiver for the first year.

Regular Salary Account FAQs

What Debit Card comes with the Regular Salary Account?

Regular Salary Account comes with a Titanium or Platinum Debit Card

Are online fund transfers chargeable?

NEFT and RTGS transfers are not chargeable online. IMPS transactions may attract nominal charges. Please refer to the service charge page for details.

Do I need to maintain a monthly balance?

No. There is no average monthly balance requirement as long as your salary is credited regularly.

Is complimentary insurance provided with this account?

Yes. You get Air and Personal Accident insurance based on your account variant, without any condition on debit card usage.

Can I update my mobile number online?

You can update it through any ICICI Bank ATM or by visiting your nearest branch with a valid photo proof of identity.
