NRI Family Banking FAQs

What is NRI Family Banking?

NRI Family Banking is a premium offering under the NRI Private Banking, Select programme and premium programme designed for global citizens. It provides:

Preferential remittance rates

Domestic & international Airport lounge access

Family banking benefits

Exclusive privileges for NRI and resident family members

Open an Account Now

What is the Money in Bank (MIB) Requirement?

(MIB)

Family Type

Monthly Average balance requirements

NMAB# charges

NRI Select family Banking

Maintain Deposits* + Account Balance of ₹ 7.5 Lac OR Deposits* + Account Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹ 75 Lac

6% of Shortfall up to ₹ 500

NRI Premium family banking

Maintain Deposits* + Account Balance of ₹ 37.5 Lac OR Deposits* + Account Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹ 1.5 Cr

6% of Shortfall up to ₹ 500

NRI Private family banking

Maintain Deposits* + Account Balance of ₹ 75 Lac or Deposits* + Account Balance + Mutual Funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹ 7.5 Cr

6% of Shortfall up to ₹ 500

**Deposits include Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits

^AUM- Mutual Funds Assets under Management

#Non-Maintenance of minimum Monthly average balance

Only Savings Account holders (excluding No Frills, HUF, and Current Accounts) are eligible for Family Banking.

Accounts like Advantage Woman Savings, Senior Citizen Savings, and Youngstars Savings can be included.

Family ID will be dissolved if the MIB is not maintained for consecutive months.

Can a Resident Indian Apply for NRI Family Banking?

Yes, a resident Indian Savings Account holder can be part of NRI Family Banking. However:

The NRI customer ID must be the primary customer ID in the NRI Family ID.

A minimum of two and a maximum of nine members can form a Family Banking relationship.

Open an Account Now

What are the different Family Banking variants?

NRI Private Banking Programme: NRI members get Private Account benefits, while resident members get upgraded to Resident Private Account benefits.

NRI Premium Programme: NRI members enjoy Premium Account benefits, while resident members receive Wealth Account benefits.

NRI Select Banking Programme: NRI members get Select Account benefits, while resident members get upgraded to Resident Select Account benefits.

Why was my NRI Family ID request rejected?

Your request may be rejected if incorrect or incomplete details are provided. To raise the request for family bankin

click here to login

I submitted a Resident form instead of an NRI form. What should I do?

You’ll need to re-submit the NRI Family ID form with complete and correct details. The form is available at your nearest branch or on the bank’s website.

My Family ID already exists. Can I create a new one?

No. If a Family ID already exists, please use the same one for any updates. If changes are needed, request for modification or deletion instead of creating a new ID.

Can a Resident family member be the primary account holder in an NRI Family ID?

No. The primary account holder must be an NRI. Requests with a Resident as primary will not be processed.

What documents should I submit with my NRI Family ID form?

Please submit:

The NRI Family ID form, fully filled and signed

KYC documents (passport, visa, and address proof) for all members

Incomplete or unsigned forms may be rejected.
