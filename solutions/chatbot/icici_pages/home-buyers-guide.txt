Home Buyers Guide FAQs

What is the best advice for buying a home?

Plan carefully, assess your budget, explore Home Loan options and choose a property that fits your needs today and tomorrow. Always prepare for a down payment and related costs.

How can I prepare to buy my first home in India?

Start with affordability checks, save for a down payment and review your credit score. Research property locations and loan eligibility to make a confident buying decision.

What are the requirements for first-time home buyers?

Stable income, good credit score, down payment savings and complete property documents are key requirements. Eligibility depends on income, age and financial profile.

Can I get 100% of the funding for my dream home?

No, banks generally finance 80-90% of the property’s value. Buyers must contribute the balance amount as a down payment.
