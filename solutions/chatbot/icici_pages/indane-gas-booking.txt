Indane Gas Bill Payment

Indane Gas Booking Online with ICICI Bank

Book your Indane Gas cylinder with ease using ICICI Bank’s secure digital platforms. Whether you are at home or on the go, book and pay for your LPG cylinder in just a few clicks via ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net  Banking.

BOOK NOW

What are the benefits of booking Indane Gas through ICICI Bank?

Instant Booking Access

Book your LPG cylinder anytime, from anywhere, using mobile or desktop. 

Seamless and Secure Transactions 

Experience a fast and secure gas booking process through ICICI Bank’s trusted digital platforms. 

Instant Confirmation 

Receive immediate confirmation upon successful booking and payment. 

Flexible Payment Options 

Choose from multiple payment modes like ICICI Bank Savings Account, or ICICI Bank Credit Card. 

No Extra Charges 

Book your cylinder without any additional fees or service charges from ICICI Bank. 

Ways to book Indane Gas online

Via iMobile App

Via Net Banking

Log in to the ICICI Bank's iMobile app and go to ‘Bill Pay, Recharge and FASTag’ tile. 

Search ‘Indane Gas’ using the Search Bar.

Alternatively, click on ‘LPG Cylinder Booking’ and choose ‘Indane Gas’ from the list.

Enter the Mobile Number, Consumer Number and Distributor Code, or Consumer ID. 

Click on ‘Fetch and Register’ 

Confirm the payment mode and complete payment.

Log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking

Navigate to ‘Pay Bills’ under ‘Payment & Transfer’

Search ‘Indane Gas’ using the Search Bar.

Alternatively, go to ‘LPG Cylinder Booking’ & select ‘Indane Gas’ from the list. 

Enter the Mobile Number, Consumer Number & Distributor Code, or Consumer ID to fetch the booking 

Confirm the payment mode and complete the payment securely 

Things you should know

The registered mobile number with Indane must be used to fetch the booking request.
Customers can book up to 2 cylinders per month and 15 cylinders in a year. 
There must be a minimum 14–15 day gap between two bookings.
Aadhaar linking may be required as per recent government norms.
You will receive SMS or email confirmation after successful booking.

Indane Gas Cylinder Booking FAQs

What is the new rule for gas booking? 

Customers must link their LPG account with Aadhaar and can book up to 2 cylinders per month and 15 per year. A gap of 14–15 days is recommended between bookings.

How do I place an Indane Gas order via ICICI Bank? 

Log in to Net Banking or the iMobile Pay app. Go to ‘Bill Pay and Recharge’, choose ‘Indane Gas’, enter your registered mobile number, fetch your bill, and complete the payment.

What is the allowed time gap between two Indane Gas bookings? 

There must be a minimum 14–15 day interval between bookings to ensure compliance with provider guidelines.

What payment options are available for Indane Gas booking? 

You can pay via ICICI Bank ICICI Bank Savings Account, or ICICI Bank Credit Card through Net Banking or iMobile app.

Which other bill payment and recharge services does ICICI Bank provide apart from Indane gas booking?

Apart from Indane gas booking, ICICI Bank offers payments for electricity, water, DTH, broadband, mobile recharge, and insurance, which can be easily done via Net Banking or iMobile app

How to check Indane gas booking status?

You can check your Indane gas online booking status easily through ICICI Bank Net Banking or the iMobile app by moving to the respective bill payment sections. Then select Indane from the billers in the LPG Gas cylinder section, enter your customer ID and fetch booking details.
