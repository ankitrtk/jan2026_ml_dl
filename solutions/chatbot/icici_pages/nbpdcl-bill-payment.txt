NBPDCL Electricity Bill Payment (NBPDCL)

Pay your NBPDCL bills seamlessly online. Avail advantages like instant payments andsecure transactions so that you continue to get uninterrupted power supply. Now, settle yourelectricity dues for the North Bihar Power Distribution Company Limited with ease,anytime and anywhere.

PAY NOW

Why Choose ICICI Bank for NBPDCL online electricity bill payment?

Instant Bill Access

Quickly retrieve and pay your NBPDCL electricity bill with just your Consumer Number.

No Queues or Delays

Skip physical visits to NBPDCL centres and pay securely through ICICI Bank’s digital channels.

24x7 Convenience

Make your payments anytime, from anywhere.

Secure Transactions

Payments are processed through encrypted and secure channels.

AutoPay Facility

Avoid missed due dates by automating your monthly NBPDCL bill payments.

Zero Extra Charges

No convenience fees are levied when paying through ICICI Bank.

Ways to pay your NBPDCL electricity bill

Via iMobile App

Via Internet Banking

Login & navigate to the ‘Bill Pay, Recharge & FASTag’ section.

Search and select North Bihar Power or, click on ‘Electricity’ category

Search and select from the biller list.

Enter your CA Number to fetch the bill

Review bill details and select payment mode: ICICI Bank Savings Account, or ICICI Bank Credit Card.

‘Swipe to Pay’ to complete the transaction. A payment success screen appears to confirm the payment. You can share/ download the receipt

Login & navigate to ‘Pay Bills’ under ‘Payments & Transfer’

Search and select North Bihar Power Distribution Company Ltd from the biller list

Enter your CA Number to fetch the bill

Review bill details and enable AutoPay if required

Choose a payment mode (ICICI Bank Savings Account/ Credit Card) and authenticate using OTP

Click on ‘Pay Bill’ to complete the transaction. A payment success screen appears to confirm the payment. You can share/ download the receipt.

NBPDCL Bill Payment FAQs

How can I download my bill payment receipt for my NBPDCL bill?

Once your payment is successful via Internet Banking or iMobile app, click on ‘DownloadReceipt’ on the confirmation screen to save your payment proof.

How can I check and pay a North Bihar electricity bill through ICICI Bank?

Log in to ICICI Bank Internet Banking or iMobile app. Go to ‘Bill Pay’, search for NBPDCL,fetch the latest bill, and proceed to pay securely.

Does ICICI Bank provide other bill payment services apart from electricity billpayment?

Yes, ICICI Bank supports payments for water bills, piped gas, 
Mobile Recharges
, 
DTH
, 
Credit Card bills
, and insurance premiums through its digital platforms.

Can I schedule automatic payments for my NBPDCL bills?

Yes, you can activate AutoPay from the payment screen via Internet Banking or iMobileapp to ensure your NBPDCL bill is paid automatically every month.
