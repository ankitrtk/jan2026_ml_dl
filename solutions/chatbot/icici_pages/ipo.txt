About IPOs

Eligibility to Invest in IPOs

To invest in IPOs through ICICI Direct, you need to have an active ICICI Direct account. Both existing and new customers can invest, provided they have completed KYC (Know Your Customer) compliance. NRIs are not eligible to invest in IPOs through this platform.

How to Apply for IPOs

Applying for IPOs is easy and paperless with ICICI Direct. Log in to your ICICI Direct account, select the IPO you're interested in, provide investment details, and make the payment using your linked bank account. Your application is processed seamlessly, with no physical paperwork involved.

Key Features of ICICI Direct IPO Investments

Zero Brokerage Fees: Invest in IPOs without paying any brokerage charges

Real-Time Price Tracking: Monitor live IPO prices to make informed investment decisions

Simplified Application Process: Apply online through your ICICI Direct account with no hassle

Comprehensive Information: Access IPO calendars, prospectus, and in-depth analyses of upcoming offerings

Important Requirements

Before applying for an IPO, ensure that you have:

An active ICICI Direct account

Completed KYC (Identity verification)

Your Aadhar card ready for verification

Bank account linked for payments

Once your account is set up, you can apply for IPOs at any time and track their progress via your ICICI Direct dashboard.

FAQs

What are the fees for applying in an IPO?

ICICI Direct charges zero brokerage fees for IPO investments, making it a cost-effective way to invest in new market offerings.

Can I invest in IPOs if I am not an ICICI Direct customer?

No, only existing ICICI Direct account holders can apply for IPOs. If you don't have an account, you can easily open one and start investing.

How can I track the performance of an IPO?

You can track IPO prices in real-time through ICICI Direct, ensuring you are always updated on the latest market trends.

What documents do I need to apply for an IPO?

You need to ensure your KYC is complete and have your Aadhar card and bank account details ready. There is no physical paperwork involved.

Can I cancel or modify my IPO application?

Once you submit your IPO application, it can be modified or cancelled for an application amount less than ₹2 lakh (Retail category), and only upward modification is allowed for other categories, viz., NIB or HNI and QIB. Ensure all details are correct before submission.
