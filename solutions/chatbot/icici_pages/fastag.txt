About FASTag 

About FASTag

FASTag is an easy-to-use, reloadable tag that is affixed on your vehicle's windscreen. It enables automatic deduction of toll charges, so you can pass through toll plazas without stopping to pay the fees manually. FASTag is linked to a prepaid account from which the applicable toll amounts are deducted.

The tag makes use of Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) technology to ensure that the relevant charges are automatically deducted from your prepaid account. So, you see, FASTag is the perfect solution on India’s national highways.

Avail monthly pass online

A monthly FASTag pass is a great choice for people who pass through toll plazas on a regular basis. You can effortlessly buy and renew this pass online using the digital interface provided by your FASTag provider. Follow the steps below to get the monthly pass online:

Steps for FASTag use

1) Affix the tag to your vehicle*

2) Maintain sufficient balance in your Prepaid Account**

3) Zip through toll plazas without stopping to pay cash

*Remove the adhesive strip and attach the tag carefully in the centre of your vehicle’s front windshield- from the inside, just behind the rearview mirror or camera. Once the FASTag has been fixed on the windshield, do not take it out as it may damage the magnetic stripe. Do not use sellotape or any other adhesives to attach the FASTag to your vehicle.

**The applicable toll amount will be automatically deducted from your Account.

NETC | FASTag

National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC) programme has been launched by NPCI, NHAI and IHMCL to automate electronic toll collection to ease traffic woes and create an efficient and interoperable system on a national scale covering all toll plazas.

The Government of India (GOI) has mandated all toll plazas on the national highways to make toll payments electronic. According to the GOI plans, FASTag will be made compulsory on other toll plazas subsequently. Following this mandate, all vehicles travelling through toll plazas will now have to mandatorily pay their toll charges using FASTag. Many parking places in the country are also implementing contactless parking payment through FASTag.

NETC programme uses FASTag device that employs RFID technology, for making electronic toll payment directly from the prepaid account linked to it. Currently the programme is live on 650+ toll plazas across the country. FASTag offers the convenience of cashless payments along with other benefits like savings on fuel and time as the customer does not have to stop at the toll plazas.

ICICI Bank FASTag programme is part of NETC initiative rolled out by NPCI under the guidelines of NHAI and IHMCL.

Features and functionalities of the National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC) FASTag

By using FASTag, you can skip the hassle of long queues at toll plazas.

 This not only reduces your travel time but also the unnecessary fuel consumption caused by waiting in the queue.

Using FASTag leads to a cost-efficient and environmentally-friendly travel experience.

You can recharge your FASTag account by making a payment through cheque or online through Net Banking or iMobile.
