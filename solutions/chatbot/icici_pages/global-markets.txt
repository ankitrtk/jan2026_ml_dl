About global markets services

Global Markets Services

ICICI Bank’s Global Markets Services offer end-to-end FX, derivatives, bonds, bullion and market research solutions, designed in line with global best practices and technology-driven execution.

Forex

Specialized Forex solutions through dedicated relationship managers, providing clients access to forex markets for multiple currencies across time zones for effective risk management.

Derivatives

Customized hedging solutions through active market making to help clients manage their specific currency and interest rate exposures at fair pricing and market views.

Bonds

Market linked debt solutions to facilitate fund raising by clients in need of funding and investment by clients with investible surplus through deal structuring and active book running.

Bullion

Supply of gold and silver on both outright and metal loan basis to jewellery manufacturers and exporters on competitive terms through access to global bullion suppliers.

Research

Deep macroeconomic research updates and market insights to help clients stay abreast of the evolving market conditions and navigate the volatility to their advantage.
