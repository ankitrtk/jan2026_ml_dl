About Defence Salary Account

Comprehensive Insurance Benefits

Enjoy complimentary insurance including ₹1 crore Air Accident cover,  ₹1 crore Personal Accident cover, and disability protection. Also included: ₹5 lakh Child Education benefit (up to 22 years).

No Minimum Balance with Premium Services

No minimum balance required. You also get a Debit Card, cheque book, and access to a wide ATM/branch network across India.

Preferential Credit & Loan Offers

Eligible personnel enjoy Credit Cards with no joining/annual fees (based on rank), loan offers, and discounted processing fees on home, auto and personal loans.

Investment & Savings Made Easy

Invest digitally in Fixed Deposits (FDs), Recurring Deposits (RDs), Mutual Funds, NPS and PPF. Open a 3-in-1 Salary, Demat and Trading Account with no opening charges and first-year AMC waiver.

Seamless Digital Banking Access

Access your account via ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking. Transfer money, apply for credit, pay bills and manage investments from anywhere, anytime.

Terms and Conditions

Complimentary Insurance Covers are valid basis last Salary or Pension, prior to date of loss, drawn in ICICI Bank Defence Salary Account. The terms and conditions as applicable are available at www.icici.bank.in

Permanent total disability and higher education fall under insurance

Child Education benefit only after grade XII and age upto 22 years and restricted upto maximum 2 children

Defence Salary Account FAQs

Is the Defence Salary Account exclusive to Defence personnel?

Yes. It is designed for Defence personnel and offers customised benefits for their banking, insurance and credit needs.

What insurance covers are included with this account?

You get Air Accidental Death cover of ₹1 crore, Personal Accident cover of ₹50 lakh, disability benefits, and a ₹5 lakh Child Education benefit.

Is there a minimum balance requirement?

No. The Defence Salary Account comes with a no minimum balance requirement.

Can I extend benefits to my family?

Yes. You can extend account benefits to your spouse, children, and parents  up to 5 family members.

Is there a dedicated customer support channel?

Yes. ICICI Bank offers a toll-free, exclusive Defence Banking Helpline for all account holders.

If we have an existing ICICI Bank Savings Account, can we convert the same to a Salary Account?

Yes, you will have to submit your bank account details to your Company HR and update Company details through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app.
