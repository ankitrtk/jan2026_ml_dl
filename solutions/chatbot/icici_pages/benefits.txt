How to get maximum benefits?

Safe and reliable investment

Fixed Deposits (FDs) help you earn assured returns that are unaffected by external factors. Enjoy interest rates up to 6.50% per annum.

Flexible tenures

With ICICI Bank FDs, you can choose a tenure from 7 days to 10 years to match your financial goals. 

Premature or partial withdrawal

Access funds in emergencies by making a partial or full withdrawal from your FD before maturity. 

Extra benefits for senior citizens

Senior citizens earn an additional 0.60% p.a. interest over regular FD rates. According to current rates, senior citizens can get an FD interest rate of up to 7.10% per annum. 

Overdraft facility

Get up to 90% of your FD amount as an overdraft, so that you get funds when needed without breaking your FD.

Automatic renewal

Select auto-renewal to ensure that your FD is renewed seamlessly without any manual intervention.

Things you should know

Minimum Amount

You require a minimum ₹ 10,000 to open an FD.

Minimum and Maximum Tenure

You can invest in FD from 7 days to 10 years.

Additional Interest rates

Senior citizens can avail of a 0.50% higher interest rate on FD.

Save on Taxes

Invest in Tax-Saver FDs to get tax deductions.

Create a Fixed Deposit (FD) in 60 seconds!

 Net Banking Or iMobile

Log into Net Banking or iMobile

Navigate to the Fixed Deposits section and click on ‘Open FD’/ ‘Create FD’

Select the FD amount & duration (tenure)

Select your interest payout plan and you are done!

Features of Fixed Deposits

Interest rate

Competitive interest rates, which vary based on FD tenure. Senior citizens get an additional interest rate of 0.50% p.a. over regular rates

Safety

FDs are low-risk investments as they are not market-linked and offer assured returns. Deposits in banks are insured up to ₹ 5 lakh

Liquidity

While FDs have a fixed tenure, premature withdrawals are allowed with a penalty, ensuring partial liquidity

Tax benefits

Tax-Saver FDs with a 5-year lock-in period offer deductions under Section 80C, reducing taxable income.

FAQ's

What is a Fixed Deposit (FD)?

A Fixed Deposit is a secure investment option provided by banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), that offers Assured returns at 
fixed interest rates
 based on the deposit tenure and amount.

What types of FDs does ICICI Bank offer?

Traditional FD: Interest is credited monthly or quarterly

Cumulative FD: Interest is compounded quarterly and paid as a lump sum when the FD matures.

Who can open a Fixed Deposit with ICICI Bank?

Individuals and entities such as:

Residents and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)

Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs)

Sole proprietorships, partnerships, companies and trusts

Minors with guardianship (KYC verification required).

What is the minimum amount required for opening an FD?

The minimum amount is ₹ 10,000 for Fixed Deposits.

What is the tenure range for ICICI Bank FDs?

You can choose a tenure from 7 days to 10 years.

How do I open an FD online with ICICI Bank

You can open an FD through:

iMobile app

Internet Banking

ICICI Bank ATMs

ICICI Bank Branch

Customer Care (1800 1080).

Can I withdraw funds from my FD before maturity?

Yes, you can opt for premature or partial withdrawal. Penalties are levied for early withdrawals as given below:

For deposits below ₹ 5 crore: Penalty of 0.50%-1.00%

For deposits of ₹ 5 crore and above: Penalty of 0.50%-1.50%.

What is the interest payout frequency?

Traditional FD: Interest is paid monthly or quarterly

Reinvestment FD: Interest is compounded quarterly and paid at maturity.

Is TDS applicable on FD interest?

TDS is applicable if the total annual interest exceeds ₹ 50,000. To avoid TDS, eligible customers can submit Form 15G / 15H based on age and income criteria.

Can I avail of a loan or overdraft against my FD?

Yes, ICICI Bank offers an overdraft of up to 90% of the FD value including accrued interest, without breaking the FD

Does ICICI Bank offer special benefits for senior citizens?

Yes, senior citizens earn an additional 0.60% interest on their Fixed Deposits.

What happens if my FD matures and I don’t withdraw funds?

If auto-renewal is enabled in your FD, it will be renewed automatically for the same tenure at the prevailing interest rate. If not, the maturity amount will be credited to your linked Account.

How can I calculate the interest on my FD?

You can use the ICICI Bank FD Calculator to estimate the returns. 
Click here
 to use the calculator.

Are there any charges for premature closure of FD within 7 days?

No interest is paid for closures within 7 days of opening an FD and no penalty is applicable.

What documents are needed to open an FD?

Valid KYC documents such as PAN Card and Aadhaar Card / passport are required to 
open a Fixed Deposit
.
