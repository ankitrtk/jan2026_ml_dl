About Home Loan Eligibility Calculator

Home Loan EMI Eligibility Calculator- Plan your dream home with ease

When you are applying for a Home Loan with ICICI Bank, you can check your Home Loan eligibility with our online calculator. By using the Home Loan Eligibility Calculator, you can find out the eligible loan amount that you can get from the bank, based on your income and existing financial commitments.

How to calculate your Home Loan EMI?

The process of calculating the Housing Loan eligibility and EMI becomes very easy with our tool. The formula used here is:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

Where:

P = Principal loan amount

N = Loan tenure in months

R = Monthly interest rate

To know the monthly interest rate (R), take the annual interest rate, divide it by 12 and then convert it to a decimal by dividing it by 100. For example, for a ₹10,00,000 Loan at an illustrative annual interest rate of 9% over 120 months (12 years), your EMI would be around ₹26,602 per month.

Steps to use ICICI Bank’s Home Loan Eligibility Calculator

The usage of the online eligibility calculator is very simple for loan applicants. Here is how you can use it:  

Start by entering your monthly income, existing other EMIs, tenure, and interest rate. 

Once you give all these details to the tool, it will instantly give you the eligible loan amount and EMI. 

Home Loan Eligibility Criteria

20 to 65 years of age for salaried individuals 

21 to 70 years of age for self-employed individuals 

Stable and regular income source 

Consistent employment or business history 

Good credit score, ideally 700 or above 

Loan amount based on income and repayment capacity 

How to Increase Home Loan Eligibility? 

Here are some ways by which you can boost your Home Loan eligibility: 

Try to maintain a strong credit score  

Adding a co-applicant can help boost the loan eligibility  

Clear existing debts or loans 

Show a stable employment and income history so that the bank can easily approve the loan 

Impact of Co-applicant, Employment Type & Credit Evaluation on Home Loan Eligibility

Adding a co-applicant with an independent source of income may strengthen the overall repayment profile and may positively impact Home Loan eligibility, subject to ICICI Bank’s internal assessment criteria. Eligibility assessment may vary for salaried and self-employed applicants based on factors such as income stability, repayment capacity, existing financial obligations, business continuity, cash flows, and supporting financial documents.

The Home Loan eligibility calculator provides indicative results for informational purposes only. Final loan eligibility, sanctioned loan amount, applicable interest rate, and loan approval are subject to detailed credit evaluation, verification, policy norms, and other terms and conditions of ICICI Bank.

Home Loan Eligibility Calculator FAQs

What is Home Loan EMI?

Equated Monthly Instalment or EMI is a fixed repayment of a 
Home Loan
, which you pay every month for a specific duration. This payment structure follows a pre-defined Amortisation Schedule.

What is an Amortisation Schedule?

An Amortisation Schedule is your financial roadmap, detailing:

The outstanding balance before each EMI payment

The total EMI amount

Breakdown of principal repayment

Breakdown of interest payment

Remaining Loan balance post EMI payment
.

How do part payments affect EMI?

With part payments, you can reduce the outstanding balance, thereby lowering the interest.

Part payment is credited as principal amount. This lowers the total amount of interest you need to pay, where you can either keep the EMI amount same by reducing tenure or keep the tenure same by reducing the EMI amount.

How does an EMI Calculator help?

An EMI Calculator is a perfect option for Home Loan borrowers. By simply entering the Loan amount, interest rate and tenure, you can:

Quickly calculate your EMIs

Understand the total interest payable

Explore various scenarios (like prepayment or interest rate changes)

Plan effectively, ensuring your Home Loan fits your budget.

Why should I calculate EMIs?

Calculating your EMIs is crucial for effective financial management. Here’s why:

Financial Planning: 
Having an idea about your EMIs allows you to plan finances, ensuring you stay within your plans

Loan Comparison:
 Start with a basic comparison of interest on Home Loans from various lenders, to assess the best deal

Interest Savings: 
Assess different scenarios and get an understanding of long-term Loan specifics

Prepayment Strategy: 
Plan for prepayments to shorten your Loan tenure and save on interest.

How does age impact Home Loan Eligibility and loan tenure?

Age is a key factor in Home Loan eligibility. Here’s how:

Lenders check Home Loan eligibility and tenure based on age.

Age-based criteria align loan terms with your income and retirement plans.

Understanding age criteria for loans, helps you choose the right tenure and customise the loan to your life stage. Use a Housing Loan eligibility calculator to compare or customise the loan terms.

The exact amount that you can get for a Home Loan depends on various factors like your age, income, and credit score. However, to get an idea of the eligible loan amount, you can use the Home Loan Eligibility Calculator from ICICI Bank and get results immediately. 

What are the Home Loan eligibility criteria?

The common Home Loan eligibility criteria of ICICI Bank are as follows: 

Available for both salaried and self-employed individuals. 

A minimum monthly salary of ₹25,000 is required for salaried individuals and ₹30,000 for self employed individuals 

Applicants must be between 20* and 70* years of age. (*varies with different conditions) 

Loan repayment period can be up to 30 years.

With a monthly income of ₹50,000, the exact loan amount depends on many important factors like your credit score, existing EMIs and tenure. Using a House Loan eligibility calculator can give a more accurate estimate.

How much EMI for a ₹40 lakh Home Loan for 20 years?

The exact EMI depends upon the interest rate you qualify for. Know your EMI by using a Housing Loan eligibility calculator for precise results. You need to enter a few important details like the loan amount, interest rate and the tenure to get the EMI.

What inputs are needed to check ICICI Bank Home Loan eligibility?

It is required to provide monthly income, date of birth, existing EMI obligations, desired loan amount, and preferred tenure. A good credit score improves eligibility.
