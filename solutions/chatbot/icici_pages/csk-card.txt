Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card: FAQs

What is the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card?

The Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card is designed for cricket enthusiasts. You will get benefits like complimentary match tickets, Meet and Greet sessions with players and a chance to attend a training session.

What are the benefits of the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card?

It offers benefits such as fuel surcharge waiver, complimentary match tickets, ICICI Bank Reward Points and Meet and Greet sessions with top players.

How to apply for the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card online?

You can visit the ICICI Bank website and choose the Card you want to apply for, follow the steps and complete your application. The Card will be issued in a few working days.

What is the joining fee for the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card? 

The joining fee for the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card is ₹500 + GST.

What are the joining benefits of the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card? 

You will receive 2,000 ICICI Bank Reward Points as a joining and renewal gift. 

What are the annual benefits of the Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card?

You will receive an annual fee waiver on spending more than ₹1,50,000 in the previous year.

What exclusive benefits can I get with this Card?

A chance to meet your favourite cricket players, attend a training session of the CSK team and receive autographed merchandise from a marquee player. 

How can I earn Reward Points on this card?

You can easily earn 10 Reward Points on every ₹100 spent (except fuel) on CSK match days. You can redeem these points for gifts and rewards.

What exclusive benefits can a top spender enjoy with the Chennai Super Kings Credit Card?

The top spender gets the following exclusive benefits:

An exclusive meet-and-greet session with select players

An opportunity to attend a practice session with the Chennai Super Kings team

Complimentary tickets to a Chennai Super Kings home match, during the playing season.

To know more about the Top Spender campaign, 
click here

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

Please note that the airport lounge access benefit is available only for primary Cardholders. For more details on the criteria, click here. To view the complete list of lounges, 
click here
.

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Chennai Super Kings ICICI Bank Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter

Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

 Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

What fraud protection benefits are available to Cardholders?

Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with Counterfeit Card Liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the Card provides an Account Takeover Cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please click here

What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?

Cardholders can avail of a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions of up to ₹4,000 at any fuel outlet in India. This benefit applies to both the primary Cardholder and the Plus (supplemental) cardholder, helping them save on regular fuel expenses. The waiver is automatically applied when the eligible transaction is completed using the Credit Card.
