What is ICICI Bank InstaBIZ?

ICICI Bank InstaBIZ is an all-in-one business banking mobile app that provides all services under one roof to help customers with their business requirements. They can apply for an overdraft, make payments, order a chequebook and do a lot more with the app anytime, anywhere. This app is now available for all ICICI Bank and non-ICICI Bank customers.

How can customers make payments through ICICI Bank InstaBIZ?

Existing ICICI Bank Current Account customers can make payments using ICICI Bank InstaBIZ through multiple modes:

Funds Transfer - NEFT, RTGS and IMPS:

Go to ‘Fund Transfer’ > Register a Beneficiary > Authenticate > Enter amount > Proceed.

(Quick Fund transfer option helps for payment ₹20,000 per day per payee without registering beneficiary)

UPI – Go to UPI > Enter UPI ID or Contact No or VPA details or Scan QR > Authenticate > Enter amount > proceed

What services are available on the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app?

Customers can use the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app for:

Payments solutions – Fund Transfer, UPI, Bill Payments and GST

Trade services – Inward and outward remittance, EEFC to INR conversion, Trade one view dashboard

Merchant services – Collections, sub merchant management, ordering QR or standee etc

Card services – Debit Card, Credit Card, Forex.

Services like - Cheque book services, nominee addition, linking of Accounts, cash management services

Download the app now and start experiencing the all-in-one Business Banking app.

Is there a fee for using the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app?

No. ICICI Bank does not charge a fee for using the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app. Your telecom operator may charge a fee for data usage (internet browsing) or for the SMS service on your mobile device.

Which services are available to a ‘Guest User’?

A ‘Guest User’ can avail the following services on the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app:

Link any bank account to start transacting

Access Merchant Services and Instant merchant onboarding for UPI or QR

Apply for Instant Overdraft

Open a Current Account, RERA Account or Trade Account

Trade Emerge platform for all export-import transactions.

Is the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app secure for transactions?

Yes, the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app is completely secure with end-to-end encryption and single or dual authorisation using grid value and OTP, ensuring that all transactions are protected.

Can we give approvals using the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app?

Yes, role-based access allows log in as either a ‘Maker’ or a ‘Checker’. A ‘Maker’ can raise an approval request and a ‘Checker’ can approve the request using the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app.
