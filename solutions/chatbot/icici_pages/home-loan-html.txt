About Home Loans

Understanding a Home Loan

A Home Loan is a financial product that helps individuals purchase, build or renovate their homes. It provides funds based on the property value and the applicant’s eligibility. The loan is typically repaid in monthly instalments (EMIs) over a long tenure.

Advantages of Home Loan

Home Loans offer significant advantages, such as attractive interest rates, the 
home loan tax benefit
 under Section 80C (old tax regime) and the ability to own a property without full upfront payment. Home Loans provide flexible repayment options, making it easier to manage finances. With a Home Loan, buying a home becomes more affordable and feasible.

Home Loan disbursement process

Click here
 to know about the Home Loan disbursement process.

Are Home Loans a good option to buy property?

Home Loans make it easier to invest in real estate, allowing individuals to purchase their dream home without having to save for and pay the entire price upfront. With attractive 
home loan interest rates
 and long repayment periods, Home Loans ensure that buying a home is more accessible, affordable and financially manageable.

Home Loan eligibility Criteria

When you apply for a Home Loan, there are a few important eligibility conditions that you have to meet. These are as follows:

Employment Type: Salaried or self-employed individuals

Age:

              Salaried Individuals: 20 to 65 years

              Self-employed Individuals: 21 to 70 years

Credit Score: 700 and above

Citizenship: Indian residents & NRIs

Income Proof: Must have stable and regular income.

Types of Home Loans

When considering a Home Loan, it is essential to understand the various options available, as listed below:

Home Loan Top-Ups: These are available to existing ICICI Bank Home Loan customers who need additional funds for home renovation or extension

Step-Up Home Loans: Tailored for young salaried individuals, this option allows you to get a higher loan amount with moderate initial EMIs, which reduces financial strain in the initial years. The EMIs increase with time, to reflect the increase in salary.

Land Loans: For purchasing land as a future investment, ICICI Bank offers Land Loans at attractive interest rates with minimal documentation.

Understanding these loan options can help you choose the one that best aligns with your financial goals and housing needs.

Home Loan FAQs

What is a Home Loan?

A Home Loan provides funds for purchasing, constructing or renovating a property. It is repaid in monthly installments (known as EMIs) over a long period.

How do I apply for a Home Loan with ICICI Bank?

You can apply through the iMobile app, Net Banking, the ICICI Bank website or by visiting any ICICI Bank Branch.

What factors affect home loan eligibility?

Home loan eligibility
  is based on the age (20–65 years for salaried individuals, 21–70 years for self-employed individuals), income, employment stability and credit score (ideally 750+) of the applicant.

What is a floating rate of interest of a Home Loan?

Floating rate of interest of a Home Loan means that the interest rate is not fixed and can change over time. The change takes place as per changes in a benchmark rate (the repo rate set by RBI (Reserve Bank of India).

What is the meaning of a fixed rate of interest on a home loan?

A fixed rate of interest of a Home Loan means that the rate of interest does not change throughout the tenure of the loan. Regardless of market fluctuations, the borrower pays interest at the same interest rate through the same Equated Monthly Instalments (EMIs), from the loan's inception to its conclusion. This stability provides predictability in monthly payments, allowing homeowners to plan their finances without being affected by interest rate variations in the market.

Is there any tax benefit available on Home Loans?

Yes, Home Loans offer tax benefits. You can claim the amount paid towards the repayment of the principal and the interest components as deductions in your income if you opt for the old tax regime. The limits on the deductible amount are governed by the applicable Income Tax laws.

How long does it take to get an ICICI Bank House Loan sanctioned? 

At ICICI Bank, the duration for sanctioning a Home Loan varies, depending on factors such as the accuracy of the applicant’s documentation, their credit history, property evaluation and the applicant's credit score. The process can take one to two weeks. ICICI Bank usually requires thorough verification of the applicant's documents such as income proof, proof of identity and property documents. The time taken for property valuation and legal checks also contributes to the overall processing time. Applicants should provide all necessary documents promptly to expedite the Home Loan approval process.

What is the maximum home loan I can get?

Your eligibility for a Home Loan is determined by factors such as your income, credit score and the value of the property. Generally, lenders offer around 80-90% of the property's value as the loan amount. Your ability to repay and financial stability are crucial in deciding the loan amount you can receive.

Who is the co-applicant in a home loan?

A co-applicant is someone who applies for the loan alongside the borrower. A co-applicant for a Home Loan can be a spouse, parent or sibling. The co-applicant’s income and credit history are taken into account, potentially leading to a more favourable loan amount and terms.

Can I repay my outstanding Home Loan amount?

You have the option to repay your Home Loan at any time. You can make full or partial pre-payments to lower your interest expenses and shorten the repayment period. It is advisable to confirm with your lender regarding any pre-payment penalties or terms.

What is the Home Loan processing fee?

A processing fee is a small charge levied by the Bank to review and process your Home Loan application before approval. Click here to know the detailed 
fees & charges
.

Is Home Loan insurance mandatory?

Home Loan insurance isn’t mandatory, but it’s highly recommended. It helps repay the loan in case of emergencies or unfortunate events like the demise of the borrower.

What are the different types of Home Loans?

Common Home Loans from ICICI Bank include:

Home Loan

Home Improvement Loan

Land Loan

Home Loan Balance Transfer

Step Up Home Loan.

How to apply for Home Loan at ICICI Bank?

With ICICI Bank, you can apply for a Home Loan online using Net Banking / iMobile or by visiting the nearest Branch.

What is the step-by-step process to get an ICICI Bank Home Loan?

Home Loan can be applied online or by visiting a branch. After submitting the required documents, eligibility will be assessed, followed by issuance of the sanction letter, property verification, and final disbursement.
