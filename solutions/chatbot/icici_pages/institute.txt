About Campus Power Institute

Digital Fee Collection

Enable quick and secure fee collection through EazyPay. Accept payments via netbanking, RTGS, and POS to simplify campus payment workflows and reduce manual tracking.

Cashless Campus Card

Empower students and staff with a dual-purpose card that acts as an ID and payment tool across campus-canteen, library and more-creating a seamless, cash-free ecosystem.

BizPay 360 Payouts

Disburse single or bulk payments like salaries and vendor bills securely online. Use ICICI’s platform to streamline finance ops with one-click digital transactions.

Inward Remittance Support

Receive 
overseas education loan
 payments swiftly with inward wire remittance solutions. ICICI Bank offers cross-border solutions tailored to international education transactions.

Easy Onboarding Process

Institutes can apply through dedicated ICICI Bank representatives. Required documents typically include institution accreditation proof, PAN and bank statements for verification.

Customisable Institutional Solutions

Campus Power products are modular-institutes can select specific services like insurance, remittance, or collections based on their digital readiness and operational scale.

Branch at Campus

Millennial-focused, Wi-Fi enabled branch with co-working space, exclusive events, mentoring and financial guidance-right within the campus to enhance student and staff experiences.

What are the key features of Corporate Net Banking?

Corporate Internet Banking provides a variety of secure and user-friendly transactional and non-transactional services. Non-transactional services include real-time account balance information, access to 'Trade MIS', and more. Transactional services include funds transfers to channel partners, online tax payment facilities, and more.
