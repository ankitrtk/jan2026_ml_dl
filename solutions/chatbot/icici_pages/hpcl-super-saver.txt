ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card FAQs

Is the HPCL Super Saver Credit Card a fuel Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

Yes. The HPCL Super Saver Credit Card is an ICICI Bank fuel Credit Card that helps you cut costs with cashback and savings on fuel, utility and departmental store transactions.

Is there a joining fee for ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

Yes. The HPCL Super Saver Credit Card has a joining fee of ₹500 + GST.

Is there an annual fee for ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

Yes. The HPCL Super Saver Credit Card has an annual fee of ₹500 + GST.

What is Card Verification Value (CVV) in a Credit Card? How do I check the same for my ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

Flip your ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card. The three-digit security code next to the signature panel is your CVV. You can also check it by logging in to your iMobile app or Internet Banking.

Which bank’s Credit Card should I choose?

If you are looking for the 
best fuel Credit Card
 then the ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card is an excellent choice for you. It offers many benefits and savings on fuel, utility and departmental store purchases.

What is the age limit to apply for an ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

You must be between the ages of 21-65 years to be eligible to 
apply for credit card
 and get ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card.

How can I know the payment due date of my ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

You can know the payment due date of your ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card through your monthly statements, iMobile app, Internet Banking or by calling 1800 1080.

Which bank gives Credit Cards easily at low cost?

You can opt for an ICICI Bank Credit Card as it offers a hassle-free application process. The HPCL Super Saver Credit Card has low joining and annual fees. For more information about the application process, call 1800 1080.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹75,000 or more on your HPCL Super Saver Credit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.

Please note that the airport lounge access benefit is available only for primary Cardholders. For more details on the criteria,

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets, applicable on BookMyShow and INOX, twice a month. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms and conditions, please

What services are included in Roadside Assistance?

Roadside Assistance includes 24X7 helpline support, on-site repairs for minor issues like flat tires or dead batteries, towing to authorised workshops for major problems, customer conference call, taxi services for up to 50 km, fuel delivery of up to 5 litres, retrieval of spare keys, custody services for vehicles and vehicle repatriation for repairs taking more than two days. To know more about roadside side assistance service and applicable terms and conditions, please 
click here (PDF, 213 KB)
 .

What is the towing benefit under Roadside Assistance?

If your vehicle is immobilised due to electrical or mechanical failure, accident or wrong fuelling, the service provider will arrange for your vehicle to be towed to the nearest authorised OEM workshop. The towing service ensures that your vehicle is safely handled and reaches the appropriate repair facility.

How does the Locked / Lost Key benefit work?

If you are locked out of your vehicle or your key is lost, the service provider will arrange for the retrieval of your spare key from your residence. This ensures that you can regain access to your vehicle without undue delay or hassle.

What is the procedure for using the Roadside Assistance service?

Customers must pay the joining/annual fee to activate Roadside Assistance. After the eligibility is confirmed, your contact details are sent to the service provider, who will then send a confirmation SMS with instructions for availing the services.

What is the Fuel Delivery benefit?

If your vehicle is immobilised due to an empty fuel tank, Roadside Assistance will arrange for the delivery of up to 5 litres of fuel as per actual cost, ensuring you can continue your journey without significant disruption.

What happens if vehicle repairs exceed two days?

If vehicle repairs take more than two days, you can opt for vehicle repatriation to your hometown for up to 100 km, free of cost. For distances beyond 100 km, repatriation services are available on a chargeable basis, ensuring flexibility for long-term repairs.

What is the Replacement / Courtesy Car benefit?

If your vehicle is immobilised and you need local mobility, a replacement or courtesy car of a higher category or the best available vehicle will be arranged at prevailing market rates.

What emergency accommodation is offered?

If your vehicle is immobilised while traveling outstation, Roadside Assistance can help arrange emergency hotel accommodations or medical services near the breakdown location, ensuring your safety and comfort during unexpected incidents.

What are the benefits of accelerated reward points on essential daily spends with ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

Now earn reward points on spends for all your daily essentials like utility, groceries and department store purchases. You get 5% cashback on spends across these categories. For every ₹100 spent, you earn 20 ICICI Bank Reward Points, capped at 400 ICICI Bank Reward points per month.

How can I earn cashback on fuel purchases with my ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card?

Save upto 5% on fuel purchases when the Card is swiped at any HPCL fuel pump on any bank’s POS machine or by using the HP Pay app.

You also get an additional 1.5% cashback (Happy Coins) on all fuel purchases through the HP Pay app at HPCL outlets.

The fuel savings of 5% comprises of 4% cashback + 1% fuel surcharge waiver (The 4% cashback is capped at ₹200 per month).

Why is the ICICI Bank HPCL Credit Card a good choice for fuel savings and daily expenses?

The ICICI Bank HPCL Super Saver Credit Card offers 5% savings on fuel spends at HPCL pumps and HP Pay, including a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between ₹400 and ₹4,000. Cardholders also earn reward points on daily and retail spends, along with movie offers and roadside assistance benefits.
