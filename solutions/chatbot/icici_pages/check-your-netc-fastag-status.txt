        NETC FASTag Status: Track FASTag Application

FASTag

Share

 Copy 

FASTag or the National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC), is an automated toll payment system with electronic tags in India. It uses Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) that allows drivers to pay without cash through the Prepaid option/Savings Account number of the car owner.

The mobile 
NETC FASTag
 has changed the system and made payment on the tolls a hassle-free task. This is the best place to track your FASTag status. In this blog, we will look at how the NETC FASTag status can be checked.

Methods to check the NETC FASTag status

There are multiple ways to check the FASTag status. These are:

1. 
Using the Bank’s website/app

Most banks, including ICICI Bank, offer FASTag services on their websites and mobile apps where you can check your NETC FASTag status. To do so, follow these steps:

Login
: Access your Bank’s FASTag portal or mobile app using your credentials
Account overview
: Go to the FASTag section to view account details
Transaction history
: Check your recent transactions and balance status.

2. Customer Care Helpline

If you prefer personal assistance, you can call the Bank's Customer Care helpline dedicated to FASTag services. Provide your FASTag details and request information regarding your account status and recent transactions.

3. SMS alerts

Banks often send SMS alerts for every toll transaction made using FASTag. While these provide real-time updates, they may not include a detailed account status. SMS alerts and other methods for comprehensive monitoring should be used.

4. Visit POS locations

The availability of certain bank branches or POS (Points of Sale) for the NETC FASTag status allows users to know about their account status in person. This option is beneficial for all those who seek a face-to-face interaction.

Benefits of Regularly Checking NETC FASTag Status

Regularly monitoring your NETC FASTag status offers several advantages. Here are some of the most important benefits:

Financial Control

To monitor your toll expenses, it is recommended that you periodically check your NETC FASTag status. You can handle your finances more effectively and steer clear of unforeseen circumstances, if you stay informed about your costs.

Error Detection

When you perform NETC FASTag status checks

regularly, you can promptly identify any incorrect charges or discrepancies in your account. Reviewing your transaction history frequently makes it easier to detect errors and address them with the appropriate authorities.

Convenience

Obtaining details of the NETC FASTag Account through online platforms or mobile applications provides convenience to users. You can also generate, control and review the account balance and transactions at any time and from any location, simply by recharging.

Balance monitoring

Another advantage of using NETC FASTag is monitoring the balance in your FASTag Account to ensure sufficient funds are available for uninterrupted toll payments. Managing the balance ensures that one does not run out of money at toll plazas, which can be time-consuming.

Transaction verification
:

Verifying transactions by regularly performing NETC FASTag status checks

allows you to identify discrepancies or any hidden charges. Reviewing your transaction history ensures that each toll payment is accurately recorded to detect any potential fraud or errors at an early stage.

Renewal and recharge

Timely renewal and 
recharge of your NETC FASTag Account
 are crucial to avoid penalties and ensure continuous service. If you check your FASTag status online regularly, it will keep you informed about upcoming renewals and low balances, allowing you to take prompt action.

Wrapping up

Checking your NETC FASTag status is essential for efficient toll management and a hassle-free travel experience. By utilising online portals, mobile apps or Customer Care services provided by ICICI Bank, you can stay informed about your FASTag Account balance, transactions and overall status. This approach saves time and enhances your control over toll payments, contributing to smoother trips on road.

For further assistance or detailed information, visit ICICI Bank’s official website or contact customer service dedicated to FASTag queries. Stay updated, stay informed and enjoy the convenience of easy toll payments with NETC FASTag.

FAQs

What should I do if my FASTag status shows inactive?

If your FASTag status shows inactive, check for insufficient balance, pending KYC, or expired validity. Recharge your account or complete KYC. If the issue persists, contact customer care for assistance. Add : Can also check tag status using WhatsApp - type “Hi” from your registered mobile number (RMN) to 8640086400. Use "Check Tag Status" feature.

Can I use one FASTag for multiple vehicles?

No, one FASTag is linked to a single vehicle registration number. Using the same FASTag for multiple vehicles may lead to penalties or deactivation of the FASTag account.
