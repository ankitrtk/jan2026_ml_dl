How to use ICICI Bank Personal Loan EMI calculator

Here's a step-by-step guide to using the Personal Loan Calculator:

Enter loan details: Enter your desired loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure in the designated fields.

Calculate EMI instantly: Once you enter the details, your EMI will be calculated instantly by the online Personal Loan calculator.

Explore customisation: Customize your repayment plans by entering the loan amount ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹50 lakhs and selecting a term between 1 year to 6 years.

Change EMI amount: To reduce your monthly payments, adjust sliders for less loan, lower interest or longer tenure. For higher EMI payments, consider a higher loan amount or shorter tenure.

Key factors affecting your Personal Loan EMI

Several factors influence the EMI payable on a Personal Loan. Understanding these factors can help borrowers select suitable repayment options and manage finances efficiently.

Loan amount selected by the borrower.

Applicable Personal Loan interest rate.

Chosen repayment tenure.

Credit profile and repayment history.

Existing financial obligations and income level.

Higher loan amounts or interest rates may increase EMI obligations, while longer repayment tenure may reduce monthly EMI amounts but increase total interest payable over time.

How is Personal Loan EMI calculated?

Personal Loan EMI is calculated using the loan amount, applicable interest rate and repayment tenure selected by the borrower. The EMI consists of both principal and interest components spread across the repayment period.

Key Components of EMI Calculation

Principal loan amount

Applicable interest rate

Loan repayment tenure

Monthly repayment structure

Customers can use the ICICI Bank Personal Loan EMI Calculator to estimate monthly instalments instantly and compare repayment scenarios conveniently. Personal Loan EMI is calculated using the standard EMI formula based on the loan amount, applicable interest rate and repayment tenure. EMI=P×R×(1+R)N(1+R)N−1EMI = \frac{P \times R \times (1+R)^N}{(1+R)^N - 1}EMI=(1+R)N−1P×R×(1+R)N

P = Principal loan amount

R = Monthly interest rate (Annual Interest Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)

N = Loan tenure in months

The EMI amount includes both the principal and interest payable on the loan.

How to Adjust Your Personal Loan EMI

Customers can adjust Personal Loan EMI amounts by selecting a suitable repayment tenure or modifying the loan amount. Increasing the tenure may reduce monthly EMI obligations, while shorter tenure may help reduce total interest outflow over the repayment period.

Personal Loan EMI calculator FAQs

What is a Personal Loan EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is a fixed amount that you pay every month towards repaying your loan. This amount includes both the principal and interest components of your loan.

What is the role of the Personal Loan EMI calculator?

The Personal Loan EMI calculator is an online tool that can help you get an estimate of the EMI payments of your Personal Loan. 

Why should I use a Personal Loan EMI calculator? 

Provides precise EMI calculations

Simplifies financial planning and helps you manage your finances effectively 

Enables you to compare different loan options.

How many years will it take to repay a Personal Loan of ₹2 lakh?

The repayment period (tenure) of a 
Personal Loan
 of ₹ 2 lakh will vary as per the interest rate. Calculate your actual loan period (tenure) by entering all the details of your Personal Loan into the Personal Loan EMI calculator. 

What is the EMI of a ₹9 lakh Personal Loan?

The EMI of a 
₹ 9 lakh Personal Loan
 varies according to the chosen tenure and interest rate. A better 
CIBIL score
 can make it easier for you to secure an attractive interest rate that might reduce your EMI amount. 

What are the advantages of ICICI Bank Personal Loans?

Here are the key advantages of ICICI Bank Personal Loans:   

Wide range of loan amounts and tenures: 
Apply for a Personal Loan
 for any amount between ₹50,000 to ₹50,00,000 and select a tenure (i.e. repayment period) between 12 months and 72 months. 

Quick online application through the ICICI Bank website or ICICI Bank's 
iMobile app
 with minimal documents. 

After approval, the sanctioned amount is deposited directly into your 
savings account
 so that you can use the funds quickly, as required. 

Get options to prepay your loan without extra charges and thus, manage your repayment as per your convenience. 

How is the interest on a Personal Loan calculated?

The 
interest on a Personal Loan
 is calculated based on the principal amount, interest rate and loan tenure. ICICI Bank uses the reducing balance method, where interest is charged only on the outstanding loan amount after each EMI payment.

What is the EMI for a ₹10 lakh Personal Loan?

The EMI for a 
₹10 lakh Personal Loan
 depends on the interest rate and tenure. You can use the ICICI Bank’s Personal Loan EMI calculator for accurate results. Enter the required details to display the EMI quickly.

What is the EMI for a ₹20 lakh Personal Loan if taken for 2 years?

You can easily get the EMI for a ₹20 lakh Personal Loan by using the interest rate and tenure on a Personal Loan EMI calculator. Just enter these details on the ICICI Bank EMI calculator and you will get the EMI basis tenure and interest rate selected by you.

What is the minimum salary requirement for a ₹20 lakh Personal Loan?

The minimum salary requirement for a ₹20 lakh Personal Loan varies and you can check your eligibility using a 
Personal Loan Eligibility
 Calculator. Monthly obligations and tenure have a significant influence on the loan amount. For example, if your monthly income is ₹80,000 and you have zero monthly obligations, you can get a Personal Loan of nearly ₹20 lakh for 60 months.

How can a Personal Loan EMI calculator help determine the loan selection?

By adjusting the loan amount, tenure and interest rate, the EMI calculator helps you compare multiple loan options easily. It ensures you select a loan with affordable EMIs and a low interest burden.

How do I determine the applicable ROI?

The Rate of Interest (ROI) depends on your credit score, income, employer type and loan tenure. ICICI Bank displays the ROI range on the website and you can check your eligible rate using a loan eligibility or EMI calculator.

What information do I need to input into the Personal Loan EMI calculator?

To use a Personal Loan EMI calculator, you need to enter your desired loan amount, loan tenure and interest rate. The calculator instantly shows your EMI amount and total repayment details.

Can I use the Personal Loan EMI calculator to check different loan amounts?

Yes, you can change the loan amount in the ICICI Bank calculator to see how your EMI varies. This helps you choose a loan that fits your repayment capacity and financial comfort.

Is there any cost to using a Personal Loan EMI calculator?

No, there is no cost to using a Personal Loan EMI calculator from ICICI Bank. It’s an online tool that helps you estimate EMIs and interest instantly with transparency.

What is a “Personal Loan Top Up calculator”?

A Personal Loan Top Up calculator helps you check new EMIs when you take an extra loan on your existing one. It shows revised payments clearly and instantly.

Can ICICI Bank’s standard EMI calculator be used for a Top Up Loan?

Yes, ICICI Bank’s standard Personal Loan EMI calculator can also be used for 
top-up loans
. Just enter the new loan amount, tenure and interest rate to know your revised EMI.

Does the EMI amount remain fixed throughout the loan tenure? 

EMI may remain constant or vary depending on the type of interest rate. With a fixed rate, the EMI remains the same, whereas a floating rate can change over the repayment period as the repo rate changes.

Can I use the Personal Loan Interest Rate Calculator to compare different loan tenures?

A Personal Loan EMI calculator helps compare different loan tenures by adjusting interest rates and repayment periods. It shows how EMI and total repayment change, helping you plan better before applying for a loan.
