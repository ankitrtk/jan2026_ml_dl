How to increase your Credit Card limit

Understanding the how and why of increasing your 
Credit Card
 limit is an important part of managing your finances. In this article, let us explore the world of credit limits, understand why they matter and learn how to increase them.

APPLY NOW

Benefits of increasing your Credit Card limit

Improved credit score

Your credit score is calculated based on several factors, including your credit utilisation ratio. By increasing your credit limit, you can increase your available credit which can lower your credit utilisation ratio and improve your credit score.

Greater purchasing power

Another significant benefit of greater your Credit Card limit is increased purchasing power. This can be especially beneficial in emergencies or for large purchases that may be difficult to pay for all at once.

Enhanced rewards

With an increase in your Credit Card limit, you can spend and thus earn more reward points. Be it accumulating points for travel or taking advantage of special promotions, a higher credit limit allows you to leverage reward point programmes more effectively.

Better financial flexibility

With a higher credit limit you have more options when it comes to making purchases and managing your finances. This can help you to feel more in control of your finances.

How the Credit Card limit works

Let us try to understand how credit limit works for users who hold multiple Credit Cards Rohan has 3 Credit Cards instead of just one. Let’s call these cards as Card A, Card B and Card C, each with a credit limit of ₹ 5 lakh, i.e.

1. Credit limit of Card A: ₹ 5 lakh

2. Credit limit of Card B: ₹ 5 lakh

3. Credit limit of Card C: ₹ 5 lakh

The maximum amount that Rohan can spend is taken collectively across all his Cards. So, the maximum amount that can be spent on Card A + Card B + Card C is ₹ 5 lakh.

ICICI Bank Credit Card Limit Increase FAQs

What are the main Credit Card types?

The main types of Credit Cards offered by ICICI Bank are Platinum, Coral, Rubyx, Sapphiro and Emeralde. You can learn more about several other Credit Cards for travel, lifestyle, shopping and more, like 
Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card,

MakeMyTrip ICICI Bank Credit Card
, 
Adani One ICICI Bank Credit Cards
 and 
Emirates Skywards Credit Card
 by exploring our website. 

What is the lowest Credit Card limit?

The Credit Card limit varies depending on the Card issuer. Please call our Customer Care on 1800 1080 to get more information.

How do I apply for a Credit Card online?

You can apply for a Credit Card on the ICICI Bank website. Select a Card that best suits your needs, check the eligibility criteria and fill out the application form. You can also visit any ICICI Bank Branch where a representative will help you through the process. Don't forget to have your documents like ID proof, address proof and income statements ready.

What are the ways to increase my ICICI Bank Credit Card limit?

You may request a Credit Card limit increase through ICICI Bank’s iMobile app or Net Banking. Eligibility depends on repayment history, income profile and internal assessment.

Is it good to increase my Credit Card limit?

A higher limit may improve spending flexibility. However, responsible usage and timely repayment remain important to maintain financial discipline and account regularity.

Why was my credit limit increase request denied?

A request may be declined due to internal assessment, repayment history or eligibility criteria. Customers may review account performance before submitting a fresh request.
