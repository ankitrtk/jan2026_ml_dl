How to activate Debit Card for online transactions

How to activate Debit Card for online transactions

With the increase in online retail, knowing how to enable Debit Cards for online transactions allows you to make purchases from a plethora of merchants of your choice.

APPLY NOW

Benefits of Activating Debit Card for Online Transactions

Seamless online transactions

Once you know how to activate Debit Cards for online transactions, the online retail world is at your very fingertips

Online bill payments

You can pay all your utility and other bills online once you have activated your Debit Card

Convenient international transactions

Once your Debit Card is activated for online transactions, you can carry out international transactions from the comfort of your home

Avail exciting welcome offers

Activating your Debit Card online can open a whole range of offers, including a free Amazon voucher worth ₹300

How to use your Debit Card for online transactions?

Net Banking 

Log on to a website for online transaction

Select the items and click on the 'Make Payment' section

Enter your debit card number, CVV and expiry date

Enter your six-digit Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the process

Enable international or online transactions:

You can enable your card for International or Online transactions 24/7 by following any of these options:

Log in to ICICI Bank's iMobile app > Services > Card Services > Modify Debit Card Limit
Log in to Net Banking > Service Requests > Bank Accounts > ATM or Debit Card Related > Increase or decrease Debit Card Limit
Log in to InstaBIZ > Services > Debit Card Services > Debit Card Limit Change
Log in to Corporate Net Banking > Service Requests > Card Related > Debit Card Limit Change

In case you require any further information, please write to us at customer.care@icicibank.com from your registered e-mail ID or call our Customer Care.

Debit Card Transaction FAQs

Do I need to go to the ATM to set my Debit Card PIN?

You can set your ATM PIN conveniently through Net Banking or ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

How do I know if I can avail EMI on my Debit Card?

On the check-out page of your online retail vendor, enter your Card details. Then, you will see whether you are eligible for EMI payments.

How can I set a transaction limit for online Debit Card payments?

Log into the ICICI Bank Net Banking website with your user ID and password. Under Service Requests, head to Bank Accounts >> ATM or Debit Card Related >> Increase or Decrease Debit Card Limit.

Is it secure for me to make online payments through my Debit Card?

For security purposes, you have the option of setting a 3D Secure PIN that can be used for all transactions. Do not share the PIN with anyone for your security.

Are there any offers for activating my Debit Card for the first time?

Yes, there are several exciting welcome offers for users who activate their Debit Card online for the first time.

How much can I spend on my Debit Card?

Through your Debit Card, you can spend the available funds in your linked Savings Account.

Can I change my Debit Card PIN online?

Yes, you can change your Debit Card PIN online - through both Net Banking and ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Can I pay bills through my Debit Card online?

Yes, you can pay all kinds of bills, including your utility bills, online.
