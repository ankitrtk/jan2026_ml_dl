About Student Forex Prepaid Cards

About ICICI Bank Student Forex Prepaid Card

The ICICI Bank Student Forex Prepaid Card is designed for students travelling abroad for studies. With competitive exchange rates, multi-currency options and special privileges, it is a secure and efficient way to manage finances without the need to carry cash. Cardholders enjoy added benefits like free international lounge access, Uber vouchers and 
ISIC
 membership to make their student life even smoother.

What documents are required for a Student Travel Forex Card?

Proof of identity: Aadhaar card, passport or PAN card

Proof of Study: University or college enrolment letter

Additional documents: Any additional verification documents as per ICICI Bank policy

Student Forex Prepaid Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Student Forex Prepaid Card?

The Student Forex Prepaid Card is a prepaid card for students studying abroad that allows them to load and spend multiple currencies securely

Why should I opt for an ICICI Bank Student Forex Prepaid Card?

The ICICI Bank Student Forex Prepaid Card is the recommended choice for students studying internationally. It provides competitive exchange rates and the option to load multiple currencies. Additionally, it has advantages like enhanced security with Chip and PIN protection, insurance coverage, easy online reloading and round-the-clock customer support.

Can I use the Card for tuition payments?

Yes, you can use the Student Forex Prepaid Card to pay tuition fees directly to your university in the local currency.

What fees apply to ATM withdrawals?

The first ATM withdrawal each month is free for 5 years. Additional withdrawals might incur fees based on the currency used.

When will I receive my Uber and lounge vouchers?

Dreamfolks and Uber voucher codes will be sent to registered mobile number or email address within 7 working days post initial load.

When will I get the Dreamfolks and Uber voucher codes?

Dreamfolks and Uber voucher codes will be sent to registered mobile number or email address within 7 working days post initial load.

How to avail an International Student Identity Card (ISIC) voucher on Student Forex Prepaid Card?

Visit 
https://order.isic.co.in/ordering/?partnerId=1121
and click on ‘Order Card’

A form will appear wherein the student/eligible customer will have to enter the required details. The e-mail address entered should be valid as the student or eligible customer will receive the serial number on the given e-mail ID. Once the e-mail ID is entered, it cannot be changed or modified

After updating the details and uploading the photograph, Click on ‘Next’

A mock-up Card will appear on the screen. The student or eligible customer has to check all the details on the mock-up Card and edit the details, if required

On the payment page, student or eligible customer has to enter the first 6 digits of ICICI Bank Visa Forex Card in the ‘Discount Voucher’ section. After entering the valid first 6 digits of ICICI Bank Visa Forex Card, the discount will be applied and the student/eligible customer has to click on ‘Place Order’

Once the order is placed, student or eligible customer will receive an e-mail with a link, on their registered e-mail ID, to upload the documents. The student or eligible customer may also e-mail their documents to info@isic.co.in

Once the documents are uploaded, their application for ISIC comes to an end. Once the documents are approved, the card (digital ISIC) will be issued and the serial number will be e-mailed to the student or eligible customer.

How to avail the international SIM?

Eligible cardholders can avail the benefit by clicking on this link: 
https://visaforex.poshvine.com/
 where they will have the option to login using the Card number of their Eligible Card and other details

Eligible cardholders can use their Card number to check the validity of this benefit

After logging in, eligible cardholders can copy the unique code from the offer tile and click on the link to visit the e-SIM or SIM card vendor's platform

On the vendor's platform, eligible cardholders have to select their preferred call plan details (as mentioned in the above table)

Eligible cardholders will receive an e-mail with a link to complete the KYC process by uploading the necessary details (as mandated by relevant telecommunications authorities)

After KYC is completed, an option to select an e-SIM or a physical SIM card will be available in the drop-down menu on the SIM card vendor's platform

For an e-SIM, a QR code will be sent via e-mail to activate the e-SIM. A list of compatible phones will be available on the platform

For a physical SIM card, Cardholders must enter their delivery address and pay a delivery charge of ₹100 for the vendor  to ship the SIM card to the Cardholder's address.
