About Corporate Fixed Deposits

What is a Corporate FD?

A fixed deposit product for companies and firms offering secure returns with flexibility in tenure and payout options.

Why choose ICICI Bank?

Backed by AAA ratings from ICRA and CARE, it ensures capital safety and competitive interest rates.

FD Options: Callable vs Non-Callable

Callable FDs allow premature withdrawal with penalties; Non-callable offer higher rates but lock-in funds.

Flexible investment and payouts

Start from ₹10,000, choose payout monthly/quarterly/on maturity based on your cash flow preference.

Easy booking channels

Book via Corporate Internet Banking (CIB), InstaBIZ, APIs, or visit any branch.

FAQs

What is the minimum investment amount?

₹10,000 for callable FDs and ₹3 Crore for non-callable FDs.

Can I withdraw my FD early?

Yes, for callable FDs. Penalty applies. Non-callable FDs do not allow premature withdrawal.

How is the interest paid?

You can choose from monthly, quarterly, periodic and on maturity

Are these deposits safe?

Yes. ICICI Bank’s FDs are rated AAA by CARE and ICRA, indicating high safety.

How can I book a Corporate FD?

You can book through CIB, APIs, InstaBIZ, or by visiting the nearest branch.
