Forex Prepaid Card Login FAQs

How can I manage my Forex Prepaid Card online?

You can easily manage your Forex Prepaid Card through ICICI Bank’s iMobile app, Net Banking, or the Self Care Portal. These digital interfaces allow you to view 
your
 transaction history, block 
or
 unblock your card, reset PINs, reload funds and transfer money between wallets – all from the comfort of your home. 

What should I do if I lose my Forex Prepaid Card?

If your Forex Prepaid Card is lost or stolen, you can immediately block the card via the Self Care Portal, iMobile app, or Net Banking. You will also receive a free replacement card (except for the Multicurrency variant), which you can activate easily through digital channels.

How can I link my Forex Prepaid Card to Net Banking?

To link your Forex Prepaid Card to Net Banking, follow these steps:

Log in to Net Banking. 

Click on ‘My Accounts’ and select ‘Forex and Forex Prepaid Cards’. 

Click on ‘Link Forex Prepaid Card’ and select ‘Self’ or ‘Close Relative’. 

For close relatives (parents, spouse, or kids), please upload a relationship proof (like a PDF of passport or marriage certificate). 

Enter the Forex Prepaid Card number and the cardholder’s passport number. 

Enter the registered mobile number and email ID as per your Savings Account to receive updates. 

Submit the request and note the reference number for future reference.

How can I link my Forex Prepaid Card using the iMobile app?

Linking your Forex Prepaid Card through the iMobile app is simple:

Log in to the iMobile app. 

Click on ‘Cards, Loans and Forex’ and select ‘Forex and Forex Prepaid Cards’. 

Click on ‘Link Now’ and select ‘Self’ or ‘Close Relative’. 

For close relatives (parents, spouse, or kids), please upload a relationship proof (like a PDF of passport or marriage certificate). 

Enter the Forex Prepaid Card number and the cardholder’s passport number. 

Enter the registered mobile number and email ID as per your Savings Account to receive updates. 

Submit the request and note the reference number for future reference. 

Are there any fees for using the Forex Prepaid Card?

Yes, there may be fees for various services like ATM withdrawals, foreign currency conversions, and card reloading. You can check the latest fee structure on the ICICI Bank website or through your Net  Banking or iMobile app to stay updated on any charges applicable to your card.

Can I reload my Forex Prepaid Card online?

Absolutely! You can reload your Forex Prepaid Card through Net  Banking or the iMobile app. Reloading is quick and convenient, allowing you to keep your card balance updated without visiting a branch. You can also transfer funds between different currency wallets through these platforms.

What are the security features of the Forex Prepaid Card?

Your ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card comes with enhanced security features such as 3D Secure authentication for online transactions and CHIP & PIN technology for added protection. These features safeguard your card against unauthorized usage, giving you peace of mind while traveling.
