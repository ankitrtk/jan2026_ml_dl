ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card FAQs

Is the HPCL Coral Credit Card from ICICI Bank?

Yes. The HPCL Coral Credit Card is an ICICI Bank Credit Card that helps you cut costs with cashback and savings on fuel transactions.

Is there a joining fee for the HPCL Coral Credit Card? 

Yes. Joining fee of ₹199 + GST.

Is there an annual fee for the HPCL Coral Credit Card? 

Yes. Annual fee of ₹199 + GST.

What is CVV on a Credit Card? How and where do I find it on my HPCL Coral Credit Card? 

Flip your HPCL Coral Credit Card to view its reverse side. The three-digit security code next to the signature panel is your CVV. You can also check it by logging in to your iMobile app or Internet Banking. 

Which bank has the best Credit Card?

ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card is an excellent choice and one of the 
best fuel credit card
, offering great savings and cashbacks on fuel transactions.

What is the age limit to apply for an ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card? 

You must be between the age group of 21 to 65 years to be eligible to 
apply for credit card
 and get an ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card.

How do I know the due date for my ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card bill payment? 

You can find the due date in your monthly statement, the iMobile app, through Internet Banking or by contacting our Customer Care on 1800 1080. 

Which bank provides a Credit Card at low rates? 

You can opt for an ICICI Bank Credit Card with a seamless application process. The HPCL Coral Credit Card has low joining and annual fees. For more information, please call our Customer Care on 1800 1080. 

What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?

Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period. 

Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:

Spends period offer -  eligibility period

Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026. 

 Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.

Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please

Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter

Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?

Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.

Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-

Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section 

 Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking 

 Choose ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount Offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards 

 Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’ 

 Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page. 

What are the movie ticket benefits available?

Cardholders are eligible for a 25% off on movie ticket offer worth up to ₹100 on the purchase of a minimum of two tickets through BookMyShow Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please 
click here
.

How can I earn cashback on fuel purchases with my ICICI Bank HPCL Coral Credit Card

Save up to 3.5% on fuel purchases at any HPCL fuel pump.

The fuel savings of 3.5% comprises of:

2.5% cashback when the card is swiped at any HPCL fuel pump (Capped at ₹100 per month).

1% fuel surcharge waiver when the card is swiped at any HPCL fuel pump using ICICI Bank POS machine.

What security features are provided by the chip and PIN?

The Credit Card comes with an embedded microchip that provides enhanced security against counterfeiting and duplication. For transactions at merchant outlets, customers must enter a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to complete the payment. However, this PIN is not required for online transactions. To know more about Chip and PIN security, please  
click here
 .

What is the difference between the HPCL Coral Credit Card and ICICI Coral Credit Card?

The HPCL Coral Credit Card offers exclusive benefits for fuel purchases at HPCL stations, providing rewards and discounts specifically for fuel transactions. The ICICI Coral Credit Card, on the other hand, offers general rewards on a wide range of categories such as dining, shopping, and travel, but does not provide the same focus on fuel benefits.
