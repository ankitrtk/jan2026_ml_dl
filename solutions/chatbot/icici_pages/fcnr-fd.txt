How to get Maximum Benefits from FCNR Deposit?

High Returns 

Avail Attractive Interest Rates

Currency Protection

No Exchange Rate Risk – Earn interest in the same currency in which you invested.

Multi-Currency Options available

FCNR(B) deposits available in USD, GBP, SGD, CAD & AUD

Flexible Investment 

Flexible tenure from 1 to 5 years

Tax-efficient Earnings

No tax on FCNR FD interest in India. 

Liquidity & Accessibility 

Principal & interest fully repatriable. Loan against FCNR FD – Avail up to 85%* against FCNR FD. 

*Conditions apply. Please contact the call centre/ Branch/ Relationship Manager for loan to value.

Things you should know about FCNR Deposit

Currency options

FCNR FDs are offered in USD, GBP, SGD, CAD, and AUD

Tenure Options

1–5 years for USD, GBP, CAD, SGD and AUD currencies

Tax Treatment

Interest earned on FCNR deposits is tax-free in India.

Repatriation Facility 

Both principal and interest are fully repatriable

Loan Facility

Avail a loan of up to 85% of the deposit value, up to ₹5 crore, on select FCNR deposits

*Conditions apply. Please contact the call centre/ Branch/ Relationship Manager for loan to value.

FCNR FD FAQs

Who can open an FCNR(B) FD?

NRIs and PIOs with foreign earnings can open an FCNR(B) Fixed Deposit in permitted currencies.

What are the tenure options for FCNR(B) FDs?

1 year to 5 years for USD and 1 year to less than 3 years for GBP, SGD, CAD & AUD.

Is the interest earned on these deposits taxable in India?

Interest earned is not taxable in India
.

Can I withdraw my deposit before maturity?

Yes, FCNR(B) deposit booked with original tenor of 12 months to < 36 months can be withdrawn before maturity.

Before completion of 12 months: No interest will be payable and no penalty will be levied..

After completion of 12 months: On premature withdrawal of the deposit, Interest shall be paid at the rate applicable, on the date of deposit booking, to the amount and period for which the deposit remained with the bank and no penalty will be levied

FCNR(B) deposit booked with original tenor of >= 36 months to <= 60 months deposit have a lock-in period of 12 months

If such deposit is prematurely withdrawn after completion of the lock-in period, including partial withdrawal, interest shall be paid at the rate applicable, on the date of deposit booking, to the amount and period for which the deposit remained with the bank, less 1% penalty

How can I book FCNR(B)?

You can apply FCNR FD via Internet Banking, iMobile App, ICICI Bank Branch, Customer Care, or Wire Transfer.
