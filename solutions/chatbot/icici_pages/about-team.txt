About ICICI Bank Economic Research
The Economic
                        Research team is the in-house research team at ICICI
                        Bank Limited. The team provides a holistic picture on
                        macroeconomics and financial markets to clients through
                        its research reports. This helps clients understand
                        underlying drivers of key macro trends, including GDP
                        growth, inflation, monetary policy, fiscal policy,
                        global trade and markets (currency, commodity and fixed
                        income), both domestically and globally, providing
                        critical insights. The reports are published under
                        various categories and at different frequencies (daily,
                        weekly, monthly), capturing the information for the
                        period as well as offering forecasts and expectations
                        for key data and policies. More specifically, important
                        aims are to understand key issues pertaining to global
                        economy and to provide high-quality analyses that are
                        relevant for the clients.
