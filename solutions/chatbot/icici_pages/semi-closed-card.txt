About Semi-Closed Prepaid Card

About ICICI Bank Semi-Closed Prepaid Card

ICICI Bank’s Semi-Closed Prepaid Card is designed specifically for corporate needs, enabling companies to manage recurring payments such as provision of dealership incentives, disbursal of reimbursement or petty cash, incentives or rewards to employees and recurring gifting solutions for your company. With wide acceptance at Visa-enabled Point of Sale (POS) terminals and online platforms, this prepaid card brings flexibility, security and efficiency to corporate disbursements. The Semi-Closed Card eliminates the need to handle cash, offering a streamlined payment alternative that enhances corporate operations and user convenience.

How it works

Card Loading: Corporates send funds to ICICI Bank to load funds onto the Semi-Closed Prepaid Card as per their disbursement schedule. Corporates with an ICICI Bank Current Account can also load funds directly onto the card through the Corporate Net Banking (CIB) portal

Employee or dealer usage: Cardholders can then use the card to make purchases at any Visa-enabled retail stores, ATMs or e-commerce sites

Tracking and control: Corporates can manage the card through SMS, ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app, Self Care portal or Customer Care

Semi-Closed Prepaid Card FAQs

What is an ICICI Bank Semi-Closed Prepaid Card?

It is a prepaid card designed for corporates to manage recurring payments, such as employee rewards or dealer commissions, accepted at Visa-enabled POS terminals and e-commerce sites.

Where can a Semi-Closed Prepaid Card be used?

This is widely accepted at all Visa-enabled physical retail stores, ATMs and online platforms, providing flexibility for recipients.

Can the Semi-Closed Prepaid Card be reloaded?

Yes.

What are the benefits of using a Semi-Closed Prepaid Card for corporate disbursements?

The card streamlines payments, reduces the need for cash handling, enhances security and provides tracking and control for corporate administrators.

How can a corporate apply for a Semi-Closed Prepaid Card?

Corporates can send an e-mail to mwcenquiry@icicibank.com or consult their Relationship Manager for assistance with the application.
