Umang Card

The ICICI Bank Umang Card offers a streamlined and secure way to receive government subsidies and benefits directly. Designed for eligible individuals, the Card allows easy access to funds from various central and state government programmes, including pensions, scholarships and subsidies. With no intermediaries, this reloadable Card promotes financial inclusion and simplifies transactions with an annual spending limit of ₹ 10 lakh. Widely accepted at ATMs, POS machines and online platforms, the Umang Card ensures efficient and direct fund access with minimal barriers. The EMV chip in the Card ensures secure transactions.

About ICICI Bank Umang Card

What is ICICI Bank UMANG Card

The ICICI Bank Umang Card supports direct benefit transfers, ensuring that eligible beneficiaries receive government aid without delays. By connecting with PFMS (Public Financial Management System), it provides transparent fund management, validation and transfer, allowing users to access subsidies like pensions, agricultural and LPG subsidies, scholarships and more through NACH/NEFT. This Card aims to simplify subsidy distribution, reduce fraud and improve financial access for users.

How does the ICICI Bank Umang Card work?

The ICICI Bank UMANG Card is designed for efficient subsidy transfers, delivering funds directly to beneficiaries while eliminating intermediaries. This Card ensures that eligible individuals receive their government benefits securely and promptly, reducing potential fraud. Users can load funds via IMPS, NEFT, UPI or the ICICI Bank corporate portal. For added security, transactions are validated by OTP for online purchases and by PIN on POS machines.

Features of the ICICI Bank Umang Card

a. Account Validation through PFMS

Validation of the beneficiary account through PFMS is enabled on this Card. It helps in the reduction of duplication of accounts, increases transparency and reduces spillover due to mismanagement or corruption.

b. Payment through NACH

The National Automated Clearing House (NACH) helps a large number of beneficiaries get funds credited in their bank accounts.

c. Dedicated Helpline

ICICI Bank has created a dedicated helpline number 18001080 that is active 24X7. So be it balance enquiry, reporting a lost Card or activating the replacement Card, users can seek assistance anytime.

d. Safe & Secured

The Umang Card has PIN-based security for transactions at point-of-sale (POS) terminals and OTP validation for online transactions.

e. Hassle-free Card activation

Enjoy the convenience of digital Card activation within 24 hours after successfully completing the KYC process.

Umang Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank UMANG Card?

The UMANG Card is a reloadable prepaid card that allows eligible beneficiaries to receive direct government subsidies and benefits.

Who qualifies for the UMANG Card?

Eligible individuals under various government schemes, such as pensions, agricultural subsidies, and scholarships, can use the UMANG Card to access their funds.

Where can I use the UMANG Card?

The card is accepted at POS terminals, online platforms, and ATMs across India, including ICICI Bank and other bank ATMs.

How can I load funds onto the UMANG Card?

Funds can be loaded through IMPS, NEFT, UPI, or the ICICI Bank corporate portal.

How do I apply for the ICICI Bank UMANG Card?

Contact your ICICI Bank Relationship Manager for assistance.
