Interest rates

Savings Account 

How are interest rates calculated?

Savings Bank Account

                                            interest is calculated on a daily basis on the daily
                                            closing balance in the Account, at the rate specified by ICICI Bank in
                                            accordance with Reserve Bank of India directives. The interest amount
                                            calculated is rounded off to the nearest rupee. With effect from March
                                            30, 2016, interest will be paid quarterly in March, June, September and
                                            December.

ICICI Bank Savings Account interest rate w.e.f 26 June 2025.

Across all Savings account balances

2.5%

PPF (Public Provident Fund)

The interest on PPF is compounded annually and calculated on the minimum account balance between the 5th and the last day of each month. The interest is credited to the account at the end of the financial year. As of now, the 
PPF interest rate
 is 7.10%, providing a safe and attractive option for long-term savings.
