FASTag from ICICI Bank is a rechargeable tag that makes your road trips more enjoyable. With this tag affixed securely on your vehicle, you can bid goodbye to long queues at toll plazas.

FASTag FAQs

What is FASTag?

FASTag is a device that employs Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology for making toll payments directly from the Prepaid Account linked to it. It is affixed on the windscreen of your vehicle and enables you to drive through toll plazas, without stopping for cash transactions.

How to purchase FASTag?

Click here
 to purchase FASTag online and fill in your details. Your RFID tag will be delivered for free at your doorstep.

You can also log in to ICICI Bank Net Banking/ICICI Bank’s iMobile app/ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app/Pockets by ICICI Bank with your credentials to apply for FASTag.

How to recharge FASTag?

You may 
recharge your FASTag Account
 by making payment through cheque or online through Credit Card/Debit Card/NEFT/RTGS/UPI or Net Banking.

For customers whose KYC documents are received, the FASTag Account recharge limit is ₹1,00,000. For non-KYC customers, the recharge limit is ₹10,000 only.

Click here
 to know more.

What is FASTag Customer Care number?

To know more about FASTag, please call our Customer Care on toll free number: 1800 2100 104 (chargeable number:1860 2670 104)

Can I buy a FASTag offline at an ICICI Bank branch or toll plaza?

Yes. FASTag can be purchased offline at authorised toll plaza Point of Sale locations. Customers must provide required KYC documents and vehicle details for issuance.

How do I find the nearest active FASTag toll plaza?

You can locate FASTag-enabled toll plazas through official National Electronic Toll Collection resources or by checking the updated toll plaza list available on ICICI Bank’s FASTag pages.

How long does it take to activate a FASTag bought offline?

Activation timelines depend on document verification and system processing. In many cases, FASTag purchased at authorised POS locations is activated shortly after successful registration and KYC validation.

How can I check my FASTag balance by a missed call?

Customers may check FASTag balance through SMS alerts, mobile banking, Net Banking or authorised missed call services, where available, linked to the registered mobile number.

What documents are required for FASTag purchase at POS?

To purchase FASTag at a POS location, customers typically need identity proof, address proof, vehicle Registration Certificate and vehicle details, subject to applicable KYC guidelines.
