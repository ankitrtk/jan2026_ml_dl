FAQs

What are the channels through which Trade Online activation can be initiated?

Trade Online activation request can be initiated through CIB or Instabiz

Can we settle inward remittance online?

Yes, inward remittance can be settled online, through CIB or Instabiz

Can we schedule remittance for a future date on Trade Online?

 “YES”, future date remittance can be scheduled on Trade Online.

Are advice and swift of processed transactions available on Trade Online? 

“YES” advice and swift of processed transactions are available on Trade Online.

What is the Bank’s TAT for PCFC or EPC disbursements?

PCFC or EPC disbursements are an Instant credit.

Does Trade Online support upload of beneficiaries in bulk?

Yes, Trade Online supports the upload of beneficiary in bulk.

Do you have a digital solution through which request can be initiated directly through our ERP?

Trade API suite would be best suited Digital solution.

What are E-Docs dashboard and I-BOE in Trade Online ?

E-docs - Outstanding trade compliance bills (IRM, Shipping or Softex bills) details are displayed in E-Docs Dashboard page

I-BOE - (ORM, Bill of entry) details are displayed in I-BOE page along with its relevant information based on IEC number and ICICI Bank AD code, enables BOE mapping and submitting for regularization through TOL

How do I settle inward remittance and give disposal instructions through InstaBIZ?

You can settle inward remittance through the below path in a few simple clicks:

Path: InstaBIZ → All Services → Export-Import → Inward Remittance → Inward Remittance Settlement

How can I download (or) share ICICI Bank swift code details through InstaBIZ to my overseas buyer or remitter to get inward remittances?

You can select the currency for which the SWIFT details or correspondent bank details are required from the ‘Share SWIFT Code’ section and download and share the same with their overseas buyer or remitter.

What is Trade One View Dashboard and what are its benefits?

It’s a unique dashboard which enables you to view all your trade related information in a single window.

Benefits:

Download transaction advices and Swift copies

Track pending compliances (Export or Import or Inland or Bank Guarantee or Capital Account) and monitor due dates

View the pending Inward remittances due for settlement

Real time status of trade transactions

Generate AD Code letter

What all details would be reflecting to me in Trade One View Dashboard ?

With Trade One View, you can view all your trade transactions, transaction status, due dates, pending compliances, share SWIFT Code and generate AD Code letter
