FAQs

Can I buy general insurance online? 

Yes, ICICI Bank offers 100% online purchase and renewal for all general insurance products.

What happens if I don’t renew my policy on time? 

 Your coverage will lapse and you may lose accumulated benefits like NCB.

How is the premium calculated? 

It is based on your age, asset type (vehicle or travel), coverage amount and policy duration. 

Is travel insurance mandatory for international trips? 

While not mandatory everywhere, it is strongly recommended to avoid high unexpected costs abroad. 

What are the tax benefits? 

You can claim deductions under Section 80D for health insurance premiums paid.
