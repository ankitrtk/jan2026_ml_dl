FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Corporate API Suite? 

The Corporate API Suite is a collection of secure APIs that let businesses embed banking services directly into their ERP or systems for real-time payments, collections, trade, tax and account management.

Who can use these APIs? 

Any ICICI Bank corporate customer including large businesses, fintechs, payment aggregators and ERP providers can use the Corporate API Suite to automate banking operations and reduce manual work.

How do I integrate these APIs into my system?

Sign up on the ICICI Developer Portal, choose and test the required APIs, provide the documents for UAT and production, then integrate with support from our technical team.

What are the benefits of using these APIs? 

Enjoy seamless ERP integration, real-time transaction status, multiple payment modes, secure data exchange and automated reconciliation all without visiting separate banking portals.

Is there any support available during onboarding? 

Yes. ICICI Bank’s teams guide you through testing, security checks, UAT, and go-live support to ensure a smooth and secure integration journey.
