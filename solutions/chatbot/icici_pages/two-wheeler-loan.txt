Two-Wheeler Loan

ICICI Bank two-wheeler loans are available for eligible Indian nationals aged between 21 and 65 years, including both salaried and self-employed individuals. Applicants can apply for financing subject to the Bank’s eligibility criteria, income assessment and required documentation. The loan facility is designed to support customers looking to purchase a new two-wheeler with convenient repayment options and a streamlined application process. Self-employed applicants are also eligible to apply, provided they meet the Bank’s income and documentation requirements. Applicant should review eligibility conditions, repayment terms and required documents before applying for an ICICI Bank two-wheeler loan

FAQs

What is a Two-Wheeler Loan?

A Two-Wheeler Loan enables you to buy a motorbike or scooter of your choice and pay for it in equated monthly instalments (EMIs). ICICI Bank offers Two-Wheeler Loans to both salaried and self-employed individuals.

How can I apply for a Two-Wheeler Loan 
with ICICI Bank?

Here is how you can apply for a Two-Wheeler Loan with ICICI Bank:

Apply Online: Apply via ICICI Bank Net Banking, iMobile or the official website

At the Dealership: Visit your preferred two-wheeler dealership and connect with an ICICI Bank representative.

Visit a branch: Walk into any ICICI Bank branch and apply in person.

What is the maximum amount that I can get as a Bike Loan?

The maximum amount you can avail is 100% of the two-wheeler’s on-road price.

How can I get 100% of the on-road price?

100% two-wheeler finance is available to ICICI Bank customers with a pre-approved loan. We also offer the same to prospective buyers based on proof of income.

Who can apply for an ICICI Bank Two-Wheeler Loan?

ICICI Bank offers Two-Wheeler Loans to Indian nationals aged between 21 and 65 years, both salaried and self-employed. 

What is the EMI of a Two-Wheeler Loan?

The EMI of a two-wheeler loan depends on factors like loan amount, tenure and interest rate.. Use a 
Bike Loan EMI Calculator
 to estimate your monthly payments accurately.

What is the Interest Rate of a Two-Wheeler Loan?

Ttwo wheeler loan interest rate
 vary based on the loan amount, tenure and the borrower's creditworthiness. Interest Rates of ICICI Bank Two-Wheeler Loan range from 10.25% p.a.*

What is the Two-Wheeler Loan Application Process?

The Two-Wheeler Loan application process involves filling out an application form, submitting required documents (proof of identity, Address proof, Income proof) and Bank verification.

What is the minimum Credit Score required for a Two-Wheeler Loan?

The minimum credit score required for a Two-Wheeler Loan varies as per the lender. Generally, a score of 750 or above is considered favourable, but some lenders may offer loans to applicants with lower scores as well.

Do I have the option to prepay the entire Two-Wheeler Loan Amount?

Yes, most lenders like ICICI Bank allow prepayment of the entire Two-Wheeler Loan amount. However, there may be certain charges associated with prepayment or specific conditions for the same. With ICICI Bank, the charge is 5% + applicable GST of the Principal outstanding.

How can I reduce the Interest Rate of a Two-Wheeler Loan?

To reduce the interest rate that you are offered, maintain a good credit score, choose a shorter tenure, make a higher down payment and negotiate with the lender for lower rates.

How can I reduce the processing fees of my Two-Wheeler Loan?

You can reduce the processing fees of your Two-Wheeler Loan by comparing offers from different lenders, negotiating with the lender for waivers or discounts and ensuring that you meet all eligibility criteria to avoid additional charges.

Who is eligible for a Two-Wheeler Loan?

ICICI Bank offers Two-Wheeler Loans to Indian nationals aged between 21 and 65 years, both salaried and self-employed. Applicants must have a CIBIL score of -1 or >= 700 to qualify for the Loan.

What are the other types of loans that ICICI Bank offers besides Two-Wheeler Loan?

Apart from Two-Wheeler Loan, ICICI Bank provides a wide range of financing options, including Personal Loan, Home Loan, 
Car Loan
, Business Loan and Education Loan. These loans cater to diverse financial needs and offer competitive interest rates and flexible repayment options.
