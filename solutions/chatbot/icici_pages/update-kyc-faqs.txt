                            ReKYC Updation

1.What are the various channels through which we can submit Re-KYC requests?

Re-KYC request can be submitted through any of the following modes:

Net Banking:
 Visit 
icicibank.com/nri
 and Log in to Net Banking > Customer Service > Service Requests > Re-KYC Updation
I-Mobile Pay:
 Download the iMobile App>Log in to I-Mobile Pay app> Customer Service > Service Requests > Re-KYC Updation
Branch in India/Overseas:
 Submit the Customer Information Updation Form duly signed by all the holders along with photographs and copies of KYC documents signed by all account holders. You are required to carry the originals KYC documents for verification.
Through Email:
 From your registered email id, you can send an email to your relationship manager / branch manager or write to 
nri@icicibank.com
 with copies of duly filled and signed Customer Information Updation Form along with photographs and copies of KYC documents signed by all account holders.

2.What are the KYC documents required to complete KYC updation?

For cases where there is no change in your demographic details including ID / Address / Country change and your KYC documents are valid (not expired), you may submit a 
duly filled

Identity proof

Indian passport holders must provide copy of the first (personal details) page and last(address) page of the passport.
foreign passport holders must provide copy of first (personal details) page of the passport

: PAN is mandatory for cases where annual income in India is greater than ₹ 3 lakh. In case PAN is not available and annual income in India is nil or less than the threshold limit of ₹ 3 lakh, then submit copy of duly filled and signed Form60. Click here to download a copy of Form 60.

Passport Related:

1. My passport has expired how can I update the details at the time of ReKYC updation

Passport is mandatory identity proof applicable for NRI customers. This document must be valid for a minimum period of 90 days at the time of submitting the ReKYC requests. In case your passport has expired, you need to submit the copy of the renewed passport

Additionally note:

Indian passport holders must provide copy of the first (personal details) page and last(address) page of the passport.
foreign passport holders must provide copy of first (personal details) page of the passport

2. The name on my passport does not match the name in the bank records – how can I change the name on the account?

In case your name change is due to marriage, then to change the name on the bank account, you need to submit the name change request form along with marriage certificate or any of the below six documents.

Valid self-attested OVD with new name.

Passport
Driving License
Voter Id card
Aadhaar
NREGA
Letter of Population Register

You need to submit clear copies of the passport first and last pages.

Yes, for Re-KYC updation passport as ID proof is mandatory.

Non-residential status proof:

1. What document can I submit as a NRI Status Proof for KYC?

You can submit any of the following documents as NRI Status Proof:

Visa / Resident / Employment Permit/ reentry permit.

2. Are foreign passport holders required to submit a visa?

No, Foreign Passport holders do not require to submit a Visa. However, OCI / PIO card with India connect-(Ex: Expired Indian passport of Spouse/ parents/ grandparents along with relationship proof)document is required for establishing NRI status. However, if foreign passport holder is staying in any other country, then valid visa/ Work Permit/Residence Permit of that country will be required along with Indian connect documents.

3. If foreign passport, place of birth is other than “India” then which document is required for NRI status proof?

OCI / PIO Card or PIO/OCI declaration with document evidencing customer’s India connect-(Ex: Expired Indian passport of Spouse/ parents/ grandparents along with relationship proof) is required. Also, if foreign passport holder is staying in any other country, then valid visa/ Work Permit/Residence Permit of that country will be required along with Indian connect documents.

4. What are document can we accept for Seafarers as Non Residential Status Proof?

Customer can provide CDC / C1D visa for Seafarers as proof of Non Residential status proof.

5. As an India connect document, can I submit expired Indian Passport of Spouse / Mother / Father / Grand Parents

Yes, you can provide expired Indian Passport of Spouse / Mother / Father / Grand Parents along with a relationship proof document to establish the Indian connection through the blood relative/spouse.

FATCA / CRS Declaration:

1. What is FATCA?

The Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) is a United States federal law that requires United States persons, including US persons who live outside the United States, to report their financial accounts held outside of the United States, and requires foreign financial institutions to report to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) about their U.S. clients.

Government of India (GOI) has signed a Model 1 Inter-Governmental Agreement (IGA) with US on July 9, 2015 which necessitates financial institutions in India to comply with FATCA.

2. What is TIN number?

Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is a unique alphanumeric code assigned to individual, business or other entities for tax purposes.

3. What do I need to mention in the field TIN document name in FATCA/CRS declaration form?

Refer to the list of documents accepted as TIN/Functional equivalent of TIN.

4. I don’t have TIN number?

FATCA/CRS declaration is mandatory. Refer to the list of documents accepted as Functional equivalent of TIN.

5. Is FATCA mandatory for all Re-KYC requests?

If there is a change of circumstances such as country change, change in citizenship, FATCA/CRS declaration is pending, you are required to submit the duly filled FATCA/CRS declaration form

                            Nomination Registration

1. Why should we add a nominee for an account?

A nominee is a person designated to receive assets like bank deposits, insurance policies or locker contents after the account holder's death.

Ease of Transfer: A nominee can quickly access the funds, avoiding long procedures and legal hurdles.

Security & peace of mind: The account holder ensures that their financial assets will be passed to the intended person.

Financial security for dependents: Appointing a nominee ensures that the dependents receive financial support promptly.

2. What are the key changes being incorporated in the nomination guidelines effective Nov 01, 2025?

The Department of Finance under the Finance Ministry has issued a Gazette notification on Nomination Facility being offered for Bank Accounts, on Oct 27, 2025. These rules are effective from Nov 01, 2025.

The new rules allow an account holder to have up to 4 nominees for an account or locker and he/she can choose any one of the two methods available below:

Successive- Up to 4 nominees can be added, where the account holder must assign a priority of 1 to 4. The claim will be settled in favour of the first nominee and in case the first nominee is deceased then in favour of second nominee and so on as per the priority assigned.
OR
Simultaneous- Up to 4 nominees can be added, where the account holder must assign a percentage of distribution of funds to each nominee to settle the claim (not applicable in case of lockers).

Example of Successive nomination: Mr A appoints four nominees, his wife, mother, son and daughter. If Mr A passes away, the entire account balance will be inherited by his wife. If his wife is deceased,  then the funds will be inherited by his mother. If his mother is also deceased, then by his son and if all the 3 are deceased, his daughter will inherit. (one can nominate up to 4 individuals).

Example of Simultaneous nomination: Mr A had ₹ 20 lakh in his Savings Account and nominated his wife, son and daughter as simultaneous nominees. He wished to allot the bank account proceeds in a 50:25:25 ratio. Upon his passing, his wife would thus receive ₹ 10 lakh and both his children would each receive ₹ 5 lakh (one can nominate up to 4 individuals with the total sum not exceeding 100%).

3) How is the new nomination facility different from the one existing currently for my Savings Account, Current Account or Fixed Deposit etc.?

The existing nomination facility has a limitation have only one nominee for your account/ locker. However, with the new facility you may allocate up to 4 nominees for your account/ locker. Thereby providing more options to enrol your near and dear ones based on your prerogative, similar to allocating nominees for Provident Funds and Demat Accounts.

Following are the two options available:

Successive- Up to 4 nominees can be added, where the account holder must assign a priority of 1 to 4. The claim will be settled in favour of the first nominee and in case the first nominee is deceased then in favour of the second nominee and so on as per the priority assigned. 
OR
Simultaneous- Up to 4 nominees can be added, where the account holder must assign a percentage of distribution of funds to each nominee to settle the claim (not applicable in case of lockers).

4) Is the new nomination rule applicable for new accounts opened after Nov 01, 2025 or for old accounts as well?

The new nomination rule is applicable to all existing as well as newly opened accounts.

5) Is the nomination facility applicable to my individual account held with the Bank or applicable to joint accounts as well?

This nomination facility is applicable to both the account types. For updation in a joint account, all the account holders need to submit the request.

6) Can a minor be appointed as a nominee?

Yes, nomination can be made in favour of a minor individual. However, a guardian needs to be appointed to receive the funds on behalf of the minor until the minor becomes a major.

7) Who cannot become a nominee?

Only individuals can be assigned as nominees. Non-individuals, including HUFs, partnership firms, body corporates, trusts, societies, etc., cannot be nominees for an account.

8) Can I appoint a joint account holder as a nominee?

No, an account holder cannot be a nominee in the same account.

9) My account already has a nominee. How can I update the nomination?

You can add a new nominee for your account (maximum up to 4 individuals) or delete the old nominee and update the new nominee for your account.

10) Can a mandate holder be a nominee as well?

Yes, a mandate holder can be a nominee in the customer’s account or deposits.

Yes, you can update different nominees for each account.

                            FATCA/CRS declaration

1. What is FATCA ?

The Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) is a United States federal law that requires United States persons, including US persons who live outside the United States, to report their financial accounts held outside of the United States, and requires foreign financial institutions to report to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) about their U.S. clients.

Government of India (GOI) has signed a Model 1 Inter-Governmental Agreement (IGA) with US on July 9, 2015 which necessitates financial institutions in India to comply with FATCA.

2. What is TIN number?

Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is a unique alphanumeric code assigned to individual, business or other entities for tax purposes.

3. What do I need to mention in the field TIN document name in FATCA/CRS declaration form?
