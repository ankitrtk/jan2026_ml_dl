SmartLock

SmartLock

SmartLock is a digital feature available on the iMobile App that allows you to lock and unlock multiple Banking Services without calling Customer Care or visiting a Bank Branch.

Benefits & Features

Comprehensive Control

Lock or unlock multiple banking services instantly with just a click, ensuring complete control over your accounts.

Enhanced Security

Protect your account from misuse by locking Banking Services during periods of inactivity or on suspicion of fraudulent activity.

Convenience at Your Fingertips

Manage iMobile App, Net Banking, UPI and card services anytime, anywhere, without visiting a Bank branch or calling customer care.

Flexibility with Scheduled Payments

Even when services are locked, scheduled Standing Instructions (SI) and E-mandates continue to process seamlessly.

SmartLock FAQs

What is SmartLock?

SmartLock is a digital feature available on the iMobile App that allows you to lock and unlock multiple Banking Services without calling Customer Care or visiting a Bank Branch.

Which services are included in SmartLock?

SmartLock covers locking and unlocking services such as iMobile App, Net Banking, UPI, Credit Card and Debit Card.

How can I access the status of my services?

You can log in to the iMobile App and click on ‘SmartLock’ available at the bottom right corner of the home screen.

Are the lock/unlock services instant?

Yes, services can be locked and unlocked in real time.

What if I lock my iMobile App and uninstall it? Can I use the iMobile App again?

Yes, you can install the iMobile App again, activate it using required authentication and start using the same.

If UPI is locked, can I still carry out transactions using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm,and other UPI apps?

No, if UPI is locked, payments from all UPI applications will be blocked.

If iMobile App is locked, can I still use other (website) services?

iMobile App Services will be unavailable and a prompt will be displayed to the user to login and unlock. However, other Banking Services will continue to be operational.

What should I do if I face issues while locking/unlocking the service?

You can reach out to our helpline at 1800-1080 for assistance 24x7.

If Net Banking is locked, can I log into the Retail Net Banking (RIB) portal from a laptop or mobile?

No, to use Net Banking again, you must unlock the service using SmartLock.

Will cards be locked temporarily or permanently?

Credit Cards and Debit Cards will be locked temporarily and can be unlocked using SmartLock without calling Customer Care or visiting a Bank Branch.

What happens to my Standing Instructions (SI) or e-mandates that are already given?

Even when Banking Services are locked using SmartLock, scheduled Standing Instructions (SI) and e-mandates continue to process seamlessly.

Steps to use the SmartLock Feature

Steps

Login to iMobile App.

Click on ‘SmartLock’ feature at the bottom right corner of the home screen.

Select the banking service(s) you wish to lock or unlock.

Swipe to confirm.
