About Family Debit Card 

Key benefits of ICICI Bank Family Debit Card

Earn points almost every time you spend

No cap on the number of Reward Points you can accumulate

Long validity—points remain valid for up to 36 months

Redeem easily for gifts, vouchers, and more for yourself or your family

Exclusive for Family 360°

Only the primary member of an ICICI Bank Family 360° account is eligible to receive the Family Debit Card. Family members must be linked under the same relationship group.

How to avail?

Open family members' ICICI Bank accounts

Link accounts via iMobile or branch

Ensure account type is Family 360° Banking

Choose family name for the card

Primary member gets the Debit Card

Easy linking process

Log in to ICICI Bank's iMobile app and tap Service

Select Account Services and click on ‘Request for Family Banking

Choose Family 360° and confirm account number

Enter member details and confirm

Family Debit Card FAQs

What is the use of ICICI Bank Family Debit Card?

The ICICI Bank Family Debit Card is a versatile debit card designed to facilitate convenient and secure banking for your family members. It offers features like online and offline transactions, reward points, complimentary airport lounge access, zero surcharge on fuel purchases at select outlets and enhanced security with Zero Liability Protection.

What kind of online transactions can I use my Family Debit Card for?

You can use your ICICI Bank Family Debit Card for a variety of online transactions, including:

Booking travel and movie tickets

Paying utility bills

Shopping on e-commerce websites

Important:

For online transactions, a 3D Secure PIN (Verified by Visa/Mastercard Secure Code) is required. You can generate this PIN during your first online purchase on the merchant’s website.

The daily online transaction limit is ₹3,00,000 (effective from 01 August 2018).

What is the zero surcharge offer on fuel purchases?

No fuel surcharge will be charged on transactions made using your ICICI Bank Debit Card at ICICI Bank swipe machines located at selected government fuel outlets (HPCL, IOCL, BPCL).

Transactions made at non-ICICI Bank swipe machines at fuel outlets may attract a surcharge, as per the acquiring bank’s discretion.

Any surcharge charged will be reversed after ICICI Bank receives a refund from the acquiring bank.

Click here
 for the list of fuel outlets where no surcharge applies.

What is the Zero Liability Protection feature?

The ICICI Bank Family Debit Card includes Zero Liability Protection, which safeguards you against unauthorised transactions resulting from loss, theft or misuse of your card.

How it works:

Report loss or unauthorised use to ICICI Bank Customer Care within 15 days.

Submit the necessary documents as required.

The balance lost due to such unauthorised transactions will be restored to your account.

Note: Terms and conditions for Zero Liability Protection changed effective 01 August 2011. For details, refer to the official terms.

How does ICICI Bank ensure better tracking of my transactions?

You will receive instant SMS alerts for every transaction carried out using your Family Debit Card. This helps you monitor your spending in real-time and enhances security.
