How to Pay Bill Payments Online 

Internet Banking

iMobile app

Log into Internet Banking with User ID and password.

Navigate to the ‘Payments & Transfer’ drop-down and click on ‘Pay Bills’.

Search for the biller name in the Search Biller Bar. You can also navigate to the required category by clicking on the category icon and then search for the biller on the specific category page.

Enter the required details and click on ‘Fetch and Register’. The bill will be registered and you will receive monthly reminders on bill generation and also before the due date.

To continue paying the bill, check bill details and select the preferred mode of payment.

Enter the OTP and click on ‘Pay’. A payment success screen will be displayed. Here, you can view the bill details and download the payment receipt.

Log into iMobile App using mPIN / Fingerprint / Face ID.

Navigate to the ‘Bill Pay, Recharge & FASTag’ section.

Search for the biller name in the Universal Search Bar. You can also navigate to the required category, e.g. Electricity and search for the biller e.g. Adani Electricity, on the specific category page.

Enter the required details and click on ‘Fetch and register’. The bill will be registered and you will receive monthly reminders on bill generation and also before the due date.

To pay the bill, select the preferred mode of payment and swipe to pay. A payment success screen will be displayed on the app. Here, you can view the payment details and download the payment receipt.

Bill Payments FAQs

How can I search for a biller?

You can search for a biller in two ways using the iMobile app:

Option 1:
 Type the biller name in the Universal Search Bar in the ‘Bill Pay’ section.

Option 2:
 Browse by category and select your biller from the listed options.

I am not able to view my biller. Why is this happening?

Your biller may not appear due to one of the following reasons:

The biller is not onboarded on Bharat Connect.

The biller was recently added to Bharat Connect but not yet updated at ICICI Bank’s end. Try again after a few days.

The biller is not integrated with ICICI Bank’s digital platforms.

What is the convenience fee charged for paying a bill?

No convenience fee is charged for bill payments or mobile/
DTH recharges
 made through ICICI Bank platforms.

Can I check details of my previous bill payments?

Yes, you can view the bill payment history for the last 15 days under the ‘Bill Payments History’ tab.

Use filters like:

Biller name

Category

Specific date or date range

to find the required transaction.

How often does the iMobile App send reminders for payment of due bills?

You will receive SMS reminders at the following stages:

When the bill is generated

7 days before the due date

3 days before the due date

On the due date

Where can I check my registered billers?

Go to the ‘Bill Pay’ section and click on ‘View My Bills’ to see all your registered billers.

When will I know that a bill has been paid successfully?

Confirmation is provided via:

On-Screen Message:
 Final screen will show payment success/failure.

SMS Alert:
 Sent to your registered mobile number upon successful payment.

Immediate Reflection:
 Payment gets updated at the biller’s end instantly after success.
