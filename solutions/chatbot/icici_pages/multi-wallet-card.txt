About Multi Wallet Prepaid Card

Multi Wallet Prepaid Card

ICICI Bank’s Multi Wallet Prepaid Card offers an efficient way to manage employee benefits, with customisable wallet options that allow you to set spending restrictions based on Merchant Category Codes (MCCs). Tailored to meet requirements of companies, this Card ensures seamless transactions and ease of use.

How to apply for the Multi Wallet Prepaid Card

For ICICI Bank Account Holders

1. Log In

Begin by logging into your ICICI Bank Net Banking account.

2. Navigate to ‘Cards and Loans’ 

Click on the ‘Cards and Loans’ tab located on the homepage. 

3. Select ‘Prepaid Cards’

From the list, select ‘Prepaid Cards’ to begin your application. 

4. Enter Corporate Details

Enter your Corporate ID (8-digit alphanumeric ID or 17-digit numeric ID) and Corporate Name. 

5. Choose Card Type

Select ‘Multi Wallet Card’ from the dropdown menu. 

6. Select Application Type 

From the dropdown menu, choose ‘New Card Application’. 

7. Verify Employee ID

Enter your Employee ID for identification. 

8. Review Personal Details

Check the personal details that get auto-populated, to ensure correctness. 

9. Submit Application

Click in the checkbox to accept the T&Cs and submit your application.

Multi Wallet Prepaid Card FAQs

What is the ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

The ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card is a magnetic strip-based reloadable Prepaid Card, which is loaded in Indian Rupees. You can use this card to shop at merchant establishments that accept Credit and Debit Cards.

Where can I use my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

The ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card can be used at any merchant establishment (shop, department store, movie theatre, restaurant etc.) that accepts VISA Credit or Debit Cards. However, the usage of the Card depends upon the wallet configuration as directed by your company. 

How soon after getting my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card can I start using it? 

Your Card is activated once KYC is completed. It is loaded with funds for use once ICICI Bank receives the funds for the Card from your company.

How long is my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card valid for?

The validity of the Card is 5 years from the date of issue on the Card.

What about the available balance on the Card after the Card has expired?

You can place a request at our Customer Care for a replacement Card. The available balance will be transferred to the new Card when it is issued. Replacement charges of ₹200 + GST will be levied for the new Card.

Are there any charges for the usage of my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

There is no charge levied on the Card for spends at any merchant establishment or for checking the Card balance at any ICICI Bank ATM. However, balance enquiry done at any other bank’s ATM shall attract charges. For detailed charges, please visit the ‘Fees and Charges’ tab under the ‘Prepaid Card’ section on our website 
www.icicibank.com
.

Can I add funds to my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

No. Funds can be added to your ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card only by your company by way of your salary and reimbursements. There is no restriction on the number of times a Card can be reloaded. 

Where can I check the balance on my Card?

You can check the balance on your Card using any of the below methods: 

Send the following SMS to 5676766 / 9215676766:  PCBAL<Space>Last 4 digits of the Card 

Call our Customer Care on 1800 1080 

Check at any VISA-enabled ATM in India 

Check using the ICICI Bank InstaBIZ app.

Where can I get details of the last transaction that I have made using my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

You can get the last 10 transactions made on your Card by calling our Customer Care on 1800 1080. Alternatively, you can also log into the 
Self Care Portal
 with your User ID and Password for getting the details. 

Do I need to maintain a minimum balance on my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card?

There is no need to maintain a minimum balance on your Card. However, keeping a healthy balance on your Card will help you in times of emergency. 

Can I use an ATM to check the amount remaining on my Card?

Most ATMs have the facility to check the balance on your Card. However, cash withdrawal is not permitted.  

What if I forget the ATM PIN of my Card?

If you forget your PIN, you can generate a new PIN using any of the below methods: 

Call our Customer Care on 1800 1080 

Log into the Prepaid Card Self Care Portal at this link: 
https://inrprepaid.icicibank.com/customer/login.jsp

Send the following SMS to 5676766: PCPIN<Space>Last 4 digits of Card 

Is the ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card a secured Card?

Absolutely. To ensure maximum security, sign inside the signature panel present on the reverse of your ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card as soon as you get it. Every time you use your Card, you need to sign on the charge slip (produced by the Electronic Data Capture machine at the time of purchase) to complete the transaction at the merchant establishment. The merchant shall match this signature with the signature present on the back on your Card, thus ensuring protection against unauthorised usage of your Card.

What happens if my ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card gets lost or stolen?

If your ICICI Bank Multi Wallet Prepaid Card is lost or stolen, report it to our Customer Care on 1800 1080 immediately, so that your Card can be blocked to prevent any misuse. We will then provide you with a replacement Card through your company.
