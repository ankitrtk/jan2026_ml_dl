Personal Loan Interest Rate, Fees & Charges

Personal Loan Interest Rate

Personal Loan interest rates are determined based on factors such as the borrower’s financial profile, loan amount requested, employment details, and credit evaluation. The applicable rate influences the EMI payable across the chosen repayment tenure.

Personal Loan Fees and Charges

Personal Loans may include certain charges related to processing and account servicing. A processing fee of up to 2% of the approved loan amount, plus applicable taxes, may apply. Other service charges may apply depending on the selected loan terms.

NRI Home Loan FAQ’s 

Can an NRI apply for a Personal Loan in India?

Many NRIs often wonder if they can apply for a Personal Loan in India when they have a stable overseas income and meet the bank's eligibility criteria. To 
apply for a Personal Loan in India
 from ICICI Bank, the NRI needs to be the primary applicant, and a blood relative residing in India can be a co-applicant. In this case, an NRO account is mandatory. Meeting documentation requirements and maintaining a good credit history ensures faster processing and approval.

How to apply for an NRI Personal Loan?

To 
apply for an NRI Personal Loan
, NRIs can complete the application online through ICICI Bank’s portal or visit a branch in India. Applicants need to submit identity proof, address verification, visa documents, income proofs, and financial statements. After eligibility verification and approval, the sanctioned loan amount is credited to the applicant's NRO account, allowing convenient access. The repayment can be done through NRE/NRO Savings account.

How to check NRI loan eligibility?

Using an NRI loan eligibility calculator, applicants can estimate the loan amount they may qualify for based on age, income, employment profile, and existing obligations. Calculating eligibility beforehand helps NRIs understand borrowing capacity, choose suitable EMI and tenure options, and prepare complete documentation, which supports a smoother application review and faster approval process.

What is the minimum income required for an NRI Personal Loan?

The minimum income required for a Personal Loan for an NRI account varies by country of employment, income profile and repayment capacity. Banks assess the applicant's ability to meet EMIs and other financial obligations before sanctioning. A consistent income stream and strong financial profile improve the likelihood of approval and may support a higher sanctioned amount when all criteria are satisfactorily met.

Is an NRE or NRO account mandatory for an NRI loan?

An NRO account is mandatory for availing an NRI loan, as the loan amount is disbursed only to the applicant’s NRO account. Repayment of EMIs can be made through an NRE account, NRO account, or a resident Savings Account, depending on the bank’s accepted payment modes. Having a valid NRO account helps ensure seamless disbursal and repayment of the loan.

How long does it take to process an NRI Personal Loan?

Processing an NRI Personal Loan generally takes a few working days to a couple of weeks, depending on document verification and eligibility checks. Submission of complete proofs improves turnaround time. Some banks also offer instant loans to NRIs in India to applicants with verified income, residence, and financial documents, enabling quicker sanction and faster access to funds.

What documents are required for an NRI Personal Loan in India?

NRIs must submit identity proof, passport, valid visa, overseas address proof, PAN card, income documents, and bank statements to determine if NRIs can get a Personal Loan in India without delays. Additional documents may include employment evidence or credit history. Providing accurate and complete supporting evidence speeds up verification and helps in the timely processing of loan applications.
