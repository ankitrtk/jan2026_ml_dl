About ​Single-Trip Insurance

Single Trip Travel Insurance 

Tailored for one-time international trips, this plan covers both medical and non-medical risks. 

Emergency Medical Coverage 

Covers hospitalisation, dental care, evacuation and repatriation of remains. 

Travel Disruptions Covered 

Compensates for trip delays, missed connections, passport loss and baggage issues. 

Visa-Compliant 

 Ideal for Schengen visa applicants with pre-approved policy documents. 

Cashless Medical Support 

Access hospital care abroad with zero upfront cost at network providers. 

FAQs

Who is eligible for Single Trip Travel Insurance?

This policy is available for individuals travelling abroad for a single round trip, covering both business and leisure travellers.

What medical expenses are covered?  

Emergency hospitalisation, medical evacuation and dental treatment are covered under the policy. The cashless facility ensures immediate medical assistance abroad.

Can I get coverage for trip cancellations or delays? 

Yes, the policy provides reimbursement for trip cancellations, interruptions and delays due to unforeseen circumstances such as natural disasters or medical emergencies.

 What happens if my checked-in baggage is lost or delayed? 

Compensation is provided for lost checked-in baggage, and a fixed allowance is given for baggage delays exceeding the specified time limit.

How do I file a claim for an emergency? 

Claims can be filed online or through the insurer’s customer support. Medical emergencies allow cashless hospitalisation, ensuring you receive immediate treatment without out-of-pocket expenses.
