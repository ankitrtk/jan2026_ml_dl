Minor Savings Account FAQs

What is the eligibility criteria for opening an ICICI Bank guardian operated or self-operated savings account?

Guardian operated 
Savings Account
 is available for children in the age group of 1 day–18 years. The Guardian can open and operate the Savings Account on behalf of the Minor.

Self-operated Savings Account is available for Minor customers above 10 years of age. Minor can independently open and operate the Savings Account.

What is the objective of an ICICI Bank guardian operated and self-operated Savings Account?

Guardian operated savings account will help you plan for your child’s needs, right from their young age.

While with the self-operated savings account, Minors above the age of 10 years can open and operate the Account all by themselves. This will inculcate a habit of saving and help them spend wisely.

What is the debit transaction limit for the ICICI Bank Guardian operated savings account?

There is no debit transaction limit for guardian operated savings account.

What happens when the debit transaction limit in the ICICI Bank self-operated savings account, is breached?

When the applicable debit transaction limit is breached, the Account is marked as frozen. To remove the freeze marked, the customer (Minor) along with Guardian will be required to visit the branch and upgrade the account variant

How can I open a minor account online with ICICI Bank?

You may initiate minor account opening online through ICICI Bank’s website. A parent or guardian must complete documentation and verification as per applicable guidelines.

What happens when the minor turns 18 years old?

Once the minor attains 18 years, the account must be converted into a regular Savings Account after completing the required KYC and documentation formalities.

Is the interest earned on a minor’s Savings Account taxable?

Interest earned on a minor Savings Account may be taxable as per prevailing income tax rules and clubbing provisions applicable to parents or guardians.

Can a debit card be issued for a minor's account?

Debit card issuance depends on the specific minor account variant and eligibility criteria. Usage may be subject to parental controls and transaction limits.
