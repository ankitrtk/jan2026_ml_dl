About Home Loan Prepayment Calculator

Home Loan Prepayment Calculator?

A Home Loan Prepayment Calculator helps understand the potential savings from making extra payments on your 
Home Loan
. By entering details such as your outstanding loan amount, interest rate and how often you plan to make prepayments, you can see how these payments affect your loan tenure and save on interest. This calculator helps you decide whether to prepay your loan based on your available funds and financial goals. Ultimately, it empowers you to manage your home loan effectively and reduce interest expenses.

How to Use Home Loan Prepayment Calculator?

Below are the steps to use the calculator for home loan part payments:

Enter the outstanding loan amount.

Select the current interest rate.

Enter the remaining tenure of the loan.

Provide the additional amount for each prepayment.

Click on the calculate button. Review the calculated results, including the new loan tenure and interest savings.

Use the information to make informed decisions regarding prepaying the home loan and reducing interest expenses.

Eligibility for Home Loan Prepayment?

Verify your lender’s policies regarding prepayment.

Review prepayment clause in your home loan agreement in case of restrictions or penalties.

Ensure that your lender provides an option for prepayment without imposing any substantial penalties.

Things to Consider Before Making Home Loan Prepayment?

Age

Your age plays a significant role here. If you're approaching retirement, it's advisable to prepay your home loan before retiring.

Future cash requirements

Future cash requirements are another major aspect. Prepaying a home loan involves a substantial sum, potentially depleting your savings.

Tax savings

You should also factor in the tax savings associated with home loan repayment. Prepaying your home loan can impact your income tax benefits.

Savings from prepayment

Savings from prepayment can be quite significant. It's wise to prepay your home loan when the savings on interest outgo are substantial.

Investment opportunities

Compare the savings achieved through home loan prepayment with the potential returns from alternative investment opportunities.

Benefits of Using a Home Loan Prepayment Calculator

A Home Loan prepayment calculator helps borrowers understand the impact of part payments or foreclosure on their Home Loan. It allows you to compare EMI reduction versus tenure reduction, estimate interest savings, and plan repayment of a housing loan more efficiently. By using a Home Loan prepayment calculator, customers can make informed decisions before making part payments or closing their loan early.

Impact of Lump-Sum & Periodic Prepayments on Home Loan EMI, Tenure & Interest Outgo

Home Loan prepayments may help reduce the outstanding principal amount and can potentially lower either the EMI or the remaining loan tenure, depending on the loan structure and repayment option selected. Lump-sum prepayments and periodic part-payments can also help reduce the overall interest outgo across the loan tenure.

Home Loan Prepayment Calculator FAQs

Can I prepay my Home Loan anytime?

Yes, most Home Loans allow prepayment at any time. However, applicable conditions, charges, or restrictions may vary based on loan type, interest rate structure, and ICICI Bank’s prevailing policies.

How does prepayment affect my tax benefits?

Prepayment reduces outstanding principal and interest. As a result, the Home Loan interest tax benefit or deduction available under applicable sections may be reduced in subsequent financial years.

Can prepayment reduce EMI or tenure?

Yes, Home Loan prepayment can either reduce the EMI amount or shorten the loan tenure. The choice depends on the borrower's preference and ICICI Bank’s loan repayment structure.
