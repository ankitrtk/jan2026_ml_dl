About Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card

About Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card

The Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card by ICICI Bank is a premium Card crafted for students heading abroad. With a host of exclusive features including fee waivers, international offers and essential protection, this Card simplifies managing expenses and enhances the study-abroad experience. From zero currency mark-up charges to multi-currency flexibility, it is tailored to meet students' unique needs in a foreign country.

How do I access the complimentary airport lounges?

Download the DreamFolks app, log in using the membership ID provided in your Welcome Kit and use the QR code generated to access partner lounges. The detailed steps are mentioned below:

Open the DreamFolks app

Type your mobile number and click on ‘Send OTP’

Enter the OTP and click on ‘Verify OTP’

Enter your membership ID and PIN sent in your Welcome Kit and click on ‘Submit’

Type your information and click on ‘Next’

Click on ‘Browse’ for the required service

Enter the airport name to view the list of lounges

Select the lounge to view its details

Click on ‘Member Access’ to proceed

Select the Card you want to use or click on ‘Details’ to see the benefits

Click on ‘Use This Card’ to proceed

Show this QR Code to get access to the lounge

Complimentary lounge access will be valid for a maximum of 1 year.

The USD 99 annual membership fee for Dreamfolks is complimentary. You will need to pay for the lounge visit charges for visits over and above the 2 complimentary accesses

Make the payment using your ICICI Bank Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card when visiting any lounge overseas.

Note:

Once the 2 complimentary accesses are used, USD 27 will be levied for Dreamfolks members on each additional lounge access.

One month is defined as per Indian Standard Time (IST) from 00:00:00 hours of Day 1 to 23:59:59 hours of the last day of the month.

Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card FAQs

How can I earn 5% cashback on grocery and transit spends?

Use your ICICI Bank Student Sapphiro Forex Prepaid Card for online grocery or transit spends via international websites and enjoy a 5% cashback, capped at ₹ 3,000 per month.

What ATM fee waivers are available on the Student Sapphiro Forex Card?

As per the Schedule of 
Forex Charges
, the fee for every withdrawal using an ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card varies depending on the currency. However, with the Student Sapphiro Card, the first 3 withdrawals every month are free of charge. Please note that extra charges may apply if the withdrawal is made from a merchant ATM.

How do I redeem the ISIC membership voucher?

Visit the ISIC website, enter your Card details to apply for the discount and complete the application to receive your 
International Student Identity Card
.

How long is the Uber voucher valid for and how do I redeem it?

The Uber voucher is valid for 12 months from Card activation. To redeem, add it to your Uber account under the ‘Gift Card’ section for ride credits. The steps are mentioned below:

Click 
here
 to redeem the Uber-e Gift Card

On the above page, click on ‘Checkout’. Enter your registered e-mail ID and mobile number

In the ‘Enter ICICI Bank Voucher Code’ box, enter the voucher code mentioned in your Welcome Kit and click on ‘Apply’

Place order to receive the voucher on your registered e-mail ID and mobile number

Go to the payment section in the Uber app

Tap ‘Add Payment Method’ and select ‘Gift Card’

Enter the gift code

Uber credits will be added to your account.

How to avail the international SIM ?

Eligible Cardholders can avail the benefit by clicking on this link: 
https://visaforex.poshvine.com/
 where they will have the option to login using the Card number of their Eligible Card and other details

Eligible Cardholders can use their Card number to check the validity of this benefit

After logging in, eligible Cardholders can copy the unique code from the offer tile and click on the link to visit the e-SIM / SIM card vendor's platform

On the vendor's platform, eligible Cardholders have to select their preferred call plan details (as mentioned in the above table)

Eligible Cardholders will receive an e-mail with a link to complete the KYC process by uploading the necessary details (as mandated by relevant telecommunications authorities)

After KYC is completed, an option to select an e-SIM or a physical SIM card will be available in the drop-down menu on the SIM card vendor's platform

For an e-SIM, a QR code will be sent via e-mail to activate the e-SIM. A list of compatible phones will be available on the platform

For a physical SIM card, Cardholders must enter their delivery address and pay a delivery charge of  ₹ 100 for the vendor to ship the SIM card to the Cardholder's address.
