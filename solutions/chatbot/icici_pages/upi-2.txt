Features and benefits of UPI for NRIs

Fast and secure payments

Carry out instantaneous payments from your ICICI Bank account backed by advanced bank-grade security using the ICICI Bank’s iMobile app

Default sender or receiver facility

Now with one UPI ID for all your accounts, set your desired account as the default account to receive and pay through the UPI ID.

UPI mandate and collect requests

Approve UPI mandates and collect requests from merchants to enable auto-debits for your bills and payment requests

Self-transfer facility

Move funds between your own accounts

Add favourite payees

Send Money to contacts and mark frequent payees as favourites for quick and easy access right from the UPI dashboard

UPI PIN facility

Authenticate every UPI transaction using UPI PIN to ensure secure and seamless transactions backed by NPCI security

How to activate UPI for NRI Account

Login to ICICI Bank’s iMobile app and click on “UPI Payments” tile at the bottom of the page

Avail UPI ID by selecting the pre suggested option on the screen

Select the account number to be linked

Click on "Set UPI PIN" and authenticate your details by using any one the below mentioned options:

1) Aadhaar authentication: Enter the first 6 digits of your Aadhaar number and authenticate yourself using OTP sent to your Aaadhar linked Number*

2) Debit Card authentication: Enter the last 6 digits of your Debit Card Number and authenticate yourself using OTP sent to your bank registered mobile number

Setup your UPI PIN (Enter the 4 digits of your choice & reconfirm the 6-digit UPI PIN entered). Post successful UPI PIN setup, your account/s will be set for transactions.

Things you will need to ‘Update UPI PIN'

To ensure smooth and secure UPI PIN setup, please keep the below details ready:

Existing UPI PIN (In case you remember your previous UPI PIN)
Last 6 digits of your Debit Card or first 6 digits of your Aadhaar number (If you do not remember your UPI PIN)

How to update UPI PIN?

Reset UPI PIN

Change UPI PIN

Login to ICICI Bank’s iMobile app and click on ‘UPI Payments’ at the bottom of the screen

Under UPI Settings, click on ‘Reset UPI PIN’

Authenticate your details by using either Aadhar (first 6 digits of your Aadhar number) or Debit Card (the last 6 digits of your Debit Card number and expiry date) based authentication followed by OTP based authentication*

Set and confirm your desired 4 or 6 digit UPI PIN

Login to iMobile and click on ‘UPI Payments’ at the bottom of the screen

Under UPI Settings, click on ‘Reset UPI PIN’

Authenticate yourself using your existing UPI PIN

FAQs for UPI for NRIs

How does Unified Payment Interface (UPI) work for NRIs?

UPI enables ICICI Bank Account Holders to send and receive money from their smartphones with a single identifier i.e. Virtual Payment Address (VPA), using their registered Indian or overseas mobile number from select geographies, i.e. Australia, Canada, France, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, UAE, UK and USA.

UPI can be used for transactions in Indian Rupees (INR) for:

Sending money/ making payments to Indian merchants or UPI or VPA IDs

Scanning any UPI QR code to send money

Sending money to any Indian mobile number (if the mobile number is enabled for UPI)

Sending money to an Indian Bank Accountow can an NRI set up a UPI or VPA ID?.

Can NRIs use UPI for transactions in India using their overseas mobile number?

Yes, NRIs can use UPI from their registered overseas mobile number from the approved geographies, i.e. Australia, Canada, France, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, UAE, UK and USA for INR transactions in India.

How can an NRI set up a UPI or VPA ID?

NRIs can set up a UPI or VPA ID using their ICICI Bank registered Indian mobile number or overseas mobile number from the approved geographies, i.e. Australia, Canada, France, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, UAE, UK and USA.

ICICI Bank NRI customers can register themselves for UPI by following these steps:

Log in to ICICI Bank’s iMobile app  → UPI Payments  → Claim UPI ID  → Link your Bank Account  → Set UPI PIN → Authenticate yourself using Aadhaar or Debit Card based OTP Authentication  → Success

Are there any transaction limits for NRIs using UPI?

According to National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) guidelines, the per day UPI transaction limit for P2P (Person to Person) based transactions is set at ₹1 lakh (₹1,00,000) cumulative transaction value or a maximum of 20 transactions and ₹5 lakh (₹5,00,000) cumulative transaction value or a maximum of 20 transactions for P2M (Person to Merchant) based transactions.

The UPI transaction limit per day has been defined by each bank in the UPI network and varies from bank to bank. ICICI Bank’s transaction limit for UPI is ₹1,00,000 per day.

However, in the case of a new UPI or VPA ID registration, the limit is ₹5,000 for the first 24 hours (Android devices) and for the first 72 hours (iOS devices). After the designated duration the limit is reset to ₹1,00,000.

How secure is it for NRIs to use UPI for transactions?

As various security processes are incorporated into the UPI payment ecosystem, NRIs can securely use UPI for their INR transactions.

The UPI or VPA ID is a unique identifier of a customer’s identity. The UPI PIN is set up by the customer and must be always kept confidential.

To process a transaction through UPI, entering the UPI PIN is mandatory, which is a 4-digit PIN set up by the customer to authenticate transactions.

As and when a customer registers for UPI, his/her mobile number is verified through SMS authentication. The application then automatically links the customer’s UPI Account to his/her registered mobile number.

In the instance that a customer’s mobile phone gets stolen/lost, the customer can block his/her mobile number as a precaution against identity theft by calling the Customer Care of their telecommunication service provider.

What are the UPI charges applicable for NRIs?

Currently, there are no charges for using UPI applicable for NRIs. However, this is subject to change as per NPCI guidelines.

Can NRIs link multiple bank accounts to a single UPI or VPA ID?

Yes, an NRI can link multiple bank accounts to a single UPI or VPA ID.

Why is my UPI transaction failing?

There are various reasons for a UPI transaction failure, including but not limited to:

Maintenance and system related upgradation, leading to downtime of Remitter bank’s system or Beneficiary bank’s system or the NPCI system

Incorrect UPI PIN

Incorrect Beneficiary UPI or VPA ID

Beneficiary does not have an active UPI or VPA ID linked with any account

My UPI transaction has failed. Why has my bank account been debited for the failed transaction amount?

In case of failed UPI transactions, the money (if debited) will be credited back to the Remitter’s account instantly. In case of timed-out transactions, the amount will be settled within 2 working days.

Can NRIs carry out UPI transactions while located outside of India?

Yes, NRIs can carry out the following UPI transactions from outside India:

Sending money/ making payments to Indian merchants or UPI or VPA IDs

Scanning any Indian QR code to send money to the QR code/ associated VPA

Sending money to any Indian mobile number (if the mobile number is enabled for UPI)

Sending money to an Indian Bank Account.

Can NRIs use UPI for transactions in India?

Yes, NRIs can use UPI for transactions in India. NRIs can generate a UPI or VPA ID on ICICI Bank iMobile application by using their ICICI Bank registered Indian mobile number or overseas mobile number from approved geographies, i.e. Australia, Canada, France, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, UAE, UK and USA.

Can NRIs carry out UPI transactions from NRO Account to NRE Account?

No, UPI transactions from NRO Account to NRE Account are not permitted

The lists of permissible transactions are:

NRE to NRE Account

NRE to NRO Account

NRE to Resident Account

NRO to NRO Account

NRO to Resident Account.

How can NRIs carry out UPI transactions?

Send Money/ Pay Indian merchants or UPI or VPA IDs Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Pay UPI ID or number → Enter amount → Pre confirmation → Enter PIN → Success

Scan any Indian QR Code Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Scan QR Code → Enter amount → Confirm to Pay → Enter PIN → Success

Send money to contacts (Only Indian numbers are allowed as Beneficiary details) Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Pay Mobile Number → Type/select any mobile number or UPI number → Pre confirmation → Enter PIN → Success

Send Money to Account Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Pay to self/ bank account → Enter details of Beneficiary → Pre confirmation → Enter PIN → Success

Can NRIs initiate UPI transactions in foreign currency?

No, currently UPI transactions are allowed in Indian Rupee (INR) only.

How long does it take for the funds to get credited into the Beneficiary’s account?

In case of successful UPI transactions, the funds get credited into the beneficiary’s Account instantaneously.

Can I cancel a UPI transaction?

No, all transactions carried out through UPI are instantaneous and cannot be cancelled or reversed once the correct UPI authorisation PIN has been entered. Please check the pre-confirmation screen or option thoroughly prior to authorising any UPI transaction.

Does the Account Holder have to be registered on UPI to receive these payments?

No, in case an Account Holder is not registered on UPI, they can still receive money through UPI. You can use the option ‘Pay to self or Bank Account’ and then enter the Bank Account details of the beneficiary.

Can a customer of ICICI Bank link an ICICI Bank NRE / NRO Account with an international registered number to any third party UPI application?

It depends on the particular third party UPI application provider.

Is a customer required to activate international roaming on his/her overseas mobile number while visiting India to make UPI payments? 

No, to process UPI transactions, a customer is not required to activate an international roaming facility. The ICICI Bank iMobile application is functional on Wi-Fi, through which an NRI can initiate and process UPI payments. However, if the NRI customer changes his/her overseas SIM to an Indian SIM upon arrival in India and in case the customer had previously enabled UPI using his/her international number, then the application will ask the customer to create a new UPI ID using his/her Indian mobile number.

Can NRIs use UPI for direct bank account transfer?

Yes, an NRI can directly transfer money to a beneficiary account using UPI by following the process below.

Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Pay to self/ bank account → Enter details of Beneficiary → Pre confirmation → Enter PIN → Success

Send Money to Account

Can NRIs carry out UPI transactions, if the beneficiary is not registered on UPI?

Yes, any customer registered on UPI can make payment to the Beneficiary’s account, even if the beneficiary is not registered on UPI. This feature works as below:

Steps: iMobile login → UPI Payments → Pay to self/ bank account → Enter details of Beneficiary → Pre confirmation → Enter PIN → Success

If a customer is making payment towards education and medical purposes, what is the UPI transfer limit?

As per NPCI, if a customer is making a payment towards educational or medical service that is verified or registered with NPCI, then the UPI transfer limit is up to ₹5 lakh (₹5,00,000.00).

While travelling to India or to any other country, does an NRI need to re-register for UPI with their local mobile number?

No. Upon registration, an NRI can carry out UPI transactions using the same device and UPI or VPA ID, through the linked mobile number.

NRIs can easily make UPI payments using Wi-Fi in case of cellular network issues when international roaming is inactive.

I have two Accounts with ICICI Bank (NRE/NRO). Do I need a separate UPI ID for each of these Accounts?

You can access your UPI associated transactions for all your NRI Accounts using a single VPA or UPI ID.

What is the advantage of UPI fund transfer over other fund transfer mechanisms?

The service provided under UPI is instant and available 24x7, even on public/bank holidays. Customers can transfer funds in simple steps by providing the UPI/VPA ID of the Beneficiary. Also, there is no pre–registration required for any Beneficiary.

How will I know if my UPI transaction is successful?

For any transaction, you will see a status instantly on your screen. If for some reason, the transaction is delayed or pending, the result will be posted on your transaction history page along with a UTR number or bank reference number. Additionally, you will also receive an SMS or e-mail from your Bank if the transaction is successful.

What are the timings for initiating and receiving UPI transactions?

UPI services are available 24 hours of the day, all days of the week, throughout the year. There are no holiday restrictions on UPI transactions.

Why can I not see any UPI or VPA ID after selecting the contact?

Currently, a payment can only be made to contacts having a valid Indian mobile number, and that too can be made only if the respective contact has a valid UPI or VPA ID linked to a bank account. Please check with your contact if they have a valid UPI or VPA ID linked with a bank account.

Alternatively, you can directly send money to their Indian bank account using ‘Pay to self or Bank Account’ option or by scanning their UPI QR Code or making a payment to the Beneficiary’s UPI ID.

While sending money to a contact, the Account Holder Name and UPI or VPA ID are different. Are they the same person?

When you enter a contact, the Account Holder’s name (as mentioned in the bank’s system) will be displayed. This is for the customer to cross-check that the beneficiary and the bank account to which the customer wants to send money belong to the same person. In case of any discrepancy, please re-confirm the UPI ID of the beneficiary. Money transfers through UPI on ICICI Bank’s iMobile app cannot be reversed.

Can I send money to the beneficiary via UPI, if the beneficiary has an international mobile number?

Currently, payment to a contact is only available to Indian numbers and that too can be made only if the contact has a valid UPI or VPA ID associated with an Indian Bank Account.

Alternatively, you can directly send money to their Indian Bank Account using ‘Pay to self or Bank Account’ option or by scanning their UPI QR Code or making a payment to the beneficiary’s UPI ID.

Is ‘UPI mandate’ functionality available for NRIs using international numbers?

Yes, NRIs can now access ‘UPI Mandate’ using international mobile numbers.

Will my UPI ID get deactivated if I don’t use it for a long time? / Why I am unable to receive credit in my account through UPI payment?

Yes, if your UPI ID remains inactive for 12 months, it will get deactivated and inward credit will be blocked through UPI for the linked account. You may initiate a PIN based UPI transaction through ICICI Bank’s iMobile app to activate or unblock the inward credit.
