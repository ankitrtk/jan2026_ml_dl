Lumpsum Investment - A Guide on One time Mutual Fund Investment

2 mins Read 

               | 2 Years 5 Months Ago

KNOW MORE

INVEST NOW

When it comes to Mutual Fund investments, ‘lumpsum investment’ has a great significance. Unlike the popular Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) method, this involves making a one-time investment in a particular Mutual Fund scheme. This blog will explore the benefits of lumpsum investments and a comparison with SIPs.

What is Lumpsum Investment?

Lump Sum Investment is a very popular way to invest in mutual funds in India. It is a style of investment in which substantial investment is made in one go rather than bifurcating it into smaller amounts at regular intervals.

Investing in a lump sum format is a very common way to 
invest in mutual funds
. Lump sums are good for investors with a substantial idle amount and are risk-friendly. 

How does lumpsum investment work?

Lumpsum investment means investing a large amount of money in one go, instead of spreading it over several months. This is common in mutual funds, where the full amount is invested based on the 
Net Asset Value
 (NAV) of the fund on that particular day.

This works best when the market is low and likely to grow in the future. Since your full amount is invested upfront, you get the benefit of full market growth from the beginning.

Here’s the formula:

Lumpsum Return = Investment Amount × (1 + r)^n

Where:

r = expected rate of return (in decimal)

n = number of years

For example, if you invest ₹1,00,000 at 10% for 5 years:

₹1,00,000 × (1 + 0.10)^5 = ₹1,61,051 (approx.)

This shows how your money grows over time with compound interest.

Features of Lumpsum Investment

Below are the important features of a lumpsum investment that you should know about:

1. One-time payment

With lumpsum investment, you invest the entire amount at once. This makes it simple and stress-free as there’s no need to track SIP dates or worry about missed monthly payments.

2. Full market entry

Since the full amount is invested on the first day, your money starts working for you right away. It allows you to take full advantage of any market rise right from the start, unlike SIPs that invest slowly.

3. Helps money grow faster

When your entire investment stays in the market for a longer period, it earns more returns. Compounding helps your gains grow over time, as you earn not only on your capital but also on the profits it makes.

4. Ideal for long-term plans

Lumpsum is ideal if you are saving for future goals like a child’s marriage or buying a home. It works best over 5 years or more, giving your money enough time to grow through market movements and compounding.

5. Market timing matters

Investing a big amount at once means market timing is important. If you invest during a high market phase, early losses may impact growth. It's wise to enter the market when prices are more stable or slightly low.

Who should invest in Lumpsum Investment?

There are certain aspects that come along with lump sums, and it is important to understand who it might be for:

Incentive Earners

If you have got a bonus or extra cash gains, then a lump sum investment is the right choice for you. It lets you invest it all right away and potentially grow it faster.

Risk Takers

If you are comfortable with risks in your investment style, you can potentially earn more profits by entering the market at a low point, but be prepared for some volatility.

Long-Term Thinkers

Lump sums work best for long-term goals, allowing you to overcome market fluctuations.

Things to consider before investing in Lumpsum investment

Thinking about putting a bigger chunk of money into a mutual fund at once? Here's what to consider with a lump sum investment:

Minimum Investment

Most funds require at least Rs.5,000 to start, but after that, you can usually add more in smaller amounts (around Rs.1,000).

Holding Period

Aim to hold your investment for at least 3 years, especially if it's an equity fund (focused on stocks). Short-term goals might be better suited for debt or liquid funds (less risky).

Market Ups & Downs

Putting a lot of money in at once means it could be affected more by market fluctuations. However, there's a chance of buying more units at a lower price during a market low, which could lead to higher profits when the market goes back up.

Benefits of Lumpsum Investment 

High growth potential: Investing a substantial amount in one go can lead to significant growth, especially during market upswings.

Ideal for long-term goals: Lumpsum investments are well-suited for long-term financial goals as they allow the investment to compound over a more extended period.

Convenience: This investment method eliminates the need to remember multiple investment dates, making it a stress-free investment option.

Best Mutual Funds for One Time Investment

Some of the ideal funds for lump sum investment can be chosen based on their AUM, as higher AUM often indicates investor trust and fund stability. AUM (
Assets Under Management
) refers to the total market value of assets that a financial institution or investment professional manages on behalf of clients.

1. Quant Small Cap Fund

Try this 
equity fund
 if you are looking for strong growth because it has offered remarkable returns over the years. The fund has an AUM of Rs. 26,670 Crore as of December 31, 2024.

2. Nippon India Small Cap Fund

If you have a high risk tolerance then this fund is for you. It focuses on small-cap equities to provide long-term growth, with an AUM of Rs. 61,974 Crore as of December 31, 2024.

3. Aditya Birla Sun Life Medium Term Fund

With an AUM of Rs. 2,004 Crore, it makes investments in medium-term debt instruments to provide steady and dependable development. It is perfect for medium-term objectives.

4. Quant Multi Asset Fund

This is a 
hybrid fund
 which makes investments in a variety of assets, including debt, stocks, and other assets. With an AUM of Rs. 3,201 Crore, this is one of the promising lump sum investment options. lculate Returns on Lumpsum Investment?

How to calculate Returns on Lumpsum Investment?

A 
lumpsum calculator
 is a very useful tool for investors who want to know how much money they might earn from their mutual fund investments. It works like this: you enter the amount of money you have invested, how long you plan to keep the investment, and the rate of return you expect. The calculator then does the math for you and shows you an estimate of how much money you could make in the future.

Using this calculator is great because it saves you time. This helps you understand how your money can grow and lets you plan your financial goals better. So, if you want to see how your investment might turn out, a lump sum 
mutual fund calculator
 is the way to go.

Lumpsum vs SIP Investments : The difference

Investors who are starting their investment journey for the first time may be confused between a lump sum and an 
SIP investment
. Here are the key differences between the two types of investment:

Factor

Lumpsum Investment

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

Investment Style

One-time investment of a large amount

Regular, smaller investments over time

Best Time to Invest

When markets are low and expected to rise

Risk Factor

Higher short-term risk due to market timing

Lower risk due to rupee cost averaging

Returns

Can generate higher returns if invested at the right time

Offers steady returns over time by averaging market ups and downs

Investor Type

Suitable for experienced or confident investors with surplus funds

Best for disciplined investors or beginners

Convenience

One-time action—easier to manage

Requires regular monthly payments

Financial Planning

Requires market awareness and timing

Promotes a habit of regular saving and budgeting

Flexibility

Less flexible once invested

Highly flexible—you can stop or change SIP anytime

Strategic use of Lumpsum Investments 

Systematic Transfer Plan (STP): Investors with a large sum to invest but wary of market timing can use STP. Here, the lumpsum investment is initially parked in a low-risk fund like a 
liquid fund
 and then systematically transferred to equity funds.

Balancing risk and opportunity: Lumpsum investments are ideal when the market is low, allowing investors to buy more units and benefit from the potential upside.

Which will give better returns – lump sum or SIP?

There is no exact answer to this question, as both options are market-dependent and should align with your financial goals. Lumpsum investments work best when the market is low and expected to rise steadily. A lumpsum investment means putting in a large amount at once, which can lead to higher returns when the market is on an upward trend.

On the other hand, a SIP helps you invest small amounts regularly, which reduces risk by averaging out the cost during market ups and downs. SIPs help manage risk during market ups and downs. Since both approaches serve different purposes, there’s no one-size-fits-all answer. The right choice depends on factors like market timing, your risk appetite, and your investment discipline.

Lumpsum Investment related FAQs

When is the right time to invest lumpsum in mutual funds?

The right time is when the market is stable or when it is a bit down. You should also avoid investing during high market peaks. Moreover, make sure that your financial goals are long-term so the investment has enough time to grow and recover from any short-term dips.

Can I add a lumpsum amount to my existing SIP?

You can easily add a lumpsum to your SIP anytime. This concept is known as a top-up. With this, you can smartly boost your total invested amount and take advantage of any market opportunity, without disturbing your regular SIP schedule.

What is bulk investment in mutual funds?

Bulk investment means putting a large amount of money into a mutual fund in one go. It’s different from SIPs and works well when markets are favourable and you have a long-term goal in mind.
