About Forex Cards

Why choose ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card?

With ICICI Bank’s Forex Prepaid Card, you can carry multiple currencies on a single Card, saving time as well as money on exchange fees. Your transactions are secure with the embedded microchip and the Card PIN. Enjoy the convenience of reloading funds on the Card online, anytime. Competitive exchange rates ensure that you get the most value. ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card is thus the ideal travel companion for both business and leisure trips.

Load up to 15 different currencies on your ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card, including:

United States Dollar (USD)

Great British Pound (GBP)

Euro (EUR)

Canadian Dollar (CAD)

Australian Dollar (AUD)

Singapore Dollar (SGD)

and more!

How to buy or reload ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card

Purchase or reload your Forex Prepaid Card by logging into Net Banking or the the ICICI Bank's iMobile app. If you're not an ICICI Bank account holder, visit any ICICI Bank Forex branch to apply. Reload anytime, anywhere, for continuous access to funds.

Get help with your Forex Prepaid Card

For queries, please call Customer Care on 1800 1080, if in India. International customers can use our 24-hour toll-free numbers specific to their country. Select ‘Option 4’ for Forex Prepaid Cards and speak to our Customer Care Executive.

Eligibility criteria for Forex Prepaid Card

To apply for a Forex Prepaid Card, you need to meet the requirements set by ICICI Bank:

Must be a resident of India travelling abroad

Must have valid travel documents, such as a passport and visa (if applicable)

Must fulfil ICICI Bank’s KYC (Know Your Customer) requirements (proof of identity, proof of address, etc.).

Documents required for Forex Prepaid Card

Here are the documents that you will need to apply for an ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card:

Passport

Form A2

PAN Card

Filled and signed application form of ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card.

Features and Benefits of Forex Prepaid Card

Multi-Currency Support: Carry multiple currencies on a single prepaid Forex Card, making it one of the best cards for international travel.

Safe and Secure: Protected by chip and PIN technology, providing a favourable alternative to carrying large amounts of foreign currency.

Instant Loading: Reload your Forex Card online instantly using Net Banking or ICICI Bank’s iMobile app.

Emergency Assistance: Access 24x7 customer support for your international card in case of loss or theft while abroad.

Fixed Exchange Rates: Lock in exchange rates at the time of loading to protect yourself against market fluctuations.

How to apply for a Forex Prepaid Card

You can apply for a Forex Prepaid Card at select ICICI Bank Branches or through the iMobile app and Net Banking.

To apply through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app or Net Banking:

Log in and navigate to the section of Forex Prepaid Cards

Select your preferred Card variant

Upload the required KYC documents

Load the Card with the foreign currency of your choice

Bank verification is completed instantly or within a day

The Card is issued and activated

New customers can apply by visiting any ICICI Bank Forex Servicing branch.

Forex Prepaid Card: Fees and charges

ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Cards come with certain charges, including a cross-currency fee of ~3.5% + GST, issuance charges, reload fees, ATM withdrawal fees and balance enquiry charges. Checking all the related charges before applying helps you understand the total cost and choose an affordable option. For detailed information, visit the 
Forex Prepaid Card fees and charges

Forex Cards FAQs

What is an ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card? 

It is a Prepaid Card that serves as a secure alternative for carrying foreign currency while travelling. You can add up to 15 currencies on a single Card.

What is a Forex Prepaid Card used for?

It is a convenient payment option during international travel, allowing easy payments without cash.

What are the benefits of having an ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid/Travel Card?

Store multiple currencies on a single Card

No extra charges for e-commerce transactions and transactions at merchant outlets

Universally accepted

Fixed exchange rates at the time of loading, avoiding dynamic currency conversion fees. 

What is a Multi-Currency Card?

A 
multi-currency
 Card has multiple wallets for different currencies loaded on it. ICICI Bank allows you to load up to 15 currencies on one Card.

Can I transfer money from my Forex Prepaid Card to my bank account?

Yes, the remaining amount can be refunded to the linked Bank Account when no longer needed.

How can I get a Forex Prepaid Card near me?

Submit your request at your nearest ICICI Bank Forex branch or through the ICICI Bank's iMobile app if you have an ICICI Bank Savings Account.

How do I load money on my Forex Prepaid Card?

You can load funds online through Net Banking or the ICICI Bank's iMobile app. Alternatively, you can visit an ICICI Bank Forex branch to load funds.

How can I activate my Forex Prepaid Card?

The Card activates automatically when you load it with funds. For inactive Cards, contact Customer Care for reactivation.

What should I do if I cannot withdraw funds from an ATM using my Forex Prepaid Card?

Check your card balance

Ensure you are within your daily withdrawal limit

If the issue persists, contact Customer Care for assistance.

What is the limit for cash withdrawals from ATMs using Forex Prepaid Card?

The daily withdrawal limit is $2,000 or its equivalent in any other currency, inclusive of charges and taxes.

Can I retain the foreign exchange on my ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card after returning to India?

Yes, but only if the remaining balance is less than $2,000. Refunds must be claimed within 180 days of return, as per RBI regulations.

What is the cost of a Forex Prepaid Card?

A Forex Prepaid Card comes with charges like issuance fees, reload charges and ATM withdrawal fees. Costs vary as per the variant of Forex Prepaid Card, but many Cards are affordable and designed to provide a safe and convenient way to spend abroad.

Can a Forex Prepaid Card be used to withdraw cash?

Yes, you can withdraw cash in local currency from international ATMs using your Forex Prepaid Card. The daily withdrawal limit is USD 2,000; or its equivalent in any other currency, including charges and taxes.

Can I get a Forex Prepaid Card in one day?

With ICICI Bank, you need to apply online at least seven working days before your travel date. If you need the Card urgently, you can visit an ICICI Bank Forex Servicing branch that might provide quicker assistance for Forex services.

Do I need a visa to get a Forex Prepaid Card?

No, you do not need a visa to get a Forex Prepaid Card. Ensure that you have the following documents:

 Passport

 Form A2

PAN Card

How much amount can I carry on a Forex Prepaid Card?

You can load up to the permitted RBI limit, which is up to USD 2,50,000 per financial year for travel under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS). The exact limit might depend on your travel purpose and bank policy.

Can money be transferred from a Forex Prepaid Card to a bank account?

Yes, the unused balance can be easily refunded to your linked bank account once you no longer need the Forex Prepaid Card. You just have to place a request with the Bank.

Are Travel Cards accepted everywhere?

Forex Prepaid Cards (also known as Travel Cards or Forex Cards) are widely accepted at international stores, hotels, online platforms and ATMs wherever the network (Visa, Mastercard, etc.) is supported.

What is the Forex limit in India?

Under RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), Indians can spend or remit up to USD <2,50,000> per financial year for travel, studies, medical treatment or other permitted uses. This limit also applies to Forex Prepaid Cards.

Can a Forex Prepaid Card be used at the airport?

Yes, you can use a Forex Prepaid Card at international airports for food, shopping or services, wherever Card payments are accepted.

What currencies can be loaded onto the ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card?

ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card is available in the following currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, CHF, JPY, AED, HKD, SGD, THB, SAR, SEK, ZAR and NZD. You can choose currencies based on your travel plan and switch between them using a Multi-Currency Card that supports 15 currencies on a single Card.

Can I use my Forex Prepaid Card on Uber?

Yes, you can use a Forex Prepaid Card on Uber abroad as long as the currency loaded matches that of the country you are travelling to. It works like a standard international Card for app-based payments and rides.

How do I buy or reload an ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card?

Purchase or reload your ICICI Bank Forex Prepaid Card by logging into Net Banking or the ICICI Bank's iMobile app. If you're not an ICICI Bank account holder, visit any ICICI Bank Forex Servicing Branch to apply. Reload anytime, anywhere, for continuous access to funds.
