iWish Flexible RD FAQs

                                     Basic Information                                     

                EXPAND ALL

                            What is the minimum amount required to open an iWish account? 

  You can start an iWish goal with a contribution as low as ₹500, making it accessible for all customers who wish to begin saving with smaller amounts.  

                            What is the tenure range for iWish goals? 

  iWish goals offer flexible tenures beginning from 6 months and going up to 5 years, allowing you to design short- and medium-term savings plans based on your specific goals.  

                            Can I deposit any amount at any time in iWish? 

Yes. iWish allows you to deposit any amount, at any frequency, based on your convenience.
 There are no restrictions on the number or size of contributions. 

                            Are there fixed monthly instalments in iWish?

No. There are no fixed monthly instalments. You can contribute freely, without the obligation to save on a specific date or for a fixed amount.You also have an option of setting up auto debit with monthly frequency

                            Can I deposit multiple times in a month into an iWish goal?

Yes. You may deposit as many times as you wish in a month. There are no upper limits on the number of contributions.

                            Are there penalties if I miss a deposit in a month under iWish?

No. iWish does not impose penalties for missed deposits.
The goal simply progresses based on how much you have deposited. You can make additional contributions later to stay on track.

                            Who is eligible to open an iWish goal?

Any resident ICICI Bank savings account holder with an active Net Banking or iMobile App profile can open an iWish goal digitally.

                            Can NRIs open an iWish goal?

No. iWish is currently available only to resident individuals.

NRIs, OCIs, and PIOs cannot open or operate iWish goals.

                            Do senior citizens get preferential interest rates under iWish?

Yes. Senior citizens earn preferential interest rates, similar to those offered on Fixed Deposits for the same tenure.

                            Are iWish interest rates same as Fixed Deposits or Recurring Deposits?

Yes. iWish earns interest at rates aligned with Fixed Deposit rates for the same tenure selected for the goal.

                                    Eligibility & Documentation                                   

                EXPAND ALL

                            What documents are required to open an iWish goal online?

No additional documents are required if your KYC is already updated.

iWish is opened instantly using your existing savings account profile through iMobile App or Net Banking.

                            What documents are needed to open iWish at a branch?

iWish is a fully digital product and cannot be opened at a branch.

You must create and manage iWish goals online through ICICI Bank’s digital platforms.

                            Is iWish available for minors?

Yes,iWish is available for minors.

                            What documents do senior citizens need for iWish?

Senior citizens do not need additional documents to open iWish.
However, age-proof should be updated in the Bank’s records to ensure preferential interest rates apply.

                            If I update my address or PAN, how does it affect my iWish account?

Updating your address or PAN at ICICI Bank automatically updates all linked accounts, including iWish.
Your iWish goal continues without interruption.

                             Can I modify nominee details for my iWish goal?

Yes. You can add or update a nominee for the savings account linked to iWish.
iWish uses the savings account nomination, so the same nominee applies. After creation if users wish to add/update nominee they can do so by visiting service request section > Add/Update nominee in RIB/iMobile

                            What documents do NRIs need to open iWish?

Not applicable.

NRIs are not eligible for iWish.

                            Are NRIs eligible for iWish and what proof is required?

iWish is only for resident customers, so no documentation framework exists for NRIs

                            Who can open an iWish goal?

Any resident Indian with an active ICICI Bank savings account and digital banking access (iMobile App or Net Banking) can open iWish goals.

                            Can I open an iWish goal online via iMobile App or Net Banking?

Yes. iWish is a 
100% digital product
, and goals can be created anytime, anywhere using:

iMobile App → 
Login > Accounts & FD/RD > Deposits > Explore iWish

Net Banking → Deposits → iWish

                            Can I open multiple iWish goals under the same customer ID?

Yes. You can create a maximum of 25 simultaneous iWish goals, each with different target amounts and tenures.

                            Can I start an iWish goal with existing savings only?

Yes. You can begin with an initial contribution and continue adding funds as per your convenience.

                            Can I switch my savings account linked to an existing iWish goal?

No. Once linked, the funding account cannot be changed.

                            Are there any residency restrictions for opening iWish?

Yes. iWish is available only for resident individuals.

                            Can NRIs open or operate an iWish goal?

No. NRIs cannot create or manage iWish goals.

                            Can I convert an existing Recurring Deposit into an iWish goal?

No. RDs cannot be converted into iWish. However, you may run both independently for diversified savings.

                                      Interest Rate , Fees & Charges                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            Are there any charges for opening or maintaining an iWish goal?

No. iWish is completely free to open and maintain.
There are no service charges, penalties, or maintenance fees.

                            What interest rates are offered on iWish goals?

iWish earns interest at Fixed Deposit rates corresponding to the chosen tenure.
Rates are published on the ICICI Bank website and updated periodically.

                            Are the interest rates fixed for the entire tenure of iWish?

Yes. Once the goal is created and tenure is chosen, the interest rate remains fixed throughout the tenure, ensuring predictable returns.

                            Do senior citizens receive higher interest rates on iWish?

Yes. Senior citizens earn preferential interest rates, similar to FDs of the same tenure.

                            Is TDS applicable on interest earned from iWish goals?

Yes. TDS may be deducted if your aggregate interest across all ICICI Bank deposits exceeds the prescribed limit in a financial year.

                            Can iWish interest rate change mid-tenure?

No. The interest rate is locked at the time of creating the goal and does not change later.

                            Are there any hidden or service charges for iWish?

No. iWish is a fully transparent savings product with no hidden fees or charges.

                            What happens if I skip deposits or make variable deposits under iWish?

Nothing negative. There are no penalties for skipped months or irregular deposits.
Your progress simply depends on how much you contribute.

                            For NRIs: Are there additional charges or tax implications for iWish?

Not applicable since NRIs cannot open iWish goals.

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I set multiple financial goals under iWish simultaneously?

Yes. You can create and manage upto 
25 iWish goals
 at the same time.
 Each goal may have its own:

Name

Target amount

Tenure

Contribution pattern
 This helps you plan separately for different needs such as gadgets, travel, education, festivals, or emergency funds.

                            Can I set custom goal names and target amounts for each iWish goal?

Yes. iWish offers complete customisation.
You can personalise your goal name (e.g., “New Laptop”, “Europe Trip”, “Festive Shopping”) and choose any target amount that suits your objective.

                            Can I nominate a beneficiary for my iWish goal?

iWish uses the nominee of your linked savings account.
Therefore, your iWish goal is automatically covered under the same nominee details.
You can update the nominee anytime through your savings account.

                            Are rewards or milestones added when I progress towards my iWish goal?

Yes. iWish includes digital milestone indicators, progress bars, and reminders that motivate you throughout your savings journey.
These features make saving engaging and goal-oriented.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs nominate beneficiaries or set goal features under iWish?

Not applicable, as iWish is only available to resident customers.

                                      Benefits                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            What are the key benefits of using iWish over a traditional RD?

iWish offers greater flexibility compared to an RD:

No fixed monthly instalments

Contribute any amount, anytime

Set personalized goals

Track progress digitally

No penalties for missed months
 This makes iWish ideal for customers seeking structured yet flexible savings.

                            How does iWish help encourage disciplined savings habits?

iWish provides regular digital reminders, progress tracking, and target-based motivation to encourage consistent saving.
While deposits are flexible, the goal structure helps instil financial discipline.

                            Does iWish offer flexibility in deposit amounts and frequency?

Yes. You can deposit any amount at any time—daily, weekly, monthly, or occasionally.
This flexibility makes it suitable for customers with fluctuating income or irregular savings patterns.

                            Can iWish help me plan for multiple financial goals at once?

Yes. You can create several goals simultaneously, each with unique names, amounts, and tenures.
This helps you save methodically for different life events.

                            Is iWish suitable for short-, medium-, and long-term goals?

Yes. With tenures ranging from 
6 months to 5 years
, iWish covers:

Short-term goals like gadgets or holidays

Medium-term goals like education expenses

Longer goals such as home improvements or emergency funds

                            Does iWish offer higher convenience through fully digital management?

Absolutely. iWish is a 100% digital product that can be opened, managed, tracked, and closed entirely through iMobile App or Net Banking.

                            Are the returns from iWish guaranteed and predictable?

Yes. iWish earns interest at Fixed Deposit rates and therefore offers assured and predictable returns upon maturity.

                            For NRIs: What benefits does iWish offer to NRIs?

Not applicable. iWish is not available to NRI customers.

                            For NRIs: Is repatriation applicable for iWish maturity proceeds?

Not applicable. Since NRIs cannot create iWish goals, repatriation rules are not relevant

                                      Repayment & Tenure                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                             How are contributions made to an iWish goal?

: From S/a via fund transfer or UPI

                            Can I deposit money into an iWish goal multiple times in a single month?

Yes. You can contribute any number of times during the month without restrictions.

                            What happens if I do not deposit for several months in an iWish goal?

Nothing is penalised.
Your goal remains active, but the progress slows until you resume depositing.
Your maturity amount depends solely on your contributions.

                            Is there a penalty for missing a target month in iWish?

No. There are no penalties for skipped months.
You remain in full control of how and when you save.

                            Can I set auto-debit instructions for my iWish deposits?

iWish does not offer mandatory auto-debit since it is designed for flexible, voluntary contributions. You can deposit manually at any time.

                            Can I change the account used for funding my iWish contributions?

No. The linked savings account cannot be changed mid-tenure.
To use another account, you must close the existing goal and create a new one.

                            Can I track past contributions made to an iWish goal?

Yes. You can view your entire contribution history through iMobile App or Net Banking under Goal Details.

                            For NRIs: Can iWish be funded from overseas bank accounts?

Not applicable since NRIs cannot operate iWish.

                                      PREMATURE WITHDRAWALS                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I close my iWish goal before maturity?

Yes. You can close your iWish goal at any time through digital channels. A penalty of 0.5% to 1% is charged on the applicable interest rate for premature withdrawal.

                            Will I receive interest if I close my iWish goal prematurely?

Yes. You will receive interest calculated at the rate applicable for the tenure completed up to the closure date.

                            What penalties apply for premature withdrawal of an iWish goal?

                            Is partial withdrawal allowed from an active iWish goal?

No. iWish does not allow partial withdrawals.
To access funds early, you must close the goal.

                            Are there lock-in conditions before allowing premature closure of iWish?

No. There is no lock-in period. iWish can be closed anytime.

                            For NRIs: Do premature withdrawal rules differ for NRIs?

Not applicable.

                            For NRIs: Is TDS applicable differently for NRIs closing iWish early?

Not applicable.

                                      EMI & LOANS ON IWISH                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I convert my iWish contributions into EMI?

No. EMI conversion applies only to payments made using credit cards or select banking transactions. iWish deposits cannot be converted into EMIs, and no instalment structure is imposed.

                            Can I link my iWish goal to my ICICI Bank Credit Card?

No. iWish contributions must come exclusively from your ICICI Savings Account. You cannot link, fund, or automate contributions via a credit card.

                            Can iWish funding generate credit card reward points if I use my card to transfer funds?

No. Since credit cards cannot be used to fund iWish, there is no way to earn reward points from iWish contributions.

                            Does iWish affect my loan eligibility?

Indirectly, yes. While iWish itself is not collateral, maintaining consistent savings can improve your financial discipline profile. Banks often review overall savings behaviour for unsecured loans, and regular iWish activity may help demonstrate responsible money management.

                            Are NRIs eligible for any loan benefit linked to iWish?

No. Even if an NRI continues an old iWish goal, it cannot be used for loans or overdraft purposes. Lending rules for NRIs do not treat iWish as collateral.

                            Can I take a loan or overdraft against my iWish balance?

No. iWish is designed strictly as a savings product and is not treated as collateral for loans or overdraft facilities. Unlike fixed deposits, iWish goals cannot be pledged or used to secure credit lines.

                                      Reload & Refunds                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I extend or reduce the tenure of my iWish goal after creating it?

No. No cannot adjust the tenure.

                            Can I pause my iWish goal temporarily?

While there is no formal “pause” option, you may simply stop contributing temporarily.
There are no penalties for taking a break, and your goal remains active until closed or matured.

                            What happens if I exceed my target amount before the end of the tenure?

If you reach your target early:

You may close the goal and earn interest for the completed tenure (Premature charges will apply), or
Continue contributing until your selected maturity date
Your goal remains flexible based on your preference.

                            Can I withdraw a portion of the amount saved under iWish before maturity?

No. Partial withdrawals are not permitted.
To access funds early, you must close the goal, after which the accumulated amount (with applicable interest) is credited to your account.

                            For NRIs: Are partial withdrawals allowed for NRIs under iWish?

Not applicable. iWish is not available to NRIs.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs modify iWish target amount or tenure from abroad?

Not applicable, as iWish cannot be created or managed by NRIs.

                                      Closure                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            What happens when my iWish goal reaches its target amount?

Once your target amount is achieved, you may:

Continue saving until maturity, or

Close the goal immediately and receive your savings along with applicable interest (Premature charges will apply)
 The choice remains fully flexible for your convenience.

                             What happens if my iWish goal reaches its maturity date but does not meet the target amount?

If your goal matures without meeting the target:

The amount accumulated until that date

Plus accrued interest

…is credited to your linked savings account.
 There are no penalties for not meeting your goal.

                            How do I close an iWish goal manually before its maturity?

You can close your iWish goal instantly through:

iMobile App
: Select your goal → Close Goal

Net Banking
: Deposits → iWish → Close Goal
 The amount saved, along with applicable interest, will be credited to your savings account.

                            How long does it take for the funds to be credited once an iWish goal is closed?

Funds are credited immediately to your linked savings account upon closing the goal.

                            Can I reopen an iWish goal after closing it?

No. Once closed, an iWish goal cannot be reopened.
You may create a new goal at any time.

                            For NRIs: Are closure rules different for NRI iWish account holders?

Not applicable. iWish is only for resident customers.

                            For NRIs: Are repatriation rules applicable for iWish closures?

Not applicable, since NRIs are not permitted to hold iWish goals.

                                       Security, Custody & Safety                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            How secure is the iWish platform for online savings?

iWish is built within ICICI Bank’s secure digital ecosystem and is protected through:

Multi-level authentication

SSL encryption

OTP validation

Device-level security
 This ensures your transactions and savings data remain fully safeguarded.

                            Are my iWish funds protected under DICGC insurance?

Yes. Deposits linked to iWish are held within your ICICI Bank savings account or deposit structure and are protected up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank under DICGC guidelines.

                            How can I verify the authenticity of my iWish balance and transactions?

You can verify your iWish details anytime through:

iMobile App → iWish → Goal Details

Net Banking → Deposits → iWish Dashboard
 All contributions, accrued interest, and maturity details are displayed transparently.

                            What should I do if I notice suspicious activity linked to my iWish goal?

Take immediate steps:

Contact ICICI Bank Customer Care

Review recent transactions on iMobile App or Net Banking

Visit the nearest branch if required
 The Bank will secure your account and initiate necessary investigations.

                            Does iWish require OTP verification for deposits or changes?

Yes. Any critical action—such as deposits, changes in tenure, or closure—requires OTP authentication to ensure secure access.

                            For NRIs: How can NRIs report iWish-related concerns from overseas?

Not applicable since NRIs cannot open iWish goals.

                                   Service Requests & Account Management                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I modify the target amount or tenure after creating an iWish goal?

No. iWish does not allow you to adjust the target amount and modify the tenure.

                            How can I update nominee details for my iWish goal?

iWish uses the nomination linked to your savings account.
You can update the nominee by updating the nomination for the linked savings account through digital channels or any ICICI branch.

                            Can I change the savings account linked to my iWish goal?

No. The funding account cannot be changed after the goal is created.
To link a different account, you must close the existing goal and create a new one.

                            Can I download statements or contribution history for my iWish goals?

Yes. Contribution history and goal statements can be downloaded through iMobile App or Net Banking.
These include detailed entries for each deposit made.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs update contact or address details linked to iWish?

Not applicable. iWish is available only to resident customers.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs access iWish goals from abroad through digital channels?

Not applicable, since NRIs cannot create or operate iWish.

                                  GOAL MANAGEMENT

                EXPAND ALL

                            Can I change the goal name of my iWish goal after creating it?

Yes. You can update the name of your iWish goal at any point through iMobile App or Net Banking.
This helps you personalise and realign the goal based on your changing priorities.

                            Can I change the target amount midway through the iWish goal?

No. iWish does not allow complete flexibility to increase or decrease the target amount.

                            Can I set reminders or notifications for my iWish goal progress?

Yes. You can enable reminders and notifications within iMobile App to track progress, stay aligned with your goal, and stay motivated throughout your savings journey.

                            How can I track if I am on schedule to meet my iWish goal?

iWish provides a dedicated goal dashboard that displays:

Progress percentage

Contributions made

Remaining amount

Tenure left
 This real-time progress tracking ensures you always know how close you are to achieving your target.

                            Can I set multiple goals with different tenures and target amounts?

Yes. You can create 
multiple iWish goals simultaneously
, each with its own:

Target amount

Tenure

Purpose

Contribution pattern

This is ideal for planning different financial milestones at the same time.

                            Can I delete or remove a goal that I no longer wish to pursue?

Yes. You may close your iWish goal at any time if you no longer want to save for it.
The amount accumulated along with applicable interest will be credited to your savings account.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs manage goals created before their status changed?

Not applicable. When a resident becomes an NRI, no new iWish goals can be created, but existing goals may continue until maturity.
However, all new contributions must follow regulatory guidelines for NRIs.

                                      DIGITAL CHANNELS & ACCESS                                      

                EXPAND ALL

                             Can I create and manage iWish goals entirely online?

Yes. iWish is a 100% digital savings facility and can be created, viewed, managed, and closed fully through iMobile App or Net Banking.

                            Is iWish available on both iMobile App and Net Banking?

Yes. iWish is accessible on both platforms:

iMobile App:
 For effortless on-the-go access

Net Banking:
 For detailed goal tracking and contribution management

Both offer complete functionality.

                            Can I create iWish goals on non-ICICI Bank apps or platforms?

No. iWish can only be created and managed through official ICICI Bank digital channels for security and authentication purposes.

                            What should I do if iWish goals do not appear in my app?

If iWish is not visible:

Update your iMobile App app

Ensure your savings account is active

Check if your KYC is updated

Clear app cache or re-login
 If the issue persists, you may contact Customer Care or visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch.

                            Can I manage iWish goals if I change my registered mobile number?

Yes. Once your mobile number is updated in bank records, you can continue operating iWish seamlessly through iMobile App and Net Banking.

                            Can I access and track my iWish goals after closing my savings account?

No. An active ICICI Bank savings account is mandatory for maintaining iWish goals.
If the linked savings account is closed, the iWish goal will also be closed and proceeds credited before closure.

                            For NRIs: Can NRIs access iWish goals while living abroad?

Not applicable. NRIs cannot create or manage iWish goals.

                            Can I add a nominee to my iWish goal?

Yes. The nominee of your linked savings account automatically applies to your iWish goal.

                            Can I change or remove a nominee during the tenure?

Yes. Any changes to the savings account nomination will automatically apply to iWish as well.
You may update nominee details through digital channels or at any branch.

                            What happens to my iWish goal if the account holder passes away?

In the unfortunate event of the account holder’s demise:

The nominee or legal heir can claim the iWish proceeds

The funds are settled as per the savings account nomination
 This ensures smooth and secure claim settlement.

                            How do legal heirs claim funds from an iWish goal?

Legal heirs or nominees can submit:

Claim form

Identity proof

Death certificate

KYC documents
 Once verified, the proceeds are released as per ICICI Bank’s standard claim settlement procedures.

                            Are minors eligible to be nominees for iWish goals?

Yes. Minors can be added as nominees.
In such cases, the guardian’s details are recorded for claim settlement.

                            For NRIs: Do NRI nomination rules apply to iWish goals?

Not applicable. iWish is available only for resident customers.

                            How is interest on iWish goals taxed?

Interest earned on iWish is treated as income from other sources and is fully taxable as per your applicable tax slab.

                            Is TDS deducted on the interest earned in iWish?

Yes. TDS may be deducted if your aggregate interest across all ICICI Bank deposits exceeds the prescribed limit in a financial year.

                            How can I view the TDS deducted on my iWish interest?

You may view or download TDS details through:

iMobile App → Services → Tax Centre

Net Banking → Tax → Interest/TDS Certificates

Form 26AS on the Income Tax portal

                            Are there any tax-saving benefits associated with iWish goals?

No. iWish does not qualify for deductions under Section 80C or any other tax-saving category.

                            Does iWish qualify under Section 80C tax savings?

No. iWish is not eligible for 80C tax deductions.

                            For NRIs: Are tax rules different for NRIs using iWish?

Not applicable, as iWish is not offered to NRIs.

Yes. iWish is well-suited for planning recurring annual expenses.
You can create dedicated goals for periodic payments and save steadily through the year.

If the linked savings account is closed, the iWish goal will also be closed automatically.
The accumulated amount will be credited before account closure.

Yes. Changing your account variant does not affect your iWish goals.
All active goals continue as long as the savings account remains operational.

If your residency status changes:

New iWish goals cannot be created

Existing goals may continue until maturity

New contributions must comply with NRI regulations

                            For NRIs: Can an iWish goal continue until maturity after residential status change?

Yes. Existing iWish goals may continue until maturity, although no new goals can be created after the status change.
