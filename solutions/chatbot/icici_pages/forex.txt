FX settlement options offered by ICICI Bank

Same-day settlement (T)

CASH transaction is for value date TODAY (T), when transaction-settlement is to be on the same day.

Settlement on T+1

A TOM transaction is for value date T+1 business day, perfect for next-business-day fund transfers.

Standard FX settlement at T+2

SPOT transaction is for value date being T+2 business days. It's noted as the most used settlement cycle.

Lock in future exchange rates

Lock in future rates dates beyond T+2 business days. It helps to manage future currency exposures and hedge against market volatility.
