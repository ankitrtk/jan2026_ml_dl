Programme Eligibility

Criteria

Conditions

Individuals

Maintain Deposits (Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits) + Account Balance of ₹5 Lakh OR
Deposits (Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits) + Account Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat holdings of ₹50 Lakh

NRI Select Family Banking

Maintain Deposits (Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits) + Balance of ₹7.5 lakh OR
Deposits (Fixed Deposits/Recurring Deposits) + Balance + Mutual funds AUM^ + Demat of ₹75 lakh

^AUM - Assets under Management

If the customer does not meet the programme eligibility criteria for 12 months, the customer will be re-graded.
Customer needs to maintain a minimum Monthly Average Balance (MAB*) of ₹15,000 at Customer ID^ Level to avoid non-maintenance charge.

*MAB is the simple average of day-end balances for a calendar month.

^Customer ID includes all your savings account cumulative balances (except Fixed deposits/Recurring deposits)

FAQs

Can I extend Select Banking benefits to my family members?

Yes, you can extend Select Banking benefits to your family members by opting for Family Banking.

How to apply for an NRI Account?

You can apply for an NRE/NRO Account either through our
 Assisted Journey
 with the help of a Relationship Manager or through a 
Do-It-Yourself (DIY) Journey
 online.

RM Assisted Journey

Sign up for an NRE/NRO Account: Fill your details to receive a call.

Live Assistance: Get help over a call to fill the account opening form and KYC documentation.

Account Opened: Your account is opened after successful scrutiny of KYC documents.

Activation: Activate your account with the first fund transfer from your own overseas account.

APPLY NOW

Do-It-Yourself (DIY) Journey

Get Started: Choose the NRE/NRO account program that suits you.

Complete Your Application: Complete and submit the digital form with your details (Keep your self-signed copies of Passport, visa, and address proof ready).

Account Opened: Your account is opened after successful scrutiny of KYC documents.

Activation: Activate your account with the first fund transfer from your own overseas account.

*Only available for customers in USA.

APPLY NOW

How many family members can be linked under Select Banking?

You can link up to 9 family members under Select Banking

Does the NRI Select Programme offer special benefits for my family members?

Yes, the NRI Select Programme extends several benefits to your family members, including access to enhanced banking privileges, ease of maintaining balances and much more.

How does the NRI Select Programme help with managing my finances across borders?

The NRI Select Programme offers convenient banking services, including easy access to both NRE and NRO accounts, competitive exchange rates and seamless international money transfers. It also provides an online platform and mobile app for effortless management of your finances from anywhere in the world.

How can I access the benefits of the NRI Select Programme through ICICI Bank's mobile app?

You can access the benefits of the NRI Select Programme through ICICI Bank’s user-friendly mobile banking app and internet Banking, which allows you to manage your accounts, transfer money, track remittances, access your debit card offers, and more, all from the convenience of your smartphone.

How to access domestic airport lounge?

Domestic airports: Get two complimentary lounge access per quarter at domestic airport/terminals in India.

How can I upgrade my account to NRI Select programme?

Existing NRI Saving account holder can upgrade to NRI Select programme upon agreement to the programme eligibility. You can upgrade from any of these options : Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, Branch and Phone banking etc. Upon upgrade, you shall be eligible for a debit card upgrade.

If you already hold the requisite balances in your account, you can upgrade your account by placing a request at your nearest ICICI Bank branch or calling our 
24*7 Customer Care
.

If you don’t hold the requisite balances in your account, fund your account to ensure required balances and then, place the request at your nearest ICICI Bank branch or call our 
24*7 Customer Care
.

You can continue with the existing card after account upgrade.

Alternatively, you can apply for a new card through any of the following ways :

Call our
 24-hour Customer Care
.

Place a request through your Internet banking account.

Visit you nearest ICICI Bank branch in India.

 Is interest earned taxable?

Interest earned on NRE accounts is tax-free in India whereas Interest earned on NRO accounts is taxable in India
