LOAN REPAYMENT SCHEDULE

How to get maximum benefits?

Quick Processing

Loan approval within 1–2 working days

Flexible Repayment

Choose monthly, quarterly or half-yearly instalments

Customised Funding

Loans tailored to match your equipment and land holding

Top-Up Options

Additional loan available on existing farm loans

Wide Coverage

Loans for new and used farm equipment as well as refinancing of farm machinery

Attractive Rates

Competitive interest to support your agricultural investment.

Things you should know

Processing Fee

2% of the loan amount + documentation charges + GST.

Eligibility

Age 18–65 years; permanent house (residential property) required.

CIBIL Score

650+ preferred, but lower scores might be considered.

Part Payment

Allowed after the first instalment; no extra charges.

Pre-payment

Subject to applicable foreclosure charges.

Loan Purpose

Funds must be used only for agricultural equipment.

Here are the steps of the process of loan application and approval:

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch or the nearest dealership

Submit the completed application form and KYC documents

Property or land and equipment evaluation (if required)

Loan eligibility assessment and loan sanction

Signing of the loan agreement and completion of all formalities

Loan disbursement will be done within 1–2 days.

About Farm Equipment Loans

New Tractor Loan

Finance the purchase of new tractors with flexible repayment options designed to match your cropping cycle and income flow.

Used Tractor Loan

Get affordable loans for pre-owned tractors with quick processing and minimal documentation.

Harvester Loan

Special loans to buy harvesters and other high-value farm equipment, enabling greater productivity in large-scale farming.

Tractor Refinance and Top-Up

Avail refinance against your existing Tractor Loan or get a top-up loan to access additional funds for your farm needs.

FAQs

What credit score is required for a Tractor Loan?

A score of 650+ is ideal. Lower scores might also be accepted based on repayment history and income profile.

Can I part-prepay my loan?

Yes, partial repayment is allowed after your first instalment, without extra charges.

How long does loan processing take?

Most Farm Equipment Loan applications are processed within 1–2 working days.

How do I apply for a Tractor Loan?

Apply via dealership stores, ICICI Bank branches or online through the ICICI Bank website.

What is the maximum tenure available?

Tenure ranges from 12 months to 72 months, depending on loan type and repayment capacity.

Is there a Customer Care number for queries?

Yes, give a missed call on 8268001808 or call us on 1800 1080.
