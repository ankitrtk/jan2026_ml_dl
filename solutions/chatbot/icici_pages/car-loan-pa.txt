FAQs

What is ICICI Bank Digital Car Loan?

ICICI Bank Digital Car Loan is a fully online, end-to-end car loan process, allowing you to apply, customise your loan, and receive funds directly to your dealer, all digitally.

How can I apply for the ICICI Bank Insta Digital Car Loan?

You can apply easily through ICICI Bank's iMobile app, Net Banking, or the Car Loan portal.

Here is the process through ICICI Bank's iMobile app:

Log in to ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Click on the Get Instant Loans or Offers’ tab

Check and click ‘Apply Now’ on the Car Loan offer

Enter the loan amount, agree to the disclaimers and click Next

Customise the loan offer

eSign the agreement and submit

How much funding can I get with the Digital Car Loan?

ICICI Bank offers up to 100% funding of the on-road price of the car, depending on your eligibility and loan approval.

Is the ICICI Bank Insta Digital Car Loan a paperless process?

Yes! The entire process, from applying to disbursing funds, is fully digital. There’s no paperwork involved, making it a quick and hassle-free experience.

Can I choose any car and dealer for the Digital Car Loan?

Yes, you can select any car and dealer of your choice. The loan process allows you to customise your loan according to your chosen car and dealer.
