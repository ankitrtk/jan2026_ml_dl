ICICI Bank's iMobile app FAQs

What is ICICI Bank's iMobile app?

ICICI Bank's iMobile app, the most comprehensive and secure Mobile Banking application, offers 300+ banking and informational services to take care of your daily banking needs conveniently from your smartphone. For easier accessibility we have made ICICI Bank's iMobile app available on Android, iOS.

To know more details of ICICI Bank's iMobile app, please visit our website www.icici.bank.in > Mobile Banking (Bottom of the page) > ICICI Bank's iMobile app - ICICI Bank Mobile Banking App.

What is the use or benefit of ICICI Bank's iMobile app?

The ICICI Bank's iMobile app, is the most comprehensive and secure Mobile Banking application, offering over 300+ banking and informational services to take care of your daily banking needs conveniently from your smartphone. For easier accessibility, we have made ICICI Bank's iMobile app available on Android, iOS.

With ICICI Bank's iMobile app you can check your account balance, transfer funds, pay utility bills, recharge your mobile phone, DTH connection, broadband, book rail, flight, hotel and much more on the go.
