Corporate Platinum Card FAQs

What does the ICICI Bank Corporate Platinum Credit Card offer?

The Corporate Platinum Credit Card is designed for mid-to-large corporates to manage employee travel, entertainment, and business expenses. It offers centralized control, detailed reporting, and a range of travel and lifestyle benefits.

What are the key benefits of this card?

Travel Privileges: Complimentary domestic airport lounge access.

Insurance Cover: Air accident and travel insurance for cardholders.

Expense Management: Consolidated statements and ERP/TMC integration.

Global Acceptance: Accepted across millions of merchants worldwide.

Rewards: Earn ICICI Bank Rewards Points on every eligible spend.

What types of expenses can be paid with the Corporate Platinum Card?

The card is designed to be used for all kinds of Travel and Entertainment expenses of Corporate employees as per their requirements.

Does the card provide lounge access?

Yes. The card offers 2 complimentary International airport lounge access per year around the world and 2 Domestic airport lounge access per quarter at select airports in India.

What insurance coverage is provided?

Cardholders are eligible for cover against:

Personal accident

Travel inconvenience cover (delayed baggage, lost card liability, etc.)

Fraudulent transactions

Are international transactions allowed?

Yes. The card can be used globally for business expenses.

What is the liability policy for fraudulent transactions?

The liability depends on the model chosen by the corporate as per its requirements. ICICI Bank Corporate Card can be issued with Sole Liability of the Corporate, Joint liability of Corporate and Employee and Individual liability of the Employee.

How can the card be blocked in case of loss/theft?

The cardholder or corporate admin can immediately call ICICI Bank’s 24x7 helpline or use SelfCare Portal for blocking the card online.

How is the ICICI Bank Corporate Platinum Card different from a personal credit card?

Unlike a personal credit card, the Corporate Platinum Card is issued under the corporate’s liability/undertaking. It provides expense control, policy compliance, and travel-related benefits tailored for businesses.
