Personal Loan for Medical Emergency FAQs

What is a Personal Loan for Medical Emergency?

A Medical Loan is a Personal Loan which covers unexpected medical expenses. It offers quick approval, minimal documentation, zero collateral, ensuring immediate financial assistance.

How fast is the loan processing and disbursal?

ICICI Bank offers instant approval and quick disbursal upon successful application. Funds are credited immediately after approval, ensuring timely support during medical emergencies.

Does a Personal Loan for medical expenses offer tax benefits?

No, Personal Loans, including those for medical emergencies, do not qualify for tax benefits under the Income Tax Act. However, they offer quick access to funds without requiring collateral.

What are the key features of an ICICI Bank Personal Loan for Medical Emergency?

Loan Amount: Based on eligibility, with quick approval

Interest Rate: Starts from 10.85% per annum

Repayment Tenure: Flexible options from 12 - 72 months

Processing Fee: Up to 2% of the loan amount + GST

Zero Collateral: No security or guarantor required

100% digital process: Apply online anytime, anywhere

Can I foreclose my loan with ICICI Bank?

Personal Loan foreclosure facility is extended to all customers availing a Personal Loan with ICICI Bank. Customers can foreclose their Personal Loan after the payment of at least one EMI. However, foreclosure charges (and taxes) will be applicable as per the 
service charges
.

What are the mandatory documents needed for a Personal Loan for medical requirements?

Documents required for a Personal Loan for ​​medical needs include identity proof, address proof and income-related documents. This information is collected from your Aadhaar/PAN/bank statements after your consent. Depending on the applicant profile, additional documents may be requested for verification. The exact documentation is confirmed during the application process when you apply for a Personal Loan for medical emergencies at ICICI Bank.

Is collateral required to apply for a Personal Loan for medical emergencies?

No collateral is required to apply for a Personal Loan for medical emergencies, as it is an unsecured loan. Eligibility and approval depend on factors such as income, credit profile and repayment capacity, rather than on providing any property or asset as security.

What are the factors to be considered while applying for a Personal Loan for a medical emergency?

When applying for a Personal Loan for a medical emergency, key factors include income stability, existing financial obligations, credit history and repayment capacity. These factors help determine eligibility and the loan amount that may be offered, subject to internal assessment and applicable lending policies.

What is the maximum loan amount I can get for a Personal Loan for a medical emergency?

The maximum loan amount for a Personal Loan for a medical emergency depends on the applicant's income, credit profile and existing liabilities. The eligible amount is assessed during the application process and may vary based on internal evaluation criteria and prevailing lending guidelines.

Can I get a Personal Loan for a medical emergency without collateral

Yes, a Personal Loan is generally unsecured and does not require collateral, subject to eligibility criteria.

What is the repayment tenure for a Personal Loan for a medical emergency?

Repayment tenure depends on loan amount and internal eligibility norms at the time of approval. ICICI Bank provides Personal Loan for a maximum tenure of 6 years.
