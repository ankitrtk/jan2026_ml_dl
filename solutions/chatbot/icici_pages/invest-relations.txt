UPDATION OF PAN, KYC DETAILS AND NOMINATION

How can I register/update my Permanent Account Number (PAN) and
                                                    KYC details?

Investors holding securities in physical form are requested to
                                                register/update their KYC details including PAN, postal address, email
                                                address, bank details, etc. by submitting duly filled and signed Form
                                                ISR-1 to the Registrar to an Issue and Share Transfer Agent (R & T Agent) of the Bank.

Investors holding securities in dematerialised form are requested to
                                                register/update their KYC details with their respective Depository
                                                Participants.

How can I register/update my choice of nomination?

Investors holding securities in physical form are requested to send their
                                                nomination in the prescribed Form SH-13 duly filled, to the R & T Agent
                                                of the Bank. If the Investor desires to opt out or cancel the earlier
                                                nomination and record a fresh nomination, he/she may submit the same in
                                                Form ISR-3 or Form SH-14 as the case may be.

Investors holding securities in dematerialised form are requested to
                                                contact their respective Depository Participants for availing this
                                                facility.

DIVIDEND/INTEREST/REDEMPTION AMOUNT

How can I receive dividend/interest/redemption amount
                                                    electronically? 

Investors holding securities in physical form are requested to
                                                register/update their KYC details including bank details by submitting
                                                duly filled and signed Form ISR-1 to the R & T Agent of the Bank.

Investors holding securities in dematerialised form are requested to
                                                approach the Depository Participant with whom they have a demat account
                                                for updation of KYC details including bank details.
