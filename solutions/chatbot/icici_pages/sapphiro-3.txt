Expressions Sapphiro Debit Card FAQs

How can I upgrade and avail my card to an Expressions Sapphiro debit card?

You can call ICICI Bank’s customer care or log in to the website to upgrade to the Expressions Sapphiro debit card.

How and who can apply for the Expressions Sapphiro debit card?

All existing ICICI customers can upgrade to the Expressions Sapphiro debit card through the bank's website or by visiting the nearest ICICI bank branch.

What are the Expressions Sapphiro debit card benefits?

A customer can get complimentary lounge access in participating airports, emergency assistance in India and abroad, fraud protection services, movie ticket discounts and reward points with a expressions Sapphiro debit card.

How can I change the image on my Expressions Sapphiro debit card?

You can apply for a change through the bank's website or at your nearby ICICI bank branch. You can opt for your desired picture to be printed on your card out of a collection of 200 photos.

What are the joining benefits for the Expressions Sapphiro debit card?

When you upgrade or enroll for an Expressions Sapphiro debit card, you will get a Tata CLiQ gift voucher worth Rs. 1500. You can redeem the voucher by logging on to the Tata CLiQ website.

How many reward points can I get when using the Expressions Sapphiro debit card?

You can earn 4 and 8 ICICI reward points for Rs. 200 spent in India and abroad, respectively. For more information, log in to the bank website.

What emergency assistance can a cardholder get through the card?

The debit card offers emergency cash, travel tickets, and hotel bill payment assistance in India and abroad. The assistance varies between Rs. 20,000 and Rs. 1,60,000.
