How to calculate Home Loan EMI?

Our Home Loan EMI Calculator uses a simple mathematical formula to ensure accurate results for your financial planning.

Here’s the formula used:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

where:

P = Principal (Loan amount)

N = Loan tenure in months

R = Monthly interest rate

The monthly interest rate (R) is calculated as the annual rate of interest divided by 12 and then by 100.

Example: For a loan amount of ₹ 10,00,000, an illustrative annual interest rate of 7.25% and a tenure of 120 months, the EMI would be approximately ₹ 11,740.

Home Loan EMI calculator FAQs

What is Home Loan EMI?

Home Loan EMIs (Equated Monthly Instalments) are fixed monthly payments towards the 
repayment of your home loan.
 These payments consist of both principal and interest components. 

Why use the Home Loan EMI calculator?

Using an online Home Loan EMI Calculator

helps you plan your finances better. It quickly shows your monthly EMI amount, total interest to be paid and the total repayment amount. The calculator saves time, avoids manual errors and lets you compare different loan options easily before applying.

What is the benefit of calculating Home Loan EMIs?

Calculating the EMIs for your Home Loan can assist you in managing your finances and making informed decisions. Here are the key advantages of calculating EMIs before applying for a Loan:

Sound Financial Planning: Knowing the EMI amount and Loan duration helps you plan your finances effectively– you can budget your expenses accordingly and ensure that your EMI falls well within your income. This allows you to maintain financial stability and avoid any last-minute financial strain.

Comparing Loan Offers: Calculating EMIs allows you to compare Loan offers from various lenders. By entering the loan amount, interest rate and tenure into an EMI calculator, you can evaluate the total repayment amount and determine the most cost-effective option. This way, you can choose the loan that meets your financial requirements.

What are the factors that affect the Home Loan EMI?

Home Loan EMI amount is influenced by several factors, such as:

Interest Rate:
 A lower interest rate results in lower EMIs whereas a higher rate leads to higher EMIs.

Loan duration:
 A shorter tenure increases the EMI but decreases the total interest paid. A longer duration reduces your EMI but increases the overall interest paid.

Loan Amount:
 A higher Loan amount means a higher EMI; likewise, a lower amount means a lower EMI.

Credit Score:
 A higher credit score can help you secure a lower interest rate, which reduces the EMI amount. 

How are the principal and the interest paid through EMIs?

Home Loan EMIs include repayment of the principal as well as the interest. Initially, EMIs focus more on interest, with later payments covering more of the principal amount.

How many times can I use the Home Loan EMI calculator?

You can use the Home Loan EMI Calculator as many times as you wish. It is available on the ICICI Bank website and allows you to adjust the loan amount, tenure and interest rate anytime. This flexibility helps you find an ideal repayment plan without any restrictions.

How can I reduce my Home Loan EMI amount?

To lower your Home Loan EMI, you can increase the loan tenure, make part pre-payments or transfer the loan to another lender at a lower interest rate. Using a Home Loan EMI Calculator helps you compare options and choose an affordable plan.

Is the EMI amount fixed?

The Home Loan EMI remains fixed for the entire loan tenure if you choose a fixed interest rate. However, in floating-rate loans, EMIs may change when the Home Loan interest rate fluctuates, depending on market conditions and the bank's policy.

What is the impact of part payments on the EMI?

Making part payments reduces your outstanding loan amount, lowering the interest due. While your Home Loan EMI remains unchanged, a larger portion goes towards repaying the principal. 

How does the payment of EMI towards your Home Loan reduce your Tax liability?

Provisions under the Income Tax Act, 1961, allow Indian nationals to claim Income Tax relief on the payments made towards repayment of Home Loans. This is subject to amendments to the Act.

What does pre-EMI interest mean in the context of Home Loans?

Pre-EMI interest denotes the interest accrued on the disbursed Loan amount before the EMI payments begin. This interest is relevant either during the construction stage or prior to complete disbursement of the Loan amount. 

How do I change my Home Loan EMI date?

You can request your bank to change the Home Loan EMI date by submitting a written application or visiting the branch. 

What is the maximum amount that can be borrowed as a Home Loan?

The maximum Home Loan amount that can be availed online from ICICI Bank is ₹ 10 crore. The final loan amount is determined by factors like your income, age and your credit score.

What is a Home Loan amortisation schedule?

The amortisation schedule is a table that offers a complete breakdown of the Home Loan EMI. The table shows the following:

Outstanding balance before payment of each EMI

Payable EMI 

The component of the EMI towards repayment of the principal

The component of the EMI towards payment of the interest 

The outstanding Loan amount after the payment of each EMI.

When do Home Loan EMIs start?

Repayment of a home loan usually starts a month after you receive the loan amount. The specific start date is mentioned in your Loan agreement. During this period, you might be required to pay pre-EMI interest depending on the terms of your loan and whether you are already paying EMIs or waiting for the full disbursement.

What are the essential inputs required for the ICICI Bank Home Loan EMI calculator?

The three mandatory inputs are loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure in months or years. These details help calculate EMI estimate, total interest payable, and total repayment amount.

How accurate are the results from the ICICI Bank Home Loan EMI calculator?

The results are close estimates based on the values entered. Actual EMI and total cost may vary due to floating-rate changes, processing fees, prepayments, insurance, taxes, or other charges. Users should treat the output as indicative and confirm the final quote with ICICI Bank.

Does the ICICI Bank Home Loan EMI calculator include property taxes and insurance?

No. The calculator estimates EMI using loan amount, interest rate, and tenure only. Property tax, home insurance, and other homeownership costs are not included and should be budgeted separately. Optionally link to the Home Buyers Guide for broader home-buying cost planning.

What are the benefits of using a home loan calculator before applying for a loan?

It helps estimate EMI in advance, understand total interest cost, compare loan options, assess affordability, and plan finances better before starting the loan application process.

How does the ICICI Bank Home Loan EMI calculator work?

The EMI calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula. Enter the desired loan amount, tenure in years, and applicable interest rate to instantly estimate the monthly EMI.

How much Home Loan can I get on a ₹60,000 salary?

The Home Loan amount you may be eligible for on a ₹60,000 monthly salary depends on factors such as your existing financial obligations, credit score, loan tenure and interest rate. Based on general eligibility criteria, banks may offer a Home Loan where the EMI remains within a manageable portion of your monthly income. A Home Loan Eligibility Calculator can help estimate the maximum loan amount you're eligible for.

What is EMI on a ₹50 lakh Home Loan?

The EMI for a ₹50 lakh Home Loan depends on the applicable interest rate and repayment tenure. For example, a longer tenure may reduce the monthly EMI but increase the total interest payable over time. Using a Home Loan EMI Calculator can help borrowers estimate EMIs instantly based on different loan amounts, tenures and interest rates.

What is the EMI on a ₹1.5 Crore Home Loan?

The EMI on a ₹1.5 Crore Home Loan varies according to the interest rate, repayment tenure and loan structure chosen by the borrower. A Home Loan EMI Calculator helps compare repayment options and provides an estimate of monthly instalments before applying for the loan.

What is the Home Loan repayment schedule?

A Home Loan repayment schedule is a detailed breakdown of monthly EMI payments over the loan tenure. It shows the principal and interest components of each EMI, outstanding loan balance, and repayment timeline. Borrowers can use a Home Loan EMI Calculator to understand their repayment structure more effectively.

How much EMI for a ₹70 lakh Home Loan?

The EMI for a ₹70 lakh Home Loan depends on factors such as loan tenure and applicable interest rate. Choosing a shorter tenure may increase the EMI but reduce the overall interest outgo, while a longer tenure may lower monthly instalments. A Home Loan EMI Calculator can help estimate suitable repayment options.

How much EMI for a ₹40 lakh Home Loan for 20 years?

The EMI for a ₹40 lakh Home Loan with a 20-year tenure depends on the applicable interest rate offered by the bank. Borrowers can use a Home Loan EMI Calculator to calculate approximate monthly instalments and understand the total repayment amount over the loan tenure.

What is the EMI for a ₹50 lakh Home Loan?

The EMI for a ₹50 lakh Home Loan is influenced by factors such as loan tenure, interest rate and repayment structure. A Home Loan EMI Calculator helps borrowers compare EMI amounts across different tenures and plan repayments more efficiently.

What is the EMI for a ₹1 Crore Home Loan?

The EMI for a 
₹1 Crore Home Loan
 varies based on the applicable interest rate and repayment tenure selected. Borrowers can use a Home Loan EMI Calculator to estimate monthly installments, compare repayment options and plan their finances before applying for the loan.
