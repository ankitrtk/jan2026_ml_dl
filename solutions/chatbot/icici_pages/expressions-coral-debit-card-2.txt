Expressions Coral Business Debit Card FAQs

What does an Expressions Coral Business Debit Card from ICICI Bank offer customers?

The Expressions Coral Business Debit Card from ICICI Bank lets you personalise your card design with ICICI Bank's secure debit card access to savings accounts as per your choice.

What are the steps for obtaining an Expressions Coral Debit Card?

The Expressions Coral Debit Card application process occurs either on ICICI Bank’s i-mobile,website or at the bank's nearest branch locations. Users obtain the Expressions Debit Card by selecting their desired design from the options, followed by KYC verification.

What fees do customers need to pay annually to use their Expressions Debit Card?

Yes, an annual fee may apply. Customers can find the precise fees for their debit card through the Schedule of Charges on the ICICI Bank website, although the rates may be adjusted according to selected design features.

Does the Expressions Debit Card allow linking to any savings account at ICICI Bank?

Any eligible ICICI Bank savings account can receive the Expressions Coral Debit Card for link establishment.

What does the Expressions Debit Card enable and restrict in terms of transactions?

Your account type and the specific Expressions design variant you selected restrict daily card transactions. Limits for this debit card can be reviewed or adjusted through iMobile Pay and ICICI Bank Internet Banking.

Can users make international transactions using this debit card?

This card has international buying capabilities. You must activate this international transaction service via Internet Banking or by contacting ICICI customer support.

What steps should I take when my Expressions Debit Card is lost or stolen?

When your Expressions Debit Card goes missing, you should use the iMobile Pay app and Internet Banking tools or ICICI Bank Customer Care to block it. A lost debit card replacement will be provided upon customer request.

Can I switch my current Expressions Debit Card image after getting it?

The ability to change images exists only during new Expression Coral Debit Cards issuance. A new Expressions Debit Card must be applied when customers want to update their account design.

What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

To enjoy complimentary domestic lounge access, you must spend ₹10,000 or more on your Expression Coral Business Debit Card in a calendar quarter. This entitles you to avail the lounge access benefit in the following calendar quarter. The benefit is exclusively applicable to the primary Cardholder.
