Connected Banking

Introducing ICICI Bank Connected Banking

ICICI Bank Connected Banking integrates your bank account directly with your ERP, accounting, payroll, or HRMS platform. Make vendor payments, salary disbursements, and reimbursements from a single interface without switching portals. With automated reconciliation and real-time updates, it reduces manual effort, improves accuracy, and helps businesses manage payments efficiently and securely.

Connected Banking FAQs

What is Connected Banking?

Connected Banking is an industry-first initiative that allows businesses to manage banking transactions directly from their ERP, accounting, payroll, or HRMS platforms, while automating the reconciliation process.

How does reconciliation work in Connected Banking?

When a payment is initiated from a pre-integrated platform, the transaction is automatically reconciled after successful processing, removing the need for manual intervention.

What are the pre-requisites for Connected Banking?

To use Connected Banking, you need:

An ICICI Bank Current Account

Access to a Connected Banking partner platform

Active Corporate Internet Banking credentials

Can I use the same Current Account for multiple ERP or accounting platforms?

Yes. A single ICICI Bank Current Account can be registered for Connected Banking across multiple ERP, accounting, or HRMS platforms.

Is Connected Banking secure?

Yes. ICICI Bank Connected Banking uses end-to-end encryption, and all transactions are secured through two-factor authentication.

What types of platforms does ICICI Bank support?

Connected Banking is available across:

Accounting platforms

HRMS and payroll platforms

Inventory management platforms

Who should I contact for support?

For assistance with ICICI Bank Connected Banking services, you can:

Call 
1800 1080

Contact your ICICI Bank Relationship Manager

How do I initiate Connected Banking services?

You can raise a request directly from your ERP platform. For guidance, contact your ERP support team or your ICICI Bank Relationship Manager.
