About Personal Loans in Ahmedabad

How to avail a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad?

A Personal Loan in Ahmedabad is an unsecured loan that helps you meet urgent financial needs such as medical expenses, travel, wedding costs, education, or home renovation. ICICI Bank offers a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad with flexible repayment tenures and competitive interest rates. Since no collateral is required, the process is simple and convenient for eligible salaried and self-employed individuals.

What are the benefits of a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad?

It offers fast approvals, minimal documentation, attractive interest rates and flexible repayment schedules. Borrowers can avail up to ₹50 lakh based on eligibility.

What are the eligibility criteria for a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad?

Applicants must be between 20 and 58 years of age, earn at least ₹30,000 per month and have at least 2 years of total work experience along with 1 year at current residence.

What are the fees and charges?

 Processing fee up to 2.50 per cent, prepayment charges as applicable, late payment charges of 24 per cent per annum, bounce charges of ₹500 and NMMP charges of ₹800 per month.

Personal Loan FAQs

Who can apply for a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad?

Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and business owners who meet the eligibility criteria can apply.

How much loan amount can I get?

Eligible applicants can get a loan amount of up to ₹50 lakh based on income and credit profile.

What is the repayment tenure?

ICICI Bank offers repayment tenures ranging from 12 to 72 months.

Can I apply for a Personal Loan online?

Yes. You can apply online or through the iMobile app with minimal documentation.

Who can apply for a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad?

Salaried professionals, self-employed individuals and business owners residing in Ahmedabad can 
apply for a Personal Loan
, subject to eligibility criteria based on age, income stability and credit history. Applicants must meet ICICI Bank’s eligibility criteria to avail a Personal Loan in Ahmedabad.
