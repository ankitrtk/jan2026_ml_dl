Features of NRI RD

The minimum tenure for an NRE Recurring Deposit is 1 year, while for an NRO Recurring Deposit, it is 6 months. 
Tenure can be extended in multiples of 3 months, with a maximum limit of 10 years. 
Interest rates are subject to change without prior notice. Please check the latest rates before opening an RD.

Penalty and charges on NRI RD installments

A penalty of ₹12 per ₹1,000 is charged for delayed installments.
A part of a month is considered as a full month for penalty calculation.
The penalty is deducted from the total interest payable at maturity.

Premature withdrawal – NRI RD interest rates

Interest is calculated based on the period the deposit remained with ICICI Bank.

FAQs on NRI RD Interest Rates

Is the interest earned on NRE and NRO Recurring Deposits taxable in India?

Interest earned on NRE Recurring Deposits is not taxable in India.

Interest earned on NRO Recurring Deposits is taxable and subject to TDS.

Can I close my NRE or NRO Recurring Deposit before maturity?

Yes, both NRE and NRO Recurring Deposits can be closed prematurely.

Interest will be paid at the rate applicable for the period the deposit remained with the bank, after deducting any penalties.

In case of NRE RD closed before 12 months, no interest is payable.

What is the penalty for delay in installment payment for Recurring Deposits? 

A penalty of ₹12 per ₹1,000 per delayed installment is applicable for both NRE and NRO Recurring Deposits.

What is the minimum and maximum tenure for NRE and NRO Recurring Deposits?

NRE RD: Minimum tenure is 1 year and maximum is 10 years (in multiples of 3 months).

NRO RD: Minimum tenure is 6 months and maximum is 10 years (in multiples of 3 months).

Can I appoint a nominee for my Recurring Deposit account? 

Yes, you can appoint one nominee per RD account. You can also change or update the nominee by submitting the required forms. Different nominees can be assigned for different RDs.

What interest rates does ICICI Bank offer on NRI RD accounts?

NRI RD interest rates at ICICI Bank vary based on the deposit tenure and the type of account selected, such as NRE or NRO. NRI RD interest rates start from 4.5% p.a. for a 6-month tenure, as per the applicable rate slabs. The rates may differ across deposit tenures. Since NRE RD interest rates are subject to periodic revision, customers are advised to refer to the latest rate details available on the ICICI Bank website before opening or renewing a deposit.

How is the interest on NRI Recurring Deposit calculated?

Interest on an NRI Recurring Deposit

is calculated on monthly installments deposited over the chosen tenure. Each instalment earns interest for a different duration, depending on when it is deposited. The final maturity amount reflects the total of monthly contributions and accumulated interest. NRI RD interest rates applicable at the time of booking are used to determine the overall returns on the deposit.

Can I close my NRI RD before maturity?

Yes, NRI Recurring Deposits can be closed before maturity, subject to ICICI Bank's applicable terms and conditions. Premature closure may attract a revised interest payout based on the completed tenure, which could be lower than the original rate. Customers holding NRE or NRO RDs should review the NRE RD Account interest rates and premature closure guidelines to understand the impact before proceeding.
