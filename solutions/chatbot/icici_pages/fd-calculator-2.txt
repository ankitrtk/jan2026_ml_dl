Features of the ICICI Bank NRI FD Calculator

The ICICI Bank NRI FD Calculator is a planning tool designed to help NRIs estimate the maturity value and interest earnings from Fixed Deposits in a structured, transparent manner. The NRI FD calculator allows users to assess different deposit scenarios based on tenure and FD type, without manual calculations. Here are some of its features:

Calculates the estimated maturity amount for NRI Fixed Deposits (NRE / NRO)

Displays interest earned basis the selected tenure

Supports both NRE and NRO Fixed Deposits

Enables comparison by adjusting the deposit amount or duration (tenure)

Provides instant results to support informed financial planning.

By offering quick and clear projections, the Calculator helps users plan their Fixed Deposits with greater clarity before proceeding.

NRI FD Calculator Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is an FD Calculator?

The FD Calculator is a tool to calculate the interest and maturity amount of Fixed Deposits based on tenure, amount and prevailing interest rates.

How do I use the FD Calculator?

Select FD type: Cumulative or Payout (Quarterly or Monthly)

Enter the FD amount

View the applicable interest rate, interest amount, maturity date and maturity amount.

How is FD interest calculated?

Cumulative FD – Interest is compounded quarterly and reinvested

Quarterly Payout FD – Simple interest is paid at the end of each quarter

Monthly Payout FD – Discounted interest is paid at the end of each month

Short-term FD – Simple interest is calculated for the tenure.

Calculate Your Returns Now!

Is the interest earned on NRO FDs taxable?

Yes, interest earned on NRO Fixed Deposits is taxable in India under the applicable income tax laws. Tax is deducted at source in accordance with the prevailing regulations. The NRI FD Calculator shows the indicative interest value but does not calculate tax implications. Users should consider taxation separately while planning investments.

Can I use the NRI FD Calculator for FCNR Deposits?

No, the NRI FD Calculator is intended only for estimating returns on NRE and NRO Fixed Deposits. FCNR Deposits are maintained in foreign currency and are subject to different interest structures. To understand FCNR returns, users should refer to information specific to FCNR Deposits that is available on the ICICI Bank website.
