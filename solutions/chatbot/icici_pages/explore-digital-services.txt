Demat

Explore Digital Services

Explore digital services of your Demat Account

If you have linked your Demat Account to your Net Banking account, please log in to access your Demat Account online. You can avail a wide range of services through Net Banking and the ICICI Bank's iMobile app.

Demat Services available through Net Banking

Transaction statement

You can view transactions in your account for a period of 15 days. Various filters are available in transaction statement for quick results. You can view transactions for:

Specific date range

Specific ISIN

Specific account type

Specific transaction type

Specific counter party

You can use one of the above mentioned filters or a combination of all the above mentioned filters to get the required transactions in the quickest manner.

Personal details

Provides various client related information like client name, client address, Joint Holder's names, account type, bank account details for a given client id.

Status of transaction

You can also view status of your transactions by using Status of Transaction. Once again various filters like:

Transaction type

Date range

Document number

Status

ISIN code

Execution date and

Settlement number

Settlement calendar

Informs you about pay-in and pay-out date for a settlement on all the exchanges electronically connected to NSDL. The facility keeps you updated on settlement cycle and delivery cycle for a given settlement.

ISIN lookup

If you are not aware of the ISIN of a particular scrip you can use ISIN Search instead of searching for this information. In addition to ISIN "ISIN Lookup" page will also inform you about face value, status of the given ISIN and if the ISIN is available for demat.

Customer ledger

All your financial transactions pertaining to your Demat Account like Bills, Payments, waivers etc. are available in Customer Ledger.

Bill details

If you want to know details of a specific bill then Bill Query is the best option for you. You can get details of any bill generated for your selected Demat Account using this link.

TIFD booklet request

Request for physical TIFD or IDT booklets with the ‘TIFD booklet request’ link. Select the booklets and the booklet size you wish and a personalised booklet for the account will be delivered to your door step by courier.

E-Instructions

One of the salient features of ICICI Bank Demat Services on ICICIBank.com is e-Instructions. This is a facility that allows you to submit TIFD or IDT and Pledge instructions from the comfort of your home or office to Five pre-notified target accounts. You can also transfer your shares online to pre-notified target accounts by sing e-Instructions.

With e-Instruction you can

Transfer your TIFDs and IDTs through Net Banking on 
www.icicibank.com

Advantages

No need to fill up physical TIFD or IDT or Pledge slips

To register for e-Instruction facility, visit the nearest Demat enabled 
ICICI Bank branch
 with your proof of identity.

To avail of e-instruction facility you should register for e-instruction.

The registration is required for enabling the account for e-Instruction. 
Click here (PDF, 47.8 KB)
 for e-Instruction.

As a safeguard against erroneous transfers or pledges being made to unknown accounts, using e-Instruction, you will be able to transfer or pledge securities only to pre-notified accounts (up to a maximum of five). You need to specify these accounts during registration.

Target accounts can be added or deleted or modified (within the limit of maximum five accounts) using the 
prescribed form (PDF, 47.8 KB)
 for the same.

The registration form should be submitted in person by at least one of the account holders who should sign in front of the branch official.

Further, the holder presenting themself in person should make their original proof of identity available at the time of submission of the registration form for verification and submit a copy of that proof.

Deadlines for submitting e-Instruction

Please submit your instructions well in time. For same day execution, e-Instructions will be accepted on best-effort basis as per the following deadlines:
