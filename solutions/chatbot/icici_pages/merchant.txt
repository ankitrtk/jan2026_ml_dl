About Prepaid Cards

What is a Prepaid Card?

A Prepaid Card is a payment card that allows users to load funds in advance and use it for transactions until the balance is exhausted. Unlike credit or debit cards, Prepaid Cards are not linked to a bank account and do not require a credit check. They offer convenience and security for various financial needs, including travel, online shopping and corporate expenses.

How long is my ICICI Bank Prepaid Card valid for?

The validity of the card is 5 years from the date of issue i.e. "Valid From' as printed on the card.

Where can I check the balance on the card?

You can check the balance on the card with the help of the below options:

Calling our Customer Care

At any Visa enabled ATM, across India

Prepaid Card FAQs

What will happen if the card gets closed after 5 years?

Customer can request for replacement card by calling our ICICI Bank Customer care at 1800 1080

Do I need to keep a minimum balance on the Card?

 There is no need to keep a minimum balance on the Card. However, keeping a healthy balance on your Card will help you utilise it, in the case of an emergency.

Can I get a refund for the unutilised amount instead of a replacement card?

Yes, you may request a refund, subject to the Bank's discretion based on the type of Prepaid Card and other factors.

What if I forget the ATM PIN of the Card?

 If you forget your PIN, you can generate a new PIN with the help of the below options: - Call our Customer Care at 1800 1080

Log in to the Prepaid Card Self Care Portal using the below link: 
https://inrprepaid.icicibank.com/customer/login.jsp
