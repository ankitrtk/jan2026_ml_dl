Update-KYC

Update your KYC details

Overview

Banks need to periodically update the customer identification documents of their account holders in their records to adhere to Know Your Customer (KYC) guidelines. Customers are required to undergo the Re-KYC process and submit relevant documents to comply with RBI guidelines.

Update KYC Now

No Change request for Re-KYC

In case of no change in your KYC details such as address, passport, NRI status proof and FATCA/CRS declaration then a self declaration to be sent from your registered email ID to nri@icici.bank.in stating ‘No change in KYC information’ to complete the Re-KYC process of your accounts with the bank

The passport and NRI status proof (Visa, residence permits etc.) should be valid in the bank’s records.
