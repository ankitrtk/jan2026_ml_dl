FAQ on NRI Mortgage Loan

Can NRI/OCI customer apply for a Home Loan in India?

Yes, NRIs and OCIs can apply for Home Loans to buy or construct a home in India.

What is the maximum loan amount for an NRI Home Loan?

There is no capping to the loan amount which can be availed by NRI. Loan amount depends on eligibility.

Can a Resident Indian (RI) or another NRI be a co-applicant for an NRI Mortgage Loan?

Yes, both NRIs and Resident Indians can be co-applicants.

Is an Aadhaar Card mandatory for an NRI/OCI customer?

No, an Aadhaar Card is not mandatory. however it can be used as address proof if available.

Is an NRE/NRO Account mandatory to apply for a Mortgage Loan?

No, but an active NRE/NRO Account is required at the time of loan disbursement.

How do I calculate my eligibility for a Home Loan as an NRI/OCI customer?

You can use the Eligibility Calculator for precise information.

Can an NRI/OCI customer from the USA apply for a Home Loan in India?

Yes, an NRI/OCI customer from the USA can apply for a Home Loan in India.

What is the difference between applying for a Mortgage Loan online vs offline for NRI/OCI customers? 

Offline: You need to be in India and submit physical documents like self-attested application form, KYC and income proof

Online: Upload scanned copies through ICICI Bank Express Home Loan (EHL) platform. Salaried NRI/OCI customers can apply as a ‘New to Bank' customer or an ‘Existing customer’. 
Click here
 to start your Mortgage Loan application.

Can a Person of Indian Origin (PIO) apply for a Home Loan?

Yes, a PIO is eligible to apply for a Home Loan in India.

How can an NRI/OCI customer apply for a Mortgage Loan?

You can apply online through our Express Home Loan platform.

 to start the application process. Our team will get in touch with you for further processing.
