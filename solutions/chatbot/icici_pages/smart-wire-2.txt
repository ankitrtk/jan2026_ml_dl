Smart Wire is one of the fastest online solutions to receive SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication) based Wire Transfer to your account held with ICICI Bank India. Benefits of Smart Wire transfers

Hassle-free

Sending pre-filled wire instructions to the remitter making things simple and hassle-free.

Fast processing

Share remittance details and the purpose of remittance in advance for fast processing

Access to real time FX rate

Get an option to pre-book exchange rates, if enabled

Enhanced experience

Enhance your experience with confirmed exchange rates, online tracking and seamless processing with Smart wire

Global reach

Funds can be received in Indian account from anywhere across the world.

Important Things to Know

Eligibilty

Available to all ICICI Bank Resident and Non-Resident Indian customers having Savings Account

No Limits for remittance

There are no upper limits on transfer amounts, making Smart Wire a flexible option for all types of remittances

Charges Applicable

Zero charges levied by ICICI Bank

Sender Bank and/or correspondent bank may levy nominal charge. We recommend using OUR charge code to minimise charge.

The applicable GST will be levied on the converted currency in accordance with the provisions of the GST Act. 
Click here
 for GST slab details

*OUR – A charge code whereby the sender of the money pays all the fees and charges related to the transaction.

About

Smart Wire

Smart wire is one of the safest online solutions for receiving SWIFT based Inward Wire transfers for ICICI Bank customers (Resident Indians & Non-Resident Indians). Receive funds in ICICI Bank Account from any country.

Pre-filled form

Pre-filled form reduces errors and ensure faster processing for a smooth experience.

Convenience

Submit all the information such as foreign currency, amount, purpose of remittance and digital declarations (if required) in advance, for seamless processing of transactions.

24x7 FX-Rate Booking

Book your exchange rates anytime, even outside banking hours. Lock in your rates for better control over currency rates and enjoy transparent conversion with GST and credit details.

FAQs

Which currencies are available for transfer through Smart Wire?

Total 22 accepted currencies available on Smart Wire, it includes:

USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, CAD, AED, CHF, ZAR, DKK, PLN, HKD, SAR, NOK, BHD, THB, QAR, KWD, SEK, NZD, JPY, OMR

Which currencies are available for FX Deal Booking?

FX (Foreign Exchange) Deal Booking is available for:

USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, CAD, AED

How can I activate Deal Booking for my ICICI Bank account?

To enable the Deal Booking feature:

Contact your Relationship Manager Or

Visit any ICICI Bank Branch.

How can I share Correspondent Bank details with my remitter?

Follow these steps:

Generate a Smart Wire Request using Internet Banking or iMobile App.

The system will auto-generate remittance and Correspondent Bank details.

Enter the remitter’s name and email ID to send the details directly.

Can NRI customers place request for Term Deposits using Smart Wire?

Yes! NRI customers can place request for following Term Deposits using Smart Wire:

FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) Deposits

NRE (Non-Resident External) / NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) Deposits (in INR)

Can I submit declarations and purpose of remittance in advance?

Yes, Smart Wire allows you to submit:

Declarations digitally

Purpose of remittance

Remitting currency & amount online before the transfer is initiated.

Can I use Smart Wire to receive funds from sale of shares/ESOPs (Employee Stock Ownership Plan) of overseas companies?

Yes, Smart Wire supports inward remittances for proceeds from overseas sale of shares/ESOPs.
