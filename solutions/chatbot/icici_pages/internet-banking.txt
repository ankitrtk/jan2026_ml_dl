About NRI Net Banking

What is NRI Net Banking?

NRI Net Banking is an online banking service offered by ICICI Bank for Non-Resident Indians, 
Persons of Indian Origin and Overseas Citizens of India
. It allows customers to manage their NRI Accounts from anywhere in the world. Through this service, customers can access and operate NRE, NRO and FCNR accounts online. They can check balances, transfer funds, open deposits, pay bills and place service requests without visiting a branch. It provides a secure and convenient way to manage banking needs remotely.

Key Features of NRI Net Banking

Net Banking
 offers several features that make managing your accounts from abroad simple and convenient. Some of the key features include:

Transfer funds between NRE and NRO accounts and send money to other bank accounts in India.

Pay utility bills and credit card bills online.

Open and manage fixed deposits digitally.

Update personal details, download statements and place service requests.

Track investments and monitor account transactions securely.

These features help customers manage their banking needs easily without visiting a branch.

Can I Use NRI Net Banking from Abroad?

Net Banking can be accessed from anywhere in the world, allowing customers to manage their NRI accounts while staying overseas. Users can log in with their credentials to view balances, transfer funds and make payments online at any time.

NRI Net Banking Security Features

Strong security measures are built into the online banking platform to protect customer information and transactions. ICICI Bank uses encrypted access to help keep data secure during online sessions. Two-factor authentication, including One-Time Password (OTP) verification, is used to authorise logins and transactions. Additional checks may be required when accessing the account from a new device. Customers are also encouraged to follow safe login practices to maintain secure and uninterrupted banking access. These measures help ensure that overseas Net Banking remains safe to use from anywhere.

NRI Net Banking FAQ's

How to access NRI Net Banking from abroad?

You can access NRI Net Banking from abroad by visiting the official ICICI Bank website and logging in with your user ID and password. Ensure your mobile number is updated to receive OTPs for authentication. After logging in, you can view balances, transfer funds and manage services securely. Always use a trusted device and a secure internet connection to protect your account while accessing NRI Net Banking overseas.

How can I register for NRI Net Banking?

You can activate access using your User ID and password. If forgotten, retrieve them via the login page by selecting ‘Get User ID’ or ‘Forgot Password’.

What services can I access online?

You can view balances, open Fixed Deposits, transfer funds, pay bills, block cards, request statements, and more.

Are there any charges for using NRI Net Banking?

No, the service is completely free for all NRI customers. 

Is my account safe while using online banking abroad?

Yes. With two-factor authentication, OTPs, and 256-bit encryption, your account is secure—even overseas.

What if my Debit Card or password is lost?

You can request a replacement Debit Card and reset your password online through the secure login portal.

Can I transfer funds internationally using Net Banking?

Yes, you can repatriate funds from your NRE or NRO account through the online platform.
