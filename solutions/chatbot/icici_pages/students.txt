3-in-1 Account for Students FAQs

What is 3-in-1 Account for Students?

3-in-1 Account for Students provides a wide range of financial services and resources that will help you to efficiently manage money throughout your academic journey and even beyond your campus when you transition into the professional world.

What is the minimum monthly balance requirement for 3-in-1 Account for Students?

3-in-1 Account for Students comes with no requirement of maintaining any minimum balance.

What are the benefits of 3-in-1 Account for Students?

Choose your Complimentary Credit Card from a range of cards

No Minimum Balance required in your Savings Account

Digital Banking Solutions via ICICI Bank's iMobile app

Easy Investment Options for stocks, mutual funds and more

Education Loan for studies in India and abroad

Complimentary Air Accident Insurance of ₹50,000

Premium Alumni Credit Card* offered on graduation

Exciting Offers on your favourite brands

Access to monthly editions of The Orange Book

How to Open a 3-in-1 Account for Students?

You can open 3-in-1 Account for Students, online by clicking here or you may visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch

Is PAN card mandatory for opening 3-in-1 Account for Students?

PAN card is mandatory for opening of 3-in-1 Account for Students digitally. In case you do not have a PAN card, you can approach the nearest ICICI Bank branch and open the account through Form 60.

What is the eligibility criteria for Credit Card provided for students? Is there any joining or annual fee?

Students from selected institutes will be offered lifetime complimentary credit card with no joining or annual fee

My Institute name is not present in the dropdown list of colleges. What should I do?

Please visit your nearest ICICI Bank branch to know if your college is a part of the selected list of institutes. You can open your 3-in-1 Account for Students through the branch itself.

In case your college is not part of selected institute, you can open an Insta Savings Account and enjoy its benefits. 
Click here to know more

Do I need consent from a parent or guardian to open a student bank account?

No consent from a parent is required for opening your 3-in-1 Account for Students

Can a 3-in-1 Account for Students be opened jointly with a parent or guardian?

Yes, joint account can be opened

Can I link my Savings Account for student to a student loan?

Yes, you can link your student loan with your Savings Account

Will I get a chequebook on student account opening?

Yes, you will receive a chequebook while opening 3-in-1 Account for Students

What are the different channels through which I can access my 3-in-1 Account for Students?

You can access your 3-in-1 Account for Students digitally through Internet Banking iMobile Pay App

How can I update my mobile number for 3-in-1 Account for Students?

You can update your mobile number through any of the following channels:

ICICI Bank ATM:

Visit the nearest ICICI Bank ATM > Select ‘More options’ > Register your mobile > Enter the mobile number. Your mobile number will be updated in the bank record immediately.

ICICI Bank branch:

Please visit the nearest ICICI Bank branch, along with your original photo ID proof. Once the request is made, your mobile number will be updated in the bank record immediately

How can I update my email id for 3-in-1 Account for Students?

To change/update your e-mail id for your Bank Account, please follow the below steps.

Net Banking:

Please log in to www.icicibank.com with your User ID and Password > My Accounts > Bank Accounts > Account Statement by e-mail > Enter the required details and submit the request. The e-mail ID will be updated immediately.
