World Business Debit Card FAQs

What are the privileges available through the World Business Debit Card?

Using the World Business Debit Card entitles cardholders to dining, hotel bookings and travel privileges.

What documents do I need to submit to avail a World Business Debit Card?

To avail a World Business Debit Card, open a Wealth Current Account with ICICI Bank and submit the necessary documents, including proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, etc.), Proof of Address (Aadhaar, Utility Bills, Passport) and Company Incorporation related details.

If required, provide proof of income or wealth, such as bank statements or salary slips. Ensure all documents are valid and up to date. Contact ICICI Bank or visit their website for details.

What is the daily withdrawal limit on the World Business Debit Card?

The card offers a daily domestic ATM withdrawal limit of ₹2,00,000 and an international withdrawal limit of ₹2,00,000. Limits help cater to both local and international business needs.

What is the daily purchase limit on the World Business Debit Card?

The daily purchase limit at retail outlets and on merchant websites using the World Business Debit Card for domestic transactions is ₹7,50,000 and ₹2,50,000 for international transactions.

How can I track my spending on the World Business Debit Card?

You can easily track your spending through ICICI Bank's mobile banking app (InstaBiz), which provides real-time transaction alerts and detailed transaction history.

Can my Debit Card be used for transactions outside India?

The World Business Debit Card can be used for transactions outside India.

* 
How many complimentary airport lounge visits do I get each quarter with the ICICI Bank World Business Debit Card?

For Current Accounts opened on or before 31st July 2025, You get a maximum of 2 domestic airport lounge visits each quarter.

For Current Accounts opened on or after 1st August 2025, You get a maximum of 2 domestic airport lounge visits each quarter, with minimum spends of  ₹10,000 quarterly or meeting program eligibility of deposits & balances of ₹. 10 Lakhs quarterly or Relationship value of ₹50 lakhs (Deposits + Account balance + Investments + Demat holdings) quarterly.

What are the advantages of using ICICI Bank Debit Cards?

ICICI Bank debit cards allow you to make payments using funds in your savings / current account. Unlike a credit card, a debit card uses your existing funds to make payments rather than you availing credit.

A debit card can be used for a range of functions. Every ICICI savings / current account holder receives a debit card. There are different account types for different customers.

With our debit card, your account security is our priority. Every transaction made using your ICICI Bank Debit Card at retail outlets or ATMs is protected by a four-digit PIN. For added security and to safeguard against fraudulent activity, the card also employs a dynamic OTP for online transactions.
